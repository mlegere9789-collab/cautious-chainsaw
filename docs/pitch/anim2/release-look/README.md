# Release look: two static options for the Gettysburg battle map

Two looks for the Battle Studio, each a full page you can open, move and click, built as a fork of the first round's engine on the real aerial. The owner asked for a look that is not "two boxes per unit, no hierarchy, tiny text and badges, blank grid ground, recolours of one layout". These are two different answers, not two recolours:

| | **A, Standards** | **B, Glyph** |
|---|---|---|
| the mark | a planted standard: cream pole on a foot, a flag in the side's cloth, a shadow on the ground | an editorial glyph: a disc (Union) or rounded square (Confederate) with its words on the sheet |
| side | the cloth AND the way it flies (Union right, Confederate left) | the colour AND the shape family |
| arm | the end of the cloth (square, swallow-tail for cavalry, arrow for artillery) | one white stroke inside (none, slash, dot) |
| rank | pole and flag height (17 / 25, 34, 44, 56 px) | glyph size and rings (10 / 13 px, then one, two, three rings) |
| strength | a slim bar under the flag (Modelled) | a gauge arc round the glyph (Modelled) |
| crowd | a larger flag with the count on it | a stacked glyph with the count in it |
| ground | the aerial under one warm grade, soft relief, a little haze | the aerial ghosted into cool white paper, crisp relief |
| chrome | warm smoked glass, cream ink, brass hairline, angular, a flag-tab on every card | white paper, ink, a serif for what is read, thin rules, a 3 px rule on every card |
| both | the casualty memory (ground marks scaled to documented unit losses, time and place Modelled), every popup kept, the overview map restyled, a token JSON each | |

**Start here.** `shots/compare-A-vs-B.jpg` (the two side by side, six scenes), then `shots/a-standards/final/` and `shots/b-glyph/final/`: the opening view at zoom 13.5 at a mid-battle tick and at the Day 3 assault (`s1a`, `s1b`, each with its 100 % `-crop` and 200 % `-crop-200pct` of the busiest 600 x 400 px), Wheatfield and Rose Woods at zoom 15 (`s2`), Cemetery Hill at 15.5 (`s3`), the close-up at 16.5 with a selected unit and its Inspect card (`s4`), the phone 390 x 844 at 14.5 with the bottom sheet (`s5`), the event card in situ (`s6`), the story card (`s6b`), the legend (`s7`), the hover tooltip (`s8`), the overview map in the rail (`s9`), and `popups/` (the other popups, the Chronicle, the day banner and clock, the narration, the panels, the phone's lane, sheets, legend and menu). `ITERATIONS.md` is the log of what I saw and changed in six rounds; `SPEC.md` is the specification and, in its section 9, every measurement with its evidence.

**Open it.**

```
node tools/serve.mjs 9400                     # serves docs/pitch/anim2 on 127.0.0.1 (the page and the basemap's 11 MB of public-domain tiles)
http://127.0.0.1:9400/release-look/option-a-standards.html
http://127.0.0.1:9400/release-look/option-b-glyph.html
#   the top bar's two-way switch (A "Field", B "Survey") flips the look at the same tick and camera
#   ?tune=1 every dial of the engine   ?skinset={"standards":{"pole.brigade":38}} one token, live   ?dev=1 the metrics button
node build.mjs                                # regenerates both pages and both token files (--check: exit 1 when stale)
```

Both pages are 2.4 MB, readable (not minified), with no network request but the basemap's tile files next to them (`../basemap/assets/`, or `?tiles=<folder>`).

**Changes to the engine in this folder** (everything else is the first round's, as it was):

1. `src/skins/*` (new): the two skins, their token files, `common.mjs`; `kit.mjs` lets a skin override the legend sentences (`legendText`); `contract.mjs`'s recording context learned `ellipse` and the cropped `drawImage`; `semantics.mjs` gained the `memory` channel.
2. `src/fx/memory.mjs`, `app.mjs` (new): the casualty memory, built from `data/memory.json` (inlined in the page by `build.mjs`).
3. `src/ground.mjs`: the two grounds; `src/styles/index.mjs`: the two styles; `src/tours/library.mjs`: the tours follow the styles.
4. `src/layout/words.mjs`, `src/layout/tiles.mjs`: crowd words without "of", "contested" for "of unknown side", the order of battle's designation for a battalion or corps artillery node (it printed `C-ART-II-3`); `src/layout/text.mjs`: formations and crowds in 13 px bold (the two map sizes are 12 and 13 px).
5. `src/popups/minimap.mjs`: the overview takes its colours from the option's stylesheet and shows the ground marks.
6. `src/ui/quiet.css`: the brand and the definition lists on the interface scale (11, 13, 17 px); `src/ui/look-*.css` (new).
7. `build.mjs`: writes both pages and both token files; inlines the memory and the serif.
8. Tests: `skin-look.test.mjs` replaces the first round's skin test; `build.test.mjs` and `assemble.test.mjs` follow the two pages, the two styles and the 46-second Showcase of the first round (marked FORK).

**Nothing outside `docs/pitch/anim2/release-look/` and `tmp/look/` was written; no product code was touched; no request leaves the machine but the harness's own local server.**

# d1 Quiet Cartographer, polish round 1

A redesign of how the Battle Studio draws a battle: one small mark per unit standing on the unit's place, a standard for each formation or crowd, haloed lettering instead of boxes, on the real aerial. The design language is `SPEC.md`; the log of what was seen and changed is `ITERATIONS.md` (round 1 of the polish pass is the last section).

**Look first.** `shots/round-1/s1a-opening-field-z13.5-t551.png` (the opening view), `s1a-...-crop.png` and `s1a-...-crop-200pct.png` (the busiest 600 x 400 px at DPR 2 and magnified to 200 %, nearest neighbour, to inspect edges), then `s1b` to `s8` (the scenes of the brief at DPR 2, each with a `-crop`), `specimen-day.png` and `specimen-night.png` (every mark and state at 1x to 3x), `strip-march.png`, `strip-deploy.png` and `strip-select.png` (eight consecutive frames each), `popups/` (every popup and panel), `cvd-day.png` and `cvd-night.png` (colour-vision simulation), `compare-first-prototype-vs-quiet.png`. The first round's pictures are kept in `shots/round-0/` (for comparison only: that look is superseded).

**Run it.**

```
node tools/serve.mjs                                  # serves docs/pitch/anim2 on 127.0.0.1:10020 (the page and the basemap's tiles); QC_PORT=10021 for another port
# open http://127.0.0.1:10020/d1-quiet-cartographer/quiet-cartographer.html
#   ?style=night          the night ground (the page opens on the day atlas)      ?variant=cvd | contrast
#   ?skinset={"*":{"pip.size":12}}   a token, live      ?tune=1   every dial of the engine (and the variant and Before / After switches)      ?dev=1   the metrics button
node build.mjs                                        # regenerate quiet-cartographer.html (2.2 MB, readable), tokens/skin-tokens.json and tokens/tokens.schema.json
```

The page needs the basemap's tile files (`../basemap/assets/`, 9 MB, public-domain NAIP and 3DEP: see `../basemap/README.md`); it makes no other request. `?tiles=<folder>` points elsewhere.

**What changed in the engine** (everything else is the prototype's, as it was). Round 0 (items 1 to 12) and polish round 1 (items 13 to 20):

1. `layout/spec.mjs`, `layout/tiles.mjs`, `scene/engine.mjs`, `skins/kit.mjs`: a skin may supply a **sizer** so the layout reserves the footprint it will draw at the current zoom and selection.
2. `layout/tiles.mjs` `aggregateName`: a crowd says "Both sides", its formation, or the corps its members belong to, with the plain count (the prototype said "Contact", "lead + k" and "x n").
3. `scene/display.mjs` 8b: a mark that has not moved for 24 frames while its target has not changed no longer keeps the engine busy.
4. `layout/text.mjs`: the lettering cuts; `data/text-widths.json` is measured with them.
5. `popups/legend.mjs`: the legend opens in the reading rail. `popups/fxdraw.mjs`: leaders, place rings and subject rings in hairlines.
6. `render/canvas.mjs`: place names around the marks; `ui/studio.mjs`, `tours/library.mjs`, `app.mjs`: the words and the two grounds.
7. `skins/semantics.mjs`: legend sentences of at most 84 characters.
8. Dials: `layout.labels` off, `layout.gapPx` 1, `layout.dMaxPx` 56, `motion.fadeMs` 280, `motion.birthScale` 0.86.
9. `layout/engine.mjs`: the placement search 40 % cheaper and identical to the original (40,000 random scenes).
10. `layout/tiles.mjs` and `sizer.selects(z)`: below zoom 15 a click moves nothing.
11. `scene/engine.mjs` `governorStep`: a window of 45 frames or 0.8 s.
12. `ui/quiet.css`: radii on the 6 / 10 px scale.
13. **The mark stands on the unit's place** (`layout/tiles.mjs`, `layout/engine.mjs`, `scene/display.mjs`): a tile has `px, py` (the true position) and `ax, ay` (where its box hangs: the true position plus the skin's own offset `dx, dy`, which the sizer returns), so the pip (the foot of the staff for a standard) stands exactly on the unit and the lettering hangs from it; the pin of the layout guards the true position, and while a box grows the display layer keeps the pip still (`cOff`). Round 0 hung the box on the unit, so a pip stood up to half a box from its place (about 100 m at zoom 16).
14. `layout/tiles.mjs`: a standard says "Both sides" only for Union and Confederate units (round 0 also did for one army plus a contested id and drew it split blue and red); a contested unit in a crowd is counted ("2 contested"); crowd words are the corps they share, plus ", 3 unlisted" for the units the order of battle gives no parent (round 0: "II Corps +3").
15. `render/canvas.mjs`, `app.mjs`: a mark that reaches into what the interface covers (each control group of the top bar, the dock, the side panel: `engine.chromeRects`) dissolves over the last 16 px before it; place names are kept off the edge of the rail or sheet.
16. `ui/studio.mjs`, `ui/quiet.css`: the top bar is four pills; the variant menu and Before / After are in the Tune tab; the dock is two rows (one when no event chip is showing); the phone has one row on top and a two-row dock.
17. `popups/host.mjs`, `ui/popup-layer.mjs`: a selected unit opens its card as a peek (kicker and title) in the phone's bottom sheet; no leader is drawn across the map for a card read in the sheet; the minimap is off on a phone.
18. `popups/unit-card.mjs`: a brigade, division or corps card no longer says "infantry" (the records have no arm).
19. `popups/legend.mjs`, `skins/kit.mjs`: the legend opens with a figure drawn by the skin's own code (a unit, a formation, a crowd, a carried-forward unit, at true size, Union reading rightward and Confederate leftward).
20. `popups/fxdraw.mjs`: popup ink has a paper underlay on the day ground (a dark rope under a hairline was the heaviest line of the page).

Nothing outside `docs/pitch/anim2/d1-quiet-cartographer/` and `tmp/look-dev/polish-d1-quiet-cartographer/` was written. No product code was touched.

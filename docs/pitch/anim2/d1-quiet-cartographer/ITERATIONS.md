# Iteration log

Method: build, render real scenes of the real data on the real ground, look at every picture as a hostile creative director who ships AAA UI, write down what is ugly or noisy, fix it, render again. Ten rounds. Two things the reader must know before trusting the log:

1. **Rounds 1 and 2 were partly blind.** The picture viewer answered "request limit" and returned no image for 13 of my reads (the first renders of rounds 1 and 2, the owner's `compare-z15.png`, the prototype's popup pictures, the basemap previews). In those rounds I worked from the measured numbers (tile counts, overlaps, aggregates), from the data (the 122 truncated labels, the 10 contested units) and from reading the code. The pictures of those rounds are kept in `shots/iterations/`; I looked at them afterwards, and what the log says about them under "seen now" is from that later look, not from the time. From round 3 on every picture was seen when it was made (the viewer then worked), except where the log says otherwise.
2. **The owner's `compare-z15.png` was never displayed to me.** I worked from the owner's list of seven faults (two boxes per unit, equal weight, heavy borders and tiny badges, four recolours, a blank grid, glitchy pins, inconsistent radii) and later looked at the prototype's own `app-war-room-fresh2-z15-paper.png`, which shows the same faults (`shots/compare-first-prototype-vs-quiet.png`).

All numbers below come from the commands named in SPEC.md section 13; "units merged" means placement records standing in a crowd standard.

## Round 1: the first mark (`it1-first-render-z15-tick551.jpg`)

**Built:** the skin of the brief: a pip (disc, diamond, triangle) and its code in haloed lettering, a swallow-tail standard for each formation or crowd, no plate, no chip, a soft glass behind each mark (22 %), the strength shown as a hairline under every unit from zoom 14.5, pins and leaders at 7 px, formation names from zoom 13, the backdrop's own place names under the marks.
**Measured (not seen):** The Angle, tick 551, zoom 15: 340 marks for 451 units, 0 overlaps, 0 unrepresented, 29 aggregates. The prototype's own tiles in the same view: 321, each unit two boxes.
**Found in the data, not in a picture:** 122 of 478 unit labels end in an ellipsis because the data cuts them at 24 characters ("11th Mississippi Infant…"); the prototype's crowd names said "Contact" for any two sides in one tile (an engagement the data does not record) and "lead + k" for the rest.
**Seen now (the picture of that build):** every unit mark has an underline (the strength hairline: a row of rules that reads as underlining), place names ("Brian Farm", "The Angle (High Water Mark)") print over marks and over each other, names end in "C...", the pins are a chain of beads in clusters.
**Changed:** `compactName` (the designation, ordinals as numerals, "Bde" and "Div", trailing parenthesis dropped; the ellipsis never reaches the map); the standard grows with the echelon (1, 1.12, 1.26); `aggregateName` (a crowd says "Both sides", its formation, or the corps its members belong to; the badge is the plain count); the place names are set by `render/places.mjs` around the marks (four slots, never under a mark), the backdrop's own are off; the first `quiet.css`.

## Round 5: measurements that found real faults (not pictures)

`tools/audit-dom.mjs` (8 interface states): four font sizes in use where I had intended three (kept as a documented four-step scale, listed as a weak point), one element at 3.94:1 (the faint ink of the day theme's section headings), and 12 px corner radii from a dial the popups read (`popup.style.radiusPx` default 12; set to 10).
`tools/contrast.mjs` (lettering against its own halo over 35 sampled bands of the real ground, marks against their hairline, the sides apart under colour-vision simulation): the first run of the six skins had **10 failures**: the day ground's lettering at 4.11:1 and marks at 2.26:1; the unknown side's gold at CIEDE2000 2.3 from the Union colour under tritanopia on the day ground; the greyscale difference between the two armies at 1.03:1.
**Changed:** the unknown side became the neutral ink of the ground, hatched (`#e3e8ee` night, `#2a323b` day), so it is apart from both armies under every vision type; the pip's hairline got its own opacity token (0.62 night, 0.92 day); the Union colour got lighter and the Confederate colour darker (greyscale ratio 1.03 -> 1.52 night, 1.57 day); the colour-blind variants separated by lightness as well as hue; the halo is modelled as the two strokes it really is (a wider soft one under the main one); `faint` ink darkened. Zero failures after (SPEC.md section 4).

## Round 8: the engine, and what it taught about "jitter"

Settling is the other half of "fix jitter". With the final dials a paused picture should stop. **Measured with `tools/rest-times.mjs`** (4 s of 4x play, pause, frames until `engine.busy()` is false; 9 real scenes): with a displacement budget of 64 px the whole field at zoom 13.5 never came to rest within 15 s; two or three displaced marks stood perfectly still but kept the engine busy for ever (a glide pulling them toward their certified place and a neighbour's push returning them to the same pixel each frame: no visible motion, but the page redrew every frame while paused). All three prototype skins show the same kind of outlier on the same probe. **Changed:** (1) the display layer settles a mark by stagnation (`scene/display.mjs` 8b: moved less than 0.02 px for 24 frames and its target unchanged); (2) the displacement budget is 56 px, the largest for which all nine probed scenes rest (the sweep over 48 / 52 / 56 / 60 / 64 is in `tools/probes/rest-probe5.mjs`); (3) the "blocked" test of a dimmed travelling mark takes the 0.05 px tolerance the safety net already uses (fuzz seed 1 found a mark waiting 3 s behind a neighbour 0.99 px away instead of 1; 12 seeds x 500 actions now pass).
**Not fixed in this round, and reported:** carry1 tick 295 zoom 14 took 1,120 frames to rest in this build (a displaced mark relaxing toward its true place at 20 px/s; a later round changed the widths of 25 crowd names and the scene then rested in 286 frames, nobody found why); and a mark that has just taken one relax step can be idle for the display layer while the next step is still to come, so a later interaction can show a last few pixels of creep (`test/rest.test.mjs` says exactly what it does and does not claim). Two numbers that looked like faults and were not: the smallest gap between two footprints in a settled frame was 0.94 px against the 1 px design clearance in 2 of 126 views at the time (the ink of two marks is at least 4 px apart because each footprint carries a 2 px ring band; round 9 shows the gaps below 1 px came from frames in motion after a click, and the final sweeps have exactly 1 px in all 952 samples), and one cold solve of the layout leaves a pair 0.999948 px apart (a rounding sliver of the exact search; the layout test's tolerance is 1e-3 px instead of 1e-6, `tools/probes/clr-diag.mjs`).
Note on `it4-displacement-budget-48.jpg`: the picture was made by a command whose argument failed to parse, so it shows the default of 48 px, as labelled; `-72` is the 72 px picture.

## Round 9: the whole field at 60 fps, and what a click does

**Seen (measured):** the opening view, the first thing anyone sees, ran at 42, 39, 48 and 41 fps in real Chrome (four rows: 1x and 4x, camera still and moving; `tools/pacing.mjs`), while The Angle ran at 60. A CPU profile of the headless engine on that view (`node --cpu-prof tools/probes/field-prof.mjs`): 41 % of the frame inside the placement search, of which the string key of the de-duplication, the sort of every candidate and the Map of the 1e-5 de-duplication were the three biggest lines. The sweep with a selected mark, run on the page, then showed 90 of 252 desktop views where a settling stage ran out of frames, and gaps down to 0.73 px in frames in motion; a new probe (`tools/probes/select-motion.mjs`) showed why: one click at zoom 11 to 13.5 moved 46 to 105 marks and dissolved crowds, and in two views four marks of a crowd breathed by 0.7 px at 5 Hz for ever.
**Changed:** (1) the placement search filters its forbidden rectangles once per column, de-duplicates through a scratch set, takes its candidates from a lazy heap and tests components by squared distance, and returns exactly what the prototype's original returns (40,000 random scenes with ties and duplicates: 0 differences; a 4,000-scene test is in `test/layout.test.mjs`): engine 20.3 ms per frame to 12.3 on the whole field measured back to back (9.1 ms on a quiet machine), 11.7 to 7.4 at The Angle at zoom 14, in real Chrome 59, 56, 60 and 58 fps on the whole field; (2) the governor's window is 45 frames or 0.8 s; (3) the layout gives the selected unit the top priority only when the selection changes what must be reserved (zoom 15 and up, wide windows, `sizer.selects`); below that a click is a ring and nothing moves (0 marks moved in all 15 probed views, 21 of 21 views still by frame 527), and the selection grows from zoom 15, not 14.
**Tried and put back, measured:** the layout dials (`layout.kCap` 35 to 40, `layout.dMaxPx` 40 to 48: frames of 16 to 28 ms instead of 28 to 33 in the stepped real-Chrome probe `tools/probes/field-ms.mjs`, at the price of more crowds and fewer exact places), the grid's cell keys as small integers (no measurable change), and removing the velocity that opposes a push in the display layer (no change in the breathing, and a 4 px late step in one view).
**Not fixed:** under a 4x CPU throttle the whole field is at 15 fps and the other places at 25 to 32 (a solve every third frame costs 1.5 times a solve every frame, so the governor saves a third); the camera in motion over the whole field is 46 to 49 fps at DPR 2.

## Round 10: three things the last pictures and probes found

**Seen:** the phone scene after round 9 showed the selected regiment inside a crowd standard that read "3rd Bde, 3rd Div, 1st C…": the data cuts 25 of 157 formation labels at 24 characters, and round 1's fix reached the unit marks but not the standards that stand for those formations. The legend row for the selection still said the mark grows to say its name, which is now true only from zoom 15. The DOM audit's radii listed seven 4 px corners.
**Changed:** crowd names use the designation, compacted as the marks do (a test compares all 157 formations with their own marks); the legend row says "(from zoom 15 it also grows its name)"; three stray 4 px radii (card tool buttons, the Chronicle count, the focus outline) are on the 6 px scale, and the seven buttons of the speed switch stay at 4 px on purpose (concentric with the switch's 6 px outline, listed in the SPEC); the specimen's caption read "at most 64 px" where the budget is 56, now it reads the dial. After the full set of sweeps, pacing and tests, only the legend sentence changed: the build was redone and the legend scene, the popup gallery, the DOM audit and the four test files that read the legend were run again.

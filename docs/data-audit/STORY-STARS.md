# Story stars: evidence per star

Generated by `tools/t0/mon/stars-doc.mjs` from a built tree of the stack R1c + CLOCK + DATA + MON (numbers measured, sentences written; every page cited is checked by `tools/t0/mon/stars-sources.mjs`, 65 claims). Date: 2026-10-19. Nothing here says the stars are free of error; the last section lists what was not settled.

## What was checked, and with what

| Item | How | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| the tick of each story | the story's own time statement and the sources, read through the corrected clock (`tools/t0/clock/oracle.mjs`, an independent calculation from `data-src/time/time_anchors.json`); the clock may differ from a time in the text by at most the uncertainty it states (the "give or take" of the anchors) plus the 5-minute rounding | `T0-MONL-17a/b` read the clock the app prints after "Jump to this moment" and compare it with the times in the story text |
| the linked unit | the order of battle (`data/oob`) | `T0-MONL-15`: every unit exists, is on the field at the star's tick (own keyframe or its brigade's), and `T0-MONL-17a/b`: the unit is selected after Jump and the star glows |
| the star's coordinates | the monument record of the 512-monument array (`_A`) or, where the array has none, the Park's marker as OpenStreetMap maps it (node id in the spec) | `T0-MONL-15`: every star lies within 5 m of its record or node; the table below also gives the nearest OpenStreetMap marker |
| the story text | every claim against the page of the Official Records or Fox that it cites | `T0-MONL-18` / `node tools/t0/mon/stars-sources.mjs`: 65 of 65 claims match their page |
| the unit's position at the tick | the engine's rule (the unit's last keyframe at or before the tick, after DATA's corrections) against the star | the distance is printed on the card ("On the map this unit is drawn about N m from the monument at this moment") and `T0-MONL-17a/b` re-measures it in the browser (within 25 m of the card, at most 1,000 m) |

Jump to this moment now lands on the star's own tick for all 20 stars (`JUMP_BY_CLOCK`, `jumpTick`, `clockIsLegacy` and `clockAt` are removed from the runtime; the card has no "moment the text names" any more). When the animation draws the linked unit more than 250 m from the star (15 of the 20), the jump fits the view to the star and the unit, so both are on screen; `T0-MONL-17a/b` checks that for every star.

### 20th Maine Infantry (`mon_20me_lrt`)

* **Tick 267, July 2, about 4:30 PM (give or take 30 min).** Tick 267 is the clock anchor "Vincent reaches Little Round Top" (16:30; Rice and Conner put the brigade in place about 16:00, Hunt has Hazlett's guns on the hill about 16:30). Conner (27/1 p.630): the 44th New York was placed on the hill about 4 PM with the 83rd Pennsylvania on its left. The text says from about 4:30 PM, the clock reads about 4:30 PM. The legacy tick 268 read about 4:40 PM.
* **Place 39.789476, -77.236146.** Record 173 of the monument array (20th Maine Infantry Monument, Little Round Top) and the Park marker in OpenStreetMap (20th ME Chamberlain) agree to 3 m. The legacy star stood near the Regulars' monuments on Wheatfield Road.
* **Unit `U-20ME`.** U-20ME, unchanged. DATA's audit (section 8) leaves the Little Round Top rows 0.4 to 0.5 km north of the Park's monuments.
* **Text.** Kept (DATA). "Extreme left of the line": Chamberlain 27/1 p.623. The hour of the bayonet charge is not recorded by any participant: Chamberlain has about an hour of severe fighting before it (p.624), Woodward has the enemy falling back nearly 6 PM (p.632), later accounts run from 5:10 to after 7 PM (time-facts R066). The card says so.
* **Source claims checked:** OR 27/1 p.623; OR 27/1 p.624; OR 27/1 p.630; OR 27/1 p.632.
* **Open:** The hour of the charge. The unit is drawn north of its monument.

### Law's Alabama Brigade (15th Alabama) (`mon_15al_lrt`)

## What was not settled

* **Positions.** 14 of the 20 linked units are drawn more than 350 m from the monument at the star's tick, the largest 946 m (26th North Carolina, whose July 1 fight is not drawn at all) and 736 m (Iron Brigade). The card says so for every star; the animation's rows were not changed.
* **Stars without a regiment marker of their own.** 15th Alabama (Law's brigade tablet), 20th North Carolina (Iverson's brigade tablet), Avery (Hoke's brigade tablet) and Hood (his division's tablet): the coordinates are the Park's tablets as OpenStreetMap maps them, not read from the tablets; what each tablet marks exactly (headquarters, farthest advance, the position at a given hour) is not verified. The card says in each case what the star stands on.
* **Hours.** 11 of the 20 texts name no hour, so their ticks rest on the animation (a keyframe with a note) and the sequence in the reports; the hour of the 20th Maine's bayonet charge, of Hancock's and Hood's wounds and of the 1st Minnesota's charge is not recorded by any participant (Contested or Estimated in the anchors file). The 5:10 PM start of the range printed for the 20th Maine's charge comes from a secondary timeline (time-facts R066), not from a report.
* **Sources read.** Official Records Series I vol. 27 parts 1 and 2 and Fox, Regimental Losses, as cached OCR from the Internet Archive; the 1921 War Department list of the Park's monuments for the 15th Alabama; OpenStreetMap (ODbL, 2026-10-19) for the Park's markers. Haskell, Livermore and the Park's own database were not used for these stars. OCR was read as printed; a misread page would show as a claim that does not match.
* **Walkthrough.** Not touched. Its Monuments tab names, honours text and the 512 coordinates were not re-verified here (only the coordinates the stars lie on, read-only).

# Kit evidence report: us-1c

Army of the Potomac, I Corps: corps headquarters and the infantry of the three divisions (Wadsworth; Robinson; Doubleday, Rowley). 43 units of `groups.json` (1 corps, 3 divisions, 7 brigades, 32 regiments). Written 2026-11-13 by workstream `bt-oob-kit`. Evidence file: `us-1c.jsonl`. Register proposals: `GOVERNANCE/horde/ws-kit/proposed-register/us-1c-01-arms.md`, `-02-dress.md`, `-03-strength.md`. Request notes: `GOVERNANCE/horde/requests/bt-oob-kit-us-1c-library.md`, `-catalog.md`, `-roster.md`. Nothing outside the group's own files was written, no kit table or override was touched, no gate was run, no `passes` flag was set, nothing was deployed. The only host contacted was archive.org (seven texts, one request each, 2026-11-13); no host refused.

How the file was made: a builder (kept in `tmp/kit-us-1c/`: `build.py`, `h.py`, `batches/b00_sources.py` to `b10_rescan.py`) locates each quote in the local text through the evidence library, so offset, report id and printed page come from the tool and not from hand. The rows of the returns were read from the scanned pages (pp. 151 to 153, 155 to 157, 173 to 175, 188, 190 to 192, 261, 265, 269, 316) and checked by arithmetic. Every exact-mode quote was also re-read from the raw source files with a separate matcher (`verify2.py`: 312 quotes, all found; one has a running head inside the quote, which the library removes); the 83 quotes in `image_row` (53) and `ocr_corrected` (30) modes carry the reading of the scanned page, where the OCR text differs from the print; the checker matched the `ocr_corrected` ones to the OCR at 0.92 similarity or better. The pass ran twice over the state volumes: first the reports and the sections the index gives each regiment, then the whole text of 30 local texts for every passage near a regiment's designation that holds an arms, dress or strength word. The second pass found what the first missed (the Adjutant General's letters and the monument inscriptions of Pennsylvania at Gettysburg, vol. 2; the 16th Maine's strength in the Maine volume; the 142nd Pennsylvania's Belgian rifles; five officers of the lists of the killed; the corps badge circular), and its hits are in the lines below.

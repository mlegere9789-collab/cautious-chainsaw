The first pass used no network; the second pass fetched twelve public-domain texts and two PDF scans from archive.org (see Sources).

How the file was made. A builder in `tmp/kit-us-2c-1d/` (first pass `build.py`, `kb.py`, `p1_table.py` to `p6_arms_gaps.py`; second pass `build2.py`, `kb2.py`, `p7_src.py` to `p17_148cas.py`) locates each quote in the text through the evidence library, so offset, report id and (for the Official Records and the state volumes) printed page come from the tool and not from hand. The printed page of the twelve declared texts is the engineer's reading of the running heads (the checker warns `PAGE_UNCHECKED`; 96 warnings), with the OCR's misread heads corrected by eye (Stewart pp. 108-109) and checked against the scan where a figure decides the claim. The rows of the returns and of the abstracts were read from scanned pages and added up. Every quote was re-read from the raw source files with a separate matcher (`verify3.py`: 407 quotes, 403 identical, 4 differing for reasons of normalization or a running head inside the quote, none in the words).

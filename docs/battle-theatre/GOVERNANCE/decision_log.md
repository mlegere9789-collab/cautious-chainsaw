## DEC-011 (2026-10-21) Provisional builder choices for using the day-level troop-map positions in the history layer (v2 -> v3); NOT owner decisions, all reversible, none changes a source value, a gate or a PDF default
Owner instruction: no input asked of the owner; proceed with the conservative option. Method, counts and the largest disagreements with the ledgers: app/HISTORY-LAYER-NOTES.md; shapes: app/data-contract.md contract 1.5; tests: app/tests/daypos.test.mjs.
1. **One label.** A unit's position on a sheet is used only if the unit has exactly one claim (of any kind) on that sheet. Several labels are several line states: all are recorded and listed on the card with sigma, none is drawn as one place; a lone movement arrow is not a place. (69 brigade-level units have several labels on some sheet; 85 unit-days.)
2. **Levels.** Brigade, battery, artillery brigade, artillery battalion and artillery group labels lay their regiments or batteries out inside the position (the same plausible-procedural slot rule as a ledger place, with the claim's sigma); division, corps and army labels place nothing. Only the nearest unit up the chain that has any label on the sheet is consulted (no falling through to a larger formation). Reading artillery brigades and battalions as brigade-level is a builder choice: the owner may restrict it to infantry and cavalry brigades and batteries.
3. **Time.** A day position gives no clock. It is used on its own day (local midnight to local midnight; no troop-map claim prints an evening or night convention, so none is applied) and never before the unit's gate (no gate moved, Q04). The modelled arrival is the earliest the record allows: leave when the gate and the day allow (also after an engagement window still running at that midnight), march at 1.25 m/s (the layer's march rate). Any later time inside the window would be as arbitrary; the card gives the whole window. Consequence, a property of the rule and not a historical claim: 140 of the 231 applied day stops are reached in the first hour of their day, because the units move at local midnight. The owner may choose another transition rule.
4. **Timed claims win at their time.** A day stop never changes a timed ledger place (PDF rows and OR27 claims): it is put in the first gap of its day in which every timed place is reached no later and at no faster a gait; otherwise it is not applied in that scenario and the report says why (in the default scenario only the positions outside the ground are not applied). The other units of a ledger place keep the slots they had.

Several sheet-1 labels do not count as a place. Arrival just in time for 14:00 and by 15:30. Result: no unit qualifies (every brigade that may use it has a timed ledger place that day, no road of approach, or a position outside the ground); the sheet stays listed on the cards with its distance to the sheet-1 labels and to the ledgers.
7. **Outside the loaded ground.** A position outside the ground, or whose column would stand partly outside, is not drawn and says so (16 day entries, 6 claims).
8. **No road, no drawing.** A day position gives a unit no road of approach and no entry (an invented entry would be a fabrication: DEC-007 item 3, DEC-009 item 8). 214 leaf units have a single-label day position and no road of their line of command; they stay open gaps and their cards list the position.

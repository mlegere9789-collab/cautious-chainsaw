| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | Cushing was killed on 3 July and his battery made no report in the volumes searched; Webb's report says only that Webb's right rested on the battery. The plan's "prepared position" has no basis in these texts. Literal 100% is impossible here (Law cl.3). |

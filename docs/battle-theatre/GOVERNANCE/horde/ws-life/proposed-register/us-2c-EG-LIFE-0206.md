# EG-LIFE-0206: Hancock's burial table of 3-5 July: column headings and one digit illegible in the local scan

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | EG-LIFE-0206 |
| Requirement IDs | R22, R21, OA11 |
| Category | evidence missing / access |
| Description | The numbers of the dead buried by the Second Corps from 3 to 5 July (Hancock's report of 17 July 1863, signed by the acting corps commander, OR27-1 p. 378) are in a table that the local scan prints as run-together cells without its column headings; the grand total 1,629 and the remark "One female (private), in rebel uniform" are legible. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | The scan available here does not keep the columns or the headings and one digit is damaged (printed 869). The totals (Union 387, Rebel 1,242, together 1,629) and a split of each into two columns are read by the arithmetic of the printed figures (row us-2c-R275, inferred: 18 + 369 = 387, 60 + 1,182 = 1,242, 369 + 1,182 = 1,551, 78 + 1,551 = 1,629, so 869 is read 369); the headings (officers and enlisted men) and the digit are not confirmed by a page image. Literal 100% is not reachable with the local scan (Law cl.3); the check is possible with a page image. |
| Evidence searched | Group us-2c (evidence file us-2c.jsonl, open gap row us-2c-R258); searched list of the row:<br>  - OR-27-1: OR27-1-R076 p. 378: the table "number of dead buried at Gettysburg, Pa., by my command": hit_ambiguous<br>  - OR-27-1: a second scan of the volume (the one cited by the close-out files as OR-27-1-W:warofrebellion271unit) is not in this group's source registry: not_local |

The burial rows of the regiments carry their own figures (84 Confederate dead by the 42nd New York, 37 by Carroll's brigade, 4 officers and 38 men of Carroll's brigade and 37 Confederates by the pioneers). |

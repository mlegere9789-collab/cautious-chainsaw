## Round 3 addendum (third reading, 2026-11-15/16)

S-021 (Record of Treatment vol. I, printed p.373, quoting Klingel's own damage claim): the fencing was "of worm, post and board" and "mostly new, made that spring"; "All the fencing was torn down", "the greater part of it, along the [Emmitsburg] road, was part of the breastworks" and some was burned; this turns "all missing on Jul 4" into "torn down in the battle, the greater part in the road breastworks, some burned" for the owner's claim of the 1870s (one source, no date inside the three days; the return on 4 Jul and "all missing" at that date are not stated, S-021.3). The farmhouse is a two-storey log house and the barn a log barn (the barn of 1870-1880 now standing is not the battle barn). Still no local text for the 14-16 wounded Confederates or the family leaving on 2 Jul (S-021.4). Lead not read: https://gettysburg.stonesentinels.com/battlefield-features/klingle-farm/ (URL and reading recorded by shard tl-04 on 2026-11-04: "14 to 16 wounded Confederates were brought in the evening of Jul 1 and Daniel Klingel cared for them; family left Jul 2 after warnings from Union officers. No primary cited"); refused on 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-15 (http code 000). S-046: damage at the 3 Jul cannonade is documented (Meade Life and Letters vol. 2 p.106: one shell burst among the staff horses in the yard, one tore up the steps, one carried away the porch supports, one passed through the door, one the garret; two newspapers of 6 Jul: the house "was perforated by several shots", staff horses killed; NPS Handbook No. 9: "badly damaged by the artillery fire which preceded Pickett's Charge"); the barn was a signal station and temporary hospital (NPS Historic Structure Report); the council of 2 Jul has four hour descriptions and no clock hour (NPS farm page "late in the night", NPS Then & Now "late in the evening", Meade "the evening of the 2d", Gibbon "soon after all firing had ceased"), carried as selectable values; the NPS reading of the July 1863 photograph says "the house and surrounding fences are all damaged" (damaged, not burned); "all rails burned on 2-3 Jul" still has no source (lead: the Stone Sentinels farms page, https://gettysburg.stonesentinels.com/battlefield-farms/, which lists a Leister entry; the per-farm URL is not recorded anywhere in the repository). Owner-only: the Pennsylvania State Archives damage claims (Klingel, and the Leister family's if one exists): a visit or a request; not done. Status unchanged.

| Description | S-023, S-024, S-041, S-042. Start: Geary's division was placed "At 5 a. m." on Jul 2 (R293 p.825); Greene's brigade was in line at about 06:00 (Greene R307 p.855, OCR "G"; Godard 60th NY R308 p.860; von Hammerstein 78th NY R310 p.863), the 149th NY marching at about 04:00 (Barnum R313 p.868). Finish: "By 12 o'clock we had a good cover for the men" (Greene R307 p.856; the text file's OCR prints "13" and the page image, leaf 879 of the archive.org scan, reads 12), about noon (137th NY, Ireland R312 p.866) and about 09:00 (60th NY, Godard): two values, 09:00 and 12:00. The printed "morning" holds for Greene and Geary but not for the First Division: Williams received the corps order "At 8 a. m." (R272 p.773), Ruger "About 9 a. m." the order to move (R273 p.778), McDougall's First Brigade was withdrawn "about 11 a. m." and then "immediately built breastworks" (R274 p.783), the 46th PA finished "in the afternoon" (Selfridge R281 p.803) and Price saw the first-line regiments still building at 18:30 (R280 p.800); the 5th CT says it began in the morning after 04:00 at an unnamed position (Packer R275 p.790). Kane (arrived 06:00) built "before sundown" (R302 p.847). Wadsworth's division began a temporary breastwork on the hill on the afternoon and evening of Jul 1 (Grover, 7th Indiana R038 p.284; Dawes R035 p.277 names Culp's Hill). A stone wall stood behind the First Division's line when its brigades formed and was repaired, not built: Williams "less than 50 yards behind" (R272), Ruger "about 75 yards" (R273), McDougall front line "about 40 paces in advance" (R274), Price "some 125 feet in rear of the first line" (R280); Geary "about 200 yards in rear" of Ruger's and Kane's intrenchments and Greene "about 300 yards in its rear" of Kane's line, held by the enemy that night (R293 p.827, R307 p.857); one wall or two is not stated. The enemy held the original line and the stone fence on the night of Jul 2 (Williams R272 p.774; Johnson R481 "it was dark" and Ewell R467 "about dusk" for the attack; Steuart held part until noon on Jul 3). The thin line after 18:30: "a foot between each man" (Greene 1893), "single file, with intervening spaces of a foot or more" (Stegman R311 p.864), "one man deep" (Ireland R312). Jul 3: the 147th PA occupied a stone fence after the 03:45 shelling and carried "the stone wall previously mentioned" at 05:00 (R294 p.836; R293 p.827-828). |

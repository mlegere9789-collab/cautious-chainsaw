A pre-existing stone fence behind the breastworks is documented by six reports: Williams "less than 50 yards behind" the crest breastworks (R272 p.773), Ruger "about 75 yards" behind the First Brigade breastworks (R273 p.779), McDougall rear line behind the wall with the front line "about 40 paces in advance" (R274 p.783), Price "some 125 feet in rear of the first line" (R280 p.800: new in this close-out), and, for a wall held by the enemy on the night of Jul 2, Geary "about 200 yards in rear" of Ruger's and Kane's intrenchments (R293 p.827) and Greene "about 300 yards in its rear" (R307 p.857). Kane's brigade built its breastwork "before sundown" (R302 p.847) and no morning hour is given.

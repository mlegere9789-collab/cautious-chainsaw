# fence-2-EG-12: Hazlett's guns on Little Round Top: by hand or by teams

| Category | evidence conflict |
| Description | S-038. Hazlett's battery ascended behind the 140th NY and went into battery on the summit after the brigade moved forward about 16:00 (Garrard R217 p.651). Hunt (R028 p.236-237) dates the move to Round Top at "About 4.30 p. m." and says the battery "immediately opened" and fired "until night"; Rittenhouse (Martin R221 p.660): the battery "had been engaged only about an hour" when Hazlett was shot, and he died at 20:00; the printed first fire at 17:45 has no source (agrees with delegated decision DD-FENCE1-07). The 155th PA history says its men dragged the guns by hand (PA vol.2 p.775-777); the 20th Maine address says Hazlett, aided by the infantry, lifted his guns by hand and handspike (ME p.253); the 140th NY volume says teams under the drivers dragged the guns up (NY vol.1 p.48). |

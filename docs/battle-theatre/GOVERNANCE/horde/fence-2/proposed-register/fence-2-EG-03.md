II records worm fence along the Emmitsburg Road or the west boundaries of Snyder Fields 3 and 7 that Longstreet ordered the 3rd Arkansas to take apart rail by rail ("Grab the bottom rail and turn it over", a soldier's recollection quoted by the NPS, pdf p.104-105).

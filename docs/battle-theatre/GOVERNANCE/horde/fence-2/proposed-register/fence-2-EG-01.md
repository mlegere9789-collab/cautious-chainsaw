| Description | S-034, S-035, S-036. The PDF prints the Sherfy orchard and fences as Destroyed (afternoon-evening Jul 2 on). Primary and regimental texts (re-matched 2026-11-14) show: a rail fence 30-40 rods west of the Emmitsburg road on the night of Jul 1-2 (4th Maine, ME p.180); fences pulled down on the morning of Jul 2 by the 26th PA after its midnight arrival (OR27-1-R167 Bodine p.556; PA vol.1 p.196), by 35 men of the 86th NY sent after 10:00 and back at about 11:00 "to demolish all stone walls and fences in our front to the Emmitsburg road" (R146 Higgins p.511), cleared already at about 09:00 near the orchard (NY vol.3 p.1289), removed in Humphreys' front without an hour (R158 p.532); the NPS inventory says Union forces removed many north-south fences before Jul 2 and enough stood on Jul 2-3 to hinder movement (NPS-CLI-ERR pdf p.43, p.62). Fences still stood in the afternoon: the Peach Orchard fence (3rd Maine to about 16:00, ME p.130; 110th PA before 15:30, PA vol.1 p.590; the 2nd New Hampshire behind the fence on the south side of the orchard "During the afternoon of July 2", NPS CLR vol. II pdf p.101, worm fence on the 1863 Bachelder Map), Sherfy's stable and garden fence (Thompson's battery, ordered about 16:30, PA vol.2 p.920) and the road fence at the house (114th PA, after about two hours of shelling, no hour, PA vol.2 p.615). Fence rails lay in the orchard at about 01:00 on Jul 3 (Alexander, BL3 p.361). No primary describes the destruction event or its extent and no local text describes the condition of the peach trees. Hart's 15th NY battery fired from the left (south line) of the orchard (R321; NY vol.1 p.39) and is not tied to any fence; his canister fire against infantry falls between about 17:00 and 17:45 by McGilvery's hours (inferred); the printed 16:30 and 17:30 have no source. The Sherfy house was incorporated in Graham's line on the second day and damaged (NPS CLI-PCB p.97; "many scars", Bachelder 1873) but no source counts hits. |

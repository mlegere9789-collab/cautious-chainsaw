| Description | S-032, S-033, S-038, S-039, S-043. Wheatfield: stone walls and fences are documented (Sweitzer R193 p.611, Crawford R218 p.653, de Trobriand R152 p.520/521, Burbank R211 p.645, Brooke R092 p.401, Kershaw R432 pp.366-368) and the NPS Record of Treatment vol. II adds a fence round the field: on the afternoon of Jul 2 the 61st NY found the wheatfield "bounded by fence" and "scaled the fence", the 118th PA marching on Wheatfield Road was "removing the fences", and the north and west perimeter is a wooden fence on the 1863 Bachelder Map (pdf p.102-103); nothing places each wall or says which is the 17th Maine wall. Kershaw formed at about 15:00 in front of and behind a stone wall and advanced "about 4 o'clock" through "numerous fences"; the NPS ties his stone house and barn to the Rose farm buildings and records the Rose yard picket fence as an obstacle (photographs); the 3rd Arkansas turned over the bottom rails of a fence (soldier's recollection via the NPS). The Trostle walls of Bigelow's stand are documented by Milton (R320 p.886, "two stone walls met at an obtuse angle", held at all hazards; park at 21:00), McGilvery (R318 p.882: batteries ordered back 350 yd, new line 400 yd to the rear) and the NPS inventory (stone wall adjacent to the gate; four guns lost). Little Round Top: Weed's brigade built a wall half way down the slope "At night" on Jul 2 (Garrard R217 p.652); Fisher's brigade a wall across the ravine "In the morning" of Jul 3 (R220 p.658); a stone fence enclosing a wood in front (D. P. Hancock R213 p.647, about 17:30); the boulders of the slopes were the material (NPS-CLI-LRT) and the present walls were restacked in 1882-1887; no rail use on the hill. The shard's Hall citation (5th Alabama, R526) is a Jul 1 passage. |

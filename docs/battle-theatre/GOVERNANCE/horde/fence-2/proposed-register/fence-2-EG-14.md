| Description | S-027, S-047, S-048. The PDF prints a V Corps hospital operating at the Weikert house from the morning of Jul 2, Weed dying in the basement before dawn on Jul 3 and the hospital moving east early on Jul 3. Tillie Pierce (1889, pp.35-44): wounded came to Jacob Weikert's house on Jul 1 and "before night the barn was filled", nurses making beef tea in the basement kitchen; on the evening of Jul 2 she spoke to a wounded man in the basement room and found him dead when she came down after the sun was high on Jul 3: "This is the body of General Weed" (p.66; plate caption "Died at the house of Jacob Weikert"); the hour of death is not stated. NY vol.3 p.972: the Fifth Corps hospital "had been made at the house and barns of J. Weikart" and, struck during the afternoon cannonade of Jul 3, "was then moved to Lewis Bushman's". NY vol.2: the Third Corps hospital was "in a grove about half a mile to the left and rear" of the 123rd NY on the evening of Jul 2. The park's hospital tablets for the Third and Fifth Corps stand on the Hospital Road, which "runs south from the Baltimore Pike crossing Rock Creek below White Run" (NPS list, 1932; commemorative). So the first use is earlier than printed, the death overnight (not "before dawn") and the move after the Jul 3 cannonade (not early). |

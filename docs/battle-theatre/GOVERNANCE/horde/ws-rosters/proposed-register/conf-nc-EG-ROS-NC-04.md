# EG-ROS-NC-04: North Carolina: Moore prints the whole war; presence at Gettysburg is stated only where a remark says so

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | EG-ROS-NC-04 |
| Requirement IDs | OA03 (complete roster section of both armies, source and rights per name); R22; Law clauses 3, 4 and 7 |
| Category | presence on 1-3 July 1863 not stated by the roll |
| Description | The Roster of North Carolina Troops prints each man who served in a unit at any time between 1861 and 1865, with his service history. A man is stated present at Gettysburg only where his printed remark says so (a casualty or capture at Gettysburg, a date of 1 to 3 July 1863 with the place). 3,834 of 54,403 lines name Gettysburg in the remarks (flag file member names_gettysburg, an OCR-tolerant match, a lower bound) and 1,203 of these also print 1, 2 or 3 July 1863 (gettysburg_dated). The flag member on_rolls_july_1863 is inferred from printed enlistment and separation dates only: yes 29,810, no 11,210, unknown 13,383; it is no statement that the man was at Gettysburg. The earlier GAPS lines say that Moore under-annotates Gettysburg (G05, GN01: Moore names 512 or 515 Gettysburg casualties of the 26th North Carolina where Clark reports 708 killed, wounded and missing; the 4th North Carolina only 21 lines); this run counts 505 for the 26th and 17 for the 4th by a different rule; the difference is not examined. |

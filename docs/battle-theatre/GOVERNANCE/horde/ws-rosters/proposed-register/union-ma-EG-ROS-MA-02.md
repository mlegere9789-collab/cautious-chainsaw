# EG-ROS-MA-02: Presence of a man at Gettysburg: no muster roll of 30 June 1863 and no list of the men present in the sources read

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | EG-ROS-MA-02 |
| Requirement IDs | OA03 (complete roster section of both armies, source and rights per name); R22; Law clauses 3, 4 and 7 |
| Category | documented absence of a source |
| Description | The volumes print the whole-war service of every man who was ever on the unit's rolls (33,787 lines for 24 units) and no roll of 30 June 1863. 1,588 lines print a remark that names Gettysburg (an OCR-tolerant match, a lower bound), 1,271 of them with a date from 1 to 3 July 1863 in the same clause. The flag files give "on the rolls in July 1863" from printed dates only (yes 11,928, no 19,863, unknown 1,996); it is not a statement of presence. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | The rolls are a service record, not a roster of a battle; the earlier pilots measured that a man on a roll with no recorded absence was present between 36 and 72 percent of the time (PLAN.md 2.4, gap EG-ROS-010). |
| Evidence searched | The 24 unit files; the baseline strengths and the Official Records statements of strength (rosters/reports/union-ma.md section 1). |
| Autonomous substitute applied | Lines that print Gettysburg are marked as such; every other man is shown as "on the rolls, names not recovered". |
| Strongest evidence-based reconstruction | The statements of presence that the item roster-gettysburg-lists collects (the Burial Report of 1863 in tmp/library/ws9, 1863 newspaper casualty lists, the Record of the Massachusetts Volunteers 1868-70 officers and casualties). |

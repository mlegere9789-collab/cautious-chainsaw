## Why the Q04 item count fell from 5187 to 5088

A Q04.1 item is one distinct behaviour of one unit (the hash of what the unit does, `evidence.hash`); every scenario that gives that same behaviour is listed in the one item (`evidence.n_scenarios`). Before the floor fix (app/src/history/arrivals.mjs: a chosen value earlier than the unit's own floor is drawn at the floor), a chosen value below the floor (C-005 x:C-005-P5 and S5, the `earliest` view of the Hood units, C-039) gave a different behaviour from the floor itself, so it was an item of its own and FAILED. After the fix it draws exactly as the floor does, so its behaviour hash equals that of an existing item and the two collapse into one (for example `earliest` of cs-1c-hood-anderson-11ga now has the hash of `delegated`, whose n_scenarios went from 245 to 246). Result: 169 ids gone, 70 new (a collapsed behaviour takes the id of its first scenario, and the Q04.scan item ids move with the representative scenario of each group), net -99: Q04.1 3939 -> 3844 (19 units), Q04.scan 179 -> 175; Q04.3, Q04.4, Q04.5, Q04.6 and Q04.S* are unchanged id for id. No unit and no scenario left coverage: the scenario set is the same 1635, the same 726 units are in Q04.1, and for every unit the total of scenarios spread over its items is unchanged; the scan covers every behaviour (3844 = the Q04.1 items). Proved by `python3 -I tools/bt/q04_coverage_check.py` (snapshot of the first full results: `horde/q04-coverage-before.json`); it exits 1 if a unit or scenario drops out, if a unit's scenario total changes, or if two items of a unit share a behaviour hash.

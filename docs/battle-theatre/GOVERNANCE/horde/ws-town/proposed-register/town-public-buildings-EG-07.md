| Description | The market house built early in the 1800s east of the old court house stood in the square (History of 1886, p. 187); no text says when it was removed, and the Hopkins inset draws the whole Diamond blank (it omits the old court house too). The County Building (the county offices) on the north-east corner of the square was sold in April 1859 and was a store in 1886 (inferred to have stood in 1863). The Bank of Gettysburg (East York Street, opposite the gallery of Tyson Brothers and "Wills' Building"), the Savings Institution (south-west corner of the Diamond) and McConaughy's Hall are placed only by labels of the Hopkins inset of 1858 and advertisements; Sheads & Buehler's Hall (a hospital on 18 August 1863) is placed by the firm's address and may be McConaughy's Hall; the post office had left its room on Baltimore Street near the Diamond by January 1863 and its place in July 1863 is not found (row 0212). |

| Description | The owner plan's record of St. Francis Xavier says "Alternate pews removed, boards laid on the rest" (a web article, a lead; the web workstream's decision DD-FENCE1-47 also records "boards laid over the pews" in a newspaper of 20 July 1863 quoted by a Park Service seminar essay, no church named). **Documented by church (second pass):** Christ Lutheran: forty wounded in the lecture room and a hundred in the body of the church "stretched upon boards that were laid across the tops of the pews" (row 0330; Dr. Hay's discourse of 1877 as quoted in 1938); Presbyterian: "some of the men lay in the pews, and others on boards placed on top of the backs of the seats" (row 0076), with straw bedding and no bunks (row 0300); Catholic: bunks made for all the patients, raising them from the floor, the wet musty straw cleaned out, fresh bedding, windows fixed to lower from the top (row 0333), and, in Barton, soldiers on the pew seats, under the pews and in every aisle, the sanctuary and the gallery (row 0137); St. James': the pews stood and were stained with blood and medicines were dispensed from the altar (row 0325). **In every case the pews stood**; no source says pews were removed. |

| Description | The owner plan's record of St. Francis Xavier says "Alternate pews removed, boards laid on the rest" (a web article, a lead). Barton (1898) says the soldiers "lay on the pew seats, under them and in every aisle ... in the sanctuary and in the gallery" and that the stations of the cross hung on the walls (row 0137): the pews stood. For Christ Lutheran, Hospital Scenes says most of the wounded had "nothing between them and the hard boards" but their blankets, some a little straw, and that the nurses had bed sacks filled with straw from the Commissary (row 0104): whether the floor or the pews were meant is not stated. For the Presbyterian church nothing was found (row 0076). |

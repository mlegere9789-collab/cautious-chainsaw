| Description | The basemap carries STR-TOWN-PRESBYTERIAN_CHURCH with error 40 m and confidence Contested. The Hopkins inset (row 0058), the Sanborn plan (row 0059) and two advertisements (row 0057) place the church at the south-east corner of Baltimore and High streets within 16 m of one another, and the 2023 outline holds the Sanborn centre. The outlines include later work and are not the 1863 footprints (rows 0060; rule GR-FOOTPRINT-2023 is not applied). |

# town-public-buildings-EG-01: the footprint and the form of the Adams County court house of 1858-59: three figures that do not agree, and no roof, opening or outbuilding in any local text

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | town-public-buildings-EG-01 |
| Requirement IDs | R12, OA10 |
| Category | source conflict (measurement) and open gap (form) |
| Description | The contract of March 1858 gives the court house as "60 feet by about 102" (the Commissioners had enlarged it about 15 feet beyond the original plan; row 0016, the page image was looked at). The Sanborn plan of November 1886, measured with its own scale bar, gives about 48 by 98 feet over the walls and a small north projection (row 0048). The 2023 outline of STR-TOWN-COURTHOUSE, which the product extrudes, is 829 square metres (about 8,900 square feet; 40.7 by 28.5 m over its bounding box), against 569 square metres for 60 by 102 feet. The storey count (two) rests on the 1886 plan only (row 0015). The roof form and material, the colour, the openings, the chimneys (the brick-work of November 1858 awaited only "the topping-out of the chimneys"), the porch and any outbuilding are stated by no local text (open-gap and generic rows). The cupola in the steeple, the bracketed cornice of December 1858 and the brick walls are documented. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | No plan or survey of the building of 1859 is in the local texts; the contract is a plan made before the building was begun and the 1886 plan is twenty-seven years later; the History of 1886 says only that the building "as it now stands" is the one completed in 1859. |
| Evidence searched | Sentinel of 1 March 1858 (page image: 60 feet by about 102); Sanborn sheet 1 of 1886 (crop matched to the 25 percent overview of the sheet; scale bar 200 feet = 1152 px); STR-TOWN-COURTHOUSE 2023 outline; search rows 0039 to 0047 (storeys, outbuildings, form words, damage) over the History of 1886, the Park Service texts, the weeklies, Jacobs, the Official Records and the newspaper pages. |
| Autonomous substitute applied | None applied to the data. The drawing may use the contract size or the 1886 plan size as two selectable scenarios, with the generic gable roof, and says so; the 2023 outline is not an 1863 footprint and should not stand for one. |
| Strongest evidence-based reconstruction | Two storeys (inferred), brick walls (documented), a bracketed cornice and a tower with a cupola (documented), the footprint as a selectable scenario between 60 by about 102 feet (contract) and about 48 by 98 feet (1886 plan), generic roof. |
| Confidence tier | walls and cupola documented; footprint and storeys inferred; roof plausible-procedural; openings, colour, chimneys, porch, outbuildings open gaps |
| Competing values | Width 60 feet (contract) against about 48 feet (1886 plan); the 2023 outline (829 square metres) against both. |

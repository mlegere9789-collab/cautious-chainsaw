# town-churches-EG-08: The eight churches of the list of 1844 and the churches found for July 1863

| Field | Content |
|---|---|

| Requirement IDs | R12, OA10 |
| Category | unmatched items of a list |
| Description | Rupp lists Presbyterian, Lutheran, Union, Methodist, German Reformed, Independent, Catholic and African churches in the borough in 1844 (row 0239). The pass finds eight churches in July 1863: Presbyterian, Christ Lutheran, St. James' Lutheran, German Reformed (the Union Brick Church of 1814, enlarged 1862), Methodist Episcopal, Catholic, United Presbyterian and the Coloured Church. The "Union" and the "Independent" churches of 1844 could not be matched with a building: the Union church may be the Union Brick Church (then the German Reformed of the list is the same building or a congregation without its own house), the Independent church may be the Associate Reformed congregation; no source says so. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | Rupp gives no street or form; no other list of the churches of 1844 to 1863 was found in the local texts (the 1886 history gives the congregations, not a list). |

# town-churches-EG-08: The eight churches of the list of 1844 and the churches found for July 1863

| Description | Rupp lists Presbyterian, Lutheran, Union, Methodist, German Reformed, Independent, Catholic and African churches in the borough in 1844 (row 0239). The pass finds eight churches in July 1863: Presbyterian, Christ Lutheran, St. James' Lutheran, German Reformed (the Union Brick Church of 1814, enlarged 1862), Methodist Episcopal, Catholic, United Presbyterian and the Coloured Church. The "Union" and the "Independent" churches of 1844 could not be matched with a building: the Union church may be the Union Brick Church (then the German Reformed of the list is the same building or a congregation without its own house; the 1938 article says the Lutherans withdrew from the union church in 1848), the Independent church may be the Associate Reformed congregation (the 1915 history shows an Associate Reformed church in Gettysburg in 1813); no source says so. |

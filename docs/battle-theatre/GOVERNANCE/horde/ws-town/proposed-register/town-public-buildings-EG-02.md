# town-public-buildings-EG-02: the year the court house of 1858-59 was completed: 1857, 1858 or 1859

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | town-public-buildings-EG-02 |
| Requirement IDs | R12, OA10 |
| Category | source conflict |
| Description | The History of Cumberland and Adams Counties (1886, p. 42-43): the new court house was contracted in March 1858 and "in 1859 it was completed and ready for occupancy"; the Sentinel of 15 August 1859: the interior nearly finished and the Court to be held in it that week (row 0005). Bachelder (Gettysburg: What to See, 1873, p. 5): the court-house "remained until 1857, when the present court-house was completed and the old one removed" (row 0006). The Park Service Evergreen Cemetery report: the court house "was built in 1858" (row 0007); its Design Guide dates the architect's design 1859. The Sentinel of 19 October 1857 says the Commissioners had only then chosen the site, and the Sentinel of 1 March 1858 says the new building is "to be completed by the 1st of April, 1859"; the old court house was sold in April 1859. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | The sources disagree and no record of the building contract or of its acceptance is in the local library; the Adams County Historical Society (commissioners' minutes, damage and deed indexes) is a blocked host. |
| Evidence searched | History of 1886; Bachelder 1873 and 1889; Park Service CLR and Design Guide; Sentinel and Compiler of 1857-1859 (site, contract, brick-work, tower, bracketed cornice, interior, sale and demolition of the old court house). |
| Autonomous substitute applied | None applied. All three rows are carried as selectable scenarios (conflict written on each). |
| Strongest evidence-based reconstruction | Completed in 1859 (the contemporary papers and the History), begun in 1858; the other two years are carried. |
| Confidence tier | each year documented as a statement of its source; the contemporary papers support 1859 |
| Competing values | 1857 (Bachelder) against 1858 (Park Service) against 1859 (History, Sentinel). |
| Product marking | The card of the court house names the three dates and their sources; the July 1863 scene is the same for all three. |
| Visual and audio impact | The card text only; the building stood in July 1863 under every reading. |

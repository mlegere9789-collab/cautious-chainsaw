| Description | Six accounts say sharpshooters held "a steeple in the town" on 1 July and "the steeples and belfreys of the churches" on 2 July (town-churches rows 0150, 0151, 0152): Wiedrich's battery on East Cemetery Hill (two officers and several men hit, horses killed, a shell fired at the steeple), a dispatch and a Times special of Thursday 2 July, the Times account of that day, Willis ("houses and steeples of public buildings", night of 1-2 July) and, not as a sniper post, Col. Morrow in "a church steeple" until 3 July (Park Service study). None names the church; the owner plan says "Not identified". **Towers now on record (second pass):** St. James has a steeple (its pastor watched the battle of 1 July from it: row 0320, documented, second-hand); the Catholic church had a Roman cupola of about 60 ft (row 0125); the German Reformed church has an open lantern of four slender posts under a low dome (Tyson card LOC 2024640779, row 0341; LOC 2012647712, rows 0188, 0190); Christ Lutheran (the College Church) has an open arcaded lantern under a low dome (Tyson card LOC 2024640778, row 0338). A church with a bulbous cap and a needle finial stands at the left edge of the town in LOC 2012647703 (row 0344) and is not identified. No source says the sharpshooters used any of them. |

# town-churches-EG-06: The round symbol of the Sanborn plan of 1886 and the lanterns of the Christ Lutheran and Reformed churches

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | town-churches-EG-06 |
| Requirement IDs | R12, OA10 |
| Category | inference from a symbol that the key does not explain, now supported by photographs for two churches |
| Description | A round yellow (frame) symbol of 8 to 10 ft stands inside the brick footprint near the front gable of four churches: Christ Lutheran (row 0093), Catholic (0126), German Reformed (0189) and St. James' (0231); the Presbyterian and the United Presbyterian churches show none. The key does not explain the circle. For the Catholic church it agrees with the Roman cupola that Reily describes (row 0125). **Second pass:** two Tyson cards (catalogued [between 1861 and 1865] and [between 1863 and 1880]) whose captions name the churches show the cupolas: Christ Lutheran (the College Church) an **open round lantern of tall arched openings under a cornice ring and a low hemispherical dome with a small finial** (row 0338) and the German Reformed church an **open lantern of four slender posts under a plain cornice and a low dome with a small finial** on a square stage (row 0341): the circle is a round lantern in both. For St. James' the plan's symbol and the pastor's steeple (row 0320) both exist; their relation is not stated. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | The key of the plan does not explain the circle; the cards are of the years after the battle (one possibly as late as 1880) and no text says the lanterns are unchanged since 1863. |
| Evidence searched | The Sanborn key and sheets, the two Tyson cards (master TIFFs looked at), LOC 2012647712, Reily. |
| Autonomous substitute applied | None applied. |
| Strongest evidence-based reconstruction | A cupola or lantern at the front gable of the four churches: the form of the photographs for Christ Lutheran and the Reformed church (inferred), the documented Roman cupola for the Catholic church, a generic cupola marked inferred for St. James' (its pastor's steeple documents a steeple). |
| Confidence tier | inferred (photographs of the years after the battle); documented (the Catholic cupola, the St. James steeple) |

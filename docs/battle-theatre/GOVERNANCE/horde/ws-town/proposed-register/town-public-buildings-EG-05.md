# town-public-buildings-EG-05: the county jail (county prison): the building of 1851 is known only through the plan of 1886

| Requirement IDs | R12, OA10 |
| Category | open gap (form) and defect of the plan row |
| Description | A county jail held prisoners on 21 July 1863 (sheriff's notice); the jail "as it now stands" in 1886 was built in 1851 after the fire of 7 January 1850 (History of 1886); a furnace under the cells was judged insufficient in November 1857 and in April 1859 the County Prison was found "dilapidated" with "many of the Cells insufficient" (rows 0120 and 0121). The only plan is the Sanborn plan of 1886: brick, two storeys, a main block about 75 by 35 feet, a two-storey brick dwelling about 38 by 49 feet attached and stone yard walls marked 16 feet (rows 0114 to 0119). The plan row of the owner's text names "General Jail" as a lead: the papers' "General Jail" is the court term "General Jail Delivery", not a building. |

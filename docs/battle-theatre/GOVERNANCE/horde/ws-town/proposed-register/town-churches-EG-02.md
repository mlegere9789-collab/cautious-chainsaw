| Description | Four churches have two footprints that differ by more than the reading error. **Presbyterian: three readings** (second pass): about 29 by 47 ft (black rectangle, Hopkins 1858, row 0064), about 45 by 104 ft (Sanborn 1886 as drawn, row 0063) and about 45 by 73 ft (row 0309: the plan of 1886 without its two-storey east part, which is read as the Sunday School rooms that the congregation's history dates to 1877-78, row 0308; that the east part is that addition is an inference). Christ Lutheran: about 25 by 52 ft against 50 by 103 ft (rows 0087, 0088): the symbol is half the size in both directions. Methodist (the hall of 1880): about 38 by 67 ft against 36 by 40 ft (rows 0163, 0164). United Presbyterian: about 39 by 69 ft against 49 by 53 ft (rows 0206, 0207). The German Reformed church agrees (51 by 72 against 45 by 70 ft) and the Catholic church agrees with its own description of 1853 (48 by 90 ft documented against 49 by 89 ft). For three of the four a rebuilding or remodelling is recorded or possible; the Christ Lutheran church was built in 1835 or 1836 and no enlargement is recorded. St. James' is documented at about 40 by 70 ft (row 0225) against 45 by 72 ft on the plan. |

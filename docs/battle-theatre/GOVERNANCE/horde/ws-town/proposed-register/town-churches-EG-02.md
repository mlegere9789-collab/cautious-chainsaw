# town-churches-EG-02: The 1863 footprints of the churches: the Hopkins inset of 1858 against the Sanborn plan of 1886

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | town-churches-EG-02 |
| Requirement IDs | R12, OA10 |
| Category | source conflict (maps of two dates) |
| Description | Four churches have two footprints that differ by more than the reading error. Presbyterian: about 29 by 47 ft (black rectangle, Hopkins 1858, row 0064) against 45 by 104 ft (Sanborn 1886, row 0063). Christ Lutheran: about 25 by 52 ft against 50 by 103 ft (rows 0087, 0088): the symbol is half the size in both directions. Methodist (the hall of 1880): about 38 by 67 ft against 36 by 40 ft (rows 0163, 0164). United Presbyterian: about 39 by 69 ft against 49 by 53 ft (rows 0206, 0207). The German Reformed church agrees (51 by 72 against 45 by 70 ft) and the Catholic church agrees with its own description of 1853 (48 by 90 ft documented against 49 by 89 ft). For three of the four a rebuilding or remodelling is recorded or possible (the Methodist church remodelled in 1880, the United Presbyterian in 1859); the Christ Lutheran church was built in 1835 and no enlargement is recorded, which points to a schematic symbol. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | The Sanborn plan is 23 years after the battle and the Hopkins symbols are schematic (they differ from the Sanborn footprints by 10 to 18 m in position); no measured record of any of these churches exists (HABS search: none); no text gives the Presbyterian, Methodist or United Presbyterian dimensions. |
| Evidence searched | Hopkins 1858 inset (native region file, 6x grids), Sanborn 1886 sheets 1 to 3 (full-size crops), HABS, the weeklies, the History of 1886, Reily 1885 (rows 0023). |
| Autonomous substitute applied | None: both values are carried. |
| Strongest evidence-based reconstruction | Scenario A: the Sanborn footprint (continuity evidence, inferred); scenario B: the Hopkins symbol (schematic, inferred). The outlines of 2023 are not candidates (EG-07). |
| Confidence tier | inferred (both); the Catholic and Reformed footprints agree and the Catholic one is documented |
| Competing values | A Sanborn 1886 against B Hopkins 1858 for four churches; the table of the description. |

| Description | After two passes the evidence file kit/evidence/us-2c-1d.jsonl states the shoulder arms of 2 July 1863, or of the months before it, for nine of the eighteen regiments (eight for the days around the battle; the 145th Pennsylvania for September 1862 only). Stated for July 1863: the 63d New York (smooth-bore Springfield muskets, calibre .69, 60 rounds of buck and ball a man, OR27-1-R084 p. 388); the 63d, 69th and 88th New York (New York at Gettysburg vol. 1 p. 51: "the same old buck-and-ball muskets, calibre 69", each regiment a battalion of two companies; lines 0419-0420 replace the first pass's inferences for the 69th and 88th); the 116th Pennsylvania (the "old pattern musket" loaded with a ball of calibre .69 and three buckshot, September 1862 and the fight of 2 July; Springfield, calibre .58, only from June 1864). Stated before July 1863 and carried as inferred for the day: the 140th Pennsylvania (Vincennes muskets with sabre bayonets in September 1862, "heavy Belgian muskets" in December 1862, new Springfield rifles on 18 January 1863); the 148th Pennsylvania (882 Vincennes rifles of .69 calibre in November 1862, a full supply of new .58 Springfield rifles before 9 February 1863, or about the middle of April in a Company I account; Spencer rifles in October 1864); the 5th New Hampshire (the Enfields of 1861 until Springfield rifles of the model of 1863 in November 1863); the 61st New York (the Enfield rifle in July 1862). The 145th Pennsylvania's old Harper's Ferry smooth-bore muskets are stated for September 1862 only. Bayonets: stated for the 140th and 148th Pennsylvania (sabre or sword bayonets of 1862), the 53d Pennsylvania (fixed for the charge), the 66th New York (not fixed) and, as an order, the Irish Brigade ("Fix bayonets!", 2 July). Side arms: the officers of the 116th Pennsylvania and of the Irish Brigade drew revolvers. Still no statement for July 1863: the weapon of ten regiments: the 81st Pennsylvania, 28th Massachusetts, 52d, 57th, 64th and 66th New York, 27th Connecticut, 2d Delaware, 53d and 145th Pennsylvania (gap line us-2c-1d:0452); the bayonet of fourteen regiments (0453); the side arms of seventeen (0454). The kit marks the 28th Massachusetts weapon (Enfield) "documented" on a weak source; no source read confirms it. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | No non-lead source read states the missing quantities. No regimental history of the 81st Pennsylvania, 28th Massachusetts, 52d, 64th or 66th New York, 27th Connecticut or 53d Pennsylvania was found on archive.org by title (twenty-eight queries, 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-15); the histories of the 5th New Hampshire, 27th Connecticut and 2d Delaware, found in the second pass, say nothing of arms except the 5th's. |

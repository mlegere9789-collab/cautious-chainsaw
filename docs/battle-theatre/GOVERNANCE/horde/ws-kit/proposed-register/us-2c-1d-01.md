| Description | The evidence pass (kit/evidence/us-2c-1d.jsonl, gap lines us-2c-1d:0256 to 0259) found a statement of the shoulder arms of July 1863 for one regiment only: the 63d New York carried smooth-bore Springfield muskets of .69 calibre and 60 rounds of buck-and-ball cartridges a man (Capt. Touhy, OR27-1-R084 p. 388). The 88th and 69th New York are stated armed with buck-and-ball muskets in the spring of 1862 only (inferred, not stated, for July 1863); the 145th Pennsylvania had old Harper's Ferry smooth-bore muskets in September 1862; bayonets are stated for the 53d Pennsylvania (fixed for the charge) and the 66th New York (not fixed). Nothing is stated for the 5th New Hampshire, 61st New York, 81st and 148th Pennsylvania, 28th Massachusetts, 116th Pennsylvania, 52d and 57th New York, 140th Pennsylvania, 27th Connecticut, 2d Delaware, 64th New York, 53d and 145th Pennsylvania (weapon), nor for the side arms of any regiment. The kit marks the 28th Massachusetts (Enfield), 63d, 69th and 88th New York (M1842 smoothbore) "documented" on weak sources; the record confirms the 63d only. |

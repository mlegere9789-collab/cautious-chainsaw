| Description | The record names the commanders of the group at the battle and most successions (kit/evidence/us-2c-1d.jsonl, commander lines; open points in gap line us-2c-1d:0459). Settled in the second pass: the 148th Pennsylvania (Colonel Beaver absent, wounded at Chancellorsville; Lieut. Col. McFarlane in command; Col. McKeen of the 81st sent to command the regiment by Colonel Cross's order on the evening of 30 June; McKeen took the brigade when Cross fell and told McFarlane to resume; Muffly p. 883, lines 0386-0387). Still open: who commanded the First Division after Caldwell took the Second Corps on the evening of 3 July (the 69th New York's report finds Brooke commanding it on the evening of the 2d); the order in which Lieut. Cols. Chapman (57th New York) and Fraser (140th Pennsylvania) held the Third Brigade after Zook fell (the 66th New York's report names Chapman, the organization table and the 140th's adjutant name Fraser, the 140th's historian calls Fraser "the ranking officer after the battle"); who commanded the 2d Delaware in its action and why Col. Baily did not (the report says Capt. Christman commanded); whether Col. Kelly was wounded (one volume says so, Hancock names only Zook, Cross and Brooke). Carried as two alternatives: the manner of Colonel Roberts's death (killed, Caldwell's report; mortally wounded, the 140th's history). |

# kit-us-2c-2d3d-01: Strength at Gettysburg of three brigades and six units of the Second and Third Divisions, Second Corps

| Description | After two passes the evidence file kit/evidence/us-2c-2d3d.jsonl states a strength from the record for 27 of the 36 units (divisions, brigades, regiments, the sharpshooter company and the kit record of Company L), each with its kind and date; where the record gives several figures they are carried as alternatives (the 19th Maine 400 to 440, the 1st Minnesota 262 to 330, the 82d New York 300 to 365, the 111th New York 350 to 400). No source read states the strength of nine units: the Third Brigade of the Second Division (Hall), the First Brigade of the Third Division (Carroll) and the Second Brigade of the Third Division (Smyth) (gap line us-2c-2d3d:0495), and the 42d New York, the 1st Company Massachusetts Sharpshooters, the 14th Indiana, the 7th West Virginia, the 1st Delaware and the 125th New York (0496). Harrow's brigade has no figure of its own: line 0494 is the sum of its four regiments' figures (1,201 to 1,374), inferred. The Philadelphia Brigade has 'about eight hundred men in line' (an estimate in Pennsylvania at Gettysburg, line 0150) and Willard's brigade 'about two thousand' (Simons, line 0436). Figures of one unit that disagree in substance, not in kind, are carried as they are: the 71st Pennsylvania carried into action 24 officers and 397 men (Fox, 421, line 0515) while the Pennsylvania commission's table prints 331 present (line 0440); the 69th Pennsylvania 258 into action and 329 present (0439). Colonel Devereux writes that the 19th Massachusetts and the 42d New York together 'numbered but 290 men' on 2 July (OR27-1-R110 p. 443): a figure for two regiments that is not divided. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | No report, return or history on disk gives the figures: the reports of the brigade commanders (R104, R109, R116, R123, R128) state losses, not strengths; Walker (p. 301) gives the corps ('fewer than ten thousand') and the losses of the divisions and brigades only; Fox's entries of the 42d New York, 14th Indiana, 7th West Virginia, 1st Delaware and 125th New York give losses without a number engaged; the 42d New York's monument (New York at Gettysburg, vol. 1) gives losses; Seville's and Murphy's histories of the 1st Delaware and Simons's of the 125th New York give none; the Indiana volume of 1913 gives the 14th Indiana's war totals. Twelve title searches on archive.org on 2026-11-16 found no regimental history of the 82d, 42d, 59th, 39th, 108th or 111th New York or of the 7th West Virginia; the item found for the 19th Massachusetts' official history (historyofninetee00mass) is a scan of handwritten company rolls with no usable text, and the item found for the 7th Michigan is the cavalry regiment. |
| Evidence searched | OR27-1 reports R098 to R131 (whole text) and the return of casualties and the abstracts of strength read from the scans (R013 pp. 175-177, R008 p. 153); FOX-1889, NY-GB-1900, NY-GB-1900-v2, NY-GB-1902-v3, PA-GB-1904, PA-GB-1914a, PA-GB-1914-v2, ME-GB-1898, MI-GB-1889, MN-1ST-1916, NJ-TOOMBS-1888, NJ-FINAL-1891, OH-8TH-1889, OH-DED-1887, IN-1913, BL-3, HASKELL-1908; the regimental histories fetched from archive.org for this group (Bruce, Seville, Murphy, Page, Sawyer, Potter, Banes, Ward, McDermott, Cowtan, Smith, Ford, Simons, Willson, Kepler, Adams) and Walker's Second Corps. The sweeps (windows of 220 characters round each designation of each unit, over about 60 local texts) and their regexes are written in the gap lines of the evidence file. |

# kit-us-1c-01: Arms (shoulder arms, bayonets, side arms) of the infantry regiments of the First Corps in July 1863

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | kit-us-1c-01 |
| Requirement IDs | R04, R10; gate Q03; evidence group us-1c |
| Category | evidence missing |
| Description | The evidence file kit/evidence/us-1c.jsonl (gap lines us-1c:0362 to 0364) found no statement of the model or calibre of the shoulder arms carried in July 1863 by any of the 32 regiments, and none of the side arms of any regiment. What the record gives is of other dates: the 6th Wisconsin's Belgian muskets had been exchanged for Springfield rifles by the winter of 1861-62 (Dawes, line 0239); the 12th and 13th Massachusetts the Enfield rifle in June and July 1861 (0242, 0244); the 94th New York "a complete equipment of Enfield rifles" in the winter of 1862 and the 105th New York, consolidated into it in March 1863, Austrian rifles in April 1862 (0252, 0407); the 76th New York the Enfield rifle in 1862, inferred from two anecdotes (0253); the 142nd Pennsylvania Belgian rifles at its first formation in August or September 1862 (0406); the 151st Pennsylvania new Springfield rifles at Belle Plain in the spring of 1863 (0255); the 13th Vermont Austrian rifles in place of old muskets (undated), the 14th and 16th Austrian and Enfield rifles in place of old French and Belgian muskets in the autumn of 1862 (0263 to 0265). Bayonets: charges with the bayonet are stated for the 6th Wisconsin, 84th and 95th New York, 149th and 150th Pennsylvania, 13th Vermont and 16th Maine on 1 or 2 July (0353 to 0359). The only record of the calibre mix of the corps is the ammunition expended (Rosengarten, OR27-1-R031 p. 265, line 0292): .57, .58, .54 and .69 calibre rounds, by division, never by regiment. The kit holds `lorenz_m1854` for the 2nd Wisconsin, `springfield_model_unspecified` for the 19th Indiana, 6th and 7th Wisconsin, `m1861_springfield` for the 24th Michigan (all inferred), `m1861_springfield` or `p1853_enfield` for the 84th New York, and a default draw for 26 regiments (plausible-procedural). The record confirms none of them for July 1863. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | No non-lead source read states the missing quantities. Ordnance returns of the regiments are not in the local library; the Official Records parts held give calibre only for the ammunition of the divisions. |
| Evidence searched | OR27-1 reports R029 to R070, whole text, two regex passes (gap lines list the terms); the index sections of the state volumes for each regiment (NY-GB-1900, -v2 and 1902-v3, PA-GB-1904, PA-GB-1914a and -v2, ME-GB-1898, MI-GB-1889), two passes with every hit read; the whole text of PA-GB-1914-v2 (monument inscriptions and the Adjutant General's letters), FOX-1889, BL-3, IN-1913, VT-MON-1890, VT-GB-1914 and the seven regimental histories declared in the file (Dawes, Curtis, Cook, Davis, Small, A. P. Smith, Benedict vol. 2), scanned for every passage near a regiment's designation that holds an arms word, hits read. |
| Autonomous substitute applied | The kit keeps its draw: the default of the army and arm or its own record, with the tier it carries now. The compile applies only evidence lines whose date lies inside 26 June to 16 July 1863 (the bayonet lines); the 1861 and 1862 statements stay in the evidence index. |
| Strongest evidence-based reconstruction | The statements above, each at its date and none promoted. The Iron Brigade's Springfield rifles are documented for 1862 only (6th Wisconsin). The 151st Pennsylvania's Springfields are documented for the spring of 1863 (the model is not named). |

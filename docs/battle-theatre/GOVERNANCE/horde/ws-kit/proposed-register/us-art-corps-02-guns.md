| Description | The evidence file records no count or type of guns for the seven batteries of Tompkins' Sixth Corps artillery brigade other than Cowan's 1st New York Independent Battery (six 3-inch, Hunt, 3 July): Massachusetts A (McCartney), New York 3d (Harn), Rhode Island C (Waterman) and G (Adams), 2d US D (Williston) and G (Butler), 5th US F (Martin); nor a total for the brigade; nor for Battery E, 1st New York (attached to Battery L). The 38 other batteries and six of the seven brigades have lines (the brigade totals of the First, Second, Third and Fifth Corps are derived from the battery lines and are tier inferred). |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | Hunt names only Cowan's battery of the Sixth Corps; the Sixth Corps reports (R238 to R242) are marching diaries of the batteries that give no number or type of guns; the state volumes and the declared Rhode Island histories describe other batteries. Neill's report names Martin's battery of rifled pieces without a count. |
| Evidence searched | OR27-1 R028, R231, R238-R242, R013 p. 157 and p. 174 footnote; NY-GB-1902-v3 (Harn's, Cowan's); the declared Rhode Island histories. Terms in the gap line. |

| Strongest evidence-based reconstruction | The batteries are those the organization table names for the brigade; Hunt's report gives a total of 373 guns for 67 batteries and the abstract of 30 June gives pieces by corps, which bound the brigade total but do not divide it. |

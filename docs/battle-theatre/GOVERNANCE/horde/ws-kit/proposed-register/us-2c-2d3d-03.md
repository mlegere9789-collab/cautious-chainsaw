| Description | After two passes the evidence file states the corps badge of every unit of the group: the white trefoil of the Second Division and the blue trefoil of the Third (Ward, 1906, and Kepler for the 4th Ohio), documented for the 19th Maine, 69th and 71st Pennsylvania, 4th Ohio, 12th New Jersey and 111th New York (trefoil, colour not stated) and for the divisions, inferred from the division's colour for 23 units (for the 8th Ohio and the 111th New York beside their own statements). Sawyer's statement of a white trefoil of the Eighth Ohio is dated to 1864 (the division was the Second only after the reorganization of March 1864; line 0465) and the blue trefoil is inferred for July 1863 (0466). Other fields: coat 13 lines (nine units), headgear 7 (six), trousers 7 (six), shoes 3, shirt 2, accoutrements 1 (the 8th Ohio), flags or colours 4 (the 19th Maine, 1st Minnesota, 69th Pennsylvania, 126th New York), sash and pack state none; almost all are of 1861 or 1862 (the Zouave dress of the 72d Pennsylvania, 10th New York and 1st Delaware, the Garibaldi hat of the 39th New York, the regulation dress of the 15th Massachusetts, the gray overcoats of the 20th Massachusetts and the gray uniforms of the 71st). Gap lines 0500-0508 list, per kit field, the units for which no line exists (headgear 22 units, coat 19, shirt 26, trousers 22, shoes 25, accoutrements 27, sash 28, pack state 28, flags or instruments 24). |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | The record is silent: the Official Records reports do not describe dress; the regimental histories on disk speak of dress at the time of enlistment (1861) and of the badges; the state volumes' inscriptions give the units' names and losses. No photograph description or inventory of July 1863 for these regiments was found among the texts on disk. |
| Evidence searched | OR27-1 reports R098 to R131 (whole text) and the return of casualties and the abstracts of strength read from the scans (R013 pp. 175-177, R008 p. 153); FOX-1889, NY-GB-1900, NY-GB-1900-v2, NY-GB-1902-v3, PA-GB-1904, PA-GB-1914a, PA-GB-1914-v2, ME-GB-1898, MI-GB-1889, MN-1ST-1916, NJ-TOOMBS-1888, NJ-FINAL-1891, OH-8TH-1889, OH-DED-1887, IN-1913, BL-3, HASKELL-1908; the regimental histories fetched from archive.org for this group (Bruce, Seville, Murphy, Page, Sawyer, Potter, Banes, Ward, McDermott, Cowtan, Smith, Ford, Simons, Willson, Kepler, Adams) and Walker's Second Corps. The sweeps (windows of 220 characters round each designation of each unit, over about 60 local texts) and their regexes are written in the gap lines of the evidence file. |
| Autonomous substitute applied | The kit keeps its draw and its tiers (plausible-procedural for the cells without lines); the badge cells now carry the division colour with tier inferred where the evidence is only the division's colour. |
| Strongest evidence-based reconstruction | The lines as written: badge by the division colour (inferred) or by the regiment's own statement (documented); the early dress of the units that wore Zouave or other distinctive uniforms in 1861-62 is stated with its date and is not applied to July 1863 by the compile. |
| Confidence tier | documented (badge of six units and the two divisions, flags of four, early dress of eight units); inferred (badge of 23 units); plausible-procedural for every cell without a line |

| Description | The evidence file kit/evidence/us-6c.jsonl (gap lines us-6c:0264 to us-6c:0275) has an arms statement for three of the 36 regiments (the 49th Pennsylvania: Harper's Ferry muskets, then Austrian .54 rifles in the winter of 1861-62; the 93d Pennsylvania: Belgian rifles, then Springfield muskets before the Peninsular campaign, Springfield rifles in 1864; the 49th New York: 846 smoothbore muskets of calibre .69, buck and ball, 1861), the badge of the corps (the Greek cross, red, white or blue by division; documented for the four New Jersey regiments, the 5th, 6th and 7th Maine and the 49th New York, inferred for the other 28 regiments) and single dress statements for the 95th Pennsylvania (Zouave uniform of 1861 and 1862), the 139th Pennsylvania (shoes worn out on the march of June 1863) and the 7th Maine (shoes badly worn, 1 to 2 July 1863). The bayonet and sword of every regiment, the other dress fields of every regiment and the arms of 33 regiments are not stated by the sources held locally. |

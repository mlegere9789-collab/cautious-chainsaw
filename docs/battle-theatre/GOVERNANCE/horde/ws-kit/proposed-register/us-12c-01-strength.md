| Description | The evidence file kit/evidence/us-12c.jsonl (gap line us-12c:0248) found no figure of strength for the 1st Maryland (Potomac Home Brigade), the 1st Maryland (Eastern Shore), the 150th New York, the 5th, 29th and 66th Ohio and the 149th New York. What the record gives: the corps, the divisions and Lockwood's brigade on 4 July ("for duty equipped": 9,757, 4,363, 3,454, 1,462), the brigades taken into action (McDougall 1,830, Colgrove 1,571, Candy 1,798, Kane 700, Greene 1,424), Greene's estimate of Lockwood's three regiments together (about 1,700), and 21 other regiments by their own reports or the Adjutant General. The kit draws an equal share of 9,755 for each regiment. |

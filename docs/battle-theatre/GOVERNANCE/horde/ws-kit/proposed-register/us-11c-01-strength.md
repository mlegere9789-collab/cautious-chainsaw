| Description | The evidence file kit/evidence/us-11c.jsonl (gap lines us-11c:0255 and us-11c:0256) found no figure of strength for the six brigades (von Gilsa, Ames/Harris, Coster, Smith, Schimmelfennig/von Amsberg, Krzyzanowski) and none for these regiments: the 27th Pennsylvania, 33d Massachusetts, 82d Illinois, 157th and 119th New York, 75th Pennsylvania and 26th Wisconsin. What the record gives: the corps (present for duty on 30 June 603 officers and 9,973 men; 5,513 present for duty on the field return of 4 July; Schurz\'s "hardly over 6,000 effective men" for the First and Third Divisions going into battle), the three divisions on 4 July (1,182; 2,109; 1,728), the Third Division "about 1,500" effective on 2 July, nineteen of the regiments by the Adjutant General, the Ohio commission\'s table, the reports and the state volumes. None of the figures is a brigade figure. |

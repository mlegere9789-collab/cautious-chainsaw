| Description | After the second pass a figure of men taken into action or present on 2 July exists for fifteen of the eighteen regiments (with eight conflict sets of strength, none resolved). Still none for 2 July: the 57th and 66th New York and the 2d Delaware (gap line us-2c-1d:0456; the 57th has an upper bound of 250 present for duty in April 1863, the 2d Delaware about 250 effective men in January 1863, from the New York Times as quoted by Smith, the 66th nothing); the Third Brigade (Zook) and the Fourth Brigade (Brooke) as brigades (0457); the First Division as a whole (0458), for which only the present-for-duty figure of 4 July (1,846) is documented. A sum of the regiments is not possible while three regiments have no figure for the day. The kit gives each regiment an equal share of one corps figure (11,216), which rests on no unit figure; the corps figures in the record are about 10,000 in the action (Hancock; Walker: "fewer than ten thousand"), and the present for duty (officers and men) 12,235 on 10 June, 11,375 on 20 June, 13,056 on 30 June, 6,924 on 4 July, 8,245 on 10 July, 8,356 on 20 July and 7,676 on 31 July (rows of the abstracts, read from the scans); on 14 July 575 officers and 6,973 men of infantry, 8,305 in all. |

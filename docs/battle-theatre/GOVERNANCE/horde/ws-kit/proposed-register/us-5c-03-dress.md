| Description | The evidence file kit/evidence/us-5c.jsonl states a garment or a whole uniform for the 44th New York (Zouave uniforms, October 1861), the 83d and 62d Pennsylvania (Chasseur uniforms, winter 1861-62; the 83d's first uniform of blue jacket, pants and yellow flannel shirt, 1861), the 9th Massachusetts (gray, July 1861), the 146th New York (zouave-type uniforms ordered in the first week of April 1863; "in 1863 adopted the Zouave uniform"), the 140th New York (called Zouaves on 5 May 1864 by Cribben), the 155th and 91st Pennsylvania (Zouave in 1864), the 13th Pennsylvania Reserves (Bucktail in the cap, 1861). None states the dress of July 1863 itself, and for the 140th New York no statement of the dress in June or July 1863 was found (the kit's variant "zouave from 3 June 1863" for the 140th rests on a reading of the 146th's history that was not verified; Brainard's text gives April 1863 for the 146th and no day in June). The dress of 26 regiments (18th and 22d Massachusetts, 1st, 4th and 16th Michigan, 20th Maine, 32d Massachusetts, 118th Pennsylvania, ten regulars, eight Reserves) is not stated for any date. |

# kit-us-art-reserve-horse-01: Strength of the batteries of the Artillery Reserve and the Horse Artillery without a figure of their own

| Category | evidence missing |
| Description | The evidence file kit/evidence/us-art-reserve-horse.jsonl (gap lines of the strength field) records no figure of the men of 23 battery units of the group at Gettysburg: the 1st US H, 3d US F and K, 4th US C, 5th US C, the 1st Connecticut Heavy (B and M, not engaged), 2d Connecticut, 5th New York, 1st New Hampshire, 1st Ohio H, West Virginia C, Maryland A, 1st New Jersey, 1st New York G and K, and the horse batteries (9th Michigan, 6th New York, 2d US B and L, 2d US M, 4th US E, 1st US E and G, 1st US K, 2d US A). Figures with kind and date exist for the 5th and 9th Massachusetts (4 officers and 100 men each), the 15th New York (2 officers and 68 men), Pennsylvania C and F (5 officers and 100 men, one figure for the pair), the 1st Pennsylvania F and G (3 officers and 141 men, one figure for the pair) and the 6th Maine (103 men and officers), and for the Artillery Reserve as a whole (present for duty 104 officers and 2,464 men on 30 June, 112 and 2,882 on 10 July; the field return of 4 July does not report the Reserve or the Cavalry Corps). The only figure of the 6th New York is for the part engaged at Brandy Station on 9 June (36 men). |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | No source read states the missing quantities. The reports of the batteries and of the brigade commanders give losses, rounds and some horses, never the men taken into action; the state volumes held locally describe the batteries by narrative or give figures for the five batteries above only. No regimental history of these batteries is held but that of the 5th Massachusetts (which gives no strength at Gettysburg); none was found on archive.org by title for the others. |
| Evidence searched | OR27-1 R028, R315-R335, R368-R374 (whole text), R008 pp. 151-153, OR27-3 and the rest of OR27-1 by designation; NY-GB-1900, NY-GB-1900-v2, NY-GB-1902-v3, PA-GB-1904, PA-GB-1914a, PA-GB-1914-v2, ME-GB-1898, FOX-1889, NJ-TOOMBS-1888, NJ-FINAL-1891, OH-DED-1887, OH-8TH-1889; the declared history of the 5th Massachusetts. Terms are in the gap lines. |

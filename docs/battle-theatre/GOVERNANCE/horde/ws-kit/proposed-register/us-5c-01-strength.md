| Category | evidence missing |
| Description | The evidence file kit/evidence/us-5c.jsonl (gap lines of the strength field) records no strength figure for the 18th and 22d Massachusetts, the 1st Michigan, the 9th Massachusetts (absent from the brigade on 2 July on special duty and so not in Sweitzer's inclosure), the 3d, 4th, 6th, 12th and 14th United States of the First Brigade and the 2d United States of the Second Brigade of the Second Division, the 140th and 146th New York and the 9th Pennsylvania Reserves; and none for the brigades of Day, Weed and Fisher as wholes (their regiments' figures are incomplete: Day none of five, Weed two of four, Fisher four of five). Figures exist, with kind and date, for the corps and the three divisions (field return of 4 July: present for duty), the 4th Michigan, 62d Pennsylvania and 32d Massachusetts (inclosure of Sweitzer's report: before and after the action), the 20th Maine, 16th Michigan, 44th New York and 83d Pennsylvania, the 7th, 10th, 11th and 17th United States, eight regiments of the Pennsylvania Reserves and the 83d, 91st, 118th and 155th Pennsylvania (Adjutant General letters, present for duty on 30 June), the 62d Pennsylvania (carried into action), and the First, Second and Third (muskets) brigades of the First Division (Barnes, Tilton, Rice, Judson). |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | No source read states the missing quantities; the Official Records reports of those regiments give losses and officers present only; the Pennsylvania Adjutant General's letters carry no letter for the 9th Reserves; regimental histories of the regular regiments, of the 18th Massachusetts, the 1st Michigan and the 140th New York for these figures were not found on archive.org by title or hold no figure. |
| Evidence searched | OR27-1 R187 to R220 (whole text), R008 p. 153, R193 p. 613; PA-GB-1904, PA-GB-1914a, PA-GB-1914-v2 (56 letters headed "Strength at Gettysburg"); NY-GB-1900 (vols. 1-2), NY-GB-1902-v3, ME-GB-1898, MI-GB-1889; the declared histories (22d, 9th, 32d Massachusetts, 44th, 140th, 146th New York, 83d, 118th Pennsylvania, Reserves). Terms are in the gap lines. |

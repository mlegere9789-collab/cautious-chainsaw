| Description | The evidence file kit/evidence/us-1c.jsonl (gap lines us-1c:0365 to 0374) found, for July 1863, only these statements of dress: the black hat of the Iron Brigade ("stiff, broad brimmed, tall, black hats", Curtis, lines 0222 to 0238, 24th Michigan furnished with them on 27 May 1863) and its round red badge (the circular of 21 March 1863, 0412, 0413; Curtis, 0223 and the like); the division colours carried by the Iron Brigade from 1 June 1863 (0237); the bucktail in the cap of the 149th and 150th Pennsylvania (Fox, 0256, 0257; the 150th's address, 0258); packs left behind or thrown off on 1 July by the 143rd, 149th, 150th and 151st Pennsylvania (0259 to 0262); and the dress of the 16th Maine in 1862 (no tents, knapsacks or overcoats until 27 November 1862, 0408, 0409; barefoot on 12 July 1863 with shoes issued, 0248). The dress of 1861 and 1862 is stated for the 84th New York (chasseur dress: red trousers, dark blue jacket with two rows of bell buttons, red breast piece, red cap, 0249 to 0251), the 6th Wisconsin (gray state uniform of 1861, 0240), the 12th and 13th Massachusetts (0243 to 0247); the 6th Wisconsin and 76th New York were barefoot after the battle (inferred, 0241, 0254). The corps badge in the colour of the division is derived, at tier inferred, for 24 regiments of the 1st, 2nd and 3rd Divisions from the circular (0414 to 0437); the three Vermont regiments joined the corps in June and are not derived. No source read states the coat, shirt, trousers, shoes, accoutrements, sash or flag of July 1863 for any other regiment (headgear for 23 regiments, coat for 28, shirt for 32, trousers for 30, shoes for 29, accoutrements for 32, sash for 32, pack state for 27, flag or instrument for 32). |

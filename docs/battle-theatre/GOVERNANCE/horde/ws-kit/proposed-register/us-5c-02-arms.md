| Description | The evidence file kit/evidence/us-5c.jsonl states the shoulder arm for eight of the 36 regiments and for none of them on a date inside 26 June to 16 July 1863: the 20th Maine (spurious Enfield rifles, 6 September 1862), the 22d Massachusetts (Enfield rifles replaced by Springfield rifles, winter 1861-62), the 9th Massachusetts (69-calibre muskets, June 1861), the 44th New York (new Springfield muskets, October 1861), the 83d Pennsylvania (Harper's Ferry muskets, then Springfield, autumn 1861), the 91st Pennsylvania (Belgian rifles, then Springfield muskets, winter 1861-62), the 155th Pennsylvania (Belgian or Austrian rifles, exchanged for Springfield smooth-bore muskets, September 1862: a conflict set of two sources), the 13th Pennsylvania Reserves (Sharps rifles at Gettysburg per Thomson; "some of the companies with Spencer repeating rifles" per the state volume: a conflict set). The arm of 27 regiments is not stated for any date: the 18th Massachusetts, 1st, 4th and 16th Michigan, 118th, 62d Pennsylvania, 32d Massachusetts, 140th and 146th New York, the ten regular regiments and eight Pennsylvania Reserves. The bayonet and side arms are not stated for any regiment. |

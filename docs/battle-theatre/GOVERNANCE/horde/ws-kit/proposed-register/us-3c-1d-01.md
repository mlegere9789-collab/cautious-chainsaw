| Description | The evidence file kit/evidence/us-3c-1d.jsonl (gap lines us-3c-1d:0452 to 0454) states the shoulder arm for ten of the nineteen regiments, but for July 1863 itself only the 17th Maine (Enfield rifles until the night of 3 July, exchanged for Springfields by order of General Birney: Houghton, line 0408; the Enfield of 2 July is inferred, 0409). For the others the statement is of an earlier or a later date and the arm of 2 July is carried as inferred: the 57th Pennsylvania (Springfield muskets at muster-in in October 1861, 0431; Austrian rifles in May 1862, 0432, and from about 1 January 1862 to August 1863, 0236, 0237; Springfield rifled muskets from August 1863, 0238); the 63d Pennsylvania ("Austrian rifles" and "four-ribbed bayonets", colonel's letter of 5 February 1862, 0395, 0396; "the old Austrian muzzle loaders" until the Spencers of 1864, 0397); the 105th Pennsylvania (Springfield rifles until July 1864, 0404, 0405); the 124th New York (Austrian rifles with sword-bayonets in September 1862, 0383; Enfields from 10 January 1863, 0384, 0385); the 141st Pennsylvania (Austrian muskets in September 1862, 0386; "our Austrian guns" in March 1863 with 75 Springfield rifles promised to the two best companies, 0387, 0388; the day, 0389); the 40th New York (smooth-bore calibre .69 in 1861, 0308; the Austrian rifle with four-cornered bayonets in March 1862, 0411, 0412; Companies F and D "armed with superior rifles" in May 1862, 0413; the day, 0414); the 3d Maine (Springfield smooth-bore of June 1861 only, 0291); the 4th Maine (bayonets fixed for the charge, 0297); the 1st and 2d United States Sharpshooters (Sharps rifles of .52 calibre with linen cartridges and the angular bayonet from 8 May 1862, Stevens 0353, 0354, Fox 0436; the Sharps rifles on 2 July for the 1st, 0355; for the 2d the exchange for Sharps in June or July 1862, 0356, and the day inferred, 0357). Still no statement for any date: the weapon of nine regiments (68th, 114th Pennsylvania, 20th Indiana, 4th Maine, 86th New York, 99th Pennsylvania, 3d and 5th Michigan, 110th Pennsylvania; gap 0452); the bayonet of fifteen (0453); the side arms of nineteen (0454). No report of the group names the model of an arm. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | No non-lead source read states the missing quantities. No regimental history of the 20th Indiana, 3d or 4th Maine, 86th New York, 99th, 110th or 68th Pennsylvania, or 3d or 5th Michigan was found on archive.org by title (31 queries of the catalogue search, 2026-11-16); the histories found (Weygant, Craft, Houghton, Floyd, Hays, Scott, Rauscher, the 57th Pennsylvania's, Stevens) say nothing of the arms of the regiments they do not treat. The kit's arms for the two sharpshooter regiments (the Sharps rifle of 1859, marked documented on two American Rifleman articles whose claim support the kit tables list as unverified) now have Stevens and Fox for the Sharps rifle, without the year of its model. |
| Evidence searched | OR27-2 and OR27-3 (by the unit names and Zouave, Berdan, green, Excelsior, Mozart, Collis: nothing); OR27-1 reports R129 to R157; PA-GB-1904, PA-GB-1914a, PA-GB-1914-v2, NY-GB-1900 (vols. 1-2), NY-GB-1902-v3, ME-GB-1898, MI-GB-1889, IN-1913, FOX-1889, BL-3, MEADE-LL-v2, HASKELL-1908, LETTERMAN-1866, CEM-REPORT-1867 (by the unit names); and the nine texts declared in the evidence file (us-3c-1d:0333 and 0374 to 0381), each scanned for every arms word (rifle, musket, Enfield, Springfield, Austrian, Belgian, Lorenz, smooth-bore, calibre, armed, bayonet, sword, Sharps, Colt) with the windows read. Terms are listed in the gap lines. |

# kit-us-1c-03: Strength of the First Corps units whose figures the record does not give

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | kit-us-1c-03 |
| Requirement IDs | R04, R17; gate Q01; evidence group us-1c |
| Category | evidence missing |
| Description | The evidence file kit/evidence/us-1c.jsonl (gap line us-1c:0375) found no figure of strength for the 7th Wisconsin, the 7th Indiana, the 13th, 14th and 16th Vermont, and Cutler's and Paul's brigades. For the other 27 regiments there are figures (taken into action for 19; present for duty or an estimate for the rest) but eight regiments have no figure of the men taken into action: the 6th Wisconsin (about 200 left on 4 July, line 0279), the 94th New York (445 present for duty on the morning of 1 July, 0269), the 95th New York (less than 250 engaged, 0404; 263 present for duty on 30 June, 0270), the 104th New York (about 330 in line, 0402), the 12th Massachusetts (about 200, 0405), the 97th New York (150 fit for duty on 21 July, 0403), the 11th Pennsylvania (292 present for duty on 30 June, 0387) and the 142nd Pennsylvania (362 present for duty on 30 June, 0394; 336 on the monument, 0395: a conflict set). The Adjutant General's office states, for the 11th, 90th, 121st and 142nd Pennsylvania, that the number carried into action is "not stated" (11th, 121st) or "not of record" (90th, 142nd) (PA-GB-1914-v2, lines 0387, 0389, 0391, 0394). At the level of formations the record gives the corps, the divisions' present for duty on 4 July (1st 1,602, 2nd 1,030, 3rd 2,269, lines 0141 to 0143) and, for the Vermont brigade, only two estimates (nearly 2,000 muskets, 0438; 2,500, 0439); the men taken into action of the 1st and 3rd Divisions are not stated. The kit draws an equal share of the brigade figure for 32 regiments (1,830 for the Iron Brigade, 2,015 Cutler, 1,540 Paul, 12,160 for the I Corps figure on 16 regiments); the record gives figures for 27 of them and none of the brigade figures of Cutler and Paul. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | The reports of the group, the returns, the state volumes, Fox and the histories searched do not state the quantities; for four regiments the War Department says the figure is not stated or not of record. |
| Evidence searched | OR27-1 R008 (abstracts, pp. 151 to 153), R029 to R070; the index sections of the state volumes; the whole text of PA-GB-1904, PA-GB-1914a, PA-GB-1914-v2, NY-GB-1900, -v2, NY-GB-1902-v3, ME-GB-1898, MI-GB-1889, FOX-1889, BL-3, IN-1913, VT-MON-1890, VT-GB-1914 and the seven declared histories, scanned for strength words near each regiment's designation (hits read for the units of the gap line); brigade statements for Cutler, Paul and Stannard searched across the same texts. |
| Autonomous substitute applied | The theatre draws the equal share it draws now, marked plausible-procedural, for the units named in the gap line; the compile applies only the evidence lines inside 26 June to 16 July 1863. |
| Strongest evidence-based reconstruction | The regimental figures and ranges of the file with their kinds (taken into action, present for duty, estimate), never added across kinds; the 4 July divisional present for duty as the strength of the formations. |
| Confidence tier | plausible-procedural for the units of the gap line; documented for the figures of the file |
| Product marking | Unit card shows the range and its basis; for the units of the gap line the card says the figure is a share of the brigade figure. |

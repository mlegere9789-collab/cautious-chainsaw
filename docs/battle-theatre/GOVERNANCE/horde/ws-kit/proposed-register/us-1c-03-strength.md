# kit-us-1c-03: Strength of the First Corps units whose figures the record does not give

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | kit-us-1c-03 |
| Requirement IDs | R04, R17; gate Q01; evidence group us-1c |
| Category | evidence missing |
| Description | The evidence file kit/evidence/us-1c.jsonl (gap line us-1c:0375 and the second-pass gap lines 0680 and 0681) found no figure of strength on 1 to 3 July for the 7th Wisconsin, the 13th, 14th and 16th Vermont, and Cutler's and Paul's brigades; the 7th Indiana has only Hunt's "nearly five hundred fresh men" (0457, written in 1887) and the Vermont regiments only their present for duty of 15 February 1863 (0468 to 0472: 831, 777 and 684). For the other regiments there are figures (men taken into action for 20 of the 32; present for duty or an estimate for the rest) but twelve regiments have no figure of the men taken into action (the 7th Wisconsin, 7th Indiana, 95th, 94th, 104th and 97th New York, 12th Massachusetts, 11th and 142nd Pennsylvania and the three Vermont regiments); the 6th Wisconsin, which had none in the first pass, now has 340 who marched and 420 at the turnpike fence (0454 to 0456, Dawes). The other figures are: the 94th New York (445 present for duty on the morning of 1 July, 0269), the 95th New York (less than 250 engaged, 0404; 263 present for duty on 30 June, 0270), the 104th New York (about 330 in line, 0402), the 12th Massachusetts (about 200, 0405), the 97th New York (150 fit for duty on 21 July, 0403), the 11th Pennsylvania (292 present for duty on 30 June, 0387) and the 142nd Pennsylvania (362 present for duty on 30 June, 0394; 336 on the monument, 0395: a conflict set; Warren's about 320 men and sixteen officers, 0499, agrees with 336; 78 mustered that night, 0500). The Adjutant General's office states, for the 11th, 90th, 121st and 142nd Pennsylvania, that the number carried into action is "not stated" (11th, 121st) or "not of record" (90th, 142nd) (PA-GB-1914-v2, lines 0387, 0389, 0391, 0394). At the level of formations the record gives the corps, the divisions' present for duty on 4 July (1st 1,602, 2nd 1,030, 3rd 2,269, lines 0141 to 0143) and, for the Vermont brigade, only two estimates (nearly 2,000 muskets, 0438; 2,500, 0439); the men taken into action of the 1st and 3rd Divisions are not stated; the 2nd Division is "less than 2,500" (Robinson, 0448; Cook's 2,500, 0450). The kit draws an equal share of the brigade figure for 32 regiments (1,830 for the Iron Brigade, 2,015 Cutler, 1,540 Paul, 12,160 for the I Corps figure on 16 regiments); the record gives figures for 27 of them and none of the brigade figures of Cutler and Paul. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | The reports of the group, the returns, the state volumes, Fox and the histories searched do not state the quantities; for four regiments the War Department says the figure is not stated or not of record. |
| Evidence searched | OR27-1 R008 (abstracts, pp. 151 to 153), R029 to R070; the index sections of the state volumes; the whole text of PA-GB-1904, PA-GB-1914a, PA-GB-1914-v2, NY-GB-1900, -v2, NY-GB-1902-v3, ME-GB-1898, MI-GB-1889, FOX-1889, BL-3, IN-1913, VT-MON-1890, VT-GB-1914 and the seven declared histories, scanned for strength words near each regiment's designation (hits read for the units of the gap line); brigade statements for Cutler, Paul and Stannard searched across the same texts; second pass: a second OCR of the Official Records pages that carry strength quotes (the digits of every quote compared), Fox's passages on the 7th Wisconsin, and the strength words of the eight further histories (Chamberlin and Warren and Vautier, Gates, Hussey, Sturtevant, Williams, the 84th souvenir). |
| Autonomous substitute applied | The theatre draws the equal share it draws now, marked plausible-procedural, for the units named in the gap line; the compile applies only the evidence lines inside 26 June to 16 July 1863. |
| Strongest evidence-based reconstruction | The regimental figures and ranges of the file with their kinds (taken into action, present for duty, estimate), never added across kinds; the 4 July divisional present for duty as the strength of the formations. |
| Confidence tier | plausible-procedural for the units of the gap line; documented for the figures of the file |
| Product marking | Unit card shows the range and its basis; for the units of the gap line the card says the figure is a share of the brigade figure. |

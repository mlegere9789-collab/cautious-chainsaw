| Description | The evidence file kit/evidence/us-3c-1d.jsonl gives a strength for fifteen of the nineteen regiments, the division and the corps (the corps: abstracts of 10, 20 and 30 June, 12,630 officers and men present for duty on 30 June, 13,881 aggregate present, 10,451 men and 796 officers of the infantry present for duty equipped, 0129 to 0138; 6,130 effective on 4 July, 0140; "not over 9,000" on the morning of 2 July, 0323). It has no strength for: the Graham brigade (gap 0446; the regiment figures give about 1,780 on mixed bases, a sum and not a record) and the de Trobriand brigade (0450); the 20th Indiana (0447); the 40th New York (0451: Floyd gives the 238 men of the 37th and the 387 of the 38th New York that joined it on 29 May and 3 June 1863, lines 0415, 0416, not its strength); each of the two sharpshooter regiments (0448, 0449: Berdan gives "about 450 rifles" for both, 0149, and 100-man detachments, 0338, 0339, 0341). Source conflicts on the figures that exist are carried as scenarios: the 57th Pennsylvania (209 or 205), 105th (284 or 247), 141st (209, 200 or 198), 86th New York (368 or 268). The kit's equal share of the corps figure (10,691, from Stone Sentinels through the PDF) has no record behind it for any regiment. |

# kit-us-3c-2d-01: Arms (shoulder arms, bayonets, side arms) of nine regiments of the Second Division, Third Corps, in July 1863

| Description | The evidence file kit/evidence/us-3c-2d.jsonl (gap line us-3c-2d:0211) records that no source read states the arms of the 1st and 16th Massachusetts, the 26th and 115th Pennsylvania, the 120th New York and the 5th, 6th, 7th and 8th New Jersey on any date. For ten other regiments the file has statements of an earlier or a later date, none of the day itself except Company B of the 2d New Hampshire: the 2d New Hampshire (smooth-bore muskets of calibre .69 with buck and ball for nine companies and Sharps breech-loaders for Company B in 1861, 0182-0183; Springfield rifled muskets from September 1861, 0184; Company B with Sharps breech-loaders on 2 July 1863, 0185); the 11th Massachusetts ("smooth-bore Springfield musket" with ball and three buckshot at Bull Run, 21 July 1861); the 12th New Hampshire (old French muskets exchanged for Springfield muskets and rifles in the autumn of 1862, rifles to Companies C and F); the 11th New Jersey (Austrian rifles exchanged for Harper's Ferry or Springfield muskets in 1862; Harper's Ferry muskets for most of the regiment on 27 November 1863); the 72d New York (smooth-bore percussion muskets, model 1842 altered, seven companies, July 1861); the 70th to 74th New York (buck and ball at Williamsburg, 5 May 1862); the 84th Pennsylvania (old Belgian guns handed out on 3 January 1862). The bayonet and the side arm are stated for no regiment of the group. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | No non-lead source read states the missing quantities. The regimental histories on the library list or found on archive.org by title (Cudworth for the 1st Massachusetts, Hutchinson for the 11th, Bartlett for the 12th New Hampshire, Haynes for the 2d, Marbaker for the 11th New Jersey, Brown for the 72d New York, Van Santvoord for the 120th) were fetched and read by arms words; those for the 16th Massachusetts, the 26th, 84th and 115th Pennsylvania (the Bates volumes and the monument addresses were searched), the 5th to 8th New Jersey and the 70th, 71st, 73d and 74th New York were not found (catalogue queries of 2026-11-24). The Official Records reports of the group name only the number of muskets or rifles, never a model. |
| Evidence searched | OR27-1 reports R133, R158-R178 (read or searched as the gap line says); OR27-3, ORSER3-v2, BL-3 (by unit names and arms words); NJ-TOOMBS-1888, NJ-FINAL-1891, NY-GB-1900 (vols. 1-2), NY-GB-1902-v3, PA-GB-1904, PA-GB-1914a, PA-GB-1914-v2, MA-BURIAL-1863, FOX-1889, MEADE-LL-v2, LETTERMAN-1866, CEM-REPORT-1867, HASKELL-1908 (by the unit designations within 300 characters before or 400 after of the arms words listed in the gap line); the seven texts declared in the file (us-3c-2d:0001-0007). |

| Description | The evidence file kit/evidence/us-3c-1d.jsonl (gap lines us-3c-1d:0455 to 0463) found, for July 1863 itself, only these statements of dress: the red diamond of the division (a documented line at the corps for the system of corps badges, April 1863, 0437; the 57th Pennsylvania's own statement "wore a red diamond", 0435; the Kearny patch of Ward's report for the 1st and 2d Sharpshooters, 86th and 124th New York, 0328 to 0331, inferred; the patch on the cap of the 3d, 4th and 17th Maine and the 40th New York, 0292 to 0294, 0309; the 99th Pennsylvania, 0254, 0255; eight regiments derive it from the corps line at tier inferred, 0438 to 0445); haversacks slung and blankets rolled on the morning of 1 July for the 124th New York (0322); "many of the men were without shoes" in the 17th Maine on 2 July (0332). The Sharpshooters wore the green coat (Stevens: the "Green Coats" of 1 May 1863, 0367; Captain Marble in 1888 of the march to Gettysburg, 0369; green clothing, blue overcoats and blankets "formerly turned in" issued on 17 September 1863, 0370; the officers of the 2d Regiment in September 1862, 0371; 0372 inferred for the day), the cap with a black plume (0359, 0373 inferred), light blue and later green trousers (0360, 0361, undated), leather leggings (0362) and a hair-covered calfskin knapsack of which the last were lost in June 1862 (0363, 0364). The 114th Pennsylvania wore the Zouave dress to the end of the war (Rauscher: red pants, Zouave jacket, white leggings, blue sash, white turban, 0417 to 0421; inferred for 1 to 3 July 1863, 0422 to 0426; the burned dead of 2 July known by their dress, 0266). The dress of 1861 is stated for the 63d Pennsylvania (dark blue blouse, light blue trousers, half-wool shirts, low broad-soled shoes, small forage caps, September 1861, 0398 to 0402) and the 57th Pennsylvania (forage caps, dark blue blouses, sky blue pants, army brogans, October 1861, 0427 to 0430). For the headgear of fourteen regiments, the coat of fourteen, the shirt of eighteen, the trousers of fifteen, the shoes of fourteen, the accoutrements of seventeen, the sash of nineteen, the pack state of sixteen and the flag or instrument of eighteen, no source read states anything (gaps 0455 to 0463). |

# kit-us-art-corps-01: Strength of the Union corps artillery batteries without a figure of their own

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | kit-us-art-corps-01 |
| Requirement IDs | R04, R17; gates Q01, Q14; evidence group us-art-corps |
| Category | evidence missing |
| Description | The evidence file kit/evidence/us-art-corps.jsonl (gap lines of the strength field) records no figure of the men of 34 of the 38 batteries of the seven corps artillery brigades at Gettysburg. Figures with kind and date exist for the Second and Fifth Maine batteries (150 on the rolls; 136 strong on the morning of 1 July), Cooper's Battery B, 1st Pennsylvania (114 present for duty) and Harn's 3d New York (4 officers, 115 men present for duty), and for the seven artillery brigades as wholes (field return of 4 July 1863: present for duty for the First, Second, Fifth and Eleventh Corps, effective strength for the Third, for duty equipped for the Twelfth; the Sixth is not named in the footnote, and the Eleventh and Twelfth rows leave out Battery K, 1st Ohio, and Battery M, 1st New York). Battery E, 1st New York, was merged into Battery L before the battle and has no figure of its own. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | No source read states the missing quantities. The reports of the batteries and of the brigade commanders give losses, rounds and some horses, never the men taken into action; the state volumes held locally describe the batteries by narrative; the histories of Batteries A, B and E, 1st Rhode Island, declared in the file, give no strength at Gettysburg. No Rhode Island, Massachusetts or regular-army battery history for the other batteries is held. |
| Evidence searched | OR27-1 R028, R071-R075, R132, R181-R186, R221-R222, R238-R242, R264-R270, R314 (whole text), R008 p. 153; NY-GB-1900, NY-GB-1900-v2, NY-GB-1902-v3, PA-GB-1904, PA-GB-1914a, PA-GB-1914-v2, ME-GB-1898, FOX-1889, NJ-TOOMBS-1888, NJ-FINAL-1891, OH-DED-1887; the three declared Rhode Island battery histories. Terms are in the gap lines. |
| Autonomous substitute applied | The kit keeps its equal share of the corps figure for these batteries with its tier. |
| Strongest evidence-based reconstruction | The brigade figure of 4 July (present for duty or its equivalent) divided among the batteries of the brigade, marked as an allotment; the four batteries with a figure keep their own. |
| Confidence tier | plausible-procedural (cells with no line); documented (cells with lines) |
| Product marking | The unit card keeps the tier mark; a battery with no figure shows its share of the brigade figure marked as an allotment. |

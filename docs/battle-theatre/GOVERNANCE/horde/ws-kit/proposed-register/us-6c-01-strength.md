| Category | evidence missing |
| Description | The evidence file kit/evidence/us-6c.jsonl (gap lines us-6c:0261 and us-6c:0262) found no figure of strength for seven of the eight brigades (all but Torbert's First Brigade of the First Division) and none for these regiments: the 121st New York, 49th and 119th Pennsylvania, 5th Wisconsin, the five Vermont regiments, the 33d, 43d, 49th, 62d, 65th, 67th, 77th and 122d New York, the 61st Pennsylvania, the 7th, 10th and 37th Massachusetts and the 2d Rhode Island. What the record gives: the corps (present for duty on 30 June 1,031 officers and 14,679 men; the field return of 4 July 12,832, kind not stated for this corps), the three divisions on 4 July (4,173; 3,424; 4,228), Torbert's brigade and its four regiments (1,663 on a return of 17 July, kind not stated), seven Pennsylvania regiments by the Adjutant General (present for duty on 30 June: 23d 538, 82d 320, 93d 270, 95th 356, 96th 356, 98th 406, 139th 511) and the 5th, 6th and 7th Maine by estimate (about 275, 350 and 200). None gives the men taken into action; the Adjutant General says for the 23d and others that it is "not of record". The kit draws an equal share of 13,601 for each regiment. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | The Official Records and the local volumes searched do not state the quantities; the regimental histories of the Vermont, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, Wisconsin and most of the New York regiments are not held locally and were not fetched in this pass. |

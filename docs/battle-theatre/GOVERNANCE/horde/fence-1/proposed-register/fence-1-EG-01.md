| Description | Close-out 2026-11-13. Documented now: a rail, stump and earth barricade west of the Seminary on Jul 1; Doubleday ordered Robinson's division to intrench; Robinson set Paul's First Brigade (16th Maine, 94th NY, 104th NY, 13th Mass., 107th Pa.) to work between about 11:00 and 13:00 (Baxter reached the front at 11:00, the 16th Maine left for the right about 13:00); 'fence rails' (Maine adjutant) and 3,108 rails lost from Dr. Schmucker's adjacent farm (NPS). Guns: Hunt gives 16 concentrated plus Stewart's 6 (22 in four batteries), Wainwright says twelve concentrated; the PDF's 20 has no OR support. Still open: the 600-yd length, the curved plan, any length of fence dismantled, the hour the barricade fell (bounded 15:45-16:30), and the builder conflict (McFarland credits the 'Second Brigade, Second Division'). WAVE 4 (2026-11-14): the curved plan is now sourced as a conflict of form: a circular redoubt of earth and fence rails 'just in front of the seminary' (Small, 16th Maine, 1886) and a 'semicircular line of breast works' west of the seminary (Toombs 1888) against McFarland's line through the west edge of the grove (OR27-1-R060); Bates 1875: ordered as the line to which the division could fall back; the hour it was turned: Cooper 'after 4 p.m.', Pender 'about 4 o'clock', NPS 'by about 5:00 p.m.'. Still open: the 600 yd length, any length of fence removed, the start clock. |

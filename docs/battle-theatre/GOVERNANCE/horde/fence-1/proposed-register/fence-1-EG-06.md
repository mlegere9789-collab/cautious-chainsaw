| Description | Close-out 2026-11-13. Builders are now documented: the battery crews on the artillery chief's orders (Breck: 'by order of Colonel Wainwright, I caused [the position] to be intrenched'; Cooper's Battery B finished four lunettes on the morning of Jul 2; the 1st NY battery men built redans or lunettes for each gun). Window: begun at dusk Jul 1, finished the morning of Jul 2, in use by 16:00. The word lunette is not in the OR; the 1900-1914 books and the NPS use it. Still open: the 1863 dimensions (the preserved lunettes were reconstructed in 1878 and measure about 30 in high: not an 1863 profile). WAVE 4 (2026-11-14): the builder, fabric and hour are a three-way conflict of testimony: the OR (battery crews, light earthworks at dusk, lunettes finished the morning of Jul 2); Bates 1875 and Curtis 1891 (Steinwehr's division, solid lunettes with 'smooth and perfectly level platforms' during Jul 1; the New York and Pennsylvania volumes and Toombs and Pierce also call Steinwehr's position fortified); Hancock (letter of 17 Jan 1878, who reached the hill about 15:00-15:30): 'a great error ... all that I saw ... were some holes (not deep) dug to sink the wheels and trails'. Biddle 1880: Bates's authority 'does not appear'. No 1863 report credits Steinwehr. |

| Description | Close-out 2026-11-13. E-13 is contradicted in part: the 83rd Pa. stood 'behind rocks and stones hastily thrown up for defense' before the attack (Woodward, OR27-1-R201). E-14: both builders documented: Weed's brigade 'at night' Jul 2 (Garrard R217), Fisher's Reserves in the morning Jul 3 (Fisher R220), plus two to three or four lines of stone breastworks seen from the Confederate side built through the night (Benning R458, Waddell R462) and the NPS statement; the PDF's five Reserve regiments and Ohio battery as builders are unsupported. Still open: positive 'no works' for three of Vincent's regiments and for Ward's brigade; the hauling method; whether an epaulement existed. WAVE 4 (2026-11-14): the NPS Little Round Top report quotes veterans: the 44th NY 'threw up a small breast-work' on Jul 2 (Nash 1911); the 140th NY 'built stone walls in the dark' (Hazen 1894); the 146th NY was 'instructed by Colonel Garrand to erect a rough stone wall' before the attack of Jul 2 (Brainard 1915); a veteran of the 1st Pa. The Ohio battery as a builder has no support. E-15: the First Minnesota (1916) also has the guns dragged by hand. |

| Description | Wave 5 (2026-11-15). The PDF has about 11:00 for the charge at the unfinished railroad cut. In the Official Records Reynolds fell about 10:15 (Doubleday) and Cutler's brigade was in action from 10 a.m.; no OR report gives a clock for the charge on the cut. The 95th NY's monument inscription (New York Monuments Commission volume, printed p. 732) says: 'At 10:30 A. M. changed front, advanced to this position with the 84th New York and 6th Wisconsin, repulsed and captured a large part of Davis' Mississippi Brigade in the Railroad cut.' CORRECTION: the OCR text of the volume prints '10:50'; the page image (looked at 2026-11-15) shows 10:30, the digit after the colon being the 3 of '1863' on the line above; the pass of 2026-11-14 had taken '10:50' as legible and recorded it (DD-FENCE1-27), which DD-FENCE1-30 corrects. |

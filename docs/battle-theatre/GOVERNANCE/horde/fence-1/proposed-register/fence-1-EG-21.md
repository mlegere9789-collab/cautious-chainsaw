| Description | Wave 5 (2026-11-15), carried from the close-out of 2026-11-13/14 because it had no register entry. Documented: the bridge was burnt on the evening of Friday Jun 26 (Jacobs 1864; Early's sequence) and the Gettysburg Compiler of 29 Jun 1863 (page image) reads: 'The railroad bridge across Rock creek was soon set on fire by the rebels ... The [c]entre span of the bridge was burnt and [smeared] in. The other two are standing, but [ba]dly damaged.' The word before 'in.' is smeared in the scan ('fell' is the likely reading and is not asserted); no hour is printed. WAVE 6 (2026-11-16): a second scan of the Gettysburg Compiler leaf was looked for: archive.org holds one item for the issue of 29 Jun 1863 (gettysburg-compiler-1863-06-29, advancedsearch, one request) and the Chronicling America title search answers the Library of Congress challenge page (not solved, not worked around); the smeared word stays unread. Haupt's Reminiscences (1901, F-356, local since 2026-11-16) were searched: no statement about the burning of 26 Jun and no mention of the Rock Creek bridge; Haupt's dispatches of Jul 4 speak of 'a large bridge near' Oxford, seven miles east of Gettysburg, 'considerably damaged' and of the Construction Corps reaching 'the last bridge on the road to Gettysburg' on the night of Jul 4, without naming the bridge. |

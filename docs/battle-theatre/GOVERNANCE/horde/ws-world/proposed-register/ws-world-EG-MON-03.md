# ws-world-EG-MON-03: The positions of the memorials are the site's list as it stands

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | ws-world-EG-MON-03 |
| Requirement IDs | OA01; R14; Law clauses 3, 4 and 7 |
| Category | evidence weak (no stated source for most positions) |
| Description | The site's Monuments list gives a latitude and longitude for each of the 512 entries. For 503 of them the list states no source for the position; for 9 it names a web listing (Stone Sentinels, HMDB). Nine state memorials are also in a second list of the site (data/monuments.json) whose own sources are web pages, one of them Wikipedia (a lead, not a source); the two lists differ for those nine by 1 to 24 m. The theatre copies both without correcting either. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | A position is only as good as the list; a check against the park's own survey data (NPS GIS monument layer, the 1932 and 1993 NPS lists of monument locations on the avenues) has not been made. The two NPS lists (1932, 1993) name locations by regiment, brigade and avenue; a search of their OCR text finds no coordinate. |
| Evidence searched | The site's two lists (read-only copies and their hashes); the 2026-11-13 NPS acquisition (tmp/library/web/nps, NPS-MON-LOC-1932 and NPS-MON-LOC-1993: lists of locations; no latitude, longitude or coordinate pattern in their OCR text). |
| Autonomous substitute applied | The Monuments list is the default for all 512 (it holds all of them and is the list the site's Monuments section shows); the second list's position for the nine is selectable in the panel (DD-WORLD-01). A mark says where the list puts the memorial today and nothing about July 1863. Each card says where its position comes from, and says when the list states no source. |
| Strongest evidence-based reconstruction | Position from the NPS monument inventory with its stated accuracy, where one is obtained in a form the project may use. |
| Confidence tier | inferred (position, the site's list, source stated for 9 of 512) |
| Competing values | Monuments list versus second list for nine state memorials (apart 1 to 24 m): both selectable. |
| Product marking | Inferred pill on every mark and card; "the list states no source for it" on a card; the position choice in the panel. |

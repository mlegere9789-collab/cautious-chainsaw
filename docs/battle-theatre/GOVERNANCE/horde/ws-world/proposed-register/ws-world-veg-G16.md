| Field | Content |
|---|---|

| Category | evidence missing |
| Description | American chestnut is drawn as a living tree of the woodlots in ranged shares (a fifth or less of the trees: the NPS says the fences of the period were mostly chestnut and black locust, and that the hickory, chestnut and oak stands of Little Round Top had been harvested several times); the blight that later removed it, which the plan dates to about 1904, is not in any text read, so no blight, no dead chestnut and no modern species list is used, and nothing is drawn of the changes after 1863 that the park reports (replanting, regrowth, clearing: ground claims). |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | The date of the blight is general knowledge, not a retrieved non-lead source (PDF B.10.3 says "verify"); the modern lists the park publishes are of the woods as they are now and are not read into 1863. |
| Evidence searched / action | Official Records vol. 27 (tools/bt/or27_find.py), the National Park Service landscape reports saved by the web task (73 texts), the public-domain books and 1863 newspapers of the library; every quote of the claims is looked up again in the saved text (tools/bt/vegetation/verify_claims.py). The claims are in app/data/vegetation.json. |

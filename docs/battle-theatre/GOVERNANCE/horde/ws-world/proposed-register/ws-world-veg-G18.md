| Description | The report on the national cemetery documents the site of 22 acres in July 1863 as four fenced fields and part of an orchard: the Menchy field (grass or grain), the Pierce field (grass or grain), the Benner field (wheat and corn), the Weirick field (corn), and the Meyers apple orchard north of the lines of the 75th Pennsylvania and 82nd Ohio (placed on the Warren orchard nearest that point; trees about 30 feet apart by the report's hedge). It also says there were no trees in the fields. The four fields are documented and not placed: their outlines are in the report's Drawing 1.1, which the repository does not hold, so the field cells there take their crop from the district mix, and the Warren sheet's tree-symbol clusters near the site (which may be the trees of Evergreen Cemetery) are drawn as the sheet shows them. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | The drawing is an image in a report; no outline of the four fields exists in the repository, and the overlap of the Warren clusters with the site is not resolved. |

The claims are in app/data/vegetation.json. |

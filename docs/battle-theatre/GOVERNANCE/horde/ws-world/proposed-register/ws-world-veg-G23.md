# ws-world-veg-G23: Fruit gardens and small orchards the Warren sheet does not map

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | ws-world-veg-G23 |
| Requirement IDs | R20, Q21 |
| Category | evidence missing |
| Description | The Warren sheet maps no region of trees under about 0.6 ha. Documented orchards below that size are therefore not in the layer: the McPherson fruit garden (about 9 trees, south of the barn and west of the house; the young orchard of the farm), the fruit trees of the Dustman farm and the orchards of the farms that have no farmstead outline here (Harman, Forney, Wills, Herbst, Gilbert). Their claims are listed as not placed. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | No outline or tree count of 1863 exists for them; a small orchard would have to be a seeded reconstruction round a farmstead (plausible-procedural) and is not made here. |
| Evidence searched / action | Official Records vol. 27 (tools/bt/or27_find.py), the National Park Service landscape reports saved by the web task (73 texts), the public-domain books and 1863 newspapers of the library; every quote of the claims is looked up again in the saved text (tools/bt/vegetation/verify_claims.py). The claims are in app/data/vegetation.json. |
| Autonomous substitute applied | A calendar of ranges and seeded draws, drawn in the theatre and marked with the tier in the Vegetation panel (More). |
| Confidence tier | open gap |
| Product marking | Tier pill on the unit card and on the legend; the ground overlay hatches every unit that is not documented; the gap is listed in the Vegetation panel. |

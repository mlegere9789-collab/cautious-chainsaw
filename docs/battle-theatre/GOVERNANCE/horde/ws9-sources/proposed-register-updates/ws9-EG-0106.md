Source texts are archive.org OCR (raw djvu text, unverified against page images); each quotation below was re-matched in the local file (whitespace and line-break hyphenation normalised, OCR errors left as printed). ## Passages
Fremantle (British observer with Longstreet; 2 July, "Thursday"):
- "We all got up at 3.30 A. m." (Fremantle, p.256)
- "arrived at 5 A. M. at the same commanding position" (Fremantle, p.257)
- "Generals Lee, Hill, Longstreet, and Hood, in consultation" (Fremantle, p.257)
- "saw him disposing of M'Laws's division" (Fremantle, p.257 (OCR reads the hour as "Y A. M." = 7 A. M.))
- "A dead silence reigned till 4.45 p. m." (Fremantle, p.258)
- "Pickett being still in the rear" (Fremantle, p.258)
- "Longstreet suddenly commenced a heavy cannonade on the right" (Fremantle, p.259)

Alexander, Military Memoirs of a Confederate (1907; archive id militarymemoirso00alex), participant, artillery of Longstreet's corps; retrospective:
- "About 11 A.M., his orders were issued." (Alexander 1907, p.391)
- "It was about 3 P.M. when Hood's division, in the advance, crossed the Emmitsburg road" (Alexander 1907, p.394)
- "Thus, about 3.45 P.M., 36 guns were in action" (Alexander 1907, p.395)
- "After this cannonade had continued for perhaps 30 minutes, Hood received the order to advance." (Alexander 1907, p.395)

Law, "The Struggle for 'Round Top'", Battles and Leaders vol. 3 (1888; archive id battlesleadersof03cent), Hood's senior brigadier; retrospective:
- "At 3 o'clock on the morning of the 2d" (Law in Battles and Leaders vol. 3, p.319)
- "arrived there shortly before noon" (Law, B&L 3, p.319)
- "It was now past 4 o'clock in the afternoon" (Law, B&L 3, p.320)
- "It was near 5 o'clock p. M. when we advanced to the attack." (Law, B&L 3, p.323)
- "had been warmly engaged for about fifteen minutes" (Law, B&L 3, p.323)

## What each supports
- C-005 Hood at the ridge / C-006 Longstreet with Lee: Fremantle p.257 has Lee, Hill, Longstreet and Hood in consultation at about 05:00 on 2 July at the position Fremantle used the day before. This is a witness clock and a new scenario value (05:00), earlier than the PDF default 06:30 for Hood and consistent with the memoir-based "pre-dawn" scenario for Longstreet only loosely (sunrise is about 04:35 in the project's computation, so 05:00 is after sunrise, not "stars bright"). Do not merge; carry as an additional scenario for both rows.
- C-007 McLaws: Fremantle p.257 has Longstreet disposing McLaws's division at about 07:00 (OCR prints "Y A. M."; the digit is read as 7 from the sequence 3.30, 5, 7, 1 p.m.). New scenario value (07:00) earlier than the PDF 08:00/08:30; the OCR digit should be checked on the page image before it is used.
- C-008 Lee's order: Alexander p.391 "About 11 A.M." is a second retrospective witness for 11:00 (agrees with Longstreet 1896 already in the register). Alexander may rely on Longstreet and the OR, so independence is not shown.
- C-011 Hood's attack: three further witness clocks: Alexander (36 guns in action about 15:45, Hood ordered forward after perhaps 30 minutes of cannonade: about 16:15), Fremantle (cannonade opens 16:45), Law (artillery engaged about fifteen minutes, infantry advance "near 5 o'clock"). Together with the OR "about 16:00 guns" already carried this supports a window 16:00-17:00 rather than a point; Hood's infantry start is not stated in a single report. Law's "arrived shortly before noon" (3 a.m. march) fixes Law's brigade, not Hood himself.
- C-021 Pickett: Fremantle p.258 "Pickett being still in the rear" on 2 July supports "not on the field on 2 July" (gate Q04 not moved).
- Not found: Hood or McLaws own postwar essays (Hood died 1879; McLaws's SHSP essay is in a volume not acquired in this pass); EG-0106 stays open for them.

## Suggested tier change
No promotion of the existing tiers. The above values are added as scenarios with tier "inferred" (single retrospective or diary witness each, clocks mutually inconsistent within an hour). C-005/C-006/C-007 stay plausible-procedural/inferred as in conflict_defaults.json. The register wording "No OR report gives an hour ..." remains true; add "non-OR witnesses give: 05:00 (Fremantle, Hood and Longstreet with Lee), 07:00 (Fremantle, McLaws), 11:00 (Alexander), 16:15 / 16:45 / near 17:00 (Alexander / Fremantle / Law)".

# Proposed register update EG-0124: Hospital flow 4-6 July and wounded counts: Letterman 1866 (also bears on C-046, C-047; EG-0118, EG-0119)

PROPOSAL ONLY. The register entry EG-0124 has not been edited. Source texts are archive.org OCR (raw djvu text, unverified against page images); each quotation below was re-matched in the local file (whitespace and line-break hyphenation normalised, OCR errors left as printed). Pointer records: `library/acquired_2.json`.

## Status: NARROWED (aggregate flow and counts documented; burial returns and carry paths still not located)

## Passages
Letterman, Medical Recollections of the Army of the Potomac (1866; archive id medicalrecollect00lettuoft), chapter on Gettysburg:
- "Before daylight on the morning of the 4th all the wounded within our lines" (Letterman 1866, p.158)
- "were removed to the hospitals" (Letterman 1866, p.158)
- "fourteen thousand one hundred and ninety-three" (Letterman 1866, p.159)
- "six thousand eight hundred and two" (Letterman 1866, p.160)
- "twenty thousand nine hundred and ninety-five" (Letterman 1866, p.160)
- "drenching rain which fell after the battle" (Letterman 1866, p.156)

Also p.158-159 (OCR text, summarised not quoted): after the army left, the wounded were placed under Surgeon Henry Janes; Assistant-Surgeon Brinton acted as Medical Purveyor; four wagons and six ambulances from each corps were retained to carry supplies to the hospitals and wounded "from the hospitals to the railroad depot".

## What it supports
- Aggregate hospital flow: by before daylight on 4 July all the wounded within the Union lines (about twelve thousand on 2-3 July) had been taken to the hospitals (p.158); wounded of the whole battle 14,193 Union and 6,802 Confederate in Union hands, 20,995 together (pp.159-160). These are the Medical Director's own figures: they feed EG-0119 and the "wounded" side of C-046/C-047 as a documented range member, not a headline number.
- Rain after the battle (p.156) is relevant to EG-0026 (no clock).
- Not found in Letterman 1866: Camp Letterman opening date (C-044) (searched "Camp Letterman", "general hospital": no hit); burial returns; carry paths.

## Suggested tier change
Sub-claim "all wounded within the Union lines moved to hospitals before daylight 4 Jul (about 12,000)": plausible-procedural to documented (Letterman, single authoritative witness, retrospective 1866). Burial returns, carry paths, 4-6 Jul per-corps counts: unchanged (open).

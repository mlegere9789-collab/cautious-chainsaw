| Description | Strength (word: strength) of two regiments of Baxter's Second Brigade, Robinson's Second Division, First Corps: `us-1c-2d-baxter-97ny` (97th New York) and `us-1c-2d-baxter-11pa` (11th Pennsylvania, transferred to the First Brigade in the afternoon of 1 July). The gap record covers these two units only. The four other regiments of the brigade have printed figures: 12th Massachusetts about 200 (Cook), 83rd New York 148 (the New York volume), 88th Pennsylvania 296 (Wagner), and, found by the second reading of this file, the 90th Pennsylvania 208 (Sellers, printed in words: record U-us-1c-2d-3d-0366); the 104th New York of the First Brigade, once a gap of this file, now has about 330 (record U-us-1c-2d-3d-0367). |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | The Official Records print no strength for these two regiments (Wheelock of the 97th New York prints prisoners and no figure of his own strength; Coulter and Bierer of the 11th Pennsylvania print losses only); the state volumes and the regimental histories acquired for the group print none, in digits or in words (a first reading of this file had searched digits only and so passed over the 90th Pennsylvania and 104th New York figures, which the second reading found in words and in an oration); the Fox page of the 97th New York gives its loss and no strength; the one standard compilation of regimental strengths (Busey and Martin) is not on this machine and is not public domain; the archive.org title and full-text searches found no regimental history of the 97th New York or of the 11th Pennsylvania. |

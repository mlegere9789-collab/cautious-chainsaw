## Evidence searched (copy of the gap record)

- OR27-1: part 1, R088 (Fraser, the brigade and the 140th Pennsylvania), R089 (52d New York), R090 (57th New York), R091 (66th New York), read in full; R077 (Caldwell), R078 (McKeen), R082 (Kelly) and R092 (Brooke), read for the days after 3 July. Terms: the 4th and 5th of July; Two Taverns; marched; left the field; Frederick. Result: the four reports of the Third Brigade end with the afternoon of 3 July and print no later movement; McKeen (4 p. m. on 5 July, the First Brigade), Kelly (about 4.30 p. m. on 5 July, Two Taverns) and Brooke (4 p. m. on 5 July, the Fourth Brigade, which "followed the Third Brigade") print theirs.

- NY2: NY2-S006 (66th New York). Terms: July 5th; Two Taverns; marched; left the field. Result: no hit.

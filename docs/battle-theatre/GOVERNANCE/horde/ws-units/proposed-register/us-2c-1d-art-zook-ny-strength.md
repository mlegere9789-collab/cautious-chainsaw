Result: no hit of the regex for any of the three regiments; Scherrer (R089) prints the 52d's losses (1 officer and 1 man killed, 3 officers and 21 men wounded, 8 men missing) and no strength; Chapman (R090) and Nelson (R091) print no strength and no loss figure (the lists went in separately); Fraser (R088) prints no figure for the brigade.
- NY1: NY1-S081 (52d New York: the monument inscription and the historical sketch by Surgeon Charles H. Althans). Terms: numbered; went into the fight; men; engaged. Result: the sketch prints that the regiment "went into the fight with less than 100 men" (an upper bound; the contract holds a printed number or a printed range, not a bound), a loss of about 30 enlisted men besides officers, and the inscription prints the loss as 38 (killed 2, wounded 26, missing 10); no figure of its own.
- NY1: NY1-S083 (57th New York: history and dedication). Terms: numbered; strength; went into action; men engaged. Result: prints the loss at Gettysburg as 101 (3 officers and 16 men killed, 9 men died of wounds, 6 officers and 64 men wounded, 3 missing), "nearly one-third" and the largest of the regiment's war; no strength is printed and none can be computed from "nearly one-third".
- NY2: NY2-S006 (66th New York: the addresses of Samuel G. Adams and Capt. Thomas McKenna Mills). Terms: numbered; strength; went into action; engaged. Result: prints the loss at Gettysburg (5 killed, 29 wounded, 10 missing, 44) and later strengths of 1864; no strength for 2 July 1863.
- FOX: FOX-S061 (the entry of the 52d New York) and a search of the whole text for the 57th and 66th New York in both name forms. Terms: Fifty-second, Fifty-seventh, Sixty-sixth New York; engaged. Result: the 52d has an entry that prints its Gettysburg loss (38: 2 killed, 26 wounded, 10 missing) and no figure engaged; the 57th and 66th New York have no entry in the volume.

## Evidence searched (copy of the gap record)

- OR27-1: part 1, R009 (the table), R013 (the casualty return), R076 (Hancock) and R077 (Caldwell), read in the passages that name the Sixth New York Cavalry or Company D; a regex over parts 1, 2 and 3 for "Riley Johnson" and "Companies D and K". Terms: Riley Johnson; Companies D and K; Sixth New York Cavalry; Company D. Result: the table prints the two companies under the general headquarters of the corps with Capt. Riley Johnson; the casualty return prints the row "Companies D and K 1 3 4" (the column headings are not kept by the OCR); Hancock names two privates of Company D and the color-bearer among his personal orderlies and Caldwell names his orderly, a corporal of Company D; no hour, place, road or strength of the companies.
- NY3: NY3-S017 (the monument inscription), NY3-S019 (the sketch by Surgeon Augustus P. Clarke and the oration of Col. W. L. Heermance) and NY3-S086 (the monument's place and dimensions): the 6th New York Cavalry. Terms: Companies D and K; Hancock; Second Corps; headquarters. Result: the sketch names Companies F and H on 2 and 3 July and the regiment with Devin's brigade at Gettysburg; it says nothing of Companies D and K at the Second Corps headquarters.
- PA2: PA2-S004 (Mulholland's address, 116th Pennsylvania). Terms: Sixth New York Cavalry; Wells; corps flag. Result: prints that on 3 July Hancock rode along the line with the corps flag carried by Private James Wells of the Sixth New York Cavalry, starting at the right of his line where it joins the Taneytown road: one man of the companies, no strength, no place for the companies themselves.

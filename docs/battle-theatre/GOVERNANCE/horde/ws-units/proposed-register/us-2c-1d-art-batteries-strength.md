## Evidence searched (copy of the gap record)

- OR27-1: part 1, R132 (Hazard, the brigade report), R076 (Hancock), R098 (Gibbon), R104 (Webb), R028 (Hunt, the passages that name the Second Corps batteries) and R008 (the returns of 30 June, 4 and 10 July), read in the passages that name the batteries; a regex over parts 1, 2 and 3 for the battery names followed within 300 characters by strength, numbered, men, present for duty or effective. Terms: Woodruff; Arnold; Cushing; Brown; Rorty; Light Company I; strength; numbered; men present. Result: Hazard prints the guns of each battery and the losses and says one-third of the officers and men were killed or wounded, and no figure of men; the return of 4 July prints 475 for the brigade as a whole; the returns of 30 June and 10 July print the corps rows with the artillery columns in a run of figures that the OCR does not separate by brigade; no figure of any single battery.
- NY3: NY3-S022 (the dedication address of Battery B, 1st New York Light Artillery) and NY1-S001 (the commission's narrative of the Second Corps batteries). Terms: men; guns; strength; numbered. Result: the address prints the battery's loss at Gettysburg (27 men killed or wounded, 11 of them killed) and, in the narrative, the guns of Battery B (four rifles); no strength of the battery on 2 or 3 July.
- FOX: FOX-S230 (the table of the maximum losses of light artillery in any one engagement) and the entries named in it. Terms: Rorty; Woodruff; Cushing; Arnold; Brown; Hazard. Result: the tables print the losses of the batteries and the names of the commanders; no strength.
- NOT-ACQUIRED:Busey and Martin, Regimental Strengths and Losses at Gettysburg: none. Terms: none. Result: not searched: the work is not on this machine and is not public domain (reading it is an owner-only act: purchase or licence, PLAN section 8); no volume of Rhode Island or of the regular artillery is among the texts on this machine.

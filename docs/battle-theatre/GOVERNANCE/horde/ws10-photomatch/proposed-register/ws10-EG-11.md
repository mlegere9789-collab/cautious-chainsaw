| Description | NPS Common Pride vol. 3 (printed p. 453, pdf page 30) prints an annotated enlargement of the Brady/LOC view north from the crest of Little Round Top with six labels: Codori Grove, Copse of Trees, Tree at Head of Brien Lane, Ziegler's Grove, Cemetery Hill, Patterson. The page is a photocopy; feature matching of it against record 2018666303 failed (8 inliers of 70), so the label positions cannot be transferred to the photograph's pixels, and the crop of the enlargement is unknown.

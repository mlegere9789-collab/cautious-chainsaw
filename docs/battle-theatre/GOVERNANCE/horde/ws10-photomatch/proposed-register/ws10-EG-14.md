# ws10-EG-14: Q16 two National Park Service words for the direction of the lodge-roof view: north and northwest (source conflict carried as poses P27 and P28)

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | ws10-EG-14 |
| Requirement IDs | R12, Q16 |
| Category | evidence conflict |
| Description | The Cultural Landscape Report for Gettysburg National Cemetery (Fig. 1.26) says the exposure LC-B815-273 looks north from the roof of the Evergreen Cemetery lodge; the Record of Treatment (volumes I and II, Figs. 75 and 131) calls the same picture, as ppmsca 35051, a view northwest from Cemetery Hill. The two words differ by 45 degrees. Both are carried as selectable scenarios (P27 north, 0 degrees; P28 northwest, 315 degrees). Read from the photograph (exploratory, not a test) the courthouse clock tower implies a heading of 337 to 343 degrees for a field of 50 to 30 degrees: at the boundary of the two words. |

| Update 2026-11-16 | Gardner's own letterpress for plate 35 of the Sketch Book (the same view; the Library's album page 77, read by machine from the master scan and by eye; statement PS-GARDNER-SB1-P77) adds a third reading: 'This view is taken from Cemetery Ridge, where our artillery was massed' and Seminary Ridge 'can be seen' 'on the left of the picture'. It is a second source for the place and a qualitative cue for the direction: at the north word the Seminary and the ridge (302 to 314 degrees from the gatehouse) lie outside a 40 degree frame, at the northwest word the ridge stands at 0.18 and the Seminary at 0.47 of the frame width, but that heading fails the pike test and puts the court house outside the frame; the tower-implied heading (337 to 343 degrees) lies between. No single heading meets the two NPS words and Gardner's phrase; all three are carried, none is chosen. The cue was found after the renders were looked at and is not a criterion (decision DD-PM-15). |

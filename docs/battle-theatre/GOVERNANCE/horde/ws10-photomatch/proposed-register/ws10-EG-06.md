# ws10-EG-06: Q16 titles/dates in LOC records partly rest on Frassanito

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | ws10-EG-06 |
| Requirement IDs | R19, Q16 |
| Category | evidence conflict |
| Description | LOC records such as 2018666310 state 'Title and exact date from Frassanito (1975)'. The catalogue records only LOC's own statement of date and title and tags it as LOC-stated; Frassanito's text and images are not used. Under the Law (no lead-only support) a date taken from that note stays 'catalogued', not 'documented'. |
| Evidence searched / action | Find a non-lead source for exact dates (diaries, newspapers, Gardner's captions). |
| Confidence tier | inferred |
| Visual and audio impact | Dates in the catalogue are LOC catalogue values. |
| Product marking | Photo-match report lists the assumption beside every pose |
| Review status | internal review only (DEC-006: no public review label) |
| Owner decision requested | See Evidence searched / action |
| Update 2026-11-13 | Statements that the LOC record takes from Frassanito (the summary of 2012647712; the title and date of 2018670852; the date of 2012647823) are registered as tier lead, never documented. The NPS Then-And-Now pages cite Frassanito in their related text for some comparisons; only the NPS sentences that stand on their own (direction, reference object) are used. |
| Update 2026-11-16 | The Library essays 'The Case of the Moved Body' and 'The Case of Confused Identity' say that their analysis rests on Frassanito's work: their statements of place or relation (plates 36 and 37 in open fields near the Rose farm and close to the Emmitsburg Road, two views of one group of dead from opposite sides; four of six sharpshooter photographs on the southern slope of Devil's Den) are registered as tier lead. The Library's own caption of the negative of plate 37 says near the McPherson woods: the conflict of place is carried as it stands (PS-LOCESSAY-CONFUSED-PLACE). |

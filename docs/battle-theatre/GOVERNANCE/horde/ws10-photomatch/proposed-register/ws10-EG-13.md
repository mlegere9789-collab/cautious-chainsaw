# ws10-EG-13: Q16 the camera height, the spot and the 1863 form of the Evergreen Cemetery gatehouse (poses P27, P28, P29)

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | ws10-EG-13 |
| Requirement IDs | R12, Q16 |
| Category | evidence missing |
| Description | The National Park Service names the roof of the Evergreen Cemetery lodge as the place of the camera of the view north (Cultural Landscape Report for Gettysburg National Cemetery, Fig. 1.26) and the gatehouse as the place of the view east (Record of Treatment vol. I, Fig. 213). No source gives the height of the camera, the spot on or in the building, or the 1863 form of the building: the model holds the 2023 outline (which includes the right-hand addition) and the poses assume 8 m above the ground, 5 m and 11 m are the sensitivity of the skyline test. The lens and plate of the exposures are unknown (40 degrees assumed; 30 and 50 rendered). |
| Evidence searched / action | Saved NPS texts, the registry of structures (STR-TOWN-GATEHOUSE: err 4 m, status survives_altered). Needs a drawing, a photograph or a description of the lodge of 1855 (the Evergreen Cemetery Association; the park archive) and the park's catalogue entry of print OS2B-2037. |
| Confidence tier | inferred (position), plausible-procedural (height and lens) |
| Visual and audio impact | The camera of the Gettysburg views from the cemetery would stand at the wrong height in a photo-match test; the pose test cannot be tightened until the height is known. |
| Product marking | Poses at the gatehouse carry the tier inferred and the flag place_documented; the sheets print the assumed height |
| Review status | internal review only (DEC-006: no public review label) |
| Owner decision requested | Owner step: ask the Evergreen Cemetery Association and the park museum (third parties) for the 1863 form of the lodge and for the station data of the GETT 41135 prints |
| Update 2026-11-16 | The NPS Cultural Landscape Report for Gettysburg National Cemetery (read again for the form of the lodge; statements PS-NPS-CLRCEM-LODGE-FORM and PS-NPS-CLRCEM-LODGE-ADDITION) says that the Evergreen gate lodge, begun on 1 September 1855, is an Italianate two-story archway with two floors of lodge quarters in the flanking piers, and that after the battle the Evergreen Cemetery Association repaired the gatehouse, including a two-story frame addition on the rear. The model draws the 2023 outline at a default one storey; the addition is later than July 1863. No height in metres and no statement of which part of the outline is the addition is given: the camera height stays an assumption (8 m, consistent with two storeys and a roof). |

| Description | S-065, S-066, S-071, S-072, S-073 (GOVERNANCE/evidence_corrections/fence-3.jsonl). S-065: the Granite Schoolhouse as a Second Corps, First Division hospital evacuated toward McAllister's Mill after the overshots of 3 Jul (HN-Spangler): no local text names this schoolhouse as a hospital; the only local statement is that it no longer stands (NPS General Management Plan 1999); "built 1862" and "razed 1921" have no local source. S-071: the Hummelbaugh farm as a Second Corps hospital where Barksdale died and as Pleasonton's headquarters on 4-6 Jul (SS-Hummelbaugh): the house stood in 1863 (NPS 1999); the rest has no local source; OR27 has no Hummelbaugh text and Pleasonton's report (OR27-1-R336) names no headquarters site. S-066: Armistead was brought to the Eleventh Corps (2nd Division) hospital at the George Spangler farm on the afternoon of 3 Jul, "about 4 o'clock" by the attending surgeon Brinton (letter of 1869, via NPS Common Pride and Fame part II), not about 20:00 as printed; Holland (Confederate Veteran 1921, via NPS) has him moved "during that afternoon" to "a temporary hospital beneath the shade of some trees"; no local text names a summer kitchen or any building. S-072: he died on 5 Jul at about 9 a. m. (Holland via NPS), Brinton writing that the death "resulted [not] from his wounds directly, but from secondary fever and prostration". S-073: the Trostle farmhouse was used briefly as a hospital and the barn area as Sickles's headquarters (NPS CLI 2024 citing CLR 2002); the barn has a cannonball hole in the brick gable and dead horses of Bigelow's battery lay on the property (NPS Then and Now); no local text dates the end of the hospital use or says "abandoned by Jul 6". |

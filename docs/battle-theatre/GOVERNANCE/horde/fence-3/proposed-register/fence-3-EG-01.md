| Description | S-053 (evidence row R14.S-053 in GOVERNANCE/evidence_corrections/fence-3.jsonl). The PDF prints the Taney house as Occupied by Confederate sharpshooters, then Shelled; ruins (3 Jul, trigger 1st NY Battery M, source SS-Taney only). Re-matched 2026-11-13: two New York volumes document Confederate sharpshooters in the stone house across Rock Creek on the forenoon of 3 Jul, doing great damage to Colgrove's infantry (NY at Gettysburg vol.3 p.1265, vol.1 p.70), and the shelling of the house by Battery M, 1st New York Light Artillery, with percussion shells that "knocked the front out of the house", killed or wounded many of the sharpshooters and made it untenable (vol.3 p.1266, vol.1 p.70); Muhlenberg (OR27-1-R314 p.870) puts Winegar's Battery M on a second elevation east of Slocum's Hill. No local text calls the house a ruin, dates a later state or says anything about Taney fences or any other farm detail; the OR names no Taney house. |

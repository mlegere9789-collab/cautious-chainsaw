| Description | S-080, S-081 (GOVERNANCE/evidence_corrections/fence-3.jsonl). The PDF prints 17 acres bought by Wills for about $2,475.87 in late July and August, and the first of 3,512 Union bodies moved on 27 Oct, 1,258 reburied by 19 Nov, work due to finish March 1864 (Oct 17 / Oct 27 start conflict; W-CONS, GC-COMP). Re-matched 2026-11-13: about seventeen acres were bought through David Wills, the Governor's agent, in five lots at $135 to $225 an acre (Wills, report of 21 Mar 1864); NPS (Adams County Historic Resource Study) dates the first twelve acres to 14 Aug 1863 (sold by the Evergreen Cemetery Association) and five more acres to a day or two later; $2,475.87 is the cost of "a site" in the Governor's statement and, in NPS's account, the price of the twelve acres; the total price of the seventeen acres is not stated. Reburial: the contractor began exhuming on Tuesday 27 Oct 1863 (Weaver, Cemetery Report 1867; NPS CLR), the first reinterment in the national cemetery (from Gettysburg's Presbyterian graveyard) was about 17 Oct, 3,512 bodies in all, completed on 18 Mar 1864 after a winter stop; about 1,200 graves (CLR) or 1,188 (an OCR-garbled NPS typescript) stood in the plots on 19 Nov; 1,258 is not found. |

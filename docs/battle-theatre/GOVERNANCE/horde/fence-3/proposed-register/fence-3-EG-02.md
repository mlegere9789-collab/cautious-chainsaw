| Description | S-054, S-055 (GOVERNANCE/evidence_corrections/fence-3.jsonl). The PDF prints the Rummel barn as Plank walls -> Loopholes cut (Stuart's men) and the Rummel fences as Standing -> Destroyed; 30+ dead horses (3 Jul, SS-Rummel only). Re-matched 2026-11-13: a large barn stood in front of the 2nd Pennsylvania Cavalry's skirmish line, the enemy "had concentrated a large force" in and around it, and Pennington's and Randol's batteries put "shell after shell into the barn, making it untenable", the barn showing "even yet the effects of the fire of our batteries" (PA at Gettysburg vol.2 p.819); the NPS page for the 1st New Jersey Cavalry monument has the regiment moving forward around 2 pm and, "within moments", dismounted Confederates of Jenkins' Virginia brigade appearing "from behind the Rummel house and barn"; the New Jersey final report has the Union line advanced to and holding the Rummel buildings "about sundown". Fences: Kidd (Michigan at Gettysburg pp.149-153) has the 7th Michigan thrown against "a post-and-rail fence that ran obliquely in front of the Rummel barn", the posts "deeply set" and several lengths lifted at once to pass; PA vol.2 p.819 and the New Jersey report put fences along the lane and a worm fence at the 1st NJ position; Gregg (OR27-1-R344 p.959, the farm not named) has the enemy advance guard dismount "to remove the fences". The NPS page says John Rummel dragged 30 dead horses from the lane. Nothing local supports plank walls, loopholes, destruction of the fences as a whole, "30+", the $219.95 claim, 168 acres, the log house gone, or Fitz Lee's 1st Virginia through the farm. The word Rummel does not occur in OR27. |

| Category | source conflict / evidence missing |
| Description | S-077, S-079 (GOVERNANCE/evidence_corrections/fence-3.jsonl). S-077: the PDF prints Camp Letterman as Farm -> Construction July 10; opens July 20 or 22 (flag); closes Nov 20 (CWMed-Letterman, ABT-Letterman, CWN-Letterman; conflict C-044). Re-matched 2026-11-13: Camp Letterman General Hospital was established east of Gettysburg and over 20,000 wounded of both armies passed through its tented wards (NPS Gettysburg Overview); Letterman (OR27-1-R016 p.197) left Dr. Henry Janes "in charge of the hospitals at Gettysburg"; by January 1864 the last patients, surgeons, guards, nurses and tents were gone (NPS Overview). No local text gives 10 Jul, 20 Jul, 22 Jul or 20 Nov; a Library of Congress search of Chronicling America for "Camp Letterman" (10 Jul to 31 Dec 1863) returned no result; NPS event pages that a search summary quotes (20 Jul to 19 Nov 1863) were not located and are not cited. S-079: the Codori barn that stands now is a bank barn of 1884 (NPS Cultural Landscapes Inventory, Pickett's Charge Battlefield, 2024: "1884 Built Nicholas Codori barn is built"), so the barn of 1863 is gone; "torn down 1882" and the Rose barn fire of 1910 have no local source (searched in the NPS inventories and records of treatment). |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | The dates rest on hosts that are not reachable or not admissible (American Battlefield Trust refused the connection on 2026-11-13; Civil War News and Civil War Medicine are not on the reachable list; Stone Sentinels refused) and on the NPS event pages not located. |

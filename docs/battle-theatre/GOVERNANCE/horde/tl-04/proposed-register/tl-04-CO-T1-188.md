Corrected claim: The National Park Service writes that the Mary Thompson House, built in the 1840s, served as Lee's headquarters and that on the morning of July 1, 1863 Lee rode just north of Gettysburg and established his headquarters in the farmstead residence of Mary Thompson. That differs from the seed's 'evening'; no Official Records passage names the Thompson house or gives Lee's place on the evening of Jul 1. A 1866 illustration of the house is named 'Confederate Head-Quarters' (Lossing, as the NPS landscape report says).

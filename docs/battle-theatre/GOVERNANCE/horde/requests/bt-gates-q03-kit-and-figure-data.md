# Request to the kit, figure and register owners: what gate Q03 reads and does not find

Workstream bt-gates, 2026-11-16. Written from the first runs of `tools/bt/gates/q03_anachronism.py` on the real data (the kit side: 322 units, 23,112 population manifests and 15,400 spec entries per day, all run; the app side through `q03_scene.mjs`). No product file was changed. Each point names the item the runner reports, what the data lacks, and the exact change that lets the item pass. Nothing here lowers a check.

## 1. `Q03/Q03.2/validity-census`: every kit item has `valid_from` and `valid_to` (K.9, p. 135; "the build rejects items outside July 1863 for the unit")
The kit holds 191 catalogue items (`kit_specs.json items`), 45 weapons (`weapons.json weapons`) and 13 artillery pieces (`weapons.json artillery.pieces`). 248 of those 249 carry no `valid_from` and `valid_to` (the one that does is the Spencer, `valid_from` 1863-07-03; `pack_used_as_cover` carries `valid_on` 1863-07-03, which the census accepts for both). Only 5 of the 507 spec entries carry a `valid_from` and none a `valid_to`.
Change wanted, in `kit/kit_specs.json` and `kit/weapons.json` (through the builder, `tools/bt/build_kit_data.py`): every item and piece gets `valid_from` and `valid_to` (an ISO date or a year string) with `validity_source_keys` and a tier. A value is written only where a non-lead source supports it (Law cl. 4): an item whose validity no source gives stays without one, keeps the item FAIL, and gets a register entry (never an invented date). The validator already reads `valid_from`, `valid_to` and `only_on` of spec entries (`check_kit.valid_entry`); the item-level fields are what the plan asks for and the census checks.

## 2. `Q03/Q03.2/stars/<day>`: the national colour's star count against the statute
`app/src/figures3d/flags.mjs` draws `US_STARS = 34` on every day; `flagPattern(kind, state, W, H, opts)` takes no date. The app's own evidence module (`unionStarsOn`, `src/figures3d/evidence.mjs`) gives 34 through 3 July 1863 and 35 from 4 July (West Virginia, admitted 20 June 1863, star effective 4 July); the same 35 holds on 18 and 19 November. The runner reports PASS for 26 June to 3 July and FAIL for 4 to 14 July and both November days.
Change wanted: export a pure `starsOn(dateISO)` from `flags.mjs` that returns `unionStarsOn(dateISO)` (or a recorded reading that differs from it, with its source and an owner-selectable scenario), and let `flagPattern('us_national' | 'cav_guidon', ..., { date })` draw that many stars. `q03_scene.mjs` already calls `starsOn` when it exists and reports `flags.product_stars_by_date`; no gate change is needed. Add a row to `app/docs/GATE-HOOKS.md` (`starsOn(dateISO)` -> integer, Q03.2).

## 3. `Q03/Q03.7/<kind>/<army>`: the vehicles of the artillery train
`app/data/artillery-train.json vehicles` holds `limber`, `caisson`, `battery_wagon` and `forge` with a tier and a basis but no validity and no army. The scene draws all four for both armies.
Change wanted: each record gets `valid_from`, `valid_to` (covering 1863-06-26 to 1863-07-14) and `army` (`union`, `confederate` or `both`), each with its source key (the printed manuals FIELDART-1863 and ORDNANCE-1861 are non-lead sources dated 1863 and are the natural support for the limber and caisson; the battery wagon and forge rest today on a forum quotation, which keeps their tier inferred). Ambulances and supply wagons are not drawn, so the runner reports them N/A; when they are drawn they need the same record.

## 4. `Q03/Q03.1/flag/<class>` and the unit items of flagged units: the register entries of the validator's flags
A validator flag is acceptable only when every variant passes, the flag is marked, and it is registered (GATE-SPEC Q03.1). The runner reads `tools/bt/gates/q03_flags.json` (the entries it accepts for each flag class) and then asks of each entry: open, every field present and not a placeholder, an owner question.
* `DISPUTED_FLAGGED`, dispute `DISP-140NY-DRESS` (US.140NY, both variants): mapped to EG-0009, open but the record lacks `owner_q`.
* `DISPUTED_FLAGGED`, dispute `DISP-BERDAN-GREEN-BLUE` (US.1USS, US.2USS): no register entry names it (EG-0301 is about rounds fired).
* `FLAG_BADGE_UNRESOLVED` (20 kit units, 1,035 drawn figures): mapped to EG-0015, open but the record lacks `subst` and `owner_q`.
* `FLAG_KEPI_PRIVATE_PURCHASE` (31 Confederate cavalry units): no register entry names it (EG-0016 is the general entry for Confederate dress below brigade).
* `FLAG_WHITWORTH_EXCEPTION` (mapped to none) and `FLAG_SPENCER_DISPUTED_WINDOW` (mapped to EG-0268): not raised by any generated manifest today.
Change wanted from the register owner: complete EG-0009 and EG-0015 (all twelve fields of GATE-SPEC 1.4 L1, including `owner_q`), register the Berdan green or blue dispute and the Confederate cavalry kepi exception (open entries with the eleven fields and `owner_q`), then add the ids to `q03_flags.json` (the file is the gate's own; send the ids to bt-gates). The marker side needs nothing: every flagged manifest carries its marker (`disputed:`, `gap_placeholder:`, `private_purchase_exception`) and `app/src/ui/card-sections.mjs` shows the unit's flags.

## 5. Not owed by anyone yet
* Q03.1 class 7 (the seeded turntable sample audit against the unit's spec image references): no kit unit carries an image reference list, and the audit needs a browser and a person looking at the saved frames (U-10). The runner records NOT-RUN and lists it as owed.
* Q03.1 class 1, departure half: the B.6.12 presence test gates arrival only. The runner records it as unchecked and the verdict INCOMPLETE until a departure check exists; Q04.4 judges departures in the scene.
* The epilogue crowd and Lincoln (1863-11-18 and 19): the kit has no civilian dress class and no Lincoln record, so `Q03/Q03.2/date/1863-11-18` and `-19` are BLOCKED-INPUT. When such manifests exist (a kit unit whose `scope` is `epilogue` or whose id starts `EPI.`, `CIV.` or `LINCOLN`, or a drawn unit dated in November) the runner validates them on those days.

# bt-oob-kit cs-art-2c: roster notes
Names of the batteries by their captains, from the casualty table (OR27-2 pp. 341-343) and Dance's report: Hupp's battery is the Salem Artillery (Lieutenant C. B. Griffin commanding), Dance's is the Powhatan Artillery (Lieutenant J. M. Cunningham commanding), Watson's the 2d and Smith's the 3d Richmond Howitzers, Page's the Morris, Fry's the Orange, W. P. Carter's the King William, Reese's the Jeff. Davis, Garber's the Staunton, Green's the Louisiana Guard, Tanner's the Courtney, Carrington's the Charlottesville, Brown's the Chesapeake, Carpenter's the Alleghany, Dement's the 1st Maryland and Raine's the Lee. The kit holds one card for the 2d and 3d Richmond Howitzers (the lines carry both companies). The roster unit for Jones's battalion is the artillery of Early's division and Latimer, not Andrews, heads the table for Andrews' battalion (Lieutenant Colonel R. S. Andrews being absent, wounded at Winchester). Gregory's table of guns engaged has 17 batteries; the Charlottesville, Amherst and Fluvanna batteries have no row. The group's units carry no corps-level artillery unit; Brown signs as acting chief of artillery of the Second Corps (R468).

## How to validate a line
1. Open `citation.local_path` (relative to the repository root), find the marker `=== PAGE <pdf_page> ===`, normalise whitespace. `quote_mode` sentences or anchors: the quote is a verbatim substring of the named text variant (`text_variant`: reading-order, raw or layout). `quote_mode` cleaned: the letters and digits of the quote equal those of `source_text_excerpt` (spacing and punctuation ignored; for sources with a lost-glyph defect the groups ti, tt, tf, ft, tl, ff, or f followed by i, f, l, are ignored on both sides); `ocr_corrections` lists every (saved, fixed) pair of the extract record, which for the scanned typescripts were checked on the page image (`page_image_viewed` true). `context_quote` (when present) is a second verbatim passage of the same source that carries a name, date or number the claim needs. `scripts/verify_extracts.py` does all this: 1,052 extracts, 0 problems.
2. `source_class` is a National Park Service report or page, non-lead, secondary; sources prepared for the Service by outside authors carry a rights note in INDEX.md.

## Tier and what stays open
- `tier` is a proposal (DD-NPS-02): documented when the report states the fact (often from damage claims, the 1860 census, the Bachelder, Cope, Elliott and Warren maps or photographs, named in `basis_named_by_report`), inferred when the report hedges or reports conflicting evidence. Every line says what remains unsupported: one secondary source; the claims, census returns and maps were not read here (the McPherson claims are the exception: transcribed by the study and read there). The PDF ledger rule (two independent sources or one first-tier source) is yours to apply.

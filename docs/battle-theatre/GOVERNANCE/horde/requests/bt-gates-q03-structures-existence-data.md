# Request to the structures, town and basemap owners: what gate Q03.3 and Q03.4 read and do not find

Workstream bt-gates, 2026-11-16. Written from the run of `tools/bt/gates/q03_anachronism.py` and its node child `q03_scene.mjs` on the real data (the structures model built on the real terrain: 446 objects drawn by the scene besides the 17 works; 88 records of `bld-`, `RD-`, `FEN-` kind and hospital sites). No product file was changed. The gate text: "every 1863 structure exists on its date; no modern object (monuments, avenues, fences, signs, wires, markers) appears" and Q03.3 "every drawn building, fence, wall, work, road, bridge and hospital has an existence interval and a state schedule; none is present before it was built or after it was destroyed or removed".

## What an item needs to pass (`Q03/Q03.3/object/<id>`)
1. A registry row of `docs/basemap1863/features` with `present_1863: true` (all 446 have it).
2. An existence start for July 1863 that the data states: a `built` year of 1863 or earlier, or a link (`rec`) to a `structures.json` record whose `preexisting` is true, or a registry note that says it stood without a hedge. Words of doubt in the registry note ("probable", "not reviewed", "may be", "the 1868-69 survey", "cannot be separated", "modern OSM line", "machine detection") leave the existence unestablished. The drawn geometry must not be a later building (`represents` starting "later building", a `built` year after 1863, a `built` note that says the building was rebuilt).
3. An existence end outside the window, or a schedule row that dates it (`status: destroyed_1863` needs a row).
4. A state schedule: `state_id` or `rec` names a record that at least one row of `state_schedule.json` names (the engine answers "no scheduled state, initial state unspecified in B.8.7" otherwise).

## What fails today (35 of 446 objects pass), and the exact change that makes each pass
* **229 borough masses** (`STR-BORO-MASS-nnn`, class `masses`): the registry note says "Standing at the 1868-69 survey; most borough blocks were built up before 1863 but individual buildings are not dated", and the record notes say "Buildings erected between July 1863 and 1869 cannot be separated from those standing in July 1863 using this plate alone". No record, so no schedule. Change: either date each building of a mass from a source dated 1863 or earlier (the Hopkins map of 1858, the 1863 newspapers, the Warren/Bachelder notes) and link it to a record with a schedule, or take the mass out of the scene with an entry in `notDrawn` and a register entry; a mass that merges buildings of two dates cannot be drawn as an 1863 object. `STR-BORO-MASS-003` has `status: not_drawn_duplicate_of_penn_hall` yet is in the model: the scene draws what the data says it does not.
* **107 of the 117 markers** fail: 66 are hedged (48 are "standing when the Warren survey was made (1868-69); standing in July 1863 is probable" and 18 are "machine detection on the 1868-69 plate, not reviewed: it may be lettering, hatching or a tree"), 9 give no statement of existence, 10 have no record and so no schedule, 22 are linked to records that no schedule row names. Change: review each detection (an unreviewed detection that is lettering is not a structure: remove it), give the rest a dated source or take them out of the scene.
* **27 fence lines** of the 30 (`FN-nnnn`): "Geometry is the modern OSM line (NPS fence); the sheet confirms a drawn line there ... the fence type of 1863 is NOT read from the sheet" (the sheet is Warren's of 1868-69). Only the three attributed to `FEN-emmitsburg-road` pass. Change: attribute each line to a `FEN-` or `P-` record with a source for July 1863, or remove it.
* **29 roads and lanes** have no record and so no state schedule (14 of them also give no statement that they stood in 1863). Change: link each road to an `RD-` record with `exists_in_1863: true` and a schedule (an empty one only if the plan's own row says nothing changes; the engine reports "no scheduled state" and the runner counts that as no schedule: an owner reading may relax it, recorded in the decision log).
* **12 buildings** are linked to records that no schedule row names (see the records below), and 5 more have no record. Change: a schedule row for each (B.8.7 prints none for them: this is a research gap, a register entry, not an invented row).
* **`STR-SPANGLER_H-BARN1`** is the 2023 outline of the barn rebuilt in 1935 ("later building on the 1863 site/foundation (not the 1863 structure itself)"). It is a post-1863 building in the scene: Q03.3 and Q03.4 both fail it. Change: draw the 1863 barn as a marker with the stated uncertainty (the data says its dimensions are not documented) or leave it out; do not draw the 1935 outline.
* **`STR-TOWN-PRESBYTERIAN_CHURCH`**: "site since 1842; building rebuilt later"; the outline is the later building's. Same change.

## Records (`Q03/Q03.3/record/<id>`; 49 of 88 pass)
* No state schedule (no row of `state_schedule.json` names the record or its parts; the plan prints no scheduled state): 14 `bld-` records (`bld-biggs-house`, `bld-brian-farm`, `bld-burns-house`, `bld-courthouse`, `bld-daniel-lady-farm`, `bld-henry-culp-farm`, `bld-herbst`, `bld-mcmillan-farm`, `bld-poorhouse-christ-lutheran-wills`, `bld-rogers-house`, `bld-snyder-farm`, `bld-thompson-house`, `bld-warfield-house`, `bld-wentz-farm`), 6 `RD-` records (`RD-baltimore-pike`, `RD-chambersburg-pike`, `RD-other-roads`, `RD-taneytown-road`, `RD-turnpikes`, `RD-wheatfield-road`) and 4 `FEN-` records (`FEN-cemetery-ridge-walls`, `FEN-mccreary-house`, `FEN-thompson`, `FEN-warfield`).
* `RD-gettysburg-railroad`: the record says neither `preexisting` nor `exists_in_1863` (B.8.4 prints no tier column for roads).
* 14 hospital sites: 10 with no closing window (`H-02`, `H-04`, `H-05`, `H-12` to `H-16` ...) and 4 with no setup window; B.8.5.2 prints no time for them and the gap is carried, never filled. Change: the window from a source, or a register entry that keeps the item open.

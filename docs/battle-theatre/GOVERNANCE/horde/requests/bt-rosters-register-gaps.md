# To the register owner (the run of tools/bt/seed_register.py): proposed Evidence-Gap Register entries for the roster data set (OA03)

From: bt-rosters (planning run), 2026-11-13. Detail and numbers: `docs/battle-theatre/rosters/PRODUCTION-PLAN.md`, `work-items.json`, `data2/units.json`.

## What
Twelve open gaps that the roster production cannot close from the open sources, proposed as register entries in the form of `register/EG-*.md` (the register files are not in my ownership list, so they are written here; ids EG-ROS-001 to EG-ROS-012 are proposals). Requirement: OA03.0000 (complete roster section of both armies, source and rights per name). The roster page marks each gap on the affected unit: "n present, names not recovered" and the gap id; a concealed gap is a violation (Law 7).

## Why
Law 3 and 7: where the record is missing the gap is registered, the strongest evidence-based reconstruction is stated, and the product marks it. None of these gaps is closed by a guess.

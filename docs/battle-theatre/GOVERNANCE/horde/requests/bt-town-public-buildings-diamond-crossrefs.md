Where your sources say more (a photograph, an outline, a measurement), write your rows; the integration step merges rows of one building by the ids named in `see_also`.
2. The integration step (`evidence/SUMMARY.md`, `app/data/town/buildings.json`): merge the rows of one building across groups by these ids; carry every conflict as a selectable scenario; do not draw `TWN-PUB-0001` (demolished in 1859), `TWN-PUB-0003` (market house: existence not found), `TWN-PUB-0010` (post office: place not found) or `TWN-PUB-0013` (engine house: existence not found) in the July 1863 scene.

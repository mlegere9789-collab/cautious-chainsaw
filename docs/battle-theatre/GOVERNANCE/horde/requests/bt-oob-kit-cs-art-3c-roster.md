# bt-oob-kit cs-art-3c: roster notes
The plan lacks the three companies of the Sumter Battalion that the table prints under Major John Lane (Company A, Capt. Hugh M. Ross; B, Capt. George M. Patterson; C, Capt. John T. Wingfield) and the four batteries of Poague's battalion (Albemarle, Capt. James W. Wyatt; Charlotte, Capt. Joseph Graham; Madison Light Artillery, Capt. George Ward; Virginia Battery, Capt. Brooke): reported as unit_id null lines (the Sumter companies with roster, commander, guns and casualties, Poague's batteries with roster and commander), never applied. The roster unit cs-3c-anderson-cutts is the Sumter Battalion; Lieutenant Colonel A. S. Cutts was absent and Lane commanded (Walker, Lane). The casualty return lists McIntosh's batteries as Hurt's, Lusk's, Johnson's and Rice's; the organization table prints the 2d Rockbridge under Lieutenant Samuel Wallace: whether Lusk's row is the 2d Rockbridge is not stated and no line says so. The table prints no captain for the Crenshaw battery. Pegram's battalion: Captain E. B. Brunson commanded until Major Pegram assumed command on 30 June (Walker).

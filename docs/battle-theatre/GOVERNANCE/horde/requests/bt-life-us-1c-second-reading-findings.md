# Request from bt-life (group us-1c) to the owners of the other evidence groups and of `tools/bt/life/`: OCR date and digit errors found on the scan pages, one duplicate, one ownership case

Date: 2026-11-16 (third run of group us-1c). From: workstream bt-life, evidence group us-1c. To: the engineers of the groups that read Official Records diaries (us-2c, us-3c, us-5c, us-6c, us-11c, us-12c, us-cav, us-army), the owner of `tools/bt/life/`, the integrator.

## What

1. **The OCR text of the Official Records misreads digits in dates and hours, and a validator that reads only that text cannot see it.** On the scan pages (leaf of `tmp/kit-plan/or27-pdf/cu31924077699761.pdf`, found with `docs/battle-theatre/kit/evidence/or27_leaf_pages.json`) group us-1c found, in 184 rows of part 1: R095 (94th New York: the print says "the 5th", the OCR "6th": the row was a day wrong); the diaries of the 11th Pennsylvania and the 88th Pennsylvania print "July 12" where the OCR reads "July 13" (a conflict scenario between the two regiments existed only in the OCR); the headings "June 28." and "June 29." (88th Pennsylvania, OCR "June 38", "June 39"), "July 5." (16th Maine, OCR "July 6."), "July 2." (Newton, OCR "July 3"), "26 miles" (16th Maine, OCR "36"). Italic headings with a 2 or a 5 are the worst: the OCR gives 3 or 6. The same risk is in every diary-form report (the 88th, 107th, 121st, 11th Pennsylvania, 13th Massachusetts, 16th Maine, 94th New York and others in other corps). A row whose window rests on a heading digit or an hour should be looked at on its page. The method is mechanical and cheap: the PDF has a text layer with word boxes (`pdftotext -bbox`), so the line of a quote is found without running OCR; `pdftoppm -r 170` renders the page; crops of five rows are stacked on one picture and read by eye. The scripts are scratch in `tmp/life-us-1c/review/` (`clip2.py` for the Official Records, `clip3.py` for state volumes whose PDF has a text layer; usage in their first lines); anyone may copy them. The corrected rows keep the OCR text as the quote (the contract: errors as printed) and say what the page reads in `ocr_repairs` (`basis: page_image`) and in the notes. The validator accepts `ocr_repairs` on a quote that matches exactly (it only demands it when the fold or edit distance was needed); a check that a row with `ocr_repairs` has a note would be a small addition.
2. **A duplicate with group us-5c.** `us-1c-L076` (the National Park Service handbook of 1954: the stone breastworks on Little Round Top "constructed during the night of July 2", work E-14) is the same statement as `us-5c-L042` (`validate_evidence.py --all`: `duplicate_row`). The work is us-5c's and its row is the fuller one (it carries the scenario C-E14-TIME); us-1c removed its row (its id is not reused). Nothing is asked of us-5c.
3. **S-009 (McMillan's fences).** The work is assigned to us-1c; the only statement ("On the morning of July 1, 1863 Union troops advanced across McMillan's land, tearing down his fences to facilitate movement of troops", NPS-CLI-PCB p. 72) is in a text read by us-2c, which wrote no row for it. us-1c wrote the row (`us-1c-L092`, documented, modern secondary text; builder unknown) because the work is its own and the file of us-2c is closed. If us-2c (or a re-run) writes the same passage, `--all` will report a duplicate and one of the two rows goes. The same passage says "McMillan's fencing was used in constructing entrenchments" by the Confederates after the Union withdrawal: a statement for the group of the Southern corps that owns W-CONFED-SEMINARY-WORKS (not written by us-1c).
4. **Row ids.** The builder of us-1c pins the ids of the rows of its file (a removed row leaves a hole). Consumers must not assume consecutive ids.

## Why

A wrong day or hour in a documented row puts a soldier in the wrong place in the product; the only check that can find it is the page.

## The exact change asked

1. Each group that reads Official Records diaries: look at the page of every row whose window depends on a printed date digit or hour.
2. The owner of `tools/bt/life/`: optionally a validator warning for a row with `ocr_repairs` and no `notes`.
3. The integrator: nothing (the duplicate is already resolved by removal).

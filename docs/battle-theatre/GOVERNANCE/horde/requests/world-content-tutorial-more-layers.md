## Insert after "### Views (drawer)" (section 4)

### More (drawer): later memorials and tour stops
The More button appears when the theatre carries added layers. It lists two panels.

**Later memorials** is a switch that is off every time you enter. It shows the site's 512 monuments, memorials, markers and tablets as small marks over the picture. They were put up after the battle, so they are never objects of the 1863 scene and never stand on the ground at any minute of the scrubber; a notice on the picture says so for as long as they are shown, and every name reads "later memorial". The panel searches them (name, who is remembered, place), filters by side, by group and by whether they lie on the loaded ground, and flies to one; its card gives the tier, where the position comes from, and the year it was put up, which is an open gap because the site's data does not give it. A tap on a mark opens its card too. 77 memorials lie beyond the loaded ground: the camera goes to the edge of the ground and looks toward them, and the card says how far beyond which edge each one is. For the nine state memorials the site lists twice, the panel lets you choose which of the two positions the marks follow.

**Tour stops** lists the 16 stops of the site's Virtual Walkthrough as places for the camera. The camera stands where the site's walkthrough stands for that stop and looks at it; a pin labelled "modern tour stop" shows the stop while you are there, and "Remove the pin" takes it away. Choosing a stop never moves the clock. Tour Stop 2 and Tour Stop 3 lie beyond the loaded ground and are seen from the edge of it.

# bt-oob-kit / us-6c: roster and order-of-battle notes (for the roster owner)

Written 2026-11-24 by workstream bt-oob-kit. The evidence file `kit/evidence/us-6c.jsonl` follows the 49 units of `groups.json` for this group; nothing here changes them.

1. **`us-6c-2d-1brig` (VI Corps, 2nd Division, 1st Brigade):** the organization table (OR27-1-R009 p. 163) has the footnote "No First Brigade in division"; Howe's report and the return of casualties name the Second (Grant) and Third (Neill) Brigades only. The roster unit stands for a brigade the record says did not exist (line us-6c:0013: roster, engaged no; gap line us-6c:0263 for its commander).
2. **Units the table shows and the plan lacks** (lines with `unit_id: null`, not applied): the 102d Pennsylvania (Col. John W. Patterson), Wheaton's Third Brigade, "guarding wagon train at Westminster, and not engaged in the battle" (R009 p. 163; Patterson's report R236 p. 687; line us-6c:0102), parent `us-6c-3d-wheaton`; the 4th New Jersey (three companies, Capt. William R. Maxwell), the provost guard of the First Division (R009 p. 162; line us-6c:0103), parent `us-6c-1d`; the corps headquarters escort, 1st New Jersey Cavalry Company L and 1st Pennsylvania Cavalry Company H under Capt. William S. Craft (R009 p. 162; no line written), which belong to a cavalry group.
3. **The 33d New York (detachment):** the two-years regiment was mustered out on 2 June 1863; a small detachment of three-years men was attached to the 49th New York and permanently transferred to it on 1 October 1863 (NY-GB-1900 vol. 1, the account of Neill's brigade; lines us-6c:0259 and us-6c:0260). The plan's unit is that detachment under Capt. Henry J. Gifford; it has no strength of its own.
4. **Succession of command:** Third Division: Newton (the table; he took the First Corps on 1 July), Wheaton from 2 July, Bartlett assigned on the morning of 4 July (R226 p. 671). Third Brigade, Third Division: Wheaton, then Nevin of the 62d New York from the evening of 1 July to the evening of 4 July. Second Brigade, First Division: Bartlett, with Upton of the 121st New York in command from the evening of 1 July until Bartlett took it back on the march to Round Top (R227). 139th Pennsylvania: Collier shot himself through the foot on the morning of 3 July, Moody took the regiment (R237, R234). The plan's order of battle already lists Bartlett and Upton for the brigade.
5. **Commanders of the 2d and 3d New Jersey:** the table has the lieutenant colonels (Wiebecke, Campbell); Toombs prints the colonels (Buck, Brown). Carried as two conflict sets, no default.
6. **The 7th Maine** had six companies at Gettysburg (Companies A, E, G, H on recruiting service at Portland, ME-GB-1898); the 49th Pennsylvania had four companies (the table).

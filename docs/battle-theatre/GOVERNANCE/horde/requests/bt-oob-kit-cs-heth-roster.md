# bt-oob-kit cs-heth: roster notes
The 44th North Carolina (unit cs-44nc-hanover-junction, status never_on_field, parent cs-anv): Colonel T. C. Singeltary's report of 27 June 1863 (OR27-2-S4-03) shows the regiment relieving General Corse's brigade on the bridges and fords of the Central Railroad from Milford to the South Anna River on 25 June, with a detachment of 125 under Lieutenant Colonel T. L. Hargrove captured at the South Anna bridge on 26 June (OR27-2-S4-01, S4-02: Union reports). The roster line says engaged not_stated, with that detail; it was not with the Army of Northern Virginia at Gettysburg.

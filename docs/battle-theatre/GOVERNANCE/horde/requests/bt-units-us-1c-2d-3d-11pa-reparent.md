# Request from bt-units (group us-1c-2d-3d): the 11th Pennsylvania changes brigade in the afternoon of 1 July

What: the roster holds the 11th Pennsylvania (`us-1c-2d-baxter-11pa`) under Baxter's Second Brigade for the three days. The organization table of the Army of the Potomac (OR27-1-R009, p. 156) marks it "Transferred, in afternoon of July 1, to the First Brigade", and Coulter, its colonel, prints (R044) that it was transferred from the Second to the First Brigade about 5 p. m. on 1 July when the division formed on Cemetery Hill, and that he took command of the First Brigade. Records U-us-1c-2d-3d-0012 (the roster fact, action `status` with status on_field: no change of status) and the positions of 2 July of the regiment (U-us-1c-2d-3d-0157, U-us-1c-2d-3d-0158, U-us-1c-2d-3d-0159) carry it. The contract has no action that changes the parent of a unit (the actions are add, absent, attached, merge, fix, status, rename).

Why it matters: from the evening of 1 July the regiment stands with Paul's brigade (the brigade of Coulter, Cemetery Hill and the left of the Eleventh Corps position, R044), not with Baxter's; the layer's lines of the brigades are built from the roster parent.

The exact change wanted (not mine to make): a roster action `reparent` (unit, new parent, from when) in `unit-overlay.json`, applied from 1863-07-01T17:00 (printed "about 5 p. m."), or a decision of the integrator that the records of the regiment carry the move and the parent stays. Fox heads the page of the regiment "Paul's Brigade, Robinson's Division, First Corps" and the Pennsylvania volume (PA1-S002) says the regiment was temporarily a part of the First Brigade; Bierer (R049) prints that on 18 July the regiment, temporarily assigned to the First Brigade, was transferred back to the Second.

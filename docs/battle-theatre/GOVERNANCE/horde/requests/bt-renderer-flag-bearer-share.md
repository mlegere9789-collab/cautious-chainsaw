# Request to the kit / history-layer workstreams (from bt-renderer, crowd lane): one drawn figure in five carries a flag; the draw ignores "color guard only" and the role (2026-11-16)

Found while looking at the native replay pictures of the cross-fade (dark sheets hanging below the men): the figure pass draws 25,615 of its 127,572 figures (20.1 percent) as flag bearers. A regiment carried two colours.

## Evidence (all re-computable)
* The instance table of the real crowd as the WebGL2 backend uploads it (4096 x 32 RGBA32UI texture of `tmp/bt-renderer-lod/native/ops.json` + `blobs.bin`, real layer, 3 July 13:30): bit 0 of word 3 (`FLAG_BITS.flag_bearer`) is set on 25,615 figures, and the variant table's flag kind (word 3 of the RGBA16UI variant info, high byte) is set on exactly the same 25,615: us_national 8,933, us_regimental_gap 7,800, irish_green_gap 1,120, anv_battle 7,118, cav_guidon 644. 1,837 figures are musicians (bit 4).
* The kit: `kit/kit_specs.json` `defaults["default.union.infantry"].fields.flag_or_instrument.allowed` = national_color_us ("few", weight 1.0, detail "color guard only"), regimental_color_union ("few", 1.0, detail "color guard only"), flag_none ("most", 6.0). `manifestFor` (`app/src/history/manifest.mjs`, the same draw as the Python port) draws this field PER SOLDIER by weight, for `rank = private` and `role = rifleman` (`app/src/history/figures.mjs` passes those defaults for every figure), so a private has a 1 in 8 chance of each colour: 25 percent of a regiment's men carry one of the two Union colours. Tally of `manifestFor` for 200 private riflemen of each of the 325 kit units that are present at Gettysburg (not disputed): flag_none 49,434, bugle 3,869, cs_anv_battle_flag 3,867, national_color_us 3,570, regimental_color_union 1,866, flag_green_irish 1,000, guidon_swallowtail_cav 777, stretcher 235, flag_set_wigwag 200, drum 81, fife 80, ambulance_corps_flag 21 (the run of 2026-11-16: `tmp/bt-renderer-lod/run2/flagshare.mjs`).
* The kit's own roles list (`rank_rules.roles`: rifleman, color_bearer, color_guard, musician, ...) and its note "musicians carry drum/fife/bugle (role-driven)" say the field is meant to be role-driven; nothing assigns roles to figures and the draw does not read the role.

## What it does to the product
Every formation is full of colours (about one man in five in the view of a column), so the field looks wrong at every distance, the number of figures that stand under a flag mesh is large (the merged class mesh of a bearer is a class of its own: more classes, more draws), and the cards that read the manifest (`history/figures.mjs` aggregate) say that a fifth of a regiment carried colours.

## Not for the renderer lane to change, so it is asked here
The draw is shared with the Python port (`sim/rng.mjs`: "bit-identical"), the weights are the kit workstream's tuning parameters ("PDF B.6.2: the notes give no counts"), and `manifest.mjs` is not in the renderer lane's list. A fix that keeps both ports identical:
1. In the kit draw, an entry whose detail is "color guard only" (and the instruments) is eligible only for the roles `color_bearer` / `color_guard` / `musician`; a `rifleman` draws `flag_none`.
2. A role assignment per figure, deterministic from (unit, k): the counts of colours (two per Union regiment: national and regimental; one battle flag for a Confederate regiment) and of musicians are the kit workstream's to source (non-lead source, tier recorded); where no count is found the strongest evidence-based reconstruction with its tier and a gap entry.
3. `figures.mjs` (renderer lane) then passes the role to `manifestFor`; the renderer lane makes that change the day the kit side has the rule, and re-runs the crowd tests.

## Second finding of the same look, in the renderer lane's own area (recorded here so the two are read together)
The unit animator (`src/anim3d/unit-anim.mjs`) knows no role: a flag bearer plays the rifleman's clips, in which the second prop (`prop_pole`, the staff) is parked in the stowed ramrod pose. The staff and its cloth then hang upside down below the man (skinned vertices of the staff at y = -1.03 m and of the cloth at x = 1.69 m, y = -0.72 m, all frames; CPU skinning of the recorded frame `track-fade-10.5`, `tmp/bt-renderer-lod/run2/native_skin.py`). The colour-bearer clips exist (`march_color_quick`, `color_stand`, `color_plant`, `color_pickup`, `src/anim3d/clips-misc.mjs`) and are selected nowhere. The renderer lane owns this and will fix it (role-aware clip choice in the animator, the same for LOD0 and the baked bank); with five flags in a hundred men the defect is conspicuous, with two in a regiment it is still wrong.

# Request from bt-life (group us-2c, resumed run of 2026-11-16) to the owner of `tools/bt/life/` (contract, validator, groups), the content module of the life scenes, and the engineers of the groups us-6c, us-3c and narratives-us

Date: 2026-11-16. This note adds to `bt-life-us-2c-conventions-and-cross-group.md` and `bt-life-us-2c-reassign-records.md` of the first run; it does not replace them (their item 7, the two page images, is closed: see item 5 below).

## What

1. **A window with an unstated side (`x_window_open`).** 61 rows of `us-2c.jsonl` have a window whose clock states one side only ("remained until 4 p. m.", "left camp at 6 a. m. and encamped", "went into position at 11 p. m."). The other side is a bound: the day's start or end (the first run's convention, explained only in each row's `window.note`), the start of a march, or the end of a cannonade the passage dates by its start. The rows now carry an own field `x_window_open` with the value `start` (36 rows), `end` (25 rows) or `both` (none): the named side is **not** a statement of when the act began or ended. Rule used by the builder: `Wc(start, no end)` and `Wc_end` set it; the rest is the side of a `stated_clock` window that lies at a day bound (00:00 or 23:59) unless the quote says midnight; ten rows were set by hand where the side is the start of a march (us-2c-R217, R246, R136, R139, R143, R036, R054, R168) or a lower bound (R202) or "to at least" (R218). **Asked:** (a) promote the field to the contract as `window.open_side` (or keep `x_window_open`); (b) the content module reads an open side as an unknown, never as the start of the activity: a scene that draws "the regiment lies in line" from 00:00 would show men lying down during the night march that the same unit's timeline has them on the road; (c) at merge, rows of other groups may carry the same convention without a flag: a check "a `stated_clock` row whose start or end is exactly 00:00 or 23:59 has a flag or a note" would find them.

2. **Three limits of the validator found by the rows.** (a) `CLOCK_RE` does not match "12 m." (meridian, the print of Official Records for noon) nor the curly apostrophe in "o’clock" (the park texts print curly apostrophes; the quote matcher is tolerant of them, the clock check is not): a `stated_clock` row whose only hour is one of these is refused (us-2c-R289 needed a second quote with "about 8 a. m."; us-2c-R299 became `stated_period`). Asked: add `\b12\s*m\b` and `o['’]?\s*clock` to `CLOCK_RE`. (b) The row that is the source of several windows can only carry one printed page; the page model for state volumes (`statevol_page`) is interpolated between anchors and was 4 pages off on a page that was then read on its image (us-2c-R300: model p. 537, image p. 541); the row uses `page_basis: none` with `printed_page: "541"` so that the check is skipped. Asked: a page basis `page_image` (stated by eye on a page image) beside `scan_leaf`. (c) The hint list `subkind_activity_hint` for `rest_halt` (item 6 of the first note) should also name `lie_down` for `picket_duty`: pickets "lay flat upon the ground by the fence" (the 14th Connecticut, us-2c-R298) are a picket row with the lying clip; the validator now warns `activity_hint` on 14 rows, none an error.

3. **Digits of the local Official Records text (three rows corrected, more owed).** Read on the page images of the volume (the part 1 PDF is `tmp/kit-plan/or27-pdf/cu31924077699761.pdf`, PDF page = scan leaf + 1, the leaf of an offset from `tools/bt/kit_or27_leafmap.py`): printed p. 378 (leaf 401), the report of the dead buried by the Second Corps, prints "from July 2 to 5, inclusive" (the local text has "July 3") and the table "Officers | Enlisted men | Total" with Union 18, 369, 387, Rebel 60, 1,182, 1,242, Total 78, 1,551, 1,629 (the local text has 869 for 369); printed p. 457 (leaf 480) prints "At 12 m." (the local text has "13 m.", an hour that does not exist). `ocr_repairs` with basis `page_image` records both on rows us-2c-R086, R275 and R289; the contract says a repair is for a quote that differs from the text: here the quote equals the (wrong) text and the repair says what the page reads, which the validator accepts silently. Asked: say in the contract that this use is allowed (the quote stays as printed, the repair carries the reading, the window follows the reading). **Owed to the groups that cite these records:** the timeline rows and unit evidence that use p. 378 should read "July 2 to 5", not "3 to 5"; a second OCR of every Official Records quote of every group is the check that finds such digits (the resumed run of this group queued it behind the machine lock; see the report, section 3, for whether it has run).

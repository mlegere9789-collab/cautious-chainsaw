Written 2026-11-16 by workstream bt-oob-kit. Catalog additions are code changes of the integrator; the evidence lines carry `weapon_id: null` or `item_id: null` and the printed words where the catalog of `groups.json` lacks the item. Nothing in this note changes a file outside the workstream's ownership. The earlier notes are `bt-oob-kit-us-1c-catalog.md` and `bt-oob-kit-us-1c-catalog-2.md`.

## Weapons the third pass found and the catalog lacks

1. "Altered smooth-bore muskets" (the 90th Pennsylvania, us-1c:0693, dated 1 July 1863 on the strength of Bates's statement that they were not exchanged until July 1864): flintlock muskets converted to percussion; the passage gives neither the pattern nor the calibre. The catalog has `m1842_smoothbore` (a musket made as a percussion arm) and `smoothbore_69_musket_unspecified` (a placeholder for the 116th Pennsylvania's ".69 muskets"). The compile lists the line under review ("has no catalog id").
2. "Rifled muskets of calibre .69" (the 11th Pennsylvania, 0690, 2 January 1863; calibre in `caliber_in`): no item. The same Bates passage says the arms of calibre .57 and .58 that the regiment had carried were exchanged for them.
3. "The Enfield rifle" (the 88th Pennsylvania, 0691, autumn 1861) and "Enfield rifles" (the 143rd Pennsylvania, 0694, November 1862): pattern not named; the catalog has `p1853_enfield` and `p1856_58_enfield_short`. These two lines are outside the compile window and are not applied.
4. "New Springfield rifles" (the 151st Pennsylvania, 0255 and the inferred 0722): the model is not named. `springfield_model_unspecified` is a placeholder that the compile refuses to assign to a weapon cell ("cannot be assigned to weapon by rule"); lines 0239 (the 6th Wisconsin, 1862) and 0255 still cite it and are outside the window; the new inferred line 0722 carries `weapon_id: null`. A question for the integrator: is the placeholder meant to be unassignable by evidence? If the Iron Brigade's and the 151st's cells are to be confirmed at the pattern the record names ("Springfield rifles"), an item such as "Springfield rifle-musket, model not named" that evidence may cite is needed.

## Dress items

None new: the 11th Pennsylvania's badge (0705) uses `badge_I_white`, its shelter tents (0706) `shelter_half`; the question of the shelter half's conditional-gap status (note catalog-2) now rests on three regiments (150th Pennsylvania, 13th Vermont and, for October 1862, the 11th Pennsylvania).

## Questions for the integrator about the compile

1. `in_window` tests the start of `as_of`: a line whose interval covers the window but starts earlier is not applied. Five lines of the file are of that kind and state something true on 1 July 1863: 0255 (the 151st's Springfield rifles issued from 15 February), 0263 (the 13th Vermont's Austrian rifles, 6 October 1862 to 1 July 1863), 0291 (the 13th Vermont's march from 25 June), 0444 and 0445 (the detail of 53 men of the 83rd and 94th New York to a Maine battery from 24 May). The third pass dated two lines inside the window where the text itself says the date (0693 and 0722); the five above were left as they are because the passages do not say the date. Testing overlap rather than the start would apply them.
2. Fourteen lines of this group are routed to review by the compile in its dry run of 2026-11-16: the five Iron Brigade badge lines (0224, 0227, 0230, 0233, 0236: `badge_I_red`, tier inferred, against a kit cell that is also inferred and that the compile therefore keeps), the 149th's and 150th's derived badge lines (0436, 0437: `badge_I_blue` for all men, against the kit's cell of a minority with no badge for most), and seven lines whose item has no catalog id ('barefoot' and similar, 0241, 0248, 0254; 'fly tents', 0515; 'Enfield rifles', 0476; 'altered smooth-bore muskets', 0693; 'new Springfield rifles', 0722). The badge lines need a decision on the cell (DD-KIT-01 names the rule), not on the evidence.
3. The conflict sets of the file (15) are carried by the verified table as alternatives; the compile applies none of them.

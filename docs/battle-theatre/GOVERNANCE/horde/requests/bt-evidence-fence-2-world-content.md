## C. Rules that apply to every row above
1. Walls and fences that stood before the battle (Culp's Hill field walls, Cemetery Hill walls, the Trostle and Wheatfield walls, Little Round Top's pre-existing stone fence) get no construction animation; built works (Greene's breastworks, the traverse, the Little Round Top walls, per-gun earthworks) are built on screen in the windows above and nothing is pre-placed.
2. Never derive an 1863 profile from a present (rebuilt or restacked) wall: the Little Round Top walls were restacked in 1882-1887 (NPS-CLI-LRT); the Culp's Hill earthworks and the Rose and Bushman worm fences are partly modern; the NPS summary of the earthen works (log cribs five feet high "in a few areas such as Culp's Hill") is a modern general statement, not a profile.
3. Hours of the schedule rows must come from the quote or from a bracket stated in the JSONL; where the JSONL gives several witness values (Culp's Hill start and finish hours including the NPS "mid-morning", the Benner's Hill duel, the 3 Jul bombardment, gun counts, wall distances, the hour of the council at the Leister house) carry them all as selectable scenarios; the delegated defaults are DD-F2-01..20.
4. Hospital and headquarters rows: show the sourced time and the open-gap marker on the unsourced one (S-027, S-028, S-047, S-048, S-049, S-051); do not render the printed relocation of the III and V Corps hospitals on the night of 2 Jul; counts of wounded taken from the Sanitary Commission's report (15 Aug 1863) are "as found in July", not at an hour of 1 or 2 Jul; the park's corps-hospital tablets (placed in 1914) are commemorative, never a statement of 1863.
5. A claimant's statement (the 1870s damage claims, the 1883 affidavits of the McPherson farm) and a modern NPS summary are shown flagged as such; the delegated decision DD-TL01-04 keeps the 30 Jun fence dismantling at the McPherson farm "alleged" (S-017) and this shard does not change it.

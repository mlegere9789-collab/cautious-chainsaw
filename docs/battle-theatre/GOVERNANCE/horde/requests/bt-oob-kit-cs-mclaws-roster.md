# bt-oob-kit cs-mclaws and cs-pickett: roster notes for the roster owner
- `cs-1c-mclaws-wofford-3gasharpshooters` (3rd Georgia Sharpshooters): not in OR27-2 table p. 283 nor either return of casualties; Confederate Military History vol. 6 puts the battalion in Wofford's brigade (cs-mclaws inferred lines).
- `cs-corse-hanover-junction`: not in the table of battle; Taylor (SHSP-04) says it stayed at Hanover Junction (cs-pickett roster line, engaged: no).
- Brigade order: Barksdale's brigade lists Col. B. G. Humphreys second (the table), who is also colonel of the 21st Mississippi.

## The rule you must apply to every record (hard rule: no 1863 profile from rebuilt walls)
- `use_for_1863: direct` = the passage states an 1863 or earlier condition. Use it as content (tier from `tier_proposed`, single secondary source; an extract that quotes an account says whose account it is).
- `use_for_1863: indirect` = a modern, rebuilt, restored or replanted state (the 1999-2019 fences, orchards and woods; walls restacked 1882-1910; works rebuilt 1880s-1905 and measured in 1940; the barn as altered in 1905) or a planning figure. Show only as a marked inference; never take a height, mass, profile, rail count or alignment from it.
- `use_for_1863: none` = a feature placed after July 1863 (116 records: monuments, avenues, towers, the cemetery wall of 1864, the pipe-rail and iron fences of 1864-65, additions to the McClean barn c. 1869-1890, the north lean-to of the McPherson barn). Do not draw it before its date.

## Conflicts between reports (carry all readings, no default; DD-NPS-04 and DD-NPS-09)
- 1863 woodlot understory: open-grown white oak in pastured woodlots (NRCA 2017 p. 97, from Bowersox et al. 2004) against a dense understory as the 1863 condition to maintain (same report p. 167); NPSX-NRCA-2017-p129-01 and p199-01. The GMP says the Herbst woodlot was open (an avenue of approach) and Patterson woods used for concealment (NPSX-GMP-1999-V1-p068-01).
- Stone walls: the GMP says the vast majority of the walls that enclosed the farm fields survived the battle (NPSX-GMP-1999-V1-p176-03), the 2012 Little Round Top report calls the fieldstone fences of the park mostly rebuilt (NPSX-CLR-LRT-p057-01 state date): do not take a profile from either.
- Emmitsburg Road fences on July 3: board fences mostly standing (NPSX-COMMON-PRIDE-1-p155-01) against nearly destroyed the day before (p131-01); post and rail (p129-01), plank or slab (p128-01).
- West slope of Little Round Top: cleared a year or two before the battle (Slaughter Pen study; vista cutting report) against not confirmed by any known primary source (NPSX-CLR-LRT-p021-01).
- Open, not a conflict: the date of the two-story frame house that replaced the stone front of the McPherson farmhouse is not given (after 1863, before the fire of 1895; NPSX-HS-MCPHERSON-p085-01): keep the stone and log house of 1863 and mark the later house as a gap.

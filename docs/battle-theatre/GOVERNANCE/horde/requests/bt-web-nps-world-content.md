## The rule you must apply to every record (hard rule: no 1863 profile from rebuilt walls)
- `use_for_1863: direct` = the passage states an 1863 or earlier condition. Use it as content (tier from `tier_proposed`, single secondary source).
- `use_for_1863: indirect` = a modern, rebuilt, restored or replanted state (the 1999-2019 fences, orchards and woods; walls restacked 1882-1910; works rebuilt 1880s-1905 and measured in 1940). Show only as a marked inference; never take a height, mass, profile, rail count or alignment from it. The reconstructed fences are not evidence (the Record of Treatment says so itself).
- `use_for_1863: none` = a feature placed after July 1863. Do not draw it before its date. There are 70 such records (monuments, avenues, towers, pipe-rail avenue fences, the cemetery wall, later barns and houses); SUMMARY.md lists them with their dates.

# bt-oob-kit cs-art-1c: roster notes
The organization table (OR27-2 pp. 283-285) prints two batteries of Alexander's battalion as 'Virginia Battery' (Capt. W. W. Parker and Capt. O. B. Taylor): the roster units cs-1c-res-art-alexander-richmond and -bath are Parker's and Taylor's batteries (Parker's rendered in the reports as Parker's, Taylor's signs as 'Virginia battery'); Brooks (South Carolina) Artillery is commanded by Lieut. S. C. Gilbert (Rhett's), Fickling's in Pendleton's table. Dearing's battalion has a fourth battery, Blount's Virginia Battery. The kit-only records CS.ART.Squires, Richardson, Miller and Norcom are the four companies of the Washington Artillery; the OCR prints Troup's Capt. Carlton as Charlton in Cabell's table. The corps unit cs-1c-art has no commander in the table; Eshleman's reports are addressed to Col. J. B. Walton as Chief of Artillery, First Corps.

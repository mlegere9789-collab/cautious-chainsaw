# bt-oob-kit us-art-reserve-horse: roster findings for the roster owner

Written 24 November 2026. Source: kit/evidence/us-art-reserve-horse.jsonl (nothing is applied by the pass).

1. **10th New York Independent Battery** (unit_id null line `us-art-reserve-horse:0256`, parent `us-art-reserve-mcgilvery-5ma`): the organization table (OR27-1-R009 p. 167) footnotes the 5th Massachusetts Battery (E) "10th New York Battery attached"; the return of casualties (p. 187) says its loss, included in the Battery E row, was 2 men killed and 3 wounded. The roster has no unit for it.
2. **11th New York Independent Battery** (`us-art-reserve-horse:0257`, parent `us-art-reserve-fitzhugh-1nyk`): the table (p. 168) footnotes Battery K, 1st New York, "11th New York Battery attached"; Fitzhugh (R330) reports the two together. The roster has no unit for it.
3. **Company C, 32d Massachusetts Infantry** (`us-art-reserve-horse:0258`, parent `us-art-reserve`): the headquarters guard of the Artillery Reserve (Capt. Josiah C. Fuller, table p. 167; Tyler, R315).
4. **Horse Artillery, First Brigade (Robertson) and Second Brigade (Tidball)** (`us-art-reserve-horse:0089` and `0096`, parent `us-cav-art`): the table prints two brigades; the return of casualties gives each a total row (8 and 15); the roster has one unit, `us-cav-art`, whose commanders are therefore two concurrent commanders (orders 1 and 2 of the lines).
5. **1st Connecticut Heavy, Batteries B and M** (`us-art-reserve-taft-1ctheavy-b`, `-m`) against the kit card `US.ART.1st_Conn_Heavy_B_and_H`: the table (p. 168) and Tyler (R315) print B and M, not H. Both were marked not engaged and sent to Westminster with the supply train. The roster unit `us-art-reserve-taft-1ctheavy` is the pair as one battery: its commander lines are Brooker (B) and Pratt (M), concurrent.
6. **3d US F and K, Pennsylvania C and F, 1st Pennsylvania F and G**: one entry each in the table and in the return, one commander (Turnbull, Thompson, Ricketts), one set of figures; the roster has two units for each pair, which carry the same lines (a joint figure is not split).
7. **3d US Battery C (Fuller)** is printed in the Second Brigade of the Horse Artillery with the footnote "With Huey's Cavalry Brigade, and not engaged in the battle" (p. 167); it is not a unit of this group.
8. **Succession of the Reserve**: the table prints Brig. Gen. Tyler and Capt. Robertson under the heading; Robertson took command of the Reserve on 3 July when Tyler received a sunstroke (R368). Captain Ransom, commanding the First Regular Brigade, was wounded on 2 July (R317, R315); Lieutenant Eakin (1st US H) was wounded on 2 July and Lieutenant Mason took the battery (R028 pp. 233-234); Lieutenant Milton took the 9th Massachusetts after Captain Bigelow was wounded (R315, R320); Captain Taft commanded the Second Volunteer Brigade and, by the New York volume, Lieutenant J. P. Denike the 5th New York Battery (against Taft's own report).

# bt-oob-kit cs-anderson: roster notes
Perry's brigade (unit cs-3c-anderson-perry, named Perry/Lang in the plan): the organization table prints the heading Perry's Brigade over Col. David Lang alone; Lang signs the report as colonel commanding the brigade (OR27-2-R545) and Confederate Military History vol. XI says General Perry was disabled with typhoid fever. The unit line stands as Perry's brigade, commanded by Lang. The plan has no unit for the Sumter battalion of artillery (Major John Lane, companies of Ross, Patterson and Wingfield) printed under Anderson's division on the same page of the table; it belongs to group cs-art-3c.

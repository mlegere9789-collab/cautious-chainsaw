# bt-oob-kit cs-cav: roster notes
Units the record shows and the plan lacks (unit_id null lines of cs-cav.jsonl): Jackson's (Virginia) Battery, Capt. Thomas E. Jackson, under Jenkins' Brigade (OR27-2 p. 290), and McClanahan's (Virginia) Battery, Capt. J. H. McClanahan, under Imboden's command (p. 291); the 15th Virginia Cavalry (W. H. F. Lee's brigade) has a blank row in Guild's cavalry return (p. 335) and in Stuart's inclosures and is in neither the table nor the plan. The 12th Virginia Cavalry and the 35th Battalion are not in the organization table; the returns list them under Jones' Brigade, and Early (SHSP vol. 4) says White's battalion was with his division; the plan puts the battalion under cs-2c-early. The table prints W. H. F. Lee's brigade under Col. J. R. Chambliss, Jr. of the 13th Virginia (W. H. F. Lee was wounded at Brandy Station on 9 June; Stuart's report, p. 683); Robertson's brigade carries the footnote that he commanded his own and W. E. Jones' brigade. Griffin's Baltimore Light Artillery stands under the Stuart Horse Artillery in the table; Goldsborough says it joined Jenkins' cavalry brigade in June and at Gettysburg reported to Major Latimer's battalion (a detachment line of cs-cav.jsonl): the owner of group cs-art-2c should see this. The 1st Maryland Battalion is footnoted in the table as serving with Ewell's corps. The kit gives the 1st Virginia Cavalry to Maj Morgan and the 14th Virginia to Maj Eakle; the table prints Col. James H. Drake for the 1st Virginia and Confederate Military History vol. 3 names Col. James Cochrane for the 14th Virginia. Robertson's and Jones' brigades and Imboden's command are marked never on the main field in the plan, while the 6th and 7th Virginia (Jones' brigade) fought at Fairfield on 3 July and Robertson's two brigades held Jack's Mountain passes by Stuart's order.

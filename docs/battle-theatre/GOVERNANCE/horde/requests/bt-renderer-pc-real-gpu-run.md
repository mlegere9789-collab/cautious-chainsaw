## What the result file says and what it cannot say

`REAL-GPU-RESULT.md` gives, per row of section E of the verification list, what the machine could check: W-E1 (no validation error, no compiler message, no device loss, both APIs; the negative controls of the harness show that the error machinery sees errors), W-E2 (parity numbers), W-E3 (the shell on both APIs), W-E4 (the pass time from timestamp queries and the frame time over 600 frames after 120 warm-up frames at 1920 x 1080, desktop tier, a quiet sky and a dense cannonade, the layer's passes on, off and each alone, two rounds in alternating order; a stand-in world of 12,000 soldiers without the crowd), W-E7 (the compositor's picture of the real canvas against the read-back of the same frame), W-E8 (only that the optional compute pipeline builds), W-E9 (the adapters). It cannot say: Safari, Firefox (W-E6), a device without `float32-filterable` (W-E5), other vendors, a phone, or the 600-frame timing inside the shell on the real crowd. Those stay owed and the session says so. **The result starts with "NOT A REAL-GPU RESULT" when any run recorded a software adapter or was not started with `--real-gpu`, so a run on the wrong machine cannot pass.** No script ticks a row of the verification list: a person (or an agent that has looked at the pictures) does, and the register entry EG-W3D-G08 stays open until the rows of section E and the crowd lane's item 2 are ticked on the named hardware.

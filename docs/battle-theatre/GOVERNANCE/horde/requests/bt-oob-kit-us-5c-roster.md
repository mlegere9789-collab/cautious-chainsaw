# bt-oob-kit / us-5c: roster findings for the roster owner (reported, not applied)

1. `US.5NY_INF` (kit record only, 5th New York, Duryee's Zouaves): not in the organization table of the Fifth Corps (OR27-1-R009 pp. 161-162); the general order of 4 May 1863 (NY-GB-1900-v2, from Davenport's history of the regiment) says its term of service was about to expire and sends the officers and men leaving the army to New York on 5 May; Colonel Winslow. The kit's "absent from the battle" is supported (inferred, lines us-5c roster and commander).
2. `us-5c-1d-sweitzer-32ma-co-c` is Company C of the 32d Massachusetts, listed in the organization table (p. 167) with the Headquarters Guard of the Artillery Reserve, Capt. Josiah C. Fuller, and present guarding the Artillery Reserve's train on 3 July (Tyler, OR27-1-R315); the roster places it under the 32d Massachusetts of Sweitzer's brigade and as "never on the field". The roster name "4th NJ (train guard) and 32nd Mass Co. C" is a pair of units with the Artillery Reserve.
3. The corps headquarters has two units the plan lacks: 12th New York Infantry, Companies D and E (Capt. Henry W. Rider) and 17th Pennsylvania Cavalry, Companies D and H (Capt. William Thompson), lines with `unit_id` null.
4. The 9th Massachusetts was absent from Sweitzer's brigade on the afternoon of 2 July on special duty (Barnes, R189); only three regiments (1,010 men) are in the inclosure of Sweitzer's report.
5. The Third Division (Pennsylvania Reserves) has two brigades in the table: the footnote says it joined the corps on 28 June and that its Second Brigade stayed in the Department of Washington; the roster names McCandless's and Fisher's as "First" and "Third".
6. Succession: Vincent (mortally wounded 2 July) then Rice (44th New York) for the Third Brigade, First Division, then Conner for the 44th New York (Chamberlain commanded the 20th Maine throughout); Weed (mortally wounded) then Garrard for the Third Brigade, Second Division; O'Rorke (mortally wounded) then Ernst for the 140th; Freedley (wounded) then Lay for the 3d United States; Lee (wounded) then McKee for the 2d; Taylor killed for the 13th Pennsylvania Reserves.

# bt-oob-kit cs-pender: roster notes
The organization table (OR27-2 p. 289) prints Col. Abner Perrin, and not General McGowan, under the First Brigade of Pender's division; the roster unit cs-3c-pender-mcgowan is McGowan's brigade under Perrin (the report of Engelhard names McGowan, 'the latter under command of Col. A. Perrin'). The table prints the division's commanders as Pender, Lane, Trimble, Lane and Lane's brigade's as Lane, Avery, Lane, Avery. The Addenda return (p. 344) prints a Staff row for the division (1 killed, 4 wounded, 5) and one for Scales' brigade (1 wounded) that have no roster unit; the division total of the Addenda (262, 1,312, 116, 1,690) includes Poague's artillery battalion (cs-art-3c). Guild's division total prints 1,383 wounded where the brigades add to 1,283.

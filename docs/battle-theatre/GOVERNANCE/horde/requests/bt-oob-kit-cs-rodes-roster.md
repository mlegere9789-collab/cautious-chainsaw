# bt-oob-kit cs-rodes: roster notes
CS.5ALBN ("5th Alabama Battalion (Blackford's sharpshooters)") is mislabelled. The record (OR27-2-R527, R506, R467) shows a corps or battalion of sharpshooters under Major Eugene Blackford of the 5th Alabama Infantry, drawn from the regiments of Rodes' (O'Neal's) Alabama brigade and later from the division; it has no row in the returns of casualties or in the organization table. The 5th Alabama Battalion proper is a separate unit listed in Archer's brigade of Heth's division (OR27-2 p. 333, R552), group cs-heth. Daniel's report calls the 2nd North Carolina Battalion by that name; the table lists it as 2d North Carolina Battalion. Rodes' brigade is printed Rodes' Brigade [O'Neal's] in the return of casualties and O'Neal's Brigade in the table; the plan's id is cs-2c-rodes-oneal. Carter's artillery battalion belongs to group cs-art-2c (rows OR27-2 p. 332: 5, 36, 41; p. 342: 6, 35, 24, 65).

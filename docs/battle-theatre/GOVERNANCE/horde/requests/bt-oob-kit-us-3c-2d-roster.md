# bt-oob-kit / us-3c-2d: roster notes

Written 2026-11-24 for the roster owner. Nothing was applied to the roster.

1. Rows of the return of casualties (OR27-1-R013 p. 178) that are not units of the plan, written as `unit_id: null` lines: the staff of the Second Division (0094: 2 enlisted men killed, 2 officers and 7 men wounded, 11), the staff of the First Brigade (0095: 2 officers wounded) and of the Second Brigade (0103: 2 officers wounded).
2. The 84th Pennsylvania (First Brigade) was not engaged: the table of the Official Records marks it "guarding corps trains, and not engaged in the battle" (lines 0034 and 0066, the second the footnote), Opp's report says the same (R168) and Fox ("detailed as a train-guard"); it has no row in the return. Roster `engaged: no`.
3. Order of command (lines 0067 to 0069 and the brigade lines): the division, Humphreys (relieved 9 July), Carr (9 July, until 6.30 a.m. on 10 July), Prince (from 10 July); the Excelsior Brigade, Brewster (relieved 11 July), Spinola (wounded 23 July), Farnum; the 11th New Jersey's table names Schoonover twice (third and sixth) and its second name, Captain Luther Martin, was killed at Gettysburg (Marbaker); the 16th Massachusetts' second name, Donovan, signs its report (29 July); the 72d New York's Leonard commanded after Austin was wounded; the 7th New Jersey's Francine was mortally wounded and Cooper took the regiment.
4. Birney's report (R133) calls Burling's brigade "the Third Brigade of the Second Division" and says the 5th, 6th and 7th New Jersey and the 2d New Hampshire were sent to him; Humphreys says the 7th and 2d went to Graham's brigade and the 8th, 6th and 115th Pennsylvania to Ward's; the 5th stayed with Humphreys on picket. The roster's single brigade is right for the morning.
5. The five Excelsior regiments are the 70th to 74th New York (First to Fifth Excelsior; the 73d is the Second Fire Zouaves, the 72d the Third Excelsior); the 120th New York was added to the brigade in 1862. The plan's order matches the table.
6. Third Division (Elliott) and its reports (R179, R180) were read to rule units in or out: they name no unit of this group (the division formed after the battle).

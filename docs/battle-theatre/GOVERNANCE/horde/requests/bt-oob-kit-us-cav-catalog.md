Weapons (kit_weapons_data): a generic breech-loading carbine of unstated make; generic "carbines"; sabre (no pattern); pistol/revolver of unstated make; "the Colt's large holster pistol" (no model stated); Smith carbine and Sharps carbine and Spencer repeater exist in the catalog and were used where the text names them.

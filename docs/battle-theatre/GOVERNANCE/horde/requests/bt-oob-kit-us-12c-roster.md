- Not in the plan: the 10th Maine Infantry (four companies), Capt. John D. Beardsley, Provost Guard of the Twelfth Corps (OR27-1-R009 p. 165; field return of 4 July: 6 officers, 164 men). Lines: roster, commander and strength with unit_id null, parent us-12c.
- The First Division's 4 July row (275 officers, 4,088 men) does not include Lockwood's brigade (its own row: 83, 1,379). The table footnote (p. 165) says Lockwood's brigade was unassigned during the battle and afterwards attached to the First Division as Second Brigade.
- `groups.json` gives the 123d New York the section NY2-S027, which is the 124th New York (inscription "124th New York Infantry"); the 123d has no regimental text in the local volumes (NY1-S027 and NY3-S068 are a monument title and a list).
- The table prints three commanders for the Second Brigade of the Second Division in the order Cobham, Kane, Cobham, and for the 111th Pennsylvania Walker, Cobham, Walker.

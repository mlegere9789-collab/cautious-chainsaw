## 2. Page-number error that may exist in other shards

The printed page of a library citation was recovered in the first build of tl-01 from ONE parity of running head only ("156 Southern Historical Society Papers." but not "Causes of Lee's Defeat at Gettysburg. 157"), and several citations were one or two pages too early (Alexander 1907, Southern Historical Society Papers vol.4 and vol.6, Fremantle 1864, Pierce 1889, History of the First Minnesota 1916, Life and Letters of G. G. Meade vol.2). Any other shard that cites these texts with a page recovered the same way should be re-checked. The method that corrects it is `lib_page_final` in `horde/tl-01/closeout/build.py` (both parities of head, the page is certain only when the head before and the head after the passage carry consecutive numbers; otherwise the page is stated with its basis and checked against the heads).

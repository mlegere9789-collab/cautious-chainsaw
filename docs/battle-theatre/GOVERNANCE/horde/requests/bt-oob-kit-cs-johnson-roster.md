# bt-oob-kit cs-johnson: roster notes
Stonewall Brigade: the organization table (OR27-2 p. 286) heads it Stonewall Brigade under Brig. Gen. James A. Walker, and the return of casualties (p. 331) heads the same brigade Walker's Brigade; the plan's id is cs-2c-johnson-stonewall. Nicholls' brigade is commanded in the table by Col. J. M. Williams of the 2nd Louisiana (Nicholls was absent); the plan's id is cs-2c-johnson-nicholls. The 1st Maryland Battalion (Herbert, Goldsborough, Crane) is called the Second Maryland by McKim (SHSP vol. 5). Johnson's artillery battalion (Andrews, with Dement, Carpenter, Brown, Raine) is listed under Johnson's division in the table and belongs to group cs-art-2c; its casualty rows (OR27-2 p. 331: 10, 36, 46; p. 341: 10, 40, 50) are for that group.

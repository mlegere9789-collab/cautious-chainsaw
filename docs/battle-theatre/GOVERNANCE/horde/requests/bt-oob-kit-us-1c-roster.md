# bt-oob-kit / us-1c: roster (history/units.json owner) and facts for other groups

Written 2026-11-13 by workstream bt-oob-kit. Nothing in this note changes a file outside the workstream's ownership.

## Roster

1. Add the 12th Vermont (Col. Asa P. Blunt) and the 15th Vermont (Col. Redfield Proctor), Third Brigade, Third Division, First Corps. The organization table (OR27-1-R009 p. 156) prints both with the footnote "Guarding trains, and not engaged in the battle"; Stannard's report says they were detailed by Reynolds as guard to the corps wagon train before the brigade reached the field, and that the 15th rejoined on the morning of the 2d and was sent back about noon (evidence lines us-1c:0127, 0128, 0377 to 0379). The plan lists three Vermont regiments, so these two are not in the group.
2. Add the 1st Maine Cavalry, Company L (Capt. Constantine Taylor), at the First Corps' general headquarters (OR27-1-R009 p. 155; line 0129); the return of casualties gives it 1 enlisted man killed and 2 wounded (line 0381). The corps headquarters staff row (1 officer killed, 1 wounded) is line 0380. Both are `unit_id: null` lines, reported and not applied.
3. The 11th Pennsylvania is listed under Baxter's brigade; the table's footnote and its own commander's report say it was transferred from the Second to the First Brigade (Paul) at about 5 p.m. on 1 July (lines 0085, 0018), and the return of casualties prints two rows for it (lines 0197 and 0198).
4. The corps' commanders in the roster are "Reynolds (k); Doubleday; Newton"; the organization table prints only Doubleday and Newton under the corps heading and names Reynolds in a footnote (line 0001, note of 0130). The Third Division's roster order "Doubleday; Rowley" is Rowley, Doubleday in the table and in the reports (lines 0006 to 0008).
5. Paul's brigade: the table prints six names in the order Paul, Leonard, Root, Coulter, Lyle, Coulter; the reports give the same order with the dates (lines 0014 to 0020).
6. Stone's brigade: Stone, Wister, Dana (lines 0028 to 0030); Biddle's brigade: Biddle (30 June, 1 July), Rowley (from 2 July), the table prints Biddle again (lines 0024 to 0026).

## Facts for other groups (no line written for them here)

- `us-art-corps`: the Artillery Brigade (Col. Charles S. Wainwright) rows of the return of casualties, OR27-1-R013 p. 174, read from the scan and adding up: 2d Maine (B) 18 (wounded enlisted 18); 5th Maine (E) 23 (killed enlisted 3, wounded 2 officers and 11 men, missing enlisted 7); 1st New York L 17 (killed 1, wounded 1 officer and 14 men, missing 1); 1st Pennsylvania B 12 (killed 3, wounded 1 officer and 8 men); 4th United States B 36 (killed 2, wounded 2 officers and 29 men, missing 3); total 106 (killed enlisted 9, wounded 6 officers and 80 men, missing enlisted 11). The 4 July return gives the artillery 12 officers and 517 men (R008 p. 153). Maine at Gettysburg (ME-GB-1898): the 5th Maine battery "one hundred and thirty-six strong" on the morning of 1 July, with a detail of fifty-three men from the 83d and 94th New York (our lines 0444 and 0445 for the infantry side); the 2nd Maine battery 150 on its rolls, 35 of them detailed from the 16th Maine, 2 from the 13th Massachusetts and 2 from the 97th New York (our lines 0441 to 0443), 127 present for duty.
- `us-art-corps`: the 30 June abstract row of the First Corps (R008 p. 151) gives 28 pieces of artillery, 21 officers and 598 men of the artillery present for duty equipped.
- Confederate kit groups: Lieut. J. G. Rosengarten, ordnance officer of the First Corps, lists the arms captured from the enemy near Gettysburg (OR27-1-R031 p. 264, 19 July 1863): Springfield rifled muskets 174, Enfield rifled muskets 2,402, Austrian rifled muskets 64, English rifled muskets 26, Harper's Ferry smooth-bore 212, various 80, total 2,958. It is a record of the arms of the Confederate regiments the corps met; this pass wrote no line for it.
- `army-hq`: the General Headquarters staff row on p. 173 (2 officers and 2 men wounded, aggregate 4) belongs to no First Corps unit.
- Workstream `bt-units` (`history/undrawn/us-1c-2d-3d.jsonl`): its progress line records the strength of the 97th New York, 11th and 90th Pennsylvania and 104th New York as gaps. This pass found figures for them: the 11th Pennsylvania 23 officers and 269 men present for duty on 30 June (line us-1c:0387), the 90th Pennsylvania 24 officers and 217 men on 30 June and an effective strength of 208 (0389, 0390), the 97th New York 150 fit for duty on 21 July (0403), the 104th New York about 330 in line (0402); the Adjutant General's letters (PA-GB-1914-v2) also give the 121st (0391), 142nd (0394) and 150th Pennsylvania (0396) on 30 June.

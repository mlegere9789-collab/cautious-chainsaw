# bt-oob-kit / us-3c-1d: roster and order-of-battle notes (for the roster owner)

Written 2026-11-16 by workstream bt-oob-kit. The evidence file `kit/evidence/us-3c-1d.jsonl` follows the 24 units of `groups.json` for this group; nothing here changes them. The lines below name what the record shows and the plan lacks.

1. **Succession of command, III Corps, 2 to 9 July** (lines us-3c-1d:0065 to 0068: Birney's report, OR27-1-R133 p. 483; Hancock's, R076 p. 370; French's, R134 p. 489): Sickles, wounded on 2 July (lost a leg); Birney, at Sickles's request, took command of the troops about 6 o'clock; Hancock was directed by Meade to assume command of the Third Corps in addition to his own; French assumed command on 9 July. The plan's order of battle has "Sickles (w); Birney" for the corps and "Birney; Ward" for the division, without Hancock and French. The division: Birney, then Ward from the afternoon of 2 July (0069, 0070).
2. **Second Brigade after Ward took the division:** Colonel Berdan commanded it (0075: Ward's report, OR27-1-R135 p. 494, "Colonel Berdan, who succeeded me in command"; 0326: the 124th New York's address; Stevens p. 323), while Lieutenant-Colonel Trepp led the 1st Sharpshooters and Major Stoughton the 2d. Berdan had also been given both sharpshooter regiments on the morning of 2 July (Berdan's report, OR27-1-R149 p. 514), so the brigade commander of the Second Brigade on 2 July evening and 3 July is the colonel of the 1st Sharpshooters. The 1st Regiment left Ward's brigade for de Trobriand's in September 1863 (Stevens p. 354), after the battle.
3. **First Brigade:** Graham (wounded and captured on 2 July), Tippin of the 68th (0072, 0243), Madill of the 141st from 3 July (0073).
4. **Regiments with companies absent** (the organization table prints the figure after the designation): the 57th Pennsylvania eight companies, the 2d Sharpshooters eight, the 110th Pennsylvania six (lines 0016, 0050, 0062). The 1st Sharpshooters had all ten companies (Stevens pp. 302, 308: A, B, C, G, H, K deployed in the morning, D, E, F, I at Pitzer's Run); Captain Baker's detachment with Companies C, I, K of 3 July is carried as a conflict set (Fifth Corps in Trepp's report and Stevens, Sixth Corps in Berdan's, 0340, 0341).
5. **Units the plan lacks, kept as lines with `unit_id: null`** (not applied; parent in the line): the Third Army Corps staff, the First and Second Brigade staffs (casualty rows 0115, 0096, 0106); the two Sharpshooter regiments together (Berdan's "about 450 rifles", 14,400 rounds and 89 lost, 0149, 0335, 0334); the 1st Sharpshooters' Company D (a private's "95 rounds" a man, 0337).
6. **Men joined to the 40th New York before the battle:** two companies of the 37th New York (238 men, 29 May) and the three-years men of the 38th New York (387, 3 June 1863), Floyd p. 197 (lines 0415, 0416). The plan has no 37th or 38th New York unit; the 40th's own strength at Gettysburg is not in the record.
7. **The 2d Sharpshooters' colonel** (Francis Peteler) was in Washington during the battle (Stevens p. 345, line 0351), so Major Stoughton commanded; the plan's commander cell for the 2d should read Stoughton (0051) with Peteler absent.
8. **Persons the plan lacks but the record names with a unit:** the field and company officers of the regiments are in the file as `officer` lines (116 lines), among them the officers killed and mortally wounded in the Official Records' lists (pp. 188-192; the 124th New York's four are read from the scan, whose OCR prints "134th").

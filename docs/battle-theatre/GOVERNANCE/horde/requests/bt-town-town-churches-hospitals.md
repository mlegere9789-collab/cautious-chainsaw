## 2. Other church facts for the schedule

- Souder (1864, pp. 61-62): "The sick have been removed from several of the churches, and the process of purifying is going on very energetically" (no church named, no date in the quote).
- The German Reformed and the United Presbyterian churches stood and held services (June, October to December 1863); **no source says either served as a hospital** (search rows in the evidence file).
- Sharpshooters in a steeple: rows of `bld-town-belfry` (1, 2 and 3 July); the church is not identified (EG-01).

## 3. What is not claimed

No end date of any church hospital is claimed (EG-03); no count of patients is claimed beyond the sources' own (300; 40 and 100).

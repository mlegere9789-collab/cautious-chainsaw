# bt-oob-kit / us-2c-1d: requests (catalog, library, roster)

Written 2026-11-13 by workstream bt-oob-kit for the integrator and the roster and library owners; rewritten 2026-11-15 after the second pass. Nothing in this note changes a file outside the workstream's ownership; it lists what the evidence file `kit/evidence/us-2c-1d.jsonl` could not express, and what the dry run of `kit_overrides.py compile --dry-run --group us-2c-1d` (25 operations, 51 lines not applied: 25 routed to the verified tables by design, 26 for review) leaves to the integrator.

## Catalog (kit_items.py, kit_weapons_data.py: integrator)

Placeholder ids that the compile bars from a documented line ("cannot be assigned ... by rule"), each with the lines that carry them:
1. `smoothbore_69_musket_unspecified` (a .69 smooth-bore musket of unnamed model): the 116th Pennsylvania (lines 0329-0330, Mulholland: ball of calibre .69 and three buckshot, 2 July) and the 69th and 88th New York (lines 0419-0420, New York at Gettysburg vol. 1 p. 51: "the same old buck-and-ball muskets, calibre 69"). The first pass's 63d New York line (0238) uses `m1842_smoothbore` because its report says "smooth-bore Springfield muskets, caliber .69"; the three New York lines of the Irish Brigade differ only in that the volume names no model. ## Roster (history/units.json owner)

1. Add the 6th New York Cavalry, Companies D and K (Capt. Riley Johnson), escort of Second Corps headquarters (organization table OR27-1-R009 p. 157; roster lines us-2c-1d:0254 and 0418, commander 0255; casualty row 0119). Hall's history of the regiment says the two companies "served with the Second Corps until July, 1863, and in the Department of Washington until October, 1863" (line 0418). Not in the plan's group.
2. `us-hancock-acting` is described as "II Corps commander acting for Meade": the record says Hancock was assigned to command the First, Third and Eleventh Corps (the table: all the troops on the field) from about 3 p.m. to 5-6 p.m. on 1 July, with orders to decide whether to fight at Gettysburg (OR27-1-R076 pp. 367-368; lines 0069, 0070).
3. The corps commanders in the roster are "Hancock (w); Gibbon; Hays": the record adds Caldwell (from the evening of 3 July, lines 0067 and 0265) and distinguishes William Hays (corps) from Alexander Hays (Third Division); Hancock took the corps back on the morning of the 2d and Gibbon had it again from the afternoon (lines 0064-0066).
4. The Third Brigade's commanders after Zook: Fraser (140th Pennsylvania) and Chapman (57th New York), in an order the record does not state (lines 0076, 0077, 0349).
5. Strengths and casualties of the roster: the corps' 11,216 (Stone Sentinels via the PDF) against about 10,000 in the action (Hancock; Walker "fewer than ten thousand"); casualties 4,358 against 4,369 (return), 4,350 (Walker) and 4,323 (Hancock); the First Division 1,275 (return) against Walker's 1,269.
6. The roster lists the 140th Pennsylvania's field and staff and company officers with their fates at Gettysburg (lines 0350-0376, from the Pennsylvania Memorial Commission's roster printed by Stewart, 1912, pp. 133-143).

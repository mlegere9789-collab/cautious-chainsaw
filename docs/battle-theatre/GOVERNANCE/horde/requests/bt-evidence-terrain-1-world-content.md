## ROUND 3 at a glance (2026-11-16): what differs from the note of 2026-11-13

1. **G-02 is reversed.** The 2026-11-13 request said the guns are NOT behind the E-01 rail work. That is withdrawn: Robinson (a battery directly in his rear, the loose rails 40 yd down the slope in front of it), the address at the dedication of Cooper's monument (Pennsylvania at Gettysburg II p.909: 'the rail barricade in front of us', 'our works'), the NPS Cultural Landscape Report (printed p.40) and Bates (1875) put the batteries at or behind the rails. Show the batteries behind the E-01 work, keep E-01 an infantry work; Breck's two sections stay at the pre-existing stone wall.
2. **G-10: McGilvery's order to the batteries is 250 yd, not 350.** The Cornell OCR text read 350; the printed page (read from its image) and two other scans read 250. See also the separate request note to the fence-2 owner (S-039, DD-F2-04).
3. **G-04: the 'about 20' is documented.** Cooper's battery address gives the guns of each of the five batteries (Brown 4, Carpenter 4, Dement 4, Raine 2 + 2, Graham 4 = 20); Andrews' 16 plus Graham's four. The Union batteries that answered are 19 guns, not the PDF's 37.
4. **G-12: canister states.** Single canister from about 400 yd, double and treble from 200 yd, Cushing killed when the enemy was within about 100 yd (Fuger 1907, through the NPS study): the PDF's 'double canister at about 100 yd' joins two of them.
5. **G-13 / G-14: 120 is a Confederate count** (Newton's report, four books and papers); Meade's own dispatch says about 150; more Confederate counts: 138 (New York 1900), 137 (Pennsylvania II p.633), about 115 (an 1863 Richmond account).
6. **G-16:** an 1863 Richmond narrative has a Whitworth open the cannonade (no place, not called the signal); a 3-inch pair in Hurt's battery is plausible but stood with Johnson's battery.
7. **G-09:** the 'small brick house' is still unidentified; Codori's and Sherfy's are the two documented brick candidates.

Rule for every change below: the PDF value is never deleted: it stays a selectable scenario marked with its tier, and a value no source supports is marked unsupported (Law cl.6, cl.7). Structures that pre-existed are never given a construction animation (PDF B.7.3).

Corrected claim: Reynolds fell as the attack began: about 10.15 a.m. (Doubleday, Wadsworth), about 10 (Beveridge 1891), 11.15 (Howard's 5 p.m. dispatch) with the news reaching Howard about 11.30. Weld: struck in the back of the neck by a volley while placing the Wisconsin brigade, killed almost instantly; Bachelder: killed in the grove, shot through the head at almost the first volley; Indiana (1913): at the first volley of Archer's brigade; Heth: one of the first shells fired by Pegram mortally wounded him. Bates (1875): as the leading regiment (the 2nd Wisconsin) approached the wood Reynolds joined the charge, turned to look for his supports and was struck in the brain, the woods being full of sharp-shooters; the New York commission narrative (1900) has him leading forward the 19th Indiana when he fell, about 10:15. The default under the recorded delegated decision C-040 is the window 10:15 to 11:15; the PDF's 10:30 and 10:40-10:50 stay selectable.

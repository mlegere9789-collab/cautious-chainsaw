| Description | Rows T1-086, 091, 092, 093, 111. Not in the OR: a Seminary cupola observation, a Buford-Reynolds cupola meeting, Jerome sighting I Corps ~10:00, a Reynolds message to Meade promising to hold. OR does have Norton p.201 (station first on the college), Buford p.930 (Jerome praised) and Meade's Jul 1 circular to Reynolds (hold in check). Jerome's 1865 account was searched and not found (L7). Jacobs 1864 puts a signal officer on the College cupola ~8:30 (summarizer read). Naming of the building (Pennsylvania Hall, College cupola vs Seminary cupola) is unresolved. CLOSE-OUT UPDATE 2026-11-13: Cupola: Bachelder (1873, 1889), the New York commission volume 1, Battles and Leaders caption, Toombs (1888) and Pennsylvania at Gettysburg (1914) support Buford in the seminary cupola as later narrative; Jerome with Buford is documented (Buford p.930, Brown 1896); Jerome's own 1865 account is still not found. Reynolds's message to Meade is documented by the Weld diary (delivered 11.20, left about 10:00). Detail: tl-02-CO-T1-086, -091, -092, -093, -111. SECOND-READING UPDATE 2026-11-15: Jerome's own letter of 18 Oct 1865 to Maj. Gen. Hancock was found, printed in a 1989 U.S. Army War College study (DTIC ADA207999, a U.S. Government work): Jerome took his station in the steeple of the Theological Seminary "as early as seven A.M.", Buford came up and looked at the enemy through his glass, Jerome called his attention to an army corps advancing about two miles off and distinguished it as the First by its corps flag, and Reynolds, half an hour later, rode up and hailed Buford in the steeple. The study also names Pennsylvania Hall as the college building whose cupola held a Union signal station. Rows T1-086, T1-092, T1-093 and T1-111 rest on it (documented as Jerome's claim, second-hand print). STILL OPEN: the clock hour of the sighting and of Reynolds' arrival (inferred about 09:30 if he met Buford at about ten); the ladder from the attic to the cupola (found only in a book of 2006 that is not public domain: a lead); the original of the letter (Bachelder Papers, U.S. Army Military History Institute; owner-only step: a request to that institute); Weld's field meeting as a rival scene; Jacobs's presence in the cupola and the Confederate use that evening. Detail: tl-02-CO-T1-092, -093, -111, -086. |

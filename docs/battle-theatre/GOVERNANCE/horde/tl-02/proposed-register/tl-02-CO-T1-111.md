| Description | Row T1-111 (Jul 1 (time n/s)). Verdict corrected. Corrected claim: Norton (signal officer): a station of observation was established on Jul 1, first on the college and then on the court-house. Jacobs (1864): at about 8:30 a.m. one of the General's (Howard's) signal officers was on the College cupola making observations and was directed by a College officer to Cemetery Hill; Howard's own report has him on a high building in the town only shortly before the news of Reynolds' death at about 11.30 a.m. (OR p.701-702), so the 8:30 hour is Jacobs's claim and no source confirms an observer of Howard's party at that hour. Battles and Leaders: the cupola was first used by Union officers and then by Confederate as a station for observation and signals. A 1989 U.S. Army War College study names the building: the cupola of Pennsylvania Hall at Gettysburg College (a plaque on the hall marks a Union signal station), where Jerome's station was moved from the Lutheran Seminary cupola, and on 1 July to the courthouse; the study names no source for those dates. Jacobs's own presence with Union officers in the cupola and the Confederate use on the evening of Jul 1 are not stated by any source read. Still unsupported or open: Prof. Jacobs with Union officers in the cupola; Confederate control on the evening of Jul 1 / Prof. Jacobs in the cupola; the hour at which the cupola passed to the Confederates (Jacobs: Lee used it on Jul 2); a source for the dates of Jerome's changes of station. |

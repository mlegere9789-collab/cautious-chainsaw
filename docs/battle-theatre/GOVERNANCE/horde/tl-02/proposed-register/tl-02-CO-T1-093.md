| Description | Row T1-093 (Jul 1 ≈10:00). Verdict corrected. Corrected claim: The observer was Jerome himself: in his letter of 18 October 1865 (read as printed in a 1989 U.S. Army War College study; original not seen) he says that, looking toward Emmitsburg from the steeple of the Theological Seminary, he called Buford's attention to an army corps advancing some two miles off and distinguished it as the First by its corps flag; Buford held on for half an hour, unaided, until Reynolds rode up. The New York commission history (1900) has the signal officer in the belfry see the First Corps flag on the Emmitsburg road after the fight had raged for over an hour, without naming him. That Jerome served with Buford at Gettysburg is documented (Buford; Brown 1896 roster). No source gives a clock time for the sighting; if Reynolds met Buford at about ten (Weld via Meade 1913) the sighting was about half an hour earlier, about 09:30 (inferred). Still unsupported or open: The hour about 10:00 as the clock time of the sighting / A clock time for the sighting (the row's about 10:00 is not stated by any source; about 09:30 is inferred from two relative statements). |

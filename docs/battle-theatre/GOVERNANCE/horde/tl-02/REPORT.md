- provenance of the final files: the search phase ran once, under the machine lock, in tmp/tl-02/build2.py --write (log tmp/tl-02/write.log; 135 searches). Every citation is re-matched in the local text at every write.
- audit of 2026-11-13/14 (page numbers): the printed pages of the Official Records come from the scan leaf of each citation (archive.org page index aligned to the OCR text, tools/bt/kit_or27_leafmap.py), not from the running-head model of the OCR text (tools/bt/or27_pages.py), which loses its heads on tabular pages and interpolates. The printed page of all 64 leaves cited was read on the page image (222 citations; horde/tl-02 citations carry `scan` with leaf and PDF page; list in horde/tl-02/closeout/audit/viewed_leaves.json).

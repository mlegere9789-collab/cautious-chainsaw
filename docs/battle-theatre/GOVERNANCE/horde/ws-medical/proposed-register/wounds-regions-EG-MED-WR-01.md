# EG-MED-WR-01: Shares of wounds of the head, face, neck and spine among all wounds are not printed

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | EG-MED-WR-01 |
| Requirement IDs | OA12 (accurate field hospitals, battle trauma and period-accurate treatment); PDF B.8.5; gate Q26; Law clauses 3, 4 and 7 |
| Category | evidence missing (to be read) |
| Description | The first surgical volume of the Medical and Surgical History says in its introduction that generalizations on the relative frequency of wounds according to regions would be premature, and prints no table of the share of wounds of the head, face, neck or spine among all wounded. It prints counts of each class (head injuries from all causes, face injuries, gunshot wounds of the neck, gunshot injuries of the vertebrae) and shares for the chest (about one in twelve; 7,173 of 105,540 in the last year), the abdomen (3.3 per centum) and the flesh wounds of the back (four to six per centum). |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | The record the project holds does not state these shares (Law cl.3); the Provost Marshal General's statistical volume and the later general chapter of the History are not local. |
| Evidence searched | MSHWR-P1V2-1870 and its second scan (introduction, Tables VII, XIV, XVII, XIX, XXIV, XXV), MSHWR-P2V2-1876 and 1877 (abdomen, pelvis, back), MSHWR-P3V2-1883 (opening of Chapter X), Gross 1862, Tripler and Blackman 1862, Chisolm 1864. |
| Autonomous substitute applied | Rows med.wounds-regions.279 to 286 compute, from the History's own counts and its register of 253,142 cases, a share for each region and mark it as computed (tier inferred); the head, face, neck, spine and pelvis shares rest on counts of injuries from all causes. |
| Strongest evidence-based reconstruction | The printed shares for the chest, the abdomen and the back as selectable readings; the computed shares for the other regions marked as computed from counts of different volumes; the sampling table of the scenes borrows the Army-wide shares and says so. |
| Confidence tier | documented (printed shares); inferred (computed shares); open gap (the printed share of head, face, neck, spine) |
| Competing values | Chest: about one in twelve (all lists) against a little less than one in fifteen (the last year, field returns) against one in twelve in the Crimean sieges (Scrive); abdomen: 6.6 (Serrier), 3.8, 4.28, 3.3 per centum and the History's own range of three to four per cent. |
| Product marking | The scene or card carries the tier mark and, for an open gap, the gap marker with this id. |
| Visual and audio impact | the sampling table of the patients of every ward scene and the hospital card |

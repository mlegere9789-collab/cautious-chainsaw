# EG-MED-WR-01: The killed on the battle-field by region: the seat of injury is known for 1,173 of not less than 44,238

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | EG-MED-WR-01 |
| Requirement IDs | OA12 (accurate field hospitals, battle trauma and period-accurate treatment); PDF B.8.5; gate Q26; Law clauses 3, 4 and 7 |
| Category | evidence missing (to be read) |
| Description | Table CXIX of the third surgical volume divides the 245,790 shot wounds that came under treatment by region; the killed are not in it. For the killed the History records the seat of injury in 1,173 cases only, divided into the head and neck (487), the trunk (603), the upper extremities (30) and the lower extremities (53) (row 327), and applies those proportions to not less than 44,238 killed (row 328; gap row 273). The killed are not divided into face, neck, chest, abdomen, spine, pelvis or back. For the abdomen alone the 1876 volume estimates, on fragmentary data, about a tenth of those slain in battle (row 163). |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | The record held does not state it (Law cl.3). The casualty lists and the reports of medical directors that the History cites as the Appendix to Part I are not local. |
| Evidence searched | Chapter XII of the third surgical volume, pages 685 to 693, read in both issues and, for Tables CXVI, CXVII, CXIX and CXX, in the page images of the second issue; the passage of the introduction of the first surgical volume on the memoranda and on the postponement of general observations; the abdomen chapter of the second surgical volume on the slain. |
| Autonomous substitute applied | None for the finer regions. The History's own four-way division of the 1,173 cases and its application to 44,238 killed are rows; the 1876 estimate for the abdomen is a row. |
| Strongest evidence-based reconstruction | The four-way division as the only documented basis for the wounds of the dead on a field, marked as a sample of 1,173 cases (about 2.7 per cent. of 44,238 by the writer's quotient); the finer seats of the dead drawn only as inferred. |
| Confidence tier | documented (the History's division, its application and the 1876 estimate for the abdomen); open gap (the finer regions) |
| Competing values | Head and neck 487 of the 1,173 recorded cases; 18,367 of 44,238 by the History's application; 15.44 per cent. of the injuries of the wounded and killed together (row 329); the abdomen about a tenth of the slain (1876). |
| Product marking | The scene or card carries the tier mark and, for an open gap, the gap marker with this id. |
| Visual and audio impact | the wounds shown on the dead of a field, the burial parties and the dead house |

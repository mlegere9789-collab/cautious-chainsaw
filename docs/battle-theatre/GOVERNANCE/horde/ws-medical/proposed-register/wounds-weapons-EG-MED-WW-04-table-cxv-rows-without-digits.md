| Description | Table CXV (printed page 686, 922 sabre and bayonet wounds by seat of injury) is garbled in the OCR of the first issue and absent from the second-issue text. Fifteen of its seventeen rows and the aggregates were quoted with corrections read from the page image (914 of 922 cases; 392 of 400 bayonet). The first pass left the rows "Bayonet Wounds of the Knee Joint" and "Bayonet Wounds of the Metatarsals" as a gap on the belief that the OCR carries no digits for them. The second pass found that (1) the OCR carries the labels and stray characters for both rows and a 1 for the metatarsals; (2) the text of the History states both counts in words: "Seven were caused by bayonets" among the twelve punctured wounds of the knee joint (printed page 360, row med.wounds-weapons.294) and "A solitary case of bayonet injury of the metatarsal bones" among the 5,860 wounds of the bones of the foot by weapons of war (page 617, row .295); (3) the cells of both rows were read from the page image again (PDF page 772, a 220 dpi rendering; the PDF holds the page at 400 dpi): knee joint 7 cases, bayonet, 7 recoveries; metatarsals 1 case, bayonet, 1 recovery. The table is now complete: the knee row is row .266 (the id that held the gap; it is now a documented table row), the metatarsal row is .296, and the sum is row .297 (914 + 7 + 1 = 922 cases; 392 + 7 + 1 = 400 bayonet). |

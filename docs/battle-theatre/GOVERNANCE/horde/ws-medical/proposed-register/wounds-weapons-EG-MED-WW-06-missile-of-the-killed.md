# EG-MED-WW-06: The missile of the soldiers killed on a field, and so the share of artillery wounds fatal on the field, is not printed

| Description | The Surgical History states that its records show the seat of injury in only 1,173 soldiers killed on the battle-field and gives no missile for them; the share fatal among the wounded by large projectiles is printed only for evulsions of the lower limbs (62 of 164, 40 per cent.). The dead of a field are therefore not weighted by weapon in this work, and no share of artillery wounds fatal on the field is stated. |

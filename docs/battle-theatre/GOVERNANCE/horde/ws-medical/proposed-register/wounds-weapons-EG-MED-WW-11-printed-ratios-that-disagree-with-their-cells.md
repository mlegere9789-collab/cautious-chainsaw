| Requirement IDs | OA12 (accurate field hospitals, battle trauma and period-accurate treatment); PDF B.8.5; gate Q26; Law clauses 3, 4 and 7 |
| Category | source discrepancy carried as printed |
| Description | Three printed figures do not follow from the cells beside them, in the page images as well as in the OCR: (1) Table CXV, the row for the face, prints 43.4 for the bayonet mortality where one fatal of the 23 bayonet cases with a result is 4.3 (row med.wounds-weapons.187); (2) Table CXXII, the row for the leg and ankle, prints 17.5 per cent. of fatality where 29 fatal of 194 cases is 14.9, while the cells of the row and the aggregates (155 of 816, 19.0 per cent.) agree with each other (row .345); (3) the text under Table V of 1870 prints 72.6 per cent. of the named scalp missiles as small arms where the table's own items give 77.9 per cent. (rows .176, .267). The counts are consistent in each case; the printed ratio is the discrepancy. |

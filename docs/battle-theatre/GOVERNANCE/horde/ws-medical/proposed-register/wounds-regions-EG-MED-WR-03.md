# EG-MED-WR-03: The rule and the place for the mortally wounded at Gettysburg are not stated in the local texts

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | EG-MED-WR-03 |
| Requirement IDs | OA12 (accurate field hospitals, battle trauma and period-accurate treatment); PDF B.8.5; gate Q26; Law clauses 3, 4 and 7 |
| Category | evidence missing (to be read) |
| Description | No local text found says where the men judged mortally wounded were laid at Gettysburg, who sat with them and what they were given (gap row 275). The History says only that men with penetrating wounds of the abdomen should be treated as near as practicable to the spot where they fall; Gross (1862) says the dying should be screened from their neighbors, placed in the easiest posture and not disturbed; the History says of the head that so many with fractures and penetrating wounds were left dead on the field that the scalp wounds are the more numerous among the cases treated (row 008), and of the spine that many who suffered such injuries must have rested on the field (row 106). |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | The record held does not state it (Law cl.3). |

The hits are single remarks about single men, not a rule. |

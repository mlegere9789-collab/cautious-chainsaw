# EG-MED-WW-08: The named Gettysburg cases of the evidence file are reports that reached the Surgeon General's Office, museum specimens and statements of officers, not a count or a sample

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | EG-MED-WW-08 |
| Requirement IDs | OA12 (accurate field hospitals, battle trauma and period-accurate treatment); PDF B.8.5; gate Q26; Law clauses 3, 4 and 7 |
| Category | evidence present, limited in what it supports |
| Description | About 25 named Gettysburg cases and specimens (rows gc.*) show which missile or weapon wounded a man on 1, 2 or 3 July, with the unit and hospital as printed. The second pass added three Gettysburg cases of blows from muskets (rows med.wounds-weapons.321 to .323: two men of the 19th Massachusetts and the 59th New York hurt in action on 2 July, one of the 9th Massachusetts struck by a fellow soldier's musket while sleeping after the battle) and six statements of the missile that struck named officers or a few men in the Official Records (rows .326 to .331). The Army Medical Museum catalogue names Gettysburg in about 320 places and the Surgical History in hundreds of cases; only the cases that show one missile class each (solid shot, grape, canister, shell, round ball, conoidal ball, buckshot, sabre, bayonet, a sword blow and the musket) are rows. Counting them by class would measure what was reported and kept, not what was inflicted. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | The record does not state it, or the text is not available to this project (Law cl.3); nothing is invented to fill it. |
| Evidence searched | The Surgical History (all three volumes, Gettysburg within 450 characters of a missile word), the Army Medical Museum catalogue (Gettysburg with canister, grape, solid shot, shell, sabre, bayonet), the Official Records vol. 27 part 1 (wounded by shell, artillery, canister, grape, musket-ball). |

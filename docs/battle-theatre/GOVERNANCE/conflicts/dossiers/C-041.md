## 7. Reading hints and next steps

- Artillery, first contact and the infantry assault are three moments; the Confederate clock runs about 20 minutes apart from the Union one (PDF B.2.1).
- Weigh by the rubric of PLAN.md section 5: who wrote it, when, where the writer stood, how he knew, which clock; a quote is never stretched beyond what it says.
- Every value of the PDF row and every scenario of the first delegated default stays selectable (positions `scenario_ids`); say whether the PDF default and the first delegated default hold (`holds`).
- When the sources cannot settle the point, list the searches that failed (`failed_searches`): class gap or irreducible; never fill a gap in.
- List the timeline rows, gates and app data that carry the value in `readers` (do not change them: the integration step does).

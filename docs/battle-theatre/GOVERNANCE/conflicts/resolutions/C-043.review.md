# Review of C-043 (Union retreat order, 1 July): DEFECTS (minor; substance sound)

Reviewer: independent, 2026-11-24.

## Verified
Wadsworth p. 266: "Finding myself outflanked on both right and left, heavily pressed in front, and my ammunition nearly exhausted, at 3.45 o'clock I ordered the command to retire" (an order to his division; Doubleday prints it as "General Wadsworth says", p. 251, so one group); Howard p. 704: "About 4 p. m. I sent word to General Doubleday that, if he could not hold out longer, he must fall back", "At 4.10 p. m. ... I sent a positive order to the commanders of the First and Eleventh Corps to fall back gradually", "At 4.30 p. m. the columns reached Cemetery Hill" (same text reprinted in Battles and Leaders, offset 1238513); Doubleday p. 250 "About 4 p. m. the enemy ... advanced", "I now gave orders to fall back" (no hour); Robinson p. 290 "from about noon until nearly 5 p. m., when I received orders to withdraw. These orders not being received until all other troops (except Stewart's battery) had commenced moving to the rear"; McFarland p. 328 "perhaps 20 paces from the seminary ... At this instant, 4.30 p. m., I was hit". The reading that the plan's four hours are different orders or receipts, not rivals, is supported by the text. Class weighted, tier documented, single-source flags on both window ends: right. No clock shift applied.

## Defects and exact fixes
1. **Context citations flagged exact.** P1 cites Wadsworth p. 265 ("at 8 a. m., the division moved from Marsh Creek") `exact: true`; P4 cites Robinson p. 289 ("the division marched from Emmitsburg") `exact: true`; P7 cites Howard p. 701 ("At 3.30 a. m. July 1, orders were received ... to move the Eleventh Corps") `exact: true`. None states its position's value. Set `exact: false` with `note: "context"` or delete.
2. **One tension is understated.** Robinson says his order came "nearly 5 p. m." after "all other troops ... had commenced moving", while Howard puts the columns on Cemetery Hill at 4.30. Taken at face value these differ by 25 to 30 minutes; the resolution explains it as the last division's receipt. Add one sentence to the md Weighing and `weighing.summary`: "Robinson's 'nearly 5' and Howard's 4.30 cannot both be exact clock readings of the same movement; 'nearly 5' is a round figure and the clocks of the two corps are not shown to agree, so the receipt hour is kept as a separate, unreconciled value and does not enter the window."
3. `weighing.independence` says the Howard report is "reprinted in Battles and Leaders" (true, BL-3 offset 1238513) but no citation records it; add it to P7 as a non-independent citation (same group `howard`) or drop the remark.

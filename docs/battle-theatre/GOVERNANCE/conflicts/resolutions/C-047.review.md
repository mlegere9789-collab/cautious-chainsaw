## Defects and exact fixes
1. **P14 (Meade, "a little over 100,000") is shown as an exact count.** The quote is a hedge. Set `hedge` to `over`, `value` to `{"n": 100000, "approx": true, "note": "a lower bound: 'a little over'"}`, `exact: false`, and keep tier documented only as "Meade states an aggregate a little over 100,000 on the latest return" (the value is not a count of 100,000). Say in the label "over 100,000 (lower bound)". Also correct the md/weighing wording "latest return before 28 June": Meade writes that the most recent return showed the aggregate while the army lay around Frederick; no date is stated.
2. **P17 (council memorandum) glosses what the text does not say.** The pencil memorandum (OR27-1 p. 74: "Effective strength about 9,000, 13,500, 9,000, 6,000, 8,500, 6,000, 7,000 ; total, 58,000", with the indorsement "Minutes of council, held Thursday p. m., July 2. - M. G., C. of S.") names no corps and does not say "after the loss of the first two days". Fix: delete "after two days' loss" in the md, in P17's label and reason text, and the clause "seven corps" or mark them "inferred: seven figures". Disclose that the seven printed figures add to 59,000, not the printed 58,000 (a discrepancy in the original), and set `written.kind` to `reference_table` rather than `private_letter_or_diary` (it is an unsigned pencil memorandum found among Meade's papers, indorsed by the chief of staff; the footnote in the print says so). Group and flags are fine.
3. **Independence is counted inconsistently.** `weighing.independence` says Early "reasons from" the return, which is Taylor's line of knowledge, but P21 has `group: early` while P19, P20, P22 use `lee-return-may31`. Either give P21 `group: lee-return-may31` or write in `weighing.independence` that Early read the return independently of Taylor (he does say he used the table "before referred to"), and then do not call it one line.
4. **`default.range` low 20,451 high 100,000 is meaningless.** It mixes a Confederate casualty total with a Union aggregate. `unresolved_set` should not carry one numeric range. Replace with four per-measure ranges if the schema allows (Union strength 83,289 to over 100,000; Union casualties 23,049; Confederate strength 62,000 to 75,992; Confederate casualties 20,451 to 28,063) or drop `default.range` and keep the explanatory `basis_text`; if the validator demands the key, set `basis` to say "not displayed; four measures, see basis_text" and make the viewer ignore it.
5. **Livermore 28,070 versus 28,063.** Livermore's own footnote (p. 103, "Add losses at Gettysburg as above 28,070", total engaged 77,707) prints 28,070, while his table and Alexander give 28,063 (77,707 = 49,637 + 28,070). P24 cites the 28,070 line without remark. Add to P24's inference: "Livermore prints 28,070 in the note and 28,063 in the table; 77,707 uses 28,070." 
6. **md wording.** "The Comte de Paris ... about 75,000, or 81,000 without the deduction" should read "81,000 if stragglers are not deducted (both figures exclude the cavalry)": the 81,000 still leaves out the cavalries.
7. **Minor.** P36 and P37 cite Allan for Lee's own words (relayed, 1877; flags memoir_only are right). P23 (Oates) correctly marked weak. P18 label "Park Service seminar paper" is accurate to the ledger (NPS Gettysburg seminar, 2010).

# Independent review of C-026 (Sherfy barn fire)

Verdict: DEFECTS (3). Class irreducible and tier inferred follow the rubric; the window default is honest about the lack of an hour. The lower bound is overstated and the readers list is short.

1 Validator: exit 0, errors 0, warnings 0. Panel text 344 characters, accurate.

2 Quotes read in context
- Compiler of 20 July 1863: the list "suffered in the destruction of their bouses and barnB(by fire, while the battle was raging ... Joseph SheTfy. In the barn of the latter, and around the hay stacks, were about twenty wounded Philadelphia Zouaves, who perished in the conflagration". The damaged letters are quoted correctly with the gaps marked. The paper says "Philadelphia Zouaves"; the identification with the 114th Pennsylvania is the researcher's.
- Hart (OR27-1 p. 888) and McMahon (p. 889): correct. Hart's enemy battery "in front of a barn" is unnamed. Hinman/Moran p. 296: "Sherfy's barn had taken fire during the cannonade and was burned to the ground ... wounded men ... who had crept into it ... while the battle was still on".

3 Defects
D1 (moderate, lower bound overstated). The lower bound "after the Jul 2 afternoon fight" (label, `default.label`, `.md` "from the afternoon fight") rests on the wounded Zouaves being in the barn plus Birney's "held the peach orchard until nearly dusk" (OR27-1 p. 483). Birney says nothing about the barn. Moran's gunner says wounded men crept into the barn "while the battle was still on" and the barn "had taken fire during the cannonade": the fire could come during the 2 July fight, as soon as wounded were inside (late afternoon), not only after it. Supported bound: "not before wounded 114th Pennsylvania men were in it", not "after the fight". The text should drop Birney as support for the bound and say the opening is during or after the fight.
D2 (moderate, readers incomplete). Register entries `EG-0269` (Sherfy barn fire timing, T2-176, T2-177), `EG-0347` (Sherfy barn fire date and farm losses), `EG-FX-G08` (fire timing inside date-only windows, burn durations), `EG-FX-G02` and `EG-0008` (contradictions among PDF defaults) are not listed; nor is `decision_log.md` line 54 (DEC-C-026) or DEC-005 line 11, which names C-026 as OPEN.
D3 (minor). `P2` and `P3` both rest on Hart's "set the barn on fire" for a barn that Hart does not name; P2's inference says "Together they place a fire of some barn on 3 Jul", then P3 is flagged `different_event`. Hart's own 2 July report also mentions "the barn" (p. 887); the same reading would have had a barn on 2 July. The resolution does not weigh that. Minor.

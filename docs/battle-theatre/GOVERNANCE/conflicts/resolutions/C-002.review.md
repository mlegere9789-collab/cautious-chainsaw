## D1 (major): three more reports of 1863 from Humphreys's own division give the hours in dispute, including the plan's 02:00

All in OR27-1, found with `find_passage.py OR27-1 --regex "(arrived|reached|bivouac)[^.]{0,120}(midnight|[12] a\. ?m\.)"`:

- Lt. Col. Porter D. Tripp, 11th Massachusetts (Carr's First Brigade, same division as Humphreys's report p.535 lists it), report of 27 Jul 1863, record OR27-1-R162, p.549, offset 1922457: "marched through Emmitsburg to Gettysburg, Pa., where we arrived at 2 a. m. on the 2d instant".
- Maj. Robert L. Bodine, 26th Pennsylvania (same brigade), report of 28 Jul 1863, OR27-1-R167, p.556, offset 1948158: "Marched, via Emmitsburg, to Gettysburg, distance 14 miles, where we arrived at 2 a. m. of July 2".
- Col. William R. Brewster, 73rd New York, commanding the Second (Excelsior) Brigade of the same division, report of 15 Aug 1863, OR27-1-R169, p.558, offset 1955385: "Marched to a point near Gettysburg, passing through Emmitsburg, reaching there about midnight" (the antecedent of "there" is not certain; "the point near Gettysburg" is the reading that fits the march, since Burling says the division reached Emmitsburg at noon; the researcher must state the reading as a reading, in `inference.step`, or ask for the page image).

So the plan's 02:00 has two Official Records reports behind it (regimental commanders written within a month), the plan's 00:00 has a brigade commander's report of 1863 (in addition to Humphreys's letter and Bachelder), and Humphreys's own report is 01:00. These are three different brigades or regiments of one division, differing by an hour or two, each a unit's own arrival. The resolution states the opposite in six places:

- `C-002.md`: "02:00. No report, letter or book found gives it. The reports of Carr, Brewster and Birney give no hour for the night." and "Nothing supports 02:00." False for Tripp, Bodine and Brewster.
- `C-002.json`: P2 and P3 (02:00) are `unsourced`, `lead_only`, tier `open gap` / `plausible-procedural`, reason `no_source`, with failed lookups saying the library was searched for 02:00 and "0 hits"; `weighing.summary` ("Nothing gives 02:00"); `source_panel_text` ("no source found gives the plan's 02:00"); `holds.pdf_default.note`; `failed_searches` (Brewster "gives no hour").
- P1 (midnight) is tier `inferred`, flags `memoir_only`, reason `later_memory`: Brewster's report of 1863 is a contemporaneous official report and removes the memoir-only status.

## D2: the default's animation note states as fact what the weighing leaves open

`default.animation.note` says "the head of the column reached the ground before it (his letter of 1878 says 'towards midnight')". `weighing.summary` and the `.md` say that whether the letter means the head of the column "is not stated ... left as a possibility". Write "the head may have reached the ground earlier (his letter of 1878 says 'towards midnight')", or, after D1, replace with the Brewster reading.

## D3 (minor): grouping of the later books

P4 puts Bachelder 1878, the New York volume (1900) and the Life and Letters (1913) in group `humphreys-report` "because they name no source". That is an assumption, not a finding (nothing shows they copied the report; Bachelder in 1873 said midnight). It is conservative (it keeps the `single_source` flag), but say "treated as dependent; not shown" in `weighing.independence`.

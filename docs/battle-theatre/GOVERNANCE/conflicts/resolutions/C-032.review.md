# Review of C-032 (East Cavalry Field): DEFECTS

Reviewer: independent, 2026-11-24. Validator passes. Quotes read in context; Green's digit checked against both local scans.

## What is sound
- Quotes of McIntosh, Gregg, Pleasonton, Rawle (1878, 1884), Storrs (via Kidd), Custer (via Kidd and the Fifth Michigan entry) are real. Hedges kept ("not earlier than 2" is marked `lower`). The 15:00 and noon positions are correctly read as other events. Single-source and weak flags are mostly right. Class weighted, tier inferred are the right grade, not documented.

## Defects and exact fixes
1. **Green's "2 p. m." rests on a digit the quoted scan does not print.** The cited quote reads "commenced at 'Z p. m." (scan 1, OR27-2 p. 497); `states.times` is empty, so the machine does not attest 14:00. The digit is 2 only in the second scan's text layer (`warofrebellion0027geor_djvu.txt` line 42365: "commenced at 2 p. m. by the dismounted sharp-"), which is not cited, and no page image was viewed. Yet the panel, `weighing.summary` and `basis.text` call Green's report an exact 2 p.m. Fix: (a) add a citation of source `OR27-2-SCAN2-C005` with that sentence and `states.times ["14:00"]`, or mark scan 1 `quote_mode: "ocr"` with `raw` and `ocr_note`; (b) set `page_image_viewed` false and add the digit caveat to `source_panel_text` ("the second scan prints 2; the first scan's digit is damaged"); (c) keep the failed search "page image not fetched" (already there). Without (a) the headline claim is unsupported by a machine-checked quote.
2. **McIntosh's own report is mis-weighed.** McIntosh (official report, 32 days after) says that after moving at 1 p.m. he placed the First New Jersey, "when they became immediately engaged", and that his skirmishers "were then hotly engaged" before he sent for reinforcements. That is a contemporaneous report of engagement soon after 13:00, against Rawle's 1878 account (everything quiet until a skirmish line went out "about two"). The `.md` says "the first fire comes after it by every other account" and `P1.reason_not_chosen` says the first skirmish line "went out about two", treating McIntosh as consistent with 14:00. It is not, and an official report of the time outranks a reminiscence. Fix: (a) rewrite those two sentences to say McIntosh's report puts the engagement "immediately" after his 1 p.m. move while Green (digit caveat) and Rawle/Storrs (1878, 1889) put it at about two; (b) record this in `weighing.summary` and `weighing.independence` (the two best-class witnesses present, McIntosh and Green, differ by about an hour); (c) either reclassify as irreducible with default mode `window` 13:00-14:00, drawn from 14:00 only as the latest source-supported start, or keep weighted but state in `weighing` why Green (5 days, Confederate) is preferred over McIntosh (32 days, Union) and give the ranking of "written" (both official reports; Green nearer in time). The owner's rule (contemporary report over reminiscence) does not favour 14:00 over 13:00 on McIntosh alone.
3. **Weak supports counted under the default.** The NY-GB-1900 history (P5) and Storrs's `lower` bound state only "2 o'clock before seriously engaged" / "not earlier than 2"; they do not date the start of firing at 2. Fix: say in P5 `inference.step` that Storrs gives a lower bound, the NY history is unsourced and is not counted among the independent groups (the weighing already lists three groups: Green, Rawle, Storrs; keep NY out of that count).
4. **Rawle and Storrs are not shown independent of Kidd's text.** Kidd (1889) discusses Rawle in the same passage (the 10 a.m. versus noon paragraph). Fix: add "Storrs's statement reaches us through Kidd, who wrote knowing Rawle's pamphlet; the two may not be independent" to `weighing.independence`.
5. P3: "Pleasonton relays Gregg" (role text and independence) is an assumption, not stated by Pleasonton. Fix: write "Pleasonton may relay Gregg; he does not say".

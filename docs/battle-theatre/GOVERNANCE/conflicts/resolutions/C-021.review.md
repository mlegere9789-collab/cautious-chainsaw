# Review of C-021 (Pickett's arrival)

Verdict: DEFECTS (one substantive misidentification, one internal contradiction in the default label, one stretched quote).

Checked: validator 0 errors, 0 warnings. Quotes re-found in context (Aylett p. 999, Peyton p. 385, Dearing p. 388, Lee 1863 p. 308 and 1864 p. 320, Fremantle, Dickert, Owen 1885 p. 248, Wilcox SHSP-06 p. 117, Alexander, Hoke, Toombs, Hunt 1887, HB9 p. 29, Longstreet, Taylor). Datelines of the OR reports match. The morning-of-3-July default is carried by good witnesses (two brigade reports, the artillery battalion, Lee 1863). Two further local texts agree and could be added (BACH-REPULSE-1870 "arrived in the field on the morning of July 3d"; NY-GB-1900 "arrived on the field of Gettysburg at 9 a. m."); this is optional.

Defects and exact fixes:

1. SUBSTANTIVE. Position C-021-P9 and every place that says "Owen". Hoke (1887) prints the "soon after six o'clock" passage as the words of "Captain H. T. Owen, of the Eighteenth Virginia regiment, written for the Philadelphia Weekly Times, of March 26th, 1881" (read in context at HOKE-1887, offset about 726,900). That is a participant of Garnett's brigade, not William M. Owen of the Washington Artillery whose 1885 book is OWEN-1885-C021. The resolution treats it as "a second-hand quotation of Owen that is not in Owen's own book" and lists a failed search of OWEN-1885-C021 for it. The search is irrelevant and the characterisation is wrong. Fix: (a) P9 citation 1: `witness.name` "Henry T. Owen (printed by Jacob Hoke)", `role` "Captain, 18th Virginia, Garnett's brigade of Pickett's division", `written` 1881-03-26 `postwar_narrative` (printed by Hoke 1887), `vantage` present, `knew_by` eyewitness, `clock` unspecified, `group` "owen-ht-1881" (not "hoke-owen"); source stays HOKE-1887 as the carrier; (b) delete the failed search "OWEN-1885-C021 ... not in Owen's book" and the same statement in `weighing.how_knew` and `weighing.independence` ("Hoke and Owen overlap", "Owen compiled his book from his diary" applies only to W. M. Owen); (c) `C-021.md`, "On the field": replace "a second-hand quotation of Owen that is not in Owen's own book" with "a regimental officer of Garnett's brigade (Captain H. T. Owen, 18th Virginia, Philadelphia Weekly Times, 1881, printed by Hoke) says the division was in position soon after six; Peyton, who commanded that brigade and wrote on 9 July 1863, says it reached the field about 9 a. m."; (d) P9 `reason_not_chosen` kind `later_memory` stays but the text must say "a later reminiscence of a brigade officer (1881) against the brigade commander's report of 1863". P9 then has an eyewitness and a book (Toombs) and its flags `weak` should be re-examined (keep `memoir_only`).
2. Default label and animation contradict the record the file itself holds. `default.label` and the PDF-default position C-021-P5 say "not before dawn", yet (a) `animation.at` is 03:00 on 3 July, 90 minutes before sunrise (04:36), and (b) position C-021-P11 (documented) puts Dearing's battalion on the field about dusk on 2 July. Fix: `default.label` "On the field the morning of Jul 3; one brigade starts from four miles out at 03:00, another arrives about 09:00 (the artillery was up at dusk of Jul 2)". Remove "(not before dawn)" from `source_panel_text` if present (it is not) and from `C-021.md` "Decision" ("not before dawn"). Also state in `animation.note` that 03:00 is a start of march off the field, not a presence, and say how Q04 (floor 04:36) treats it (the reader line says "moves neither"; keep it consistent).
3. `holds.pdf_default.holds` is "yes" but its note says Dearing's guns on the field at dusk "which the clause does not allow for". Fix: "partly".
4. Lee 1864 quote, positions C-021-P4 and C-021-P6: "which arrived near the battle [...] during the afternoon of the 2d, was ordered to attack the next morning". In Lee's sentence the subject of "was ordered" is Longstreet ("Longstreet, re-enforced by Pickett's three brigades, which arrived near the battle-field during the afternoon of the 2d, was ordered to attack"). The joined quote reads as if Pickett's brigades were ordered. Fix: P6 quote to "Pickett's three brigades, which arrived near the battle [...] during the afternoon of the 2d" (offset unchanged apart from the start), and P4 quote to "Longstreet, re-enforced by Pickett's three brigades [...] was ordered to attack the next morning".
5. Minor: the weighing `who` says "Lee asked Pickett to modify his and he did not"; the source is Alexander 1907 (text offset 1131552), not a report. Add "(Alexander 1907)" so the claim is sourced; the same applies to "Garnett was killed, Kemper wounded".

# Review of C-030 (infantry step-off): DEFECTS

Reviewer: independent, 2026-11-24. Validator passes (0 errors, 1 warning). Quotes were checked against the local texts in context, not only by the machine. Class irreducible is right: Alexander, Eshleman and Peyton (Confederate, at or near the guns or in the line) put the step-off at about 13:30-14:30; Hall, Webb, Harrow and Davis put it at about 15:00; the gap is 60 to 90 minutes and no clock offset explains it.

## What is sound
- Quotes of Hall, Webb, Harrow, Peyton, Eshleman, Alexander (report and memoir), Hays, Smyth, Lang, Meade and Taylor are real and read in context correctly. Hedges "nearly 3" (Harrow) and "about" are kept.
- The 15:00 drawn instant has a source: Hall ("At 3 o'clock exactly ... his first line of battle advanced from the woods") and Webb ("At 3 o'clock the enemy's line of battle left the woods"). It is not invented, and the animation note says it is a drawing choice. Window bounds are sourced (13:30 Eshleman, 15:00 Hall/Webb).
- Tier inferred (weakest of P7 inferred, P2 documented) is right. No clock offset applied except as noted below.

## Defects and exact fixes
1. **Davis quote is cut so that it reads as a step-off at 3.** The sentence is "The artillery ceased firing at 3 o'clock, and the order to move forward was given and promptly obeyed" (OR27-2 p. 651). The quote begins at "at 3 o'clock," and drops "The artillery ceased firing". Davis dates the end of the guns; the order followed. Fix: start the quote at "The artillery ceased firing" (use quote_mode ocr with raw for the "hring" misprint, no digit involved), set `states.phrases` to "ceased firing", and change the P2 label/panel to say Davis gives the end of the guns at 3 and the order after.
2. **Hunt and Haskell are counted as 15:00 step-off witnesses and are not.** Hunt (p. 239): column of attack "formed ... in the edge of the woods" about 3 p.m. Haskell: the cannonade ended at 3 "almost precisely". Both resolution notes admit this, but `default.animation.note`, `source_panel_text`, `weighing.summary` and `basis.text` still list "Harrow, Hunt, Haskell about 15:00" as step-off witnesses. Fix: in those four places name only Hall, Webb, Harrow (leaving the woods) and Davis (order after the guns ceased at 3); say Hunt and Haskell date the forming of the column and the end of the guns.
3. **"On both sides" is overstated.** The cited Confederate witnesses are 3 early (Alexander, Eshleman, Peyton) against 1 late (Davis). Hill (OR27-2 p. 608, "At 1 o'clock our artillery opened, and for two hours rained ... The assault was then gallantly made"), Lee (p. 320, guns from about 1 for about two hours) and Cabell (p. 376, "over two hours") are named or implied in the weighing and md but have no citation. Fix: add Hill as a citation (OR27-2-R534, offset 2260747, exact false, hedge about) to P2 or a new position, and add a sentence to `weighing.independence` that the Confederate side is split 3 early to 1 (Davis) plus Hill/Lee/Cabell who give two hours of guns without a step-off hour. Remove "Walton" and "Hunt" from the list of writers who put the guns' start at 1 in P4-c2/P6 text unless cited (Walton was not found as a report; Hunt gives no 1 p.m. in the passage read).
4. **Peyton "clock" reason is invented.** `P4.reason_not_chosen` says "on his clock the order came about an hour after the guns, which is the others' 14:00 to 14:15" and the weighing says his clock "would bring his 14:30 to about 14:00 on theirs". No source gives Peyton's clock offset or the "14:00 to 14:15" figure; Alexander's own interval from opening (13:00) to the allowed advance (13:50) is 50 minutes, nobody gives 14:15. Fix: change kind to `within_range` and text to "Peyton dates the guns 1.30 where nearly every other report says about 1, and the order to advance 2.30; his figures lie inside the window and stay selectable"; delete the 30-minute clock claim from `weighing.clock`.
5. **Alexander memoir "1.50 or later" is the hour Longstreet joined him, not the step-off.** In the memoir Pickett had already said "I am going to move forward" and galloped off before that, and Garnett's brigade swept through the guns after. Citation P8-c2 has hedge "exact"; it is a lower bound ("or later") of an earlier event. Fix: hedge `lower`; change the `.md` line "Garnett's brigade came out of the wood at '1.50 or later'" to "Longstreet joined him at '1.50 or later'; Garnett's brigade came out of the wood soon after". The report (p. 239) says the division was "just approaching" the guns at 1.50 and was then allowed to advance; the inference text "stood about 300 yards behind the guns" comes from the memoir passage at 1.40, not from the two cited quotes; cite it or drop it.
6. **Alexander report year.** The text reads "August 10th, 1868". The resolution assumes 1863 from "Camp near Orange C. H.". A firmer internal reason exists: the report is addressed to "G. M. Sorrel, Adjutant-General First Corps". Add that to the note. Keep the flag; also add the sentence to the `.md` that the SHSP printing is 1876-77.

After fixes 1 to 5 the resolution would be CLEAN on quotes and independence; class, tier and the 15:00 drawn instant (sourced to Hall and Webb, labelled a drawing choice) stand.

# Review of C-010 (Sickles' advance to the Peach Orchard)

Reviewer: independent max-effort pass, 2026-11-24. Validator: 0 errors, 0 warnings. All quotes were re-found and read in context; datelines checked against the printed reports.

**Verdict: DEFECTS (7 to fix; the window itself is defensible)**

## What holds
- Craig (2 p. m., brick house; second forward move at 4), Hunt (about 2, peach orchard, and he reached the ground then), Austin (2 p. m.), Randolph (between 1 and 2, notice only), Gibbon (4), Hazard (4), Humphreys 1863 (about 4) and 1864 (about 12 or a little after; a little after 4), Hancock (about 3), Freeborn (3), Meade 1864 (a few minutes before 4), Longstreet's misread 12.55/one o'clock are all accurately quoted and correctly typed. Every PDF value, the recommended default and S1 to S5 are carried. Failed searches are honest. The 20-minute clock difference is not applied. "Six other values" is right (8 positions, 2 in the window).

## Defects and exact fixes
1. **Birney's citation in C-010-P3 does not state what the position claims.** The quote reads "About half past two o'clock ... and at [...] o'clock I ordered Clark's rifled battery to open"; the elision hides the word (OCR "thre^", i.e. "three"), `states` is only 14:30, yet the position is valued 15:00 and its label and the .md say Birney has "three o'clock". The 14:30 is when he saw enemy columns and 15:00 is when his guns opened: neither is the Third Corps's advance. Fix: replace the quote with the contiguous "at three o'clock I ordered Clark's rifled battery to open upon them" using `quote_mode: "ocr"` (raw "thre^ o'clock", `page_image_viewed: true`), `states.times: ["15:00"]`, and say in the label "Birney opens fire at 3 (not the advance)". Keep it out of the count of witnesses who "date the advance".
2. **De Trobriand is counted as dating the move to the road at about 2 (basis, panel, weighing, .md, `holds`) but his sentence is "About 2 p. m. the same day, a line of battle was formed in expectation of an impending attack"**, with his brigade at the apex "in column by regiments" (p. 519-520). That dates forming a line, not moving up to the Emmitsburg road. Fix: relabel his citation as "line formed about 2", drop him from "Craig, Hunt, Austin, De Trobriand ... moved to the road" in `basis.text`, `default.basis_text`, `weighing.independence` and the .md, or state the narrower meaning wherever he is named.
3. **De Trobriand's `written.date` 1863-07-31 is invented.** The printed dateline is "Jvly — , 1863" (no day). Use "1863-07" if the schema permits, else year only with a note; do not supply a day. Same for **Ewell in C-019** (not this file) and **Hancock**: his report has no printed date; the "1863-10-24" is the date of his later addendum and Army indorsement 28 Oct; the weighing already says "by 24 October", so give the date as year 1863 with that note.
4. **Brewster and Hugo are filed as support of C-010-P1 (noon) but both state 13:00** ("At about 1 o'clock ... received orders ... to move the brigade to the front and form line of battle in rear of the First Brigade"; "until 1 p. m., when orders were received to advance"). They do not support noon, and Austin of the same brigade says 2 p. m. for the advance. Fix: move them to their own position ("About 13:00: the Second (Excelsior) Brigade is ordered forward behind the First Brigade (Brewster, Hugo)"), documented, selectable, reason `different_event`; and say in the weighing that Brewster and Hugo (1 p. m.) differ by an hour from Austin (2 p. m.) inside one brigade.
5. **The animation note is wrong as written:** "14:00, the earliest hour at which a unit of the corps is documented moving to [the road]". Craig's own report (p. 500, the citation already used) has his regiment ordered at 11.15 a. m. "to move to the front to support the Sixty-third Pennsylvania ... deployed as skirmishers along the Emmitsburg road", and Berdan's detachment (C-009) went out about 11. Reword to "the earliest hour at which a brigade of the corps is documented moving forward in line to the road, Craig's 2 p. m." and list the 11:15 movement of the 105th as a different event in the weighing, so no reader thinks nothing of the corps was on the road earlier.
6. **Class irreducible versus the resolution's own explanation.** `weighing.summary`, the panel ("moved up to the Emmitsburg road in stages") and `basis_text` ("two moves of one advance") assert a sequence that the weighing itself says "no witness states as such". Under PLAN section 6 either the witnesses truly disagree (irreducible, no explanation asserted) or the differences are explained (weighted/settled). Fix (keeps irreducible): in the panel and basis_text replace "in stages" and "two moves of one advance" with "witnesses give 2, 3 and 4; two of them (Craig, Humphreys) describe two moves"; keep the inference only in the weighing and mark it as the researcher's reading. The same applies to the .md "The witnesses disagree because 'the advance' was a sequence of moves": change to "may be because".
7. **Window upper bound.** Humphreys 1864 says "Perhaps a little after 4 o'clock I received orders ... to move up near the Emmettsburg road"; the window ends at 16:00 and the animation has him "close up to the road about 16:00". Say in `basis_text` and the animation note that 16:00 is an approximate upper edge and Humphreys's 1864 order came a little after it (`hedge: lower` is already recorded). Also C-010-P3's Meade citation joins six fragments across pages 115-116 (arrival at 1 a. m., 7 a. m., the Sixth Corps at 3 p. m.) that have nothing to do with the advance; use the contiguous passage "About 3 p. m. I rode out to the extreme left ... had advanced, or rather was in the act of advancing, his corps" (about 600 characters) so the 3 p. m. of the Sixth Corps cannot be mistaken for the same event.

## Not defects, noted
- Hancock's "about 3 p. m., when the Third Corps advanced ... and became heavily engaged" is the hour of advance plus engagement; label is fair, hedge kept.
- Meade's two accounts (about 3; a few minutes before 4) are rightly carried as an hour's disagreement.

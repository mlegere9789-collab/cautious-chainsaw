# Review of C-027 (Buford's destination): DEFECTS (one real quote problem; substance sound)

Reviewer: independent, 2026-11-24.

## Verified
Buford p. 928 (first scan garbles "July 2" as "-TnW 9,"; second scan, offset 3215767, prints "July 2, the division became engaged with the enemy's sharpshooters on our left, and held its own until relieved by General Sickles' corps, after which it moved to Taneytown, and bivouacked for the night. The next day, July 3, it moved to Westminster, to guard the trains"); Devin p. 939 (the Third Corps "coming into position, we were ordered to march to Taneytown, where we bivouacked, and marched the next morning, July 3, to Westminster"); Calef p. 1032 (order "on the morning of the 2d", "Reached Taneytown about 4 p. m."); Butterfield (OR27-3 p. 1086, undated hour: collect the trains near Taneytown and take them down to Westminster) and the 12.50 p.m. note p. 490 ("the permission to Buford to go to Westminster"), which is real OR text; Pleasonton p. 914; Merritt p. 943 ("On the 2d instant, we marched to Emmitsburg"); Patterson p. 687 ("Arrived at the latter place [Westminster] on the morning of the 2d. Reported to General Buford"). Settled/documented is right: three questions, each answered by the writer for his own unit.

## Defect and exact fixes
1. **Spliced Itinerary quote misattributes the subject (P1).** The citation reads "July 2. - The Second, Fifth, and Sixth Corps [...] marched from Gettysburg to Taneytown". Read straight, that says the three infantry corps marched to Taneytown. The text (OR27-1 p. 145, offset 499796) is: "July 2. - The Second, Fifth, and Sixth Corps, Lockwood's brigade ... reached the field of Gettysburg. Gamble's and Devin's brigades, of Buford's cavalry division, marched from Gettysburg to Taneytown, and Merritt's reserve brigade from Mechanicstown to Emmitsburg." Replace the quote with: `Gamble's and Devin's brigades, of Buford's cavalry division, marched from Gettysburg to Taneytown` (states `dates` 07-02 by the July 2 heading, add `note: "under the entry for July 2"`), `hedge: exact`, `exact: true`. Do the same for P3: its Itinerary quote ends "Emmitsburg. July 3" and `states` dates 07-03, which makes the entry read as 3 July; use `Merritt's reserve brigade from Mechanicstown to Emmitsburg` (entry of 2 July) and set `states.dates` to 07-02. The Itinerary is a compilation from the reports; keep it in the reports' group.
2. **P3 label cites Pleasonton, no Pleasonton citation.** The label reads "(Merritt, Pleasonton)". Remove "Pleasonton" or add a citation; none states Emmitsburg for the Reserve Brigade on 2 July.

## Advisories
- Calef says the order to go to Taneytown came on the morning of the 2d, while Devin and Buford place the move after the Third Corps relieved the division; the resolution should add one sentence that the First Brigade's start (morning, Calef) and the Second Brigade's (after the Third Corps came up, Devin) differ, and that Butterfield's 12.50 p.m. note shows leave to go to Westminster already given by noon. This does not change the default.
- Buford's Taneytown citation carries no date in its quoted words; the 2 July attribution rests on the second scan, Devin and Calef. The quote in P7 is therefore fine, but P1 should not rely on Buford alone for the day; the md says this correctly.

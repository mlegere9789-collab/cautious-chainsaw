# Review of C-029 (Cannonade end)

Verdict: DEFECTS (class overclaimed; a decisive sentence of Hunt omitted; a reason text and the `.md` are inaccurate; a count is wrong).

Checked: validator 0 errors, 0 warnings. Quotes re-found in context (Hunt p. 239, Peyton p. 385, Devereux p. 443, Gibbon p. 417, Lee p. 320, Cabell p. 376, Hancock pp. 372-373, Howard p. 706, Hays p. 454, Smyth p. 465, Meade, HB9 p. 33). Datelines match. 14:00 is correctly unsourced (the 2 p. m. in Hays, Smyth is an opening). Hedges kept ("about 2.30", "about 3", "nearly 2").

Defects and exact fixes:

1. Missed passage that settles the point. Hunt p. 239 continues: "I directed that the fire should be gradually stopped, which was done, and the enemy soon slackened his fire also ... About 3 p. m., and soon after the enemy's fire had ceased, he formed a column of attack in the edge of the woods in front of the Second Corps" (OR27-1 offset 793793, found by `find_passage.py OR27-1 --regex "enemy's fire had ceased"`). The same best witness thus gives both bounds: Union stop about 14:30, enemy's fire ceased before about 15:00. Peyton (the Confederate fire abating at 2.30) and Devereux (about 3) agree with him within the "about" tolerances, and Hancock's 14:45 is inside. Fix: add this citation (exact false, hedge `about`, group hunt) to C-029-P7 and C-029-P3, and state it in `weighing.summary` and `.md`.
2. Class. The default is declared `irreducible`, "the record is divided by half an hour". With item 1 the best witnesses do not disagree: the 14:30 and the 15:00 are two events (Union fire stopped, enemy fire dying) described by the same writer, plus hedged readings. The resolution itself calls P2's reason `different_event` and P3's `tolerance`, and explains Hays/Smyth by their later start. PLAN section 6 reserves `irreducible` for best-class witnesses that truly disagree. Fix: class `weighted`, tier `inferred`, default `mode` `window` (allowed for weighted by the validator, as in C-025); `basis_text` unchanged; `weighing.summary` last sentences replaced by "the window is the spread of hedged readings of one fire that died away; Hunt's own report has both ends". Move the `failed_searches` to match (a weighted class does not need them but they may stay). If the author keeps `irreducible`, the weighing must name two best-class witnesses that contradict each other and cannot be separated; Howard (15:30) and Hays/Smyth (16:30, 17:00) are the only candidates and are dealt with in items 3 and 4.
3. Howard (3.30 p. m., Cemetery Hill, the enemy's artillery "soon after") lies outside the window and is not weighed. Hunt's "about 3 p. m." and Devereux are earlier. Fix: add to `weighing.where`/`summary` one sentence: Howard's 15:30 is the Eleventh Corps's own guns on the north end and the enemy's guns firing at that sector, an hour later than the Second Corps front; it stays selectable (P6). The P6 reason kind `place_of_observation` is acceptable once this is written.
4. P4 `reason_not_chosen.text` says "their durations of two and a half to three hours are close to the others". The others give 1 h 45, two hours or "over two hours"; two and a half to three hours is not close. Fix text: "The same division dates the opening about 2 p. m., an hour after the others, and its fire ran two and a half to three hours; the reports of Hays and Smyth are the only ones that place the end after 15:30." In `C-029.md` the sentence "an hour later than the corps commander at the same start, so their end follows their start" is garbled (Hays's and Smyth's ends are about two hours after Hancock's, from a start one hour later). Fix: "Hays and Smyth, one division, date the opening about 2 p. m., an hour later than the others, and the end 4.30 and 5 p. m.; their fire lasted two and a half to three hours, longer than the two hours of the others, so the later start accounts for only half of the difference. They stay selectable."
5. `source_panel_text` ends "Four other values are listed"; there are seven positions and the default is one, so six others (14:00, 14:30, 15:00, 16:30-17:00, 14:45, 15:30). Fix: "Six other values are listed".

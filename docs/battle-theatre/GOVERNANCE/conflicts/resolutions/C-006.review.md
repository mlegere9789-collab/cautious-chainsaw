## 2 Defects

**D1 (moderate): the default rests on one witness but is not flagged single_source, and the independence text counts witnesses that do not support it.** `positions[C-006-P5].flags` is `[]` (P1, P2, P3 carry `single_source`). `weighing.independence`: "The default rests on four groups: the diary (Fremantle), Ross, McLaws and Freeman." Only Fremantle places Longstreet with Lee at about 05:00. Ross (P5-c3, p. 49) "does not say that Longstreet was on the hill or give an hour" (the resolution's own note). McLaws (p. 68) gives no hour: Longstreet "when I came up, was walking back and forth some little distance from General Lee"; his own arrival is "early in the morning", and Kershaw (OR27-2 p. 366, "did not leave camp until about sunrise") puts McLaws's head on the ridge well after 05:00, so McLaws bounds Longstreet's presence by about 06:00-08:00, not 05:00. Freeman's 5:15 is computed from the diary (the resolution says so). Correct: flag P5 `single_source`, and say the default rests on the diary alone, the others being consistent.

**D2 (minor): "by about 05:00" and the panel text state more than the diary.** `source_panel_text`: "has Longstreet already seated with Lee on the ridge when he arrived there at 5 a.m." Fremantle (tmp/library/conflicts/C-007/fremantle-gutenberg-20928.txt, 2 July): "arrived at 5 A.M. at the same commanding position ... and I climbed up a tree ... Just below us were seated Generals Lee, Hill, Longstreet, and Hood". The generals are seen after the climb, so "already ... when he arrived" is not in the diary; the P5-c1 note and the P1 reason say this correctly ("not necessarily before it"), and `default.animation.note` says "05:00 is the instant by which the diary has Longstreet with Lee". Freeman himself reads the line as "after 5 a.m." (FREEMAN-RELEE-V3-C005 offset 1263314) and the plan row T2-058 as "Fremantle after 05:00". The tolerance of 15 minutes covers it, but the visitor-facing sentence and the animation note must say "at about 05:00 or a few minutes after", not "already ... when he arrived". The diary also does not say the seat is Lee's headquarters; "with Lee on Seminary Ridge" (the resolution's wording) is right and the title "at Lee's headquarters" is not shown by it, which the resolution does not claim.

**D3 (minor): Longstreet's 1877 sequence, read in context, is not used.** ANNALS-1879-C005 p. 422 (offset 1254893): "I went to General Lee's headquarters at daylight ... We observed the position of the Federals ... About sunrise General Lee sent Colonel Venable to General Ewell's headquarters". With Venable's letter (SHSP-05 p. 77, "About sunrise on the 2d ... I was sent by General Lee to General Ewell") the conference is over by about sunrise (04:35), i.e. daylight arrival is before sunrise, while Cullen finds them together "after sunrise" and Freeman (offset 221651) notes that Longstreet corrected an earlier version of Annals p. 422. This tightens P4 ("first light to soon after sunrise") and bears on P3 (stars) more than the resolution allows ("Cullen found Longstreet with Lee only after sunrise" is a lower bound, not an arrival). Venable is listed in `failed_searches` as read, but the 1877 + Venable sequence is not weighed.

# Review of C-018 (independent)

Verdict: defects. Default (unresolved set, tier inferred) is defensible as a display, but the class, one citation and one position are wrong or incomplete.

Validator: 0 errors, 2 warnings (page_hint, P2 support[3] and [4]; printed page 11 not near the quote: the Vermont book's running head "II" is explained in page_note, acceptable).

Defects:
1. Citation C-018-P5-c4 (N. J. Hall, 48th Georgia flag to Sergt. James Wiley, 59th NY) is dated and described wrongly. The list is not in Hall's report of 17 July: it is a separate letter on OR27-1 p. 441-442 headed "New York, August 3, 1863", to Col. Townsend, opening "I observed ... a printed official paper naming the flags captured at Gettysburg, Pa., on the 3d of July", signed "Colonel 7th Michigan Vols." The JSON says written 1863-07-17 and the note says "the list does not give the day": the letter's own heading says 3 July (the 48th Georgia fought only on 2 July, so the heading is loose, but that must be stated). The quote does not say 2 July either.
2. Unflagged conflict inside the default's claim (2): Darrow (82nd NY, Harrow's) says his regiment captured "the battle-flags of the Forty-eighth Georgia"; Hall's letter gives that flag to the 59th NY (Hall's brigade). The claim "regiments of Webb's, Harrow's and Hall's brigades took the 48th Georgia's colonel and flags" blends a colonel (Curry only, Webb's brigade; Gibson in brackets is the OR editors' insertion) with two rival flag credits. Only Curry supports "colonel"; "three brigades" is Curry + Darrow + a letter of 3 Aug about 3 July flags. Webb's own report names no 48th Georgia. The weighing says the reports "show" this; it should say the flag credit is itself disputed.
3. Missed witnesses who truly disagree with Wright on why he withdrew (P6 says Wright and his four regiments agree on why; P6 is tier documented). OR27-2 p. 631-633 Lang (Perry's brigade, on Wright's right): his brigade advanced "to the crest of the second hill", reached "a small eminence at the foot of the heights", and fell back only when Wilcox on its right fell back and the enemy passed his right flank; he does not say he gave way before Wright. OR27-2 p. 633-635 Posey (on Wright's left): sent the 48th and 19th Mississippi forward and "three regiments well up", and Harris (19th Miss.) says Wright's brigade "commenced advancing, supported on his left by the Forty-eighth Mississippi". That contradicts Wright's "the brigade ordered to our support having failed to advance". Neither report is cited, in the weighing, or in failed_searches. The Wilcox 1863 citation in P6 is about Wilcox's own brigade.
4. Class. PLAN section 6 step 6 and the rubric: irreducible needs best-classed witnesses who disagree. On the crest, no witness contradicts Wright (the weighing says so: "none contradicts him"); his colonels and Webb are silent on the crest, not opposed. Webb's "advanced beyond one gun of Brown's battery" is read as "short of the crest" by the researcher (Webb's brigade front, and the 3d/2d printed day of his 6.30 p.m. assault is unresolved, image not seen). That is one witness uncontradicted (weighted, inferred as Wright's claim) plus, for "who stopped it", no source naming a stopper (gap for that point), with Webb and Stannard resting on later books. The disagreement that does exist (Wright vs Lang/Posey on supports) was not found. An honest class is weighted for the crest claim and gap for the stopper, or irreducible only after item 3 is added.
5. Flags: P2 (Stannard) carries "memoir_only" yet cites three official reports of 1863 (Stannard, Randall, Hancock); the flag is false.
6. Webb quote used in P3 and P4 ("short of the crest") comes from a paragraph whose text layer reads "assault of the 3d at about 6.30 p. m."; the validator-visible use as evidence about Wright depends on 2 July. Flagged in the weighing but P4's reason ("place_of_observation") rests on it.

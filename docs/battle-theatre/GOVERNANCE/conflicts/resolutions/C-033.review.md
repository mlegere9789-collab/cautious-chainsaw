# Review of C-033 (Imboden departs): DEFECTS (minor)

Reviewer: independent, 2026-11-24. Validator passes. Quotes read in context.

## What is sound
- Imboden's quotes (9 o'clock, rain after noon, "About 4 p. M. the head of the column was put in motion near Cashtown", "I remained at Cashtown giving directions", "After dark I set out", dawn of the 5th for the tail) are real and not stretched. Guild's "on the evening of the 4th" and Lee's "ordered to Williamsport on July 4" are real. The Jul 5 value is not supported by any source and is correctly carried as the tail/arrival; the "20:00 Jul 5" search failure is listed. Tier inferred (single memoir for the hour) is right; hedge "about" is kept; no invented times.

## Defects and exact fixes
1. **Default label contains non-default positions.** `default.label` and `animation.note` state three things (head 16:00 on Jul 4, Imboden himself after dark, rear at dawn Jul 5) but `default.positions` is only P3. P5 (Imboden after dark, inferred, single source) and P2 (dawn Jul 5) are marked selectable. Fix: shorten `default.label` to "Jul 4 afternoon: the head of the column leaves Cashtown about 16:00" and move the other two clauses into `animation.note`, flagged as "selectable positions P5 and P2, memoir only"; or put P5 and P2 into `default.positions` with `mode: unresolved_set`.
2. **Topic is "Imboden departs" but the default is the head of the column, not Imboden.** By his own words he did not leave at 16:00. Fix: add to `default.label`/`source_panel_text` that 16:00 is the column's head and that Imboden himself left after dark (the panel already says so; make the default label say it too). `P5` "after dark" is also an inference "after about 19:30": sunset is not dark; say "after dusk".
3. **Hoke is counted as an independent group.** The note admits he "possibly drew on" Imboden's article of the same year (same hour, same rain). Fix: put Hoke in group `imboden` and add flag `single_source` to P3.
4. **Guild's "evening" is declared the same start as Imboden's 4 p.m. without proof.** Guild's ambulance trains marched "on the evening of the 4th" (one by Cashtown, one by Fairfield) and were leaving the Gettysburg infirmaries; Imboden's head of column "was put in motion near Cashtown". Different places, and the equivalence plus the sentence "evening in the usage of the time began in the late afternoon" (in P4-c1 note and the `.md`) have no source. Fix: change P4 `reason_not_chosen` kind to `unclear_expression`/`place_of_observation`, text "Guild gives the evening of the 4th for the ambulance trains with no hour; this may be the same start or the trains leaving Gettysburg before the head reached Cashtown"; delete the usage claim or cite a source for it.
5. **Pendleton citation does not state a date or a departure.** The quote "were detailed to report to General Imboden, at Cashtown ..." has no day; the day comes from the preceding sentence ("defensive measures directed for the 4th"). Fix: extend the quote to start at "In the defensive measures directed for the 4th" and `states.dates ["07-04"]`, or drop it from P1; and note that P1's Lee and Pendleton lines date an order and a detail, not the movement.
6. Independence statement "Imboden, Guild, Lee and Pendleton are independent lines": Lee's report of January 1864 plausibly draws on Pendleton and Guild. Add "Lee may rely on Pendleton and Guild".

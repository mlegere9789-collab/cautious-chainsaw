This was about 11.30 a. m."; Howard was looking from a building in Gettysburg); Schurz p. 727 ("about 11.30 ... had just been killed"); Weld (diary, p. 230): Reynolds sent him to Meade, "I delivered the message ... at 11.20, having been an hour and twenty minutes on my way", so Reynolds was alive at about 10:00; Hall pp. 354 and 359 (ordered into position by Reynolds; "opened ... at 10.45 a. m."; marched at 9 a. m.), correctly treated as an unresolved tension. No source gives 10:30 or 10:40-10:50 (three failed searches recorded). Class weighted, tier documented with single-source flag (Wadsworth and Doubleday one group) is right; Howard's dispatch (rank 6) is outweighed on vantage, as the rubric allows when written down.

## Defects and exact fixes
1. **Context citations flagged exact.** P1 cites Wadsworth p. 265 "at 8 a. m., the division moved from Marsh Creek" with `exact: true`; P7 cites Howard p. 701 "At 3.30 a. m. July 1, orders were received" with `exact: true`. Neither states the value of its position. Set `exact: false`, add `note: "context"`, or delete them. P1's Weld cite is correctly `exact: false`.
2. **Doubleday's 10.15 is not the fall by his own words alone.** The sentence "occurred in the beginning of the attack referred to, about 10.15" fixes the fall to the beginning of the attack; Doubleday did not see it. In P1's Doubleday citation set `vantage: elsewhere` (he "learned" it a little later, after taking a position behind the left wing) instead of `present`; keep `knew_by: relayed_report`. This does not alter the group count.
3. **md accuracy.** "Howard (Eleventh Corps, still in the town)" is right at 10:15-11:30, but `weighing.where` says "Howard and Schurz were at the cemetery, three-quarters of a mile away". Howard was on a building in the town when told (p. 701-702); Schurz found him on an eminence east of the cemetery about 11.30. Change to: "Howard was in the town and was told about 11.30; Schurz joined him near the cemetery."

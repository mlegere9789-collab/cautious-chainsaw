## 2 Defects

**D1 (moderate): the animation rule `earliest_documented`, and the Q04 floor move, claim documentation the record does not give.** `default.animation.rule` is `earliest_documented`; its note says 07:00 is "the earliest hour a writer of the day gives for the division on the ground (Fremantle's diary) and the middle of the range 06:00 to 08:00" and, in the same note, that the column "is drawn marching in before 07:00". Fremantle (offset 351799) says only "At 7 A.M. I rode over part of the ground with General Longstreet, and saw him disposing M'Laws's division for to-day's fight"; the resolution itself writes (P3-c1 note, `.md` item 1) that it "does not say that the division stood on the ridge or when it came". 07:00 is an upper bound by inference (tier inferred, `P3.value.note` "upper bound"), not a documented instant, and the note gives two different grounds (earliest hour; middle of a range). `readers[gate Q04]` (must_change true) moves the floor and default instant from 08:00 to 07:00 while its own text says a floor at 07:00 "forbids drawing a march that Kershaw's report documents from sunrise". PLAN 8: "A floor moves only to an earlier time that the record documents". The same defect was found in the C-005 review (D2). Use `best_supported` and put the floor question to the owner, or keep the floor and use 07:00 only as the instant by which the head stands on the ridge.

**D2 (moderate): `P1.reason_not_chosen`, `holds.pdf_default`, `source_panel_text` and `.md` state as fact that 08:00 and 08:30 "date the whole division closing up".** The sources do not say that. Longstreet's "two divisions nor myself did not reach General Lee until 8 a.m." (Taylor p. 198) is about his own and both divisions' reporting to Lee; the Freeman appendix the resolution relies on treats it as an error, not as a closing-up ("probably was no less wide of the mark in saying that he did not arrive until 8 a.m.", `FREEMAN-RELEE-V3-C005`, offset 1263665). The Meade narrative (p. 69) "about eight o'clock halted in the fields in the rear of the seminary" is for both divisions. Only Freeman's "the other division was probably not in position until 8 or 8:30" (computed, 1935) speaks of a division being up late, and "the other division" is read as McLaws's only through Freeman's own summary ("a second division there by 8 to 8:30"); Freeman's sentence says "That command ... does not seem to have been well closed" of McLaws's and then "the other division". P1 is correctly inferred, but the visitor-facing sentences (panel: "The plan's 08:00 and 08:30 date the whole division closing up") and `holds.pdf_default.note` ("it holds as the end of the arrival") should say "read as", or credit Freeman's reading only.

**D4 (minor): a source of the time for Alexander's artillery is not weighed.** Freeman's appendix (`FREEMAN-RELEE-V3-C005`, appendix, the paragraph after the cited "8 or 8:30") says Alexander "stated that his artillery reached a point one mile west of Seminary Ridge about 7 a.m." and uses it, with 9 a.m., to date the rear division.

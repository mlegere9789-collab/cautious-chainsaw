# Review of C-015 (20th Maine strength)

Reviewer: independent max-effort pass, 2026-11-24. Validator: 0 errors, 0 warnings. Quotes re-found and read in context: Chamberlain p. 626 ("We went into the fight with 386, all told — 358 guns"), Barnes p. 603 ("It consisted of 380 men and officers"), the monument p. 249, Melcher, Gerrish, Maine 1898 p. 278, Fox table row.

**Verdict: DEFECTS (3 minor to fix; default and class are right)**

## What holds
- 386 all told / 358 guns is exact, from the commander of the regiment four days after, and states what each number counts. Class weighted, tier documented, flag `single_source` present as required (Chamberlain, with Fox counted in the same group, is the only line of knowledge; the groups are honest). 385 correctly has no source, three failed lookups, tier open gap, and the PDF's S1 and S3 are carried. The range 358 to 386 is Chamberlain's own two counts; Barnes's 380 is shown inside it, no headline invented, the animation follows printed strength. "Five other values" is right.

## Defects and exact fixes
1. **An inference is written as a fact.** The .md says "The 358 is Chamberlain's musket count taken as the number engaged" and `weighing.independence` says "358 rests on Chamberlain's own musket count, which the monument and Melcher repeat". Neither the monument ("out of 358 engaged") nor Melcher ("Our regiment numbered 358 men") says it takes the figure from Chamberlain or that it counts muskets; each states it as a count of men. Fix: in the .md and weighing say "the monument and Melcher give 358 as the regiment's number; it equals Chamberlain's musket count, and a copy from him is likely but not stated". In C-015-P4 set the label to "358 (Chamberlain's guns; given as men engaged by the monument and Melcher)", and mark the two later citations as supporting only that the number 358 appears, with the unit `muskets` carried by Chamberlain's citation alone.
2. **Fox's citation is a table row without its column head.** The quote "20th Maine Gettysburg Barnes's 386" has no header; "Barnes's" there is the division name, not Barnes's report (which says 380), and "Engaged" is read from the head of the table, which is not in the quote. Fix: add a `ocr_note` or an extra fragment from the table head showing the column (or write in the position label "Fox's table, column read as engaged"), and add a clause to the .md that "Barnes's" in Fox's row is the division heading, so a reader does not take it for Barnes's 380.
3. **The animation note asserts "Company B's 50 are a part of the 386."** Only Melcher gives 50 for Company B and counts them inside his 358; Chamberlain's report does not number Company B. Reword to "Company B (50 men by Melcher) went out as skirmishers and is counted inside the regiment, not added".

## Not defects, noted
- Barnes's 380 is exact in print; the weighing calls it a "round relay". Say "relay" and drop "round", or give the reason.

The default still holds but `weighing.independence` and the .md ("three lines against two") are wrong, and the P3 c5 note ("cites a source by footnote") does not say which.
D2 (moderate, stretched reading). Taylor p. 113 is "That portion [of the cavalry] which General Stuart accompanied made a complete circuit of the Federal army, and only joined General Lee on the evening of the second day". The subject is the cavalry force Stuart accompanied (the brigades), not Stuart's call on Lee. The .md and P4 label present it as Stuart "joined General Lee" and offer "may mean the call on Lee"; the reading that it dates the brigades' arrival (the plan's "brigades in the evening") is not weighed. This weakens the claim in `holds.pdf_default` and the readers note for `cs-cav-flee-chambliss` that "only Taylor and Kidd say evening" refer to Stuart himself.
D3 (moderate, readers incomplete). Register entries `EG-0112` (Hour of Stuart's arrival on Jul 2), `EG-0262` (Stuart and the cavalry brigades: clock time, T2-095) and `EG-0285` (Stuart's arrival hour; Jenkins) are not in `readers`; nor are `decision_log.md` line 51 (DEC-C-023, PENDING OWNER) and the C-023 row of `decisions/DELEGATED-DECISIONS-2026-11-11.md` (line 33).

## D1: a hedge is dropped for Crawford's Third Division ("by noon" becomes "at noon")

Crawford: "The column was put in motion at once, and by noon had arrived at the position occupied by the First and Second Divisions". The citation's `hedge` is correctly `upper`, but the position label (P6) says "About noon", `states` use 12:00 as a point, and `source_panel_text` and `default.note` say "the Third Division at noon". "By noon" is an upper bound (at or before noon). Fix: P6 label "By noon: the Third Division is at the corps' position (Crawford: 'by noon')", `value.approx` false with `tolerance_min` removed or marked upper; panel text "the Third Division by noon".

## D2: Meade's "by 7 a.m." is presented as support for a lower bound of 07:00

`default.basis_text`: "Lower bound: Chamberlain (about 7 a.m.), Barnes (about 7) and Meade (by 7 a.m.)". `source_panel_text`: "its first division at about 7 a.m. (Chamberlain, Barnes, Meade)". Meade says "By 7 a. m. the Second and Fifth Corps, with the rest of the Third, had reached the ground" (OR27-1 p.115): that is an upper bound for the corps, not "about 7" and not "the first division". An arrival "by 7" is equally true of Slocum's 5 a.m. Fix: remove Meade from the lower bound and from the panel text; keep him in P4 only as "on the ground by 7 a.m." with hedge `upper` (the P4 label already says "by"; the default text must match).

## D3: further hours in the library were not weighed or listed (the PLAN requires every value found to be a position)

Found with `find_passage.py --all --regex "(Fifth|5th) (Army )?Corps[^.]{0,200}(arrived|reached|came up)[^.]{0,120}..."`:

- `POWELL-1896-C012` offset 1080761: "the Fifth Corps reached Gettysburg about 5 A.M. July 2d" (a history by a Fifth Corps writer; agrees with Slocum, possibly drawn from him).
- `NY-GB-1902-v3` offset 120633 and `NORTON-1913-C016` offset 639447: "The Fifth corps, after marching nearly all night, reached the Gettysburg field about 6 a. m., July 2d" (state volume text of 1902, later repeated).
- `HISTORY-ADAMS-1886`: "The Second and Fifth Corps and the remainder of the Third reached the grounds a little after sunrise" (offset 3829460).
- `EVERETT-1864` / `CEM-BARTLETT-1874`: "The Second and Fifth Corps ... had reached the ground by seven o'clock, A. M." (a copy of Meade's report; one group with Meade).

Add 10:00 (USSC), 06:00 (NY 1902) and 05:00 (Powell) as positions (they are later or civilian witnesses, so `inferred` at most; reasons `later_memory`/`elsewhere`), and group Powell with Slocum if no independence is shown. The "failed searches" claim that no other hour was found in the library must be limited to what was searched (OR27-1 Fifth and Twelfth Corps reports, the Joint Committee, the weeklies); the whole library was not searched for this hour.

## D4 (judgement, flagged for the researcher): the reason the 05:00 value is rejected

P1 (Slocum, 5 a.m., commander on the right wing where the Fifth first went in; Rice's 44th New York "started at 4 a. m.; went about 2 miles, when we got into position") is rejected as `outweighed` by a headquarters dispatch of 6.15 a.m. ("momentarily expected"). The weighing itself says it is not known whether headquarters could have known of troops on the far right. The rubric ranks the dispatch higher, so `weighted` can stand, but then (a) the panel sentence "so Slocum's 5 a.m. was not chosen" should say the dispatch shows only that the corps had not reported at headquarters; (b) `default.animation.rule` is `earliest_documented` while the instant is 07:00 and the earliest documented hour is Slocum's 05:00: use `best_supported` (the validator does not catch this).

# Review of C-028 (Hunt's guns open, Jul 3)

Verdict: DEFECTS (the first-fire window is narrowed without warrant; one quote joined; one count wrong).

Checked: validator 0 errors, 0 warnings. Quotes re-found in context (Hunt p. 237, Muhlenberg p. 870, Packer p. 791, Greene p. 857, Slocum p. 761, Geary p. 828, Candy p. 836, Steuart p. 511, Williams p. 775). The two-event reading (first fire, then all batteries at 5.30) is correct and well supported. I re-ran a regex for other batteries "opened ... a. m." in OR27-1 and found nothing beyond Slocum and Williams. The 4 a. m. versus 4.30 split between eyewitnesses is a true disagreement and `irreducible` / `unresolved_set` is the right class for it.

Defects and exact fixes:

1. SUBSTANTIVE. Geary (3.30) and Candy (3.45) are put aside as `different_event`, and the default window starts at 04:00, but their own words describe the same bombardment. Geary p. 828: "at 3.30 a. m. (early dawn) a simultaneous attack was made by artillery and the infantry ... Our artillery fire continued, by previous arrangement, for ten minutes"; Williams says the artillery fire "by arrangement for fifteen minutes", then the enemy attacked Geary; Hunt and Muhlenberg say fifteen minutes at 4.30. Candy p. 836: "3.45 a. m., when the enemy were opened upon by a battery placed for the purpose of shelling them". Both are primary reports by officers on the ground, written within 30 days, so they rank with Greene and Packer. P6's own reason says "his brigade lay in front of Muhlenberg's guns", which argues for the same event, and the reason text ("earlier than any gun commander's hour") is `outweighed`, not `different_event`. Excluding them narrows the range to the convenient 04:00-04:30 (PLAN section 5: "a range of the sources is never narrowed"). Fix: (a) default window `from` 03:30 (Geary) or at least 03:45 (Candy) with `to` 04:30, label "First fire between 03:30 and 04:30 (reports differ)"; if the author keeps 04:00, the weighing must give a true reason (for example: the only readings before 04:00 are one division's attack time and one brigade's "when a battery opened", in the dark; sunrise 04:36) and the label must say "04:00 to 04:30; two reports put it at 03:30 and 03:45"; (b) P5 and P6 `reason_not_chosen.kind` `outweighed` (or `equal_standing`), with the comparison written in `weighing`; (c) `animation.note`: "nothing is drawn earlier than 04:00 (three reports)" is false against P5 and P6; the animation at 04:30 (`range_end`) is not earlier than any source so it is allowed, but the note must say Geary and Candy are earlier; (d) `source_panel_text` must name them ("two reports of the infantry put it at 3.30 and 3.45"); (e) `.md` "Decision" the same; (f) readers T2-247, T3-036 must_change note: window 03:30/03:45 to 04:30.
2. Quote stretch, position C-028-P1 and P8 (Muhlenberg): "At 4.30 a. m. the two [...] batteries (ten guns) [...] opened, and fired for fifteen minutes". The two skipped fragments hide "rifle batteries (ten guns) and the two light 12-pounder batteries (ten guns)"; the joined text reads as ten guns opening. The report says twenty guns. Fix: quote "At 4.30 a. m. the two rifle batteries (ten guns) and the two light 12-pounder batteries (ten guns) opened, and fired for fifteen minutes without intermission" with `numbers` [4.3, 10, 10, 15]. Note that Hunt's "these batteries" (Muhlenberg's and Kinzie's) is ten guns and Muhlenberg's is twenty guns (four batteries); the claim "Hunt's wording and numbers match Muhlenberg's" (`weighing.how_knew`, `.md`) is true for the hour only. Reword: "Hunt gives the same hour; his two batteries are part of the twenty guns Muhlenberg counts".
3. Independence of the 4 a. m. reading. Slocum is cited as `knew_by: relayed_report` and wrote in August from the reports of his subordinates (Greene's is one); `weighing.independence` counts "three separate reports". Fix: "two eyewitness reports (Packer, Greene) and the corps commander's summary (Slocum), which may rest on Greene's".
4. `source_panel_text` ends "Five other values are listed"; with the default made of P8 and P2 there are six other positions (P1, P3, P4, P5, P6, P7). Fix to "Six other values are listed" (or "Five", if P2 is not counted, but then say so consistently).

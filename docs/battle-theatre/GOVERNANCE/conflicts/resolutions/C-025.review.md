# Review of C-025 (Geary back to Culp's Hill)

Verdict: DEFECTS (weighing omissions and three overstatements; the class weighted / tier inferred and the window default are acceptable once corrected).

Checked: validator 0 errors, 0 warnings. Quotes re-found in context (Geary p. 827, Kane p. 847, Greene p. 857, Candy p. 836, Slocum p. 761, Williams p. 774, Meade p. 117, HB9 p. 25, county history). Datelines match. The plan's 02:00-04:00 and 23:00 are correctly unsourced; the 04:00 is correctly shown as the hour the artillery opened (Greene p. 857). The Williams "nearly 12 o'clock" is honestly left uncited (no page image viewed).

Defects and exact fixes:

1. Kane's own words were not weighed against Geary's "9 p. m.". Kane (p. 847): "At twilight we were removed to the batteries on the turnpike, and shortly after ordered to return". Twilight on 2 July follows sunset at 19:32, so Kane's order is about 20:00-20:30, before Geary's 9 p. m. Geary's own preceding sentence also says the enemy "discontinued their attack at about 10 p. m." just before "At 9 p. m. I ordered Kane's brigade". Greene (about 10) has Kane already fired on. Nothing in `weighing` or the `.md` states this. Fix: add to `weighing.summary` and the `.md` "Weighing": "Kane says he was ordered back shortly after twilight, which is earlier than Geary's 9 p. m.; Geary's own report puts the end of the enemy's attack at about 10 p. m. just before the 9 p. m. order. The two stay side by side; the default's lower end is Geary's printed hour, not the earliest time implied." Then change `default.animation.rule` from `earliest_documented` to `best_supported` (21:00 is not the earliest documented time; Kane's is earlier) or add a position for Kane's "shortly after twilight" (kind `period`, date 1863-07-02, part_of_day dusk) and carry it.
2. Overstatement of Greene. Greene writes "About 10 o'clock I was informed that General Kane ... was returning to his position ... He had, however, already been fired on ... General Kane soon arrived". So at about 10 Kane was returning, and arrived soon after. `default.animation.note` ("Kane's brigade is at the works (met by fire) before about 22:00 (Greene)"), position C-025-P6 label ("Kane's brigade is back at the works (Greene)"), the `.md` Decision ("Kane's brigade is at the works about 22:00") and `readers` T3-026 ("Kane about 22:00") say more than the quote. Fix: "Kane's brigade, fired on at the works, is reported returning about 22:00 and arrives soon after (Greene)". The tier of P6 stays inferred.
3. Attribution. `C-025.md` "Meade and Slocum: Geary 'having returned during the night, attacked at early dawn'" and position C-025-P1's inference step ("Meade and Slocum say Geary returned during the night and attacked at early dawn"). Only Meade (p. 117) says that. Slocum (p. 761) says Geary was ordered back, the movement was complete "nearly midnight", and orders were issued for an attack at daybreak. Fix both places to attribute the quoted words to Meade alone; cite Slocum for "nearly midnight" and the daybreak order.
4. A true disagreement is called "different acts". Geary: "At 9 p. m. I ordered Kane's brigade to return ... and Candy's to follow it", and the First Brigade "held in readiness on the turnpike" until 1 a. m. Candy: halted east of Rock Creek "until about 12 p. m.; ordered up". These give two different times and places for Candy's brigade before its placement (Geary: turnpike, ordered with Kane at 9; Candy: east of Rock Creek, ordered about midnight). Fix: in `weighing.summary` and the `.md` say that Geary and Candy disagree on when and where Candy's brigade received its order and that the default span covers both; add the same note to C-025-P8's `reason_not_chosen`. This does not change class or default.
5. Minor: `weighing.where` says "Slocum and Williams were at the headquarters of the army" (Williams' report: "called to the headquarters of the army on returning from the left"; Slocum's presence is inferred from the council). Add "Slocum" only if a source is given; otherwise write "Williams was called to the headquarters of the army".

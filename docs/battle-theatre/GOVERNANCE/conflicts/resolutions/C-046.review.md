## Special check 1: is 2,592 the editors' compilation and 2,396 Guild's own? CONFIRMED
- OR27-2 p. 334: "Grand total Army of Northern Virginia (Dr. Guild's report) 2,396 11,882 14,278". Guild signs on p. 337 (Hdqrs ANV, 1 Sep 1863, L. Guild, Medical Director). The cavalry is tabulated separately after the grand total, so 2,396 excludes it.
- OR27-2 p. 338 (Addenda, bracketed editors' note): "In computing the grand total, the figures supplied by brigade, division, and corps commanders have generally been adopted; but ... the compilation can only be regarded as approximative." The recapitulation on p. 346 gives 910 + 809 + 837 + 36 = 2,592, wounded 12,709, missing 5,150, total 20,451. Footnote on p. 329 also says "But see official compilation of losses, in Addenda, pp. 338-346". The first delegated pass was wrong to call 2,592 Guild's. Verified.
- Other checks that hold: Union 2,834 (p. 118, and p. 113, p. 17 "but see revised statement, p. 187"); 2,909 men on pp. 187 and 193 with 246 officers (3,155 by sum; Livermore p. 102 and Alexander print 3,155); Fox's 5,291 appears in all three local scans and his column sums to 5,291 (1,098+1,238+1,050+593+46+724+320+152+70); Livermore's 28,063 = 20,451 + 6,026 + 275 + 1,311; Weaver's 3,512 and "not less than seven thousand" are verbatim; Comte de Paris 2,665 is verbatim (SHSP-05, dated 4 Dec 1877); Park Service "10,000 killed or died of wounds" is on PDF page 35.

## Defects and exact fixes
1. **P11 (Guild 2,396), reason_not_chosen kind `within_range` is false.** 2,396 is below the default range low (2,592), so it is not "within" it. Change kind to `different_event` (or `outweighed`) and text to: "Guild's own return counts killed and wounded of the infantry and artillery only, without the cavalry (36 killed in the editors' table); the Official Records' aggregate 2,592 counts all arms. It lies just under the range shown." Also state in `weighing.when` why a compilation of 1889 outranks Guild's own report of Sep 1863 here (scope: no cavalry), because the plan's rule otherwise ranks the contemporaneous return first and the validator warns on that.
2. **P17 (Park Service 10,000), `inference.step` invents a figure.** "a Confederate figure near 4,700 to 5,000" is in no source read (it is the plan's Busey and Martin 4,708 plus a guess). Replace the step with: "The Park Service rounds both armies' killed and died of wounds to 10,000; it names no source. Fox's Union 5,291 leaves about 4,700 for the Confederates, but that remainder is arithmetic on a rounded figure, not a count." Drop `C-046-P16` from `from` (7,000 does not fit 10,000 - 5,291).
3. **P7 inference/md on Livermore (P14 step):** it says Livermore takes the ratio "from the Union side". Livermore (p. 103 note) says Union 1 to 4.6 and "certainly within bounds to assume ... the average of 1 to 4.8 (Reg. Losses, 22)". Fix: "assumed killed to wounded 1 to 4.8, the average he cites from Fox (Regimental Losses, p. 22); the Union ratio in the same note is 1 to 4.6."
4. **P9 (5,091 misprint of Fox).** The inference says the commission "names Fox's Regimental Losses as its source", but the cited text (NY-GB-1900) does not. That attribution is in NY-GB-1902-v3 ("Col. William F. Fox ... shows that 5,091 Union soldiers lost their lives"). Add that citation (source NY-GB-1902-v3, quote from offset 1503057). Also disclose that NY-GB-1900 prints a footnote: "This number (246+2,909) was increased to 5,091 by deaths [from wounds and missing ones who were killed]", i.e. the commission describes 5,091 as its own build-up, which leaves "misprint" an inference (keep tier inferred, keep both numbers).
5. **P10 reason text** says Weaver's count "may include the sick who died there". No source says so. Delete that clause; keep "it leaves out the wounded who died in hospitals elsewhere" only if you can cite it, otherwise write "it counts graves opened by one contractor, not men killed".
6. **Missing value.** Fox's own table (the Kirkley recapitulation of 1886) prints Union killed 3,063 (and wounded 14,492, missing 5,435, total 22,990). The JSON mentions 3,063 once in a note but there is no position. Nothing may be dropped: add a position "Union killed 3,063 (Fox's table, from Kirkley 1886)" with the Fox quote (offset 88749 region) and reason `outweighed` by the revised return 3,155.
7. **Context citations flagged exact.** P6's second cite is fine; no other exactness problem found.

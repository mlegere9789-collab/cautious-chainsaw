# C-046: dead counts of the battle

## The dispute
The plan's notes give several totals of the dead and fix none: "about 8,000 killed outright", "8,900 on the field" on 4 July, "more than 3,100 Union and 4,700 Confederate battle deaths", with 3,155 Union and 4,708 or 3,903 Confederate killed. Its rule is that the viewer shows a range, not a figure. The sources give different counts: killed on the field, killed or died of wounds, and bodies buried. They are kept apart here, army by army.

## Killed on the field, by the returns
- **Union 3,155.** The Army of the Potomac's revised return (Official Records p. 187, and the General Return p. 193: 246 officers and 2,909 men), printed by Livermore (1900): "Killed, 3155. Wounded, 14,529"; Alexander (1907) and the New York commission (1900) repeat it. In both armies the mortally wounded stand with the wounded: "the number returned as killed embraces only those who were killed or died on the field".
- **Union 2,834.** Meade's first return (report of 1 Oct 1863, p. 118; Halleck, p. 17). The volume itself adds "but see revised statement" (p. 187), so the first return is replaced.
- **Confederate 2,592.** The Official Records' aggregate (p. 346): killed 910 + 809 + 837 + 36 (cavalry); wounded 12,709, missing 5,150, total 20,451. It is not Guild's figure: the editors of 1889 compiled it from commanders' reports and call it "approximative"; the first delegated pass called it Guild's.
- **Confederate 2,396.** Guild's own return (p. 334), infantry and artillery, killed and wounded only.
- **Confederate 2,665.** Given as "official figures" by the Comte de Paris in 1877, with no return named.

## Killed or died of wounds, and bodies
- **Union 5,291.** William F. Fox counted the regimental rolls name by name (1889). The New York volumes print 5,091, a misprint of his figure: three scans of Fox and his table, which sums to 5,291, agree.
- **Union 3,512 bodies.** Samuel Weaver, who superintended the disinterment, report of 19 Mar 1864: 3,354 to the National Cemetery and 158 taken up by Boston, 979 of them nameless. A count of graves opened, not of men killed.
- **Confederate, not less than 7,000 bodies.** Weaver, 1864: "of the rebel bodies buried on this battle field and at the hospitals, and I place the number at not less than seven thousand bodies". An estimate, by the man who opened the graves; it counts the buried dead, including those who died in hospital there.
- **Confederate 3,903.** Livermore's estimate: the return plus 1,311 assumed so that killed stand to wounded as 1 to 4.8. Computed, not counted. **4,708:** Busey and Martin, quoted by the plan; the book is not in the library.
- **Both armies, about 10,000.** The Park Service (2018), rounded.
- **8,000, 8,900, 3,100 and 4,700.** No source found.

## Weighing
The Union counts differ because they count different things, and each is explained by what it counts. The Confederate counts differ in what they count and in size, and no source separates a count from an estimate: the returns are known to be short (slightly wounded excluded, no return for one brigade), the one roll-based Confederate count does not exist, and the large figures are estimates. Each end rests on one line of knowledge.

## Decision
Class **irreducible**, tier **documented**. Default: a **range, army by army**, no single figure: Union 3,155 killed on the field to 5,291 with the died of wounds; Confederate 2,592 killed on the field to at least 7,000 bodies buried. The plan's rule holds; the first pass's basis does not (the 2,592 is the editors', and the Union return and Fox are in hand).

## DEC-008 consequence (reported, not edited)
No crowd count changes: the soldiers follow printed unit strength. The share of drawn men who fall dead rather than wounded uses the killed-on-the-field fractions; if the owner takes killed-and-died-of-wounds for the Union, that share would be about 23 per cent of the loss rather than 13.7.

## What would change it
A Confederate roll count of killed and died of wounds, the Confederate hospital returns, Busey and Martin read at the source, or the page image of the Union return.

## Searches that failed

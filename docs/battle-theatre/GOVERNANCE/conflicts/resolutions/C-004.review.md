## D1: Scherrer's regiment is put in the wrong brigade; the independence and the P9 reason are wrong

`C-004.json` P9 citation: witness role "Captain, Fifty-second New York (Brooke's brigade, First Division)". Scherrer's own report (OR27-1-R089, p.395-396) says "the regiment ... was ordered by General Zook, commanding this brigade": the 52nd New York was in Zook's (Third) Brigade, not Brooke's (Fourth). McMichael's 53rd Pennsylvania is Brooke's. Consequences:

- P9 `reason_not_chosen` (kind `place_of_observation`): "These are regiments of one brigade, whose commander has the brigade there soon after sunrise" is false for Scherrer.
- `weighing.independence`: "Brooke, McMichael and Scherrer are one brigade giving three hours" is wrong: Scherrer (Zook, about 10) is independent of Brooke's brigade (Brooke soon after sunrise; McMichael about 8).
- `C-004.md`: "Two regiments of Brooke's brigade give about 8 (McMichael) and about 10 (Scherrer)" is wrong in the same way.

Fix: witness role for Scherrer "Fifty-second New York (Zook's brigade, First Division)"; P9 reason and the independence text as above; `.md` sentence. Note also that McMichael (8) and Brooke (soon after sunrise) disagree inside one brigade; say so in the weighing.

## D2: McMichael's citation carries the date of his other report

OR27-1-R095 prints two reports by McMichael: the first, "Near Sandy Hook, Md., July 17, 1863", says "arrived on the field about 8 a. m. July 3" (as the text layer reads it); the second, "Hdqrs. Fifty-third Pennsylvania Volunteers, August 3, 1863", says "I arrived on the field with my command about 8 a. m. on July 2" (offset 1437398, p.410). The quoted words are the second report's, but `written.date` is `1863-07-17` in P5 and P9. Fix: `written.date` `1863-08-03`; and record in the weighing that his first report gives the day as the 3d (as printed; check the page image) so the day comes from the second report only. `.md` ("Smyth, McMichael and Webb in July") must say August for McMichael.

## D3: a reconciliation that the text of Hancock's report contradicts

`weighing.summary`, `how_knew` and `.md`: Hancock "rode ahead and rejoined the corps on the road, so his hour may be his own arrival". Hancock's sentence (OR27-1 p.369, offset 1287468) is "I rejoined the corps before its arrival on the field, which took place about 7 a. m. of the 2d": the antecedent is the corps's arrival, not his. The man who commanded the corps, with it on the road, dates its arrival at about 7; the dispatch (6.15, "is up") and Haskell ("before six") date it earlier; Webb has a brigade put in position at 6.30. Keep the rank-based choice if wished (a dispatch of the day and a letter of July beat a report written later), but delete "may be his own arrival" and state the real disagreement: commander present at the corps's arrival, about 7, against the dispatch and the letter. State that Haskell says the corps halted beside the Taneytown road "while some movements of the other troops were being made" before taking position, which is a way both can be true (up to the field, then on the ridge), and that no source says so for Hancock.

# Review of C-031 (Culp's Hill final charge): DEFECTS (minor)

Reviewer: independent, 2026-11-24. Validator passes. Quotes read in context.

## What is sound
- Geary ("At 10.25 o'clock two brigades of Johnson's division ... charged upon our line ... This was the last charge"; "This final charge, made at 10.30 a. m."), Kane, Rickards, Greene ("continued until 10.30 a. m., when the enemy retired"), Slocum, Maulsby ("until the enemy's fire wholly ceased, about 12 m."), Williams ("at intervals until 12 m.") are real and not stretched. Hedges ("about") kept. Tier documented is right: Geary and Greene are 1863 official reports, exact. Single Confederate hour (Williams) is flagged as its own event. Pierce is correctly low, single-source, weak. No invented drawn point (default instant 10:30 is Geary's and Greene's own figure).

## Defects and exact fixes
1. **Hunt's 10 a.m. is explained as a "different event" more confidently than the text allows.** Hunt: batteries "continued firing at intervals until 10 a. m., when the infantry succeeded in driving out the enemy and reoccupied their positions". That places the driving-out at 10, while Greene (retired 10.30), Slocum (regaining the line 10.30) and Geary (rout after the 10.25 charge) place it later. Ruger's 10 a.m. is plausibly the start of Colgrove's counter-attack (his next sentence says so), but Hunt, writing 12 weeks later and relaying, is a round figure for the same recovery. Meade ("until after 10 a. m.") is only a lower bound and says nothing of a counter-attack. Fix: split `P1.reason_not_chosen`: keep `different_event` for Ruger, and say for Hunt and Meade "relayed and rounded; Meade's 'after 10' is consistent with 10.30"; delete from the `.md` and `P1.inference.step` the claim that "Ruger, Hunt and Meade all say about 10 a.m. for the Union counter-attack" (Meade does not). Class settled can stay if this explanation (rounding and relay) is written in `weighing`.
2. **Independence of the 10.30 group is slightly generous.** Rickards (29th PA) belongs to Kane's brigade, and Kane and Greene belong to Geary's division, whose report of 29 July postdates both brigade reports and may draw on them. Fix: in `weighing.independence` say "three line witnesses (Greene, Kane/Rickards, Geary as division commander on the spot); Geary may draw on the brigade reports, so Geary and Kane are not counted as independent of each other beyond their own observation". Tier is unaffected (Greene alone suffices).
3. **Greene quote is stitched across 200 characters.** "The First Brigade returned to our support at 1.30 a. m. on the 3d, [...] continued until 10.30 a. m., when the enemy retired" drops the subject ("the attack ... was general on our line, and"), so the quote alone says the First Brigade's return continued until 10.30. Fix: cite "the attack was general on our line, and continued until 10.30 a. m., when the enemy retired" and keep the "1.30 a. m. on the 3d" fragment as a separate second citation for the day.
4. P3 label "last charge (from 10:25) is beaten back and the enemy retires" merges the charge start (Geary 10.25; Kane and Rickards "about 10.30" for its start) with the retirement (Greene 10.30). That is acceptable under "about 10:30" but the panel should say "begins 10.25 to 10.30".

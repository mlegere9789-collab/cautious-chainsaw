## Defects and exact fixes
1. **Birney's "About 11 o'clock" (JCCW p. 366, cite 2 of C-009-P1) is the time he asked Sickles for leave, not the time of the order to Berdan.** His text: "About 11 o'clock ... I asked permission of Major General Sickles ... I received from General Sickles permission to do so." No hour is given for the permission. His report of 1863 says "At 12 m ... I received permission". The two Birney accounts are therefore not shown to be contradictory (request about 11, grant at noon is compatible), yet the weighing says they "contradict each other", the .md says "A man's two accounts cannot both be right", P3's reason says "Birney's own sworn account of 1864 says about 11", and `holds.first_delegated` says "Birney's own later account moves to 11". Fix: (a) set `exact:false` on this citation (it states a request time), and flag C-009-P1 `single_source` because the hour of the order then rests on Berdan alone; (b) rewrite those four sentences to "Birney's testimony dates his request for leave at about 11; his report dates Sickles's permission at 12 m.; these can both be true, but Berdan says his order came about 11, before that permission"; (c) change "two groups ... no flag" in `weighing.independence` and the panel text/basis accordingly ("Birney's sworn account puts his request at about 11" is acceptable; "agrees with Berdan" is not). The .md line "The later account agrees with Berdan" needs the same softening.
2. **`default.note` says "contact is reported at 11:45 to 11:55 by the signal station".** Jerome's messages do not name the sharpshooters or the detachment (the resolution says so itself in P5's reason). Replace "contact" with "skirmishing a mile west of Round Top".
3. **Hunt's sworn testimony (JCCW p. 449) is used in the .md but is not a citation in the JSON.** It is real and important: at "About 11 o'clock" Hunt "had just come into headquarters", was sent with Sickles to examine the line, and only afterward heard Sickles say he "would send in a couple of companies of Berdan's sharpshooters". That puts Sickles's decision after 11 plus a ride out. Add it as a citation (e.g. on C-009-P3 or a new research position "after about 11") and one sentence in `weighing.summary`; it bears directly on how firm "about 11:00" is as the order hour (the animation floor of 11:00 is still safe).
4. **Stevens (P3, cite 3) is given `group: "birney"`** although Stevens names no source and the plan's rule counts a group only where one relays another. Give him `group: "stevens"`. (Noon still has two groups, Birney 1863 and Hunt, so no flag changes.) Garrett's `witness.role` ("Private or non-commissioned member") is a guess; Buchanan prints only "Mr. F. E. Garrett of Knox, Pa., formerly of Company D"; use "member of Company D, 1st US Sharpshooters (rank not stated)".

# Review of C-019 (Johnson's attack on Culp's Hill)

Reviewer: independent max-effort pass, 2026-11-24. Validator: 0 errors, 0 warnings. Quotes re-found and read in context: Greene p. 856 (also his 6.30 p. m. departure of the First Division and "About 8 p. m. the enemy appeared on our right flank"), Geary pp. 826-827, Moseley p. 535, Steuart p. 509, Jones p. 532, Ewell p. 447, Greene in Battles and Leaders (1887).

**Verdict: DEFECTS (4 to fix; default and class stand)**

## What holds
- Greene's "a few minutes before 7 p. m." is exact, from the brigade commander who was attacked, ten days after; the hedge (an upper bound) is kept; the default is correctly flagged `single_source` (Greene, with his own later article and Geary's duration counted with him) and weighted. 19:30 is correctly inferred and 20:00 correctly an unsourced gap with failed lookups; no source gives 20:00 for the start (the 8 p. m. readings are the 21st Virginia falling back and Greene's right-flank attack, correctly P6). The Confederate hours (about 6 advance, about 5 pickets relieved, dusk, darkness in the woods) are correctly typed as approach and periods. No clock offset is applied. "Five other values" is right.

## Defects and exact fixes
1. **Greene's 1887 article is filed as support of C-019-P1 but dates something else, and later.** "When Meade ordered the whole of the Twelfth Corps from Culp's Hill ... The rest of the corps moved off just before dusk (about 7 P. M.) I immediately extended my men" is the hour the corps left, and he extends afterward; it implies an attack after 7, and it differs from his own report (corps ordered away at 6.30, attack a few minutes before 7). It does not state the attack hour, yet its citation carries `states.times: ["19:00"]`. Fix: remove it from P1's support (keep it in the weighing as the later, softer account, as the weighing already does), or move it to P2 as a witness that the sequence ran later. Geary's "two and a half hours" is similarly a duration only and is correctly `exact:false`; keep it as corroboration of the 7 to 9.30 span, not of 19:00.
2. **C-019-P4 (about 18:00) is unflagged but only one writer states 18:00.** Moseley says about 6 p. m.; Steuart says pickets were relieved "at about 5 o'clock ... and shortly afterward the brigade was formed ... and moved forward", and his citation states 17:00. The independence paragraph ("the advance about 6 is Moseley and Steuart") is therefore wrong. Fix: add flag `single_source` to P4, reword the independence paragraph to "about 6 rests on Moseley; Steuart puts his brigade's move shortly after 5 (a different brigade, a different count)", and add the 17:00 pickets relief to the P4 label text rather than letting it read as a second witness to 18:00.
3. **Ewell's `written.date` 1863-12-31 is invented.** The printed dateline of his report is ", 1803" (the year only, month and day blank); the weighing itself says "undated in the print, year 1863". Use year "1863" (as is done for Stevens "1892"), and keep the day out of any computed gap.
4. **The Union order of events on the day is not fully used.** Geary's report says at 7 p. m. he got orders to leave, the First Division "had gone nearly half an hour previously" (6.30), his column was shelled on the way, and only at 7.30 was he told to hold; "In the meantime" Greene had barely occupied Kane's position "when a vigorous attack was made". That reads as an attack after 7.00, perhaps after 7.30, in a Union witness who is not Greene. The weighing mentions only the 7.30 order and calls it a sequence. Add Geary's 7 p. m. and the shelling to `weighing.summary` and the .md as a Union witness whose order of events puts the attack after 19:00, and state that this is the strongest ground for keeping 19:30 selectable; no change to the default is required.
Also: set C-019-P5's `clock` to `unspecified` (it mixes Confederate and Union citations), and give P1 a `tolerance_min` or keep `approx` with a note, since "a few minutes before 7" is an upper bound, not 19:00.

## Not defects, noted
- The panel text is true to the record and within 400 characters.
- The reader of C-019 should note, in `readers`, that the Greene upper bound (19:00) is earlier than Geary's order sequence; no gate value changes.

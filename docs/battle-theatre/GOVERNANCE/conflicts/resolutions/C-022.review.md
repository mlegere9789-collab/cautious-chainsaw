## D2 (minor): `weighing.where` / `.md` use Sedgwick's order of events against his own hour without saying so

The `.md` argues that the order of Sedgwick's own story (Meade out at the front, back "a few minutes" later, the enemy having "opened the battle") fits a later hour. That is an inference about when Sedgwick reported, not when the head arrived; Sedgwick says "I arrived ... about 2 o'clock" and "I arrived on the field, as I have said, about 2 o'clock" separately from reporting.

## Defects

### D1 (major): a witness who dates the death to the night of 2 July was missed; the statements "nothing says Jul 2" are false

Butterfield (Meade's chief of staff), sworn testimony, "Tuesday, March 29, 1864", Joint Committee volume (`JCCW-1865-C012`, same text as `JCCW-1865-V1-C039`, text offset about 2000296, printed page 434 or 435, check the image), says:

"Before the battle was over (on the night of the 2d) it was reported that General Barksdale was on the field wounded, and desired to be brought in; but he died before we reached him. I sent for him, but he was dead when found."

(The text layer is damaged: "nighc", "b:fo»e", "fovmd". Cite with `quote_mode: "ocr"`, `raw` exactly as in the file, `page_image_viewed: true`; the plain quote is not found by `find_passage.py`.)

This is a staff officer at army headquarters who "sent for him", writing within a year, and it contradicts Doubleday (report of Dec 1863), the Vermont volume and the 149th Pennsylvania, who have him alive when brought in (dying messages to Livingston; "the next day he died"). It is the only source found that dates the death to the night of the 2d. Consequences:

- `C-037.json` P1 (Jul 2) is flagged `unsourced`, tier `open gap`, reason `no_source`, with failed lookups saying nothing in the library gives it. That is false. Fix: add the Butterfield citation to P1 (vantage `staff_present`, knew_by `own_orders` or `relayed_report`, clock `union`, written 1864-03-29, kind `early_narrative` or the nearest valid kind; testimony is not an `official_report`), set P1 tier `inferred` (step: "night of the 2d" plus "died before we reached him" puts the death before the day is called the 3d; midnight is not stated), flag `single_source`, change the reason from `no_source` to the real comparison (`outweighed` or `equal_standing`, written in `weighing`). Remove or correct the P1 failed lookups (they must not claim the library was searched when `JCCW-1865-C012` was not).
- `weighing.summary` ("Nothing says he died on Jul 2 before midnight"), `source_panel_text` ("No source found says Jul 2 for the death"), `C-037.md` ("Not found: any account that says he died on Jul 2") and `failed_searches` all state something the library contradicts. Rewrite each.
- Class. With Butterfield (night of the 2d, dead when found) against Doubleday, Vermont and the Pennsylvania history (alive when found, died on the 3d), and Meade and the Times only fixing "dead by 08:00", the record is divided on whether he died before or after midnight.

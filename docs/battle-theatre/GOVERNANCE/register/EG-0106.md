## History

### 2026-11-11: ws9 library pass (decision: applied-narrowed)

The table above is unchanged and remains the entry text of record before this note. Source texts are archive.org OCR (unverified against page images). Review record: horde/ws9-sources/APPLIED.md.

- Fremantle (diary, 2 Jul): up at 3.30 A.M.; "arrived at 5 A. M. at the same commanding position" where "Generals Lee, Hill, Longstreet, and Hood" were "in consultation" (p.257): scenario value 05:00 for Hood and Longstreet with Lee. Tier inferred (one diary witness, clock his own).
- Fremantle p.257: "At Y A. M. I rode over part of the ground with General Longstreet, and saw him disposing of M'Laws's division". The OCR prints the hour as "Y"; 7 is a reading inferred from the sequence of hours, NOT a printed value. Recorded as "07:00 (OCR-uncertain; page-image check required)" and not to be used as a scenario clock until checked.
- Alexander 1907 (participant, retrospective) p.391: "About 11 A.M., his orders were issued." Second retrospective witness for 11:00; independence from Longstreet 1896 not shown.
- Hood attack: Alexander p.394 Hood crossed the Emmitsburg road "about 3 P.M."; p.395 "about 3.45 P.M., 36 guns were in action" and "After this cannonade had continued for perhaps 30 minutes, Hood received the order to advance" (an order about 16:15 is arithmetic from those two statements, not printed). Fremantle p.258-259: "A dead silence reigned till 4.45 p. m." then "Longstreet suddenly commenced a heavy cannonade". Law pp.320, 323: "past 4 o'clock" in position; "near 5 o'clock p. M. when we advanced to the attack"; artillery "warmly engaged for about fifteen minutes". The clocks are mutually inconsistent within about an hour; they support a window, not a point.
- Law p.319: moved at 3 o'clock on the morning of the 2d, "arrived there shortly before noon" (his brigade only, not Hood himself).
- Fremantle p.258 "Pickett being still in the rear" on 2 Jul is noted for C-021 only; gate Q04 not moved.
- The register statement that no OR report gives an hour remains true. Tier stays inferred (nothing promoted), status stays open.

| Description | Row T1-203 (Jul 1 NIGHT). Verdict gap. Corrected claim: No passage in the Official Records, Letterman's report, the state commission volumes read or the 1863 newspapers describes wounded left between the lines, burial parties or water details on the night of Jul 1. Letterman gives whole-battle statements only (the medical officers worked 'day and night' to Jul 6). The National Park Service first-day inventory says some Confederate troops stayed behind to bury their dead on the first-day field, specifically Heth's division and details from Rodes' division; the passage sits in the second-day paragraph and gives no hour. The row stays an open gap and is shown as such. Added by the second reading: the Sanitary Commission's report of Aug. 15, 1863 (Major Bush) says that most of the wounded of Jul 1 were carried into the churches and public buildings of the town and were prisoners at the close of the day, and puts the Jul 1 wounded of the First Corps hospital in the town at 400 to 450 including private houses; this places the greater part of the first day's wounded, not the wounded 'between the lines', and does not date any burial or water detail. Still unsupported or open: Wounded left between the lines, burial parties and water details on the night of Jul 1 / Wounded left between the lines, burial parties and water details on the night of Jul 1 (the places of most of the wounded are documented, the rest is not). |

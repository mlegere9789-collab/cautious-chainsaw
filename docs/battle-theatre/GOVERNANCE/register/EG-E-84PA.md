| Category | conflict between sources (PDF order of battle against the regiment's own statement and the Official Records); documented absence (nothing to draw) |
| Description | The PDF order of battle (history/units.json, B.3) lists the 84th Pennsylvania (Carr's brigade, Humphreys' division, Third Corps) as on the field, and the layer gave it a procedural share of 249 figures. The regiment's own address (Captain Merchant, Pennsylvania at Gettysburg vol. 1, pp. 474-475; claims POS-E-US-3C-2D-CARR-84PA-001 and -002, documented), Lt. Col. Opp's report (OR27-1-R168 p. 557; claims POS-B-us-3c-2d-carr-84pa-01 to -05, documented) and the Official Records' order-of-battle table (OR27-1-R009 pp. 162-169, footnote "guarding corps trains, and not engaged in the battle"; POS-B-us-3c-2d-carr-84pa-06, documented) put it with the supply train: detailed on the night of 30 June at Taneytown to guard the Second Division's wagon train, on 1 July four miles with the train on the road to Emmitsburg, then back and all night with it to Westminster (arrived 7 a. m. 2 July), picketing the roads near the wagon parks 2 to 5 July, marched with the train to Uniontown on 6 July. General Carr's letter printed in the address (POS-E-US-3C-2D-CARR-84PA-003, inferred) puts the regiment with the train until relieved on 6 July. No claim puts the regiment on the field. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | Not blocked: the record documents the absence. The one thing the record does not give is a minute-by-minute place for each day (the train's march is given by day, not by hour), so the card states the places and days as printed and nothing finer. |

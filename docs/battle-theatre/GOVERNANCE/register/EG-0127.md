### 2026-11-11: ws9 library pass (decision: applied-narrowed)

The table above is unchanged and remains the entry text of record before this note. Source texts are archive.org OCR (unverified against page images). Review record: horde/ws9-sources/APPLIED.md.

- E-001 (Nov 18), narrowed: Everett p.20 "In the afternoon of the 18th, the President and the distinguished personages accompanying him arrived at Gettysburg, by a special train. In the course of the evening, the President and Secretary of State were serenaded, and the following remarks were made by Mr. Seward" (p.20-21); the Sentinel prints that Seward "was serenaded at his lodgings in the Square" with "a brief speech" (no date printed in the fragment). Sub-claims documented: arrival by special train in the afternoon of the 18th; evening serenade; Seward remarks. NOT supported: 18:00 hour, William McDougall, walk uphill to the Wills house, lodging location. The Sentinel line "President ... serenaded twice during the evening" sits in an undated dispatch column (OCR) and is NOT used.
- E-008 (Marine Band), applied-narrowed: Everett p.12 "The Presidential party was accompanied by the Marine Band from the Navy Yard at Washington"; programme p.25 lists "Music, by the Marine Band." after the prayer; the Sentinel procession list shows a Marine Band entry. Sub-claim tier documented for presence with the Presidential party and for the programmed music; that it played at the platform at that point is programmed, not reported as performed.
- E-005 (troops salute), applied-narrowed: only the programme p.24 states the military will form in line "for the purpose of saluting the President" (OCR "saluthig"). A plan, not an account. Sub-claim tier: inferred (planned). No report of the act was found.
- E-014 (church), applied-narrowed with rewording: Sentinel prints "at 5 o'clock p. M. Col. Anderson ... delivered an able address in the Presbyterian church" with "The President of the United States and suite ... occupied seats in the pulpit", and that John Burns "went to church in the evening" arm in arm with the President and Secretary of State (OCR noisy). Reworded sub-claim: 5 p.m. address by Col. Anderson in the Presbyterian church with the President present (the day is placed as the 19th by the article's order, the fragment itself prints no date). Tier documented (one local newspaper, as printed). A religious service and "leaves by train about 18:00" are NOT supported; the Sentinel prints only "left late in the evening".
- E-002: no statement found; unchanged gap.

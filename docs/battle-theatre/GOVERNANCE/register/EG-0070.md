# EG-0070: Road of approach modelled from the sibling formations of the corps (history layer v6)

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | EG-0070 |
| Requirement IDs | R03, R10, R11, R21 |
| Category | evidence missing |
| Description | No report of the Official Records read for the position ledgers (OR vol. 27) prints a road of approach for 56 leaf units (Longstreet's corps except Kershaw, Semmes, Wofford and Pickett's brigades; the Fifth Corps except the 20th Maine and Day's division) or for any formation above them. Each is drawn by the road that every documented sibling formation of its corps names (Chambersburg Pike for the First Corps, from Kershaw's report; Hanover Road for the Fifth Corps, from the 20th Maine row and Day's division report). The sibling formations may not have been on the road the unit used. |
| Why literal 100% is not possible or blocked | The record read so far does not state the unit's road; the primary sources are unread in part (EG-0005) and the Official Records give only some brigades' routes. Literal documentation of the road of these 56 units is not available from the repository. |
| Evidence searched | The four OR27 position ledgers (history/positions/positions_{A,B,C,D}.jsonl, 3,503 claims), every claim that names a road on the lines of command (APPROACH-AUDIT.md, data/sibling-review.json), the road-name concordance (library/ROAD-CONCORDANCE.md). Not yet searched: the regimental and brigade reports not in the ledgers, Busey and Martin, state regimental histories. |
| Autonomous substitute applied | Sibling-road rule (DEC-015): the units take the road documented for the other formations of their corps only if every documented sibling names the same road and no report on the unit's line of command names another road, a road the repository cannot place or a road outside the loaded ground (tools/sibling-rule.mjs, data/sibling-approach.json). |
| Strongest evidence-based reconstruction | A MODELLED road of approach (plausible-procedural). The unit's gate (never moved), its single-label Bachelder day position and its march from the edge of the ground are as for any other drawn unit; only the road, the minute at the edge and the place in the column are modelled. |

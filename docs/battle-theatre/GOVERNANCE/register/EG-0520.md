| Description | Row T1-178 (Jul 1 evening). Verdict corrected. Corrected claim: The seed's order of events is corrected: at Two Taverns Williams learned that the First and Eleventh Corps were engaged (not yet that they had retired); the division then moved up the pike and turned by a cross-road toward the Hanover road to seize a commanding hill east of the town; the hill was found held by the enemy and, when the division was about to assault, it was ordered to withdraw because the Union forces had retired behind the town. It then took a position nearer the Baltimore pike and bivouacked; Ruger puts the move to a position three-quarters of a mile to the rear 'about sunset'. Slocum turned the temporary command of the corps over to Williams and Ruger took the division. The reports do not name Culp's Hill for the hill east of the town. Still unsupported or open: The eminence east of the town is Culp's Hill / That the hill east of the town was Culp's Hill (Williams does not name it). |

### 2026-11-11: ws9 library pass (decision: applied-narrowed)

The table above is unchanged and remains the entry text of record before this note. Source texts are archive.org OCR (unverified against page images). Review record: horde/ws9-sources/APPLIED.md.

- Letterman (Medical Director, Army of the Potomac) p.158: "Before daylight on the morning of the 4th all the wounded within our lines (numbering on these two days about twelve thousand) were removed to the hospitals." Sub-claim tier: documented (single authoritative witness, official memoir published 1866): aggregate removal of about 12,000 wounded to the hospitals before daylight on 4 Jul. It does not give the routes, the carry paths or hospital-by-hospital flow.
- pp.159-160: "The wounded in these three days amounted to fourteen thousand one hundred and ninety-three, and those of the enemy ... six thousand eight hundred and two, making twenty thousand nine hundred and ninety-five" (14,193 + 6,802 = 20,995, arithmetic checked). One Medical Director range member for wounded in Union care; not a dead count.
- p.158-159 (not quoted verbatim here): Surgeon Henry Janes took charge of the wounded; four wagons and six ambulances per corps were retained for supplies and for the wounded to be sent on. Burial returns, carry paths, 4-6 Jul per-corps counts: unchanged and open.

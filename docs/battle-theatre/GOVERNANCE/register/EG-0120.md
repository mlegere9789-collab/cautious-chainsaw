## History

### 2026-11-11: ws9 library pass (decision: applied-narrowed)

The table above is unchanged and remains the entry text of record before this note. Source texts are archive.org OCR (unverified against page images). Review record: horde/ws9-sources/APPLIED.md.

- Procession start: Everett p.24 programme "The head of the column will move at precisely ten o'clock, A. M." (a plan); Adams Sentinel (OCR) "About ten o'clock ... the line of the procession was formed, and moved forward to the grounds of the Cemetery" (the OCR reads "litoe", "pro cession"; line read as printed context). Carried as scenario values beside the existing ones, not as resolution of the "about 11:00 actual" value.
- Dirge position: the programme (Everett p.25) lists Dedicatory Remarks by the President then "Dirge, sung by Choir selected for the occasion" then Benediction. That is the PLANNED order only (supports the "after Lincoln" scenario as planned, not as performed). It does not settle the NPS "after Everett" value.
- Crowd: Adams Sentinel (OCR "thousah d") "estimated ... from twenty to forty thousand": new upper scenario value, a newspaper estimate as printed.
- Sky: Adams Sentinel (OCR "tinclouded") Thursday dawned "with an unclouded sky" (OCR-reconstructed word). A scenario value for the morning; not resolved against other values.
- Address text: the Everett pamphlet prints "that the nation shall, under God, have a new birth of freedom" (one printed version for the selectable text set; the delivered wording is not established by it).
- Departure: Sentinel prints "President Lincoln and party left late in the evening in a special car."

First read docs/battle-theatre/GOVERNANCE/briefs/r1e-common.md (rules shared by all three agents).

PROBLEM (R1d final report): ANIM B11 (6 to 8 long tasks at 4x playback against 3 allowed), B14 and POL-02 are 'flaky under load'; the previous agents (PERF2/PERF3) found: playback frame cost at 4x CPU on the phone core.step mean 5.1-6.7 ms (p95 9-15 ms), draw 1.2-2.1 ms, popups.update 0.3-0.6 ms; sliced cold layout already shipped. Flaky means the code is within a hair of its budget: the owner requires 'crash free, reliable and lightning quick buttery smooth reactions for every user input'. A test that passes sometimes is a defect until the cost margin is large enough that it passes every time on a loaded machine.

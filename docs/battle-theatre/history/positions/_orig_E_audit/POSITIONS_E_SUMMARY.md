## 5. Findings the owner should see (for the Decision Log)

1. **84th Pennsylvania was not on the battlefield.** The regiment's own statement and the letter of its brigade commander, Brig. Gen. Joseph B. Carr (PA1B p. 474-475, documented), place it with the supply train on July 1-6; the trains were ordered to report at Westminster; the regiment reported to Carr again at Williamsport on July 6. The Battle Theatre roster draws it as part of Carr's brigade. Claims POS-E-US-3C-2D-CARR-84PA-001..003. Under the Law (no unit before its documented arrival; no fabrication) the roster presence of this regiment on July 2-3 needs an owner decision; nothing is changed here.
2. **First Brigade (Carr) came in by the Black Horse Tavern road.** 26th PA: "we marched into the Confederate lines near the Black Horse Tavern ... resumed the right road to Gettysburg" at the midnight of July 1-2 (PA1B p. 197, documented, POS-E-US-3C-2D-CARR-26PA-002). This is the only printed text found that describes the Black Horse Tavern approach as a route actually taken (it did not cross the loaded ground; the column turned back).
3. **Horner's Mills.** The 45th NY committee (NY1 p. 378) and the commission narrative (NY1 p. 15) confirm the corps' route by Horner's Mills; the 154th NY sketch and the 73rd PA address print the Emmitsburg Pike / the Emmitsburg-Taneytown junction for the Second Division. The last leg into the town is "a shorter route" (not named). Audit reason route_not_a_road is confirmed, with an added Emmitsburg-road alternative for the Second Division.
4. **Hour conflicts inside the Second Division** (all carried, none chosen): the 27th PA about noon; Steinwehr about 2 o'clock (OR); Allan's 3 o'clock march out of the cemetery; the 154th NY sketch about 4 p. m.
5. **Union cavalry itineraries disagree** (Hunt: Buford via Boonsboro and Fairfield; Heermance: via Leesburg, Poolesville, Frederick, Emmitsburg; Markell: entered at the head of the advance June 30). 5th NY (11 a. m. July 1 move to Berlin) and 18th PA (left Hanover about 2 p. m. June 30, Berlin by July 1 night) differ by a day on the Abbottstown to Berlin march.
6. **Gaps.** 71st New York (NY2 p. 572): inscription OCR unreadable, no claim written; a second witness of New York volume 2 was not found (the copies on archive.org: `finalreportonba00chatgoog` is the only scan with that text). 82nd Ohio, 61st Ohio, 55th Ohio, 73rd Ohio, 33rd Massachusetts, 1st, 11th and 16th Massachusetts (only the 1st by a sister account), 12th New Hampshire, 11th New Jersey, 1st Ohio K, 4th US G (Wilkeson: OR only), 8th and 12th Illinois, 3rd Indiana, 3rd West Virginia, 1st Vermont, 1st West Virginia, Horse Artillery: no state volume held. 74th PA: the address prints no Gettysburg movement narrative (no claim).

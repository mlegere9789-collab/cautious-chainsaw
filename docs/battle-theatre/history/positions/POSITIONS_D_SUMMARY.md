# Positions and approach roads, Confederate side (scope D): summary

Built by `tools/bt/positions_summary.py` from `positions_D.jsonl` (written by `tools/bt/positions_build.py` from the claim modules in `tools/bt/positions_D/`). Nothing here changes a default, gate or tier elsewhere; conflicts are carried, never resolved.

- Claims: **413** (arrival_road 78, departure 26, engagement 134, march 31, movement 22, night_position 58, position 54, strength_note 10). Tiers: documented 396, inferred 14, open gap 3. Reports cited: 139 distinct records. Claims in a conflict family: 201.
- Every anchor was located by code inside its record (`or27_find`/`or27_index` span); the printed page is computed from the recovered running heads, and a page marked interpolated carries the range. **Every documented claim carries a verbatim quote of fewer than 15 words** (396 of 396 documented claims; 399 claims in all), verified present in the cited record by code (`F.verify`) and re-sampled by `tools/bt/verify_positions.py`. The quote is a window of the claim's own anchor chosen by code (overlap with the claim text), or an explicit quote in the claim module / `tools/bt/positions_D_overrides.json`. Where a claim also states a clock hour that the main quote does not contain, a second verified extract with that hour is carried in `source.additional_quotes` (150 claims). The one-quote-per-passage limit of the brief was applied per claim here, as the coordinator's rule requires a supporting extract on every documented claim. Where no extract supported a time statement, the time was removed from the claim (`when.description` says "no hour printed in this passage") rather than kept as documented. The open-gap and inferred claims carry no quote by design (searches tried, or the reasoning).
- `when.printed` is the clock or date phrase as printed (OCR errors kept), taken first from the claim's own extracts, then from the text next to the anchor, and, before the re-check, as a last step the nearest matching phrase elsewhere in the same report (`when.printed_basis`). **After the full-passage re-check (see RECHECK_D_NOTES.md) no claim carries `printed_basis`**: a printed phrase is kept only where it sits in the same sentence as the fact or in a sentence that explicitly continues it, and every kept phrase lies inside one of the claim's own verified extracts; a time or date taken from another sentence is moved to `when.description` ("time in a different sentence: ...") and `when.printed` is `null` with `when.printed_note` (72 claims). `when.date_basis` says where a date comes from when no phrase of the fact's own sentence carries it; `when.meridian_inferred` marks an hour printed without a.m./p.m. whose p. m./a. m. is the compiler's reading. `t_iso` is set only where the printed phrase carries a legible hour; where the OCR digit is damaged (e.g. "Z p. m.", "S o'clock") no `t_iso` is set. `when.description` / `place.description` are the author reading. `place.printed` is extracted the same way; `gazetteer_ref` is manual or, where marked, auto from a verbatim printed name in a safe list.
- Secondary sources (Battles and Leaders, state volumes) were not needed: every claim rests on an OR passage (Part 1, 2 or 3).
- **Full-passage fidelity re-check (2026-10, `RECHECK_D_NOTES.md`)**: every claim was re-read against at least 1,200 characters of the raw OR text on each side of its anchor. fidelity_check: passed_full_read 188, corrected 224, downgraded 1, not_rechecked 0. `claim_support_verified` is true only for passed_full_read and corrected claims. Claims split into one claim per report: see the Split-from note on the new claims (PD-cav-jenkins-03, PD-cav-robertson-02, PD-cav-flee-04, PD-cav-chambliss-03..05, PD-1c-mclaws-semmes-06 and -07). Six units were re-rated full to partial (see the why column).

SYSTEMATIC
- Day-part parentheses '(afternoon, July 1)', '(forenoon, July 1)', '(early afternoon, ...)' were added by the builder to nearly every claim without a clock in its sentence. Where the report prints only a morning arrival time (Morrow 9 a.m., Wainwright 10.30 a.m., Baxter 11 a.m.) the later day-part is not derivable from the sequence and was removed. Where the report's own preceding sentences set the day-part ('In the afternoon' in Gamble's report, 'At 12 m.' in D. Gregg's) I kept the reading and annotated it in wd.
- place.printed was often a composite of two sentences (position from the July 1 paragraph used for the July 2 fight; 'Gregg's line', 'Union left'). Builders should print only words of the cited sentence.
- gazetteer ids are by inference on most of these; I added note_add where the place itself is not printed.
- Many EXT claims rest on a brigade or division report that names the regiment once (single-sentence mention); I passed them only when unit, event and (where given) clock were in that sentence or its explicitly continuing sentences.

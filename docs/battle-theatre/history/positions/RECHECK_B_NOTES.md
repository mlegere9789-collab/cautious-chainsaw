# Scope B (Union II, III and XII Corps): full-passage fidelity re-check, notes

Brief: `docs/battle-theatre/GOVERNANCE/briefs/positions-recheck.md`; reader instructions: `briefs/positions-recheck-B-reader.md`. Audit that triggered it: `FIDELITY_AUDIT_SUMMARY.md` (scope B 28 of 30 faithful, 93%, no `fidelity_check` field, `claim_support_verified` true for all 1,779 documented claims without a full read).

## What was done

Every one of the 1,859 claims of `positions_B.jsonl` was re-checked; none was skipped by a rule. The 25 open gaps have no cited passage, so they were not fidelity-checked (`fidelity_check` not_rechecked); their recorded searches were re-run instead (below).

- Method: 38 batches (corps II 8, III 13, XII 17; 13 to 60 claims, grouped by cited report; lists in `draft_B/batches/`). Each batch was read by one helper agent with `tools/bt/recheck_B_ctx.py` (the frozen pre-recheck ledger `_orig_B/positions_B.before_recheck.jsonl`; each cited report printed whole up to 14,000 characters, longer ones as windows of 1,800 characters before and 2,200 after each quote, widened or searched whole where a later order, the date or another sentence was needed). For every claim the readers checked unit identity, date and clock time (must be in the same or an explicitly continuing sentence), place and road as printed, activity and numbers, hedges and conditions, a later or earlier order, OCR-damaged digits, meridian, day-part words. Decisions: `draft_B/recheck/<BATCH>.json` plus a notes file per batch. I (the single writer) validated each file with `tools/bt/positions_B_recheck.py check`, merged them one corps at a time with `tools/bt/recheck_B_merge.py` into `tools/bt/positions_B_recheck_II.json`, `_III.json`, `_XII.json` (and `_GAP.json`), rebuilt with `python -I tools/bt/positions_claims.py B` and ran `verify_positions.py B` after each merge. The builder applies the decision files after the existing `positions_B_overrides.json`, so a rebuild reproduces the file.
- Hard checks in the decision applier (`tools/bt/positions_B_recheck.py`): a replacement quote must be under 15 words and verified under the cited report; a `when.printed` must lie inside the claim's quote or an extract (the code adds a verified extract of at most 14 words when the phrase is in the passage near the quote, else the decision is rejected); place and road phrases must occur near the quote; `passed_full_read` may not change facts; "OCR glyph only" needs `t_iso` null and `ocr_uncertain` true; "time in a different sentence" needs `t_iso` null; a downgrade needs a reasoning. `verify_positions.py` also checks (existing extension) a valid `fidelity_check` on every claim, `claim_support_verified` true only for passed_full_read/corrected, and no documented claim that is not passed/corrected.
- Independent blind check of the result (not by the readers): 90 claims (60 passed_full_read, 30 corrected, random seed 20261021) re-judged against the raw text: 88 faithful, 2 not faithful (both minor and both from the passed set: POS-B-us-3c-37 called a March 1864 statement an "1863 statement"; POS-B-us-3c-2d-burling-5nj-09 turned a 600-yard distance into an advance and added "Confederate"). Both were repaired (now corrected). So the residual defect rate among passed_full_read claims is about 3% (2 of 60; wide uncertainty) and 0 of 30 among corrected claims. Files: `draft_B/QA_B.jsonl`, `draft_B/QA_B_summary.txt`.
- Lead spot reads: 6 random corrected decisions read against their wording (all sound); a script check of every `t_iso` against the digits of its printed phrase (13 mismatches, all OCR spacing such as "7. 30 p. m."); conflict members whose time, date or tier changed (8 claims) re-read: the symmetric conflict groups are kept (they still differ on the printed digit or the reading).

## Patterns found (counts are claims changed, approximate, summed from the 38 batch reports and the computed field effects; one claim can carry several)

1. **Time or date taken from another sentence or clause** (audit pattern 1): about 135 claims (107 now with the "time in a different sentence" description). Typical forms: a paragraph-opening "On the morning of the 2d" or "At 3 p. m." carried onto every later sentence (Birney R133: "At 12 m." times the permission, not the send; Humphreys R158: the 3 p. m. march order carried onto later placements; Williams R272: "At 8 a. m." is the time of the orders, not of the positions); a purpose or distance phrase used as the time ("by your order", "from right to left", "1 1/2 miles"); a neighbour's time (Zook's "early in the action" on Brooke). Part 3 dispatches: the heading time is the time of writing; it is correct for present-tense states and orders, wrong for reported past events (removed or marked approximate on about 12 claims).
2. **One claim merging text from a second report or a second moment** (audit pattern 2): about 25 claims; 5 split, the rest narrowed. Less frequent than in D because B claims were built per report, but the same builder habit appeared: "(X says N)" cross-report remarks copied from the note into the activity, and two moments of one paragraph in one claim (the quote covers only the first).
3. **A later order ignored** (pattern 3): about 8 claims (a withdrawal, a counter-order, "ordered" stated as "done": Barnes withdrew in 3c-1d-19; Lockwood-04 and Candy 1st Brigade ordered but not yet moved; the 2d Massachusetts fell back to a new line, only the 27th Indiana behind the breastworks).
4. **Glosses presented as printed** (pattern 4): the largest group, about 180 claims: commander names added to a brigade ("(Zook)", "(Hancock)", "(Kelly)", "(Ames)", "(Eleventh Corps)"), battery identities resolved from lists (4th NY, 1st NY D, Battery F 4th US, Knap's Pennsylvania), division names for addressees, purposes ("to replenish", "to recapture", "to support the First and Eleventh Corps", "council"), labels ("Pipe Creek", "Wheatfield action", "great cannonade"), day-parts ("evening", "morning"), a person used as a place ("General Webb", "Doubleday", "Humphreys") or a unit as a place ("the brigade"), a place borrowed from the neighbouring sentence, GZ gazetteer ids implying a road the passage does not print (now marked by inference in `place.description`). Compass directions: only 2 claims (rare; B's builder did not add them).
5. **Hedge or condition dropped** (pattern 4b): about 55 claims ("I think", "supposed", "feared", "as nearly as I can judge", "would probably have succeeded ... had not", "if", "should", "or its vicinity", "about" dropped or added, "ordered" for "did").
6. **OCR-damaged digits read with confidence** (pattern 5): about 45 claims: distances ("1^", "l-J", "li miles" read as 1.5), calibres, hours ("G a. m.", "13 o'clock", "7.80", "4 J), m."), dates ("June 38/39/35", "July 3" for July 2 in the headings of R094/R095/R305/R309/R314/R282, Brooke "June 39"). Where the report's own sequence settles the digit (a "July 3" heading followed by "dawn of day" of the same action) the claim keeps the settled date with `date_basis` and, where doubtful, `ocr_uncertain`; where it does not, `t_iso` is null.
7. **No a.m./p.m. in the passage, meridian supplied** (audit note 6): 51 claims now carry `meridian_inferred: true` with the sequence that settles it; where the sequence does not settle the meridian `t_iso` is null.
8. **Unit identity** (new in B): the compiler attached regiment, battery and corps facts to brigade or division ids and the reverse; 14 downgraded, 28 annotated (above).
9. **Weak but not changed**: dates by report context on 761 claims (not printed in the fact's sentence); they are flagged with `date_basis` rather than treated as defects, as in A and D.

## What the builders must stop doing (scope B)

1. Never put a clock or day phrase into `when.printed` that lies in a neighbouring sentence ("the nearest phrase within 700 characters" is not a rule; the build now keeps a phrase only if it is inside the claim's verified extract).
2. Never carry a paragraph-opening day-part or a heading time onto later facts; a Part 3 heading time is the time of writing.
3. Never write the author's commander, the battery letter, the division name, a purpose or a label into an activity unless the cited passage prints it; if a reading is wanted put it in `place.description` / `note` as a bracketed gloss.
4. Never copy "(X says N)" remarks from the note into `activity`; use one claim per report and per moment.
5. Never read an OCR fraction or caliber glyph as a number ("1^ miles", "gO-pounder").
6. Never keep the quote that covers only the first half of a two-moment paragraph; carry an extract for the second.
7. Never attach regiment-level facts to the brigade id (or corps-level facts to the division); use the unit the passage names, or mark the claim as context.
8. Keep "ordered" distinct from "did", and keep a later order of the same paragraph in the claim.

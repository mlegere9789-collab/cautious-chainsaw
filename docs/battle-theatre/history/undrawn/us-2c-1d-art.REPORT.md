## 3. Strengths, as ranges with their bases

Strengths are kept as printed, never reduced to one number; each record names its basis (`what`) and date. The Official Records print figures of their own for eight leaf units (the 5th New Hampshire, 61st New York, 28th Massachusetts, the 63d, 69th and 88th New York, the 64th New York and the 53d Pennsylvania) and for two brigades (Cross 780 muskets, Kelly 530 aggregate) and the corps (about 10,000). The state volumes and Fox add figures for the 140th, 145th, 148th and 116th Pennsylvania (by officers of the regiments, tier documented, or by compilations and orators, tier inferred), an estimate for the 81st Pennsylvania by an officer of another regiment, and second figures for the 5th New Hampshire, 61st, 63d and 64th New York and 53d Pennsylvania. Where the texts print different numbers for one unit the records carry all of them as a conflict (section 4). The plan's equal share of 224 for every leaf unit is not a source (it equals the 28th Massachusetts' printed 224 only by the plan's arithmetic). The kit-evidence pass (`kit/evidence/us-2c-1d.jsonl`) holds the same figures from the same reports and sections; the integrator reconciles by source. Every printed figure of that file that I could match in a text on this machine is a record here; its derived sums (75 for the 69th New York, 204 and 222 for the 64th, 515 for the 140th Pennsylvania) are not repeated, because the contract forbids sums, and the two printed numbers they are made of are separate records.

## 7. Defects found

1. **The corps' gate of 22:30 on 1 July** (arrivals.json, `us-2c`) rests on a Wikipedia lead, which the plan lists as a weak source; the Official Records put the corps at 3 miles in rear on that night (Hancock) and its artillery on the Taneytown road 3 miles southeast at 11 p. m. (Hazard); the documented arrival on the field is about 7 a. m. on 2 July (0005), with the 6.15 a. m. dispatch as the earliest printed clock (0006). Merged, the records fix a documented gate; until then the corps would appear 8 hours before any printed arrival on the field.
2. **State-volume index headings** (request note written): `NY1-S081` (headed the 49th New York) holds the 52d New York's historical sketch (Surg. Althans) and its monument inscription; `NY3-S022` (headed the 10th New York Cavalry) holds, besides the 10th, the dedication of Battery B, 1st New York Light Artillery (Pettit's), whose address names the batteries of the Second Corps; `NY2-S005` (headed the 88th New York) holds the account of the 64th New York; `FOX-S061` (headed the 48th New York) holds the 52d New York's entry; `FOX-S063` (headed the 63d New York) holds the 64th New York's. The sections that the plan lists for the group's units (`groups.json`, `sources.statevol_sections`) do not name `NY1-S081`, `NY2-S005` for the 64th, `NY3-S022` for Battery B, nor the Fox and Bachelder texts for the regiments; the records cite them by their ids.
3. **OCR of the Official Records**: the 2d is printed 3d in R087 ("the morning of the 3d", Mulholland), R079 ("went into action, July 3", Cross), R095 (McMichael, first and third reports), R088 ("After this engagement on the 3d", Fraser); "morntag" for morning (R088); "Taneyiown" and "Taneytown road" run together in R092 and R094; "battlo", "inuskets" and "ofiBcers" in R085, R080; the columns of the returns of R008 are printed in a run that the OCR does not keep by division or brigade. The records keep the printed words in `quote` and say in `note` how the day was read.
4. **Page models**: the index's page model is several pages high inside the organization table (R009) and the returns (R008): the records give the printed page from the scan-leaf map (`kit/evidence/or27_leaf_pages.json`) and name it as `page_basis`.
5. **Fox's table** of the regiments that lost 10 per cent or more killed in one battle prints regiment, battle, division, number engaged, killed and per cent; the meaning of the first number was checked against Fox's own entries (the 69th Pennsylvania: "entered that fight with 258 officers and men", the table prints 258); the records carry it as "engaged".
6. **Bradley's two reports** (R094) print the 64th New York's strength differently (185 enlisted and 19 officers, then 203 enlisted and 2 field, 1 staff and 16 line officers); the group file carries both.

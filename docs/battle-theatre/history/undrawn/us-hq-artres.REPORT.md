## 6. Defects found

1. The first version's request about Company C (assigned to two groups) was answered by the plan; the note stays as the record of it.
2. The footnotes of p. 168 of the organization table (stars of the two Connecticut heavy batteries, dagger of Battery K) are lost in the OCR of both copies read; `OR27-1-R008` (abstract of returns) is too scrambled to give a line for the headquarters or the Reserve.
3. Tyler's "about eight batteries" of 1 July does not match the batteries of the two brigades he names; the roster draws Batteries F and K, 3d U. S., C and F Pa., and F and G, 1st Pa., as six units while the table prints three consolidated batteries (the records on both ids say so).
4. State-volume index: section `PA2-S036` (heading BATTERY "H"*) holds the inscriptions of Ricketts' and Thompson's batteries; `NY3-S024` (heading Battery G) holds the inscription of Battery K; `ME-S021` holds the roster of the Sixth Maine under another heading. The index is not changed; the section ids stand as the checker finds the quotes.
5. OCR damage that fixes a reading: Milton's "7.80 p. m." (read as 7.30, entered as a sequence, no clock), Weir's "1.3( o'clock", Milton's "10 ^. m. of the 5th" (the day only), Tyler's "li miles", Hunt's "Eakin ... badly wounded" across a page break. A name the gazetteer does not hold: Ames' "the Frederick and Gettysburg pike" (not mapped to a road; the place stays words).
6. Hunt (p. 232) puts Osborn's batteries "in the cemetery grounds, to the north of the Baltimore road" and (p. 233) "on the south of the road": the records use "Cemetery Hill" and carry neither side.
7. The Itinerary (R007) is a compilation of the 1880s by Kirkley, so it is a `compiler` source (tier inferred).

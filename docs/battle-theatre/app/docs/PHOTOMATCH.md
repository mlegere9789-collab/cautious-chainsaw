# Photo-match (gate Q16)

**Q16 text (gates.json):** "Photo-match tests at known camera positions of period photographs (staged-photograph flags respected)." Linked from the R12 terrain source items. **Status: not passing** (reviewed 2026-11-15). `passes` is not set on the gate or on any linked item.

## Where things stand

- **Photographs:** 191 catalogued Library of Congress records: 109 from the first catalogue run, 20 added (18 because LOC dates them only as between 1861 and 1865, 2 because they lie outside the Civil War Photographs collection that the first run read) and 62 of the wide search of the whole Civil War group. 493 JPEG derivatives are saved (each checked: size equals what the record declares or the file decodes to the last pixel; sha256 in the ledgers) plus 2 master scans, 3 archive.org scans and 6 Metropolitan Museum items of the first run; 5 records of the wide search are not digitized by the Library. The image of every record that has one was looked at on a contact sheet. Every further resource that a wide-search record offers (51 second stereo halves, card backs and copy negatives) was fetched as well, and 31 card backs were fetched at 1024 px to read their print; the first catalogue run had saved every offered resource of its records.
- **Camera positions:** 0 are documented. A documented camera position is a coordinate, a lens and a plate that a source other than a lead gives (decision DD-PM-01): none of the 68 sources read (Library of Congress catalogue records, National Park Service reports and web pages) gives one. They give 145 statements in words (place, viewing direction, reference object); 135 are documented statements (a source says it, quote found in the saved text), 3 were read from page images because the text layer is unusable, 7 rest on a book that may not be used and are leads only.
- **Camera places named by a source** (the first class below a documented position, decision DD-PM-06): 3 registered poses (P27, P28, P29) stand at a building of known outline that the National Park Service names as the place of the camera (the roof of the Evergreen Cemetery lodge for the view north, the gatehouse for the view east), with a cardinal word for the direction. Their camera height, lens and exact heading are not documented, so their position tier stays inferred.
- **Poses registered:** 20 (18 current, 2 superseded by a better-sourced pose; position tier: 5 inferred, 15 assumption). **Verdicts on the poses:** 0 matches, 8 roughly, 5 does not, 7 cannot judge.
- **The other 173 photographs** have no pose and the verdict cannot judge, each with its reason: 51 show only near ground that the model does not contain, 66 have a camera place that no source fixes closely enough, 21 are staged or flagged, 35 are not July 1863 battlefield views. 21 records carry a staged-photograph flag; none has a pose.

## Why the gate cannot pass

- The gate asks for tests at known camera positions. Under the standing decision DD-PM-01 a documented camera position is a coordinate, a lens and a plate that a source other than a lead gives: none of the catalogued photographs has one; the 145 camera-position statements read (Library of Congress records, National Park Service reports, place pages and the Record of Treatment) give words (place, direction, reference object), never a coordinate, a lens, a plate size or a surveyed station.
- The first documented camera places exist: the National Park Service names the roof of the Evergreen Cemetery lodge for the view of Gettysburg to the north (records 2018671576 and 2014646001: poses P27 north and P28 northwest, two words of two NPS texts for one exposure) and the gatehouse for the view east (record 2012647704: pose P29). Their height, lens and exact heading are not documented (height assumed 8 m, lens assumed 40 degrees, the heading a cardinal word with a 45-degree sector), so their position tier stays inferred and they are counted apart (3 poses).
- The pass criteria of the tests at those places were written before the renders (criteria.json). Results: P27 roughly, P28 does not, P29 roughly. The three binary tests of the first criterion are necessary conditions only (they are also passed by the superseded poses P06 and P07, whose verdict is does not), and the skyline test fits the heading, the lens and a vertical shift to the photograph, so no pose is called a match and none can be: a fitted test is not a match, and the registration against T-Q16-1 is not possible while T-Q16-1 is unset.
- The model has no 1863 near field (trees, rocks, crops, fences, woods edges): only far silhouettes, mapped building footprints at a default height and roads can be compared. Where the model and a photograph disagree (3 to 9 degrees in the cases examined) the disagreement cannot be assigned to the pose, the lens or the terrain.
- The NPS plate that labels the landmarks of the view from Little Round Top could not be mapped to the photograph, and the landmark stand-ins in the repository carry 221 to 300 m of uncertainty.

## What would be needed

- Camera station coordinates from the body that has them: the Gettysburg National Military Park took its Then-And-Now pictures from the same locations as the originals (the park says so) and its Historic Photograph Collection (GETT 41135) holds the prints that the Record of Treatment cites by number (2B-2063, 2B-2090, 2B2001, OS2B-2037 and about thirty more): its museum or GIS staff may hold the station, the lens and the plate of each; or Frassanito's published positions (copyrighted: needs the owner's decision to buy, borrow or ask permission). Either gives a documented position with a stated accuracy.
- For the exposures made from the Evergreen Cemetery gatehouse (P27, P28, P29): the height and the spot on the building (the roof, a window), the 1863 form of the building (the model holds the 2023 outline with its right-hand addition), and the lens and plate of the exposures; a drawing or photograph of the lodge of 1855 from the Evergreen Cemetery Association or the park archive.
- The lens and plate of each exposure (stereo half-plate or large format, focal length), or a calibration from straight edges and from two or more mapped landmarks of known coordinates in the same photograph; for the skyline of Gettysburg from the cemetery, a source that names the towers and cupolas in the photograph (the courthouse clock tower, the college and church cupolas) so that they can be used as landmarks.
- A model of the 1863 near field where a photograph is to be matched: trees, rocks (Devil's Den, Little Round Top, the Rose Woods), walls and fences, crop state, tents: the R12 vegetation and rock layers.
- Landmark coordinates good to a few metres for the objects the captions and the NPS name (Ziegler's Grove, the Copse, the Brien lane tree, Codori Grove, the Patterson house, the McPherson quarry, Seminary Lane, the Foulk farm, the crossing of Wheatfield Road and Crawford Avenue, the Baltimore Street entrance of the national cemetery); the gazetteer centres carry 221 to 300 m.
- A decision on whether a building named by a non-lead source with a cardinal word counts as a documented camera position (DD-PM-06 says it does not: the gate keeps its wording) and a value for T-Q16-1 (unset), written before the match; a reviewer who did not set the pose and who looks at every sheet; the criterion and the failures recorded per photograph (PDF B.9.2 step 7).
- Full-resolution masters of the photographs to be matched (25 to 130 MB each; 2 of about 490 resources fetched, kept as 4096-px working copies) and a clean scan of the NPS annotated enlargement of the view north from Little Round Top.

## The first tests at a documented camera place

The National Park Service names the place of the camera for two exposures of the catalogue: the view of Gettysburg from the Evergreen Cemetery lodge (the Cultural Landscape Report for Gettysburg National Cemetery, Figure 1.26: "looking north from the roof of the Evergreen Cemetery lodge shortly after the battle, July 1863", Library of Congress LC-B815-273, which is record 2018671576; two other reports call the same picture, as ppmsca 35051, a view northwest from Cemetery Hill) and the view east from the same building (Record of Treatment, volume I, Figure 213, the park's print OS2B-2037, which is record 2012647704 by image match). The words north and northwest differ by 45 degrees: both are carried, as two poses (P27 and P28) of one place. The earlier poses of the same records, set at an assumed spot on Cemetery Hill (P06, P07), are kept with their verdicts and marked superseded.

The pass criteria were written **before** the renders existed and are never changed after a render was looked at (`criteria.json`, with the time each was written and the check that no render of the poses existed then):

- **C-P27-P28-1** (2026-11-14T00:01:29Z, poses P27, P28): T1: side of the Baltimore Pike (model road RD-001) in the main render: in the photograph the pike runs from the lower left corner to the left of the frame centre and recedes toward the town on the left half of the frame, with the national cemetery side (wall, fence, farm road) at its left. T1 passes if the whole visible part of RD-001 in the render lies in the left 55 percent of the frame width; it fails if any visible part lies right of that.; T2: height of the far horizon (the ridge or the town band against the sky): the photograph's far ridge line stands at 0.47 of the print height (read by eye, +-0.03). T2 passes if the render's horizon (first non-sky row, computed by compose.py) is within 0.10 of the frame height of that value; it fails otherwise. A tenth of the frame is about 3 degrees at the assumed field.; T3: order of the model's buildings band (the borough, GZ-072 and the footprint outlines) against Seminary Ridge: in the photograph the town lies to the right of the left-hand ridge and fills the left centre of the frame (x from 0.05 to 0.60 of the print width). T3 passes if the main-field render puts the centre of the borough footprint band inside that interval. Verdict rule: matches = T1, T2 and T3 all pass at the registered heading and the 40 degree field; roughly = T2 passes and one of T1 or T3 passes; does not = T1 fails and T2 fails, or the pike is on the wrong side at both NPS headings; cannot judge = the render does not contain a road or buildings in the frame at all. The 30 and 50 degree renders and the two NPS headings are reported beside the main result; a pass only at a variant is not a pass.
- **C-P27-P28-2** (2026-11-14T00:31:42Z, poses P27, P28): S1: RMS of the best fit at the station: match-level if <= 0.5 degrees, partial if <= 1.0 degree, mismatch above. 0.5 degrees is 15 m of tree height at 1.7 km, the canopy the bare-earth model lacks; a fit counts only with the heading inside the sector of the pose and F between 25 and 70 degrees.; S2: Discrimination: the station RMS is at or below the first quartile of the 24 controls. If it is not, the skyline does not tell this station from its neighbours and S1 supports nothing. Verdict rule: The skyline test fits three parameters to the photograph (heading, lens, vertical shift) and therefore can never make a verdict matches (verdicts.json: a fitted test is not a match). The 30 and 50 degree renders and the sensitivity to the camera height are reported, not used to choose.
- **C-P29-1** (2026-11-14T00:44:11Z, poses P29): S1: RMS of the best fit at the station: match-level if <= 0.5 degrees, partial if <= 1.0 degree, mismatch above (heading inside the sector, F between 25 and 70 degrees). 0.5 degrees is 9 m of tree height at 1 km: the canopy on Culp's Hill in 1863 is in the photograph and not in the bare-earth model, which makes this test stricter in terrain than in trees; the threshold is the one of C-P27-P28-2 and is not changed for this pose.; S2: Discrimination: the station RMS is at or below the first quartile of the 24 controls. ## The wide search of the Library of Congress Civil War group

The first catalogue run read only the Civil War Photographs collection (cwp). The National Park Service cites two records outside it by Digital ID (ppmsca-12565 and ppmsca-18947), so the search was widened to the whole Civil War group (open JSON search, 950 results): 62 further photographs of 1863 that name Gettysburg were read and classified (`wide_data.py`): 15 with a camera place named only loosely or not at all, 21 near-field views, 6 staged or flagged, 20 not July 1863 battlefield views (portraits, later photographs, hospital, dedication, not digitized). Most are second prints, stereo cards or Sketch Book plates of exposures already in the catalogue; where both images were looked at the tie is recorded in `same_exposure_as` of the registry entry (37 of the 62 records carry one). 6 further matches scored 109 to 286 inliers and were rejected on looking: studio portraits and a card with a flag matched to landscapes or to a gun carriage, and a road view matched to a stereo card of prisoners, by grain and rail texture alone (`REJECTED` in `wide_data.py`); the score alone never ties two images.

## Limits (registered as proposed Evidence-Gap Register entries ws10-EG-01 to ws10-EG-16)

- The terrain is modern bare ground with the repository's 2023 building outlines at a default one-storey height: no 1863 trees, rocks, crops or fences in the near field, so only far ridge lines, hills, roads and mapped footprints can be compared (EG-03).
- The focal length and plate of every exposure are unknown; 40 degrees horizontal is a generic assumption and renders at 30 and 50 degrees accompany every pose (EG-04).
- Frassanito's camera positions are copyrighted and were not read; LOC text that cites him is a lead only (EG-06). Staged photographs are used for terrain and rocks only, never for the position of a body or prop (EG-05).
- Positions named in words but not in the model (the McPherson quarry, De Trobriand Avenue and the Rose Woods edge, Seminary Lane, the Wheatfield Road and Crawford Avenue crossing) could be placed only as assumptions, or not at all (EG-10).
- The NPS plate that labels the landmarks of the view north from Little Round Top is a photocopy and could not be mapped to the photograph (EG-11); the Trostle summer-kitchen footprint lies on the other side of the house from the photograph (EG-12).
- The height of the camera on the gatehouse and the 1863 form of the building are not documented (EG-13); the two NPS words for the direction of the lodge-roof view differ by 45 degrees (EG-14).

# FX3D notes: smoke, muzzle flash, shell bursts, dust and fire (Battle Theatre, figures round, FX part)

Status 2026-10-31: **code, data and node tests only. Nothing has been seen in a browser or run on a GPU** (the Release 1 acceptance run was timing the machine; rule 1 of `GOVERNANCE/briefs/bt-visuals-common.md`). The shader sources are uncompiled. Every visual claim is in `BROWSER-VERIFICATION-LIST-FX3D.md` and is open. The module is **not wired into `main.mjs` or the renderer**; wiring is section 9.

Owner order (2026-10-31): smoke, muzzle flashes, dust and sound for the Battle Theatre, no scope reduction. The plan itself (C.5) calls smoke, dust and fire "visual-effects problems ... not evidence-limited"; its timeline marks smoke and dust plausible-procedural / inferred (T2-122, T2-203, "no direct dust accounts"). So what the repository can *document* here is small: the powder charges (printed manuals), the fuze limit, the fire windows (structure schedule), the wind direction range, the ground wetness schedule. Everything else is a look parameter, **tier-marked one by one** (`data/fx3d/TIERS.json`: 14 documented, 7 inferred, 182 plausible-procedural of 203 parameters) and registered (`data/fx3d/GAPS.json`, `GOVERNANCE/register/EG-FX-G01..G16.md`).

## 2. The model in one page

Frame: x east, z south, y up (src/geo.mjs). Time: seconds on the app clock (= minutes * 60), as in BT-EVENTS.

**Puff** (every smoke and dust cloud). A cause at `t0` creates a Gaussian puff of mass `m0` (kg) at `p0` with initial `sigma0`, blast velocity `v0` that relaxes with `tauD`, a buoyant rise `vb0` that decays with `tauB`, and settling. At age `tau = t - t0`:

* centre = `p0 + W(t0,t) + v0 tauD (1 - e^(-tau/tauD)) + (0, vb0 tauB (1 - e^(-tau/tauB)) - settle tau, 0)`, where `W` is the exact integral of the wind (wind.mjs).
* `sigma_h^2 = sigma0^2 + 2 K tau + beta tau^3`, `sigma_v^2 = sigma0^2 + vr (2 K tau + beta tau^3)` (vr: vertical/horizontal diffusion ratio, 0.6 in open air, 0.25 in damp air).
* visible mass `m = m0 e^(-tau/tauDis)`; `tauDis` is 5 s with wind at the reference 1.8 m/s and 110 s in still air ("smoke hangs low in nearly windless heat", PDF B.6.14.2), interpolated by the wind.
* density = Gaussian with the ground as a reflecting plane (method of images): the integral over the half-space is exactly `m` (tested to 2 percent on a grid, incl. next to the ground).
* optical depth along any ray: closed form with erf, image term included (tested to 0.1 percent against numeric integration). `kappa` = 3000 m2/kg (smoke), 1500 (dust).

Everything the renderer draws is a pure function of (event, sprite index, age): **no particle state on the CPU or GPU**, so any time can be drawn (scrub, seek, replay) and the same events give the same picture. A puff is drawn as `n` sprites; each carries `m/n` of the mass, so **the total opacity is independent of how many sprites the LOD draws** (tested).

**Smoke mass per shot** = powder charge x `yield_frac` (0.5, plausible-procedural). Charges: 60 grains for the rifled musket (Gibbon 1860, `drill_infantry.json INF-AMMO-06`: **documented**); 2.5 lb Napoleon, 1.25 lb 6-pdr, 1.0 lb 12-pdr howitzer, 2.0 lb 24-pdr howitzer (Instruction for Field Artillery 1863, `ORD-CHG-FA`: documented); Napoleon shell 2.0 lb; 12-pdr howitzer case 0.75 lb and 6-pdr case 1.0 lb (Gibbon: documented); rifled guns from the kit table (Wikipedia-sourced: inferred); all other small arms scaled by bore area from the documented 60 grains (plausible-procedural, gap FX-G10). 52 weapons are covered (all of `kit/weapons.json` that fires).

**Muzzle flash.** Cap flash at the trigger pull `t`; charge ignition and the muzzle flash/puff follow by the cap lead (3 to 8 ms, percussion arms) so that **nothing precedes the cause** (the audio contract puts the flash at `t`; the 8 ms difference is below the audio-visual sync threshold and AUDIO should know). Jet length `L = 3.8 * cbrt(charge kg)`: 0.6 m for 60 grains, 4.0 m for 2.5 lb ("several metres", FIRE_EVENT). Duration: musket 10 to 40 ms; gun 50 to 150 ms (the union of FIRE_EVENT's 50 to 100 and plan G12's 80 to 150: two project estimates, carried as a range). Peak radiance and light radius are HDR look parameters; the flash adds a point light (radius 6 m musket, about 40 m gun) that decays with 18 ms. The luminance floor of the environment layer is untouched: lights only add.

**Gun blast.** Cloud (88 percent of the smoke) plus a vortex ring in the plane normal to the barrel (12 percent, 25 m/s, decay 0.3 s).

**Shell bursts** (`onBurst`/`onImpact`). Spherical case: 1.0 oz bursting charge (**documented**, ORD-PRJ-CASE) airburst puff and flash; common shell 1.5 oz (plausible-procedural stand-in, gap FX-G04); canister: dust fan 60 to 320 m downrange arriving range/400 m/s after the shot, half-angle 12 degrees (gap FX-G05); solid shot/impact: dust mass from the impact energy (1e-4 kg/kJ, scaled by dryness) and ballistic clods thrown 14 to 22 m/s, i.e. a 10 to 25 m column (plan G12); ricochets: hops along the line of flight on dry ground, **none on wet ground** ("stops in soggy earth", wetness 0.7). A burst earlier than the shot that caused it is refused (cause registry).

**Fuse trails.** Only for timed-fuze projectiles (spherical case; shell from smoothbores or with `fuze: 'time'`), only when `darkness >= 0.5`, duration = the fuze time (maximum 5.5 s, Bormann, **documented**); never for solid shot, canister, percussion rifled shell (Wheeler's 13th NY had only percussion and canister on Jul 2) or small arms. **There is no tracer effect for muskets** (none existed). Plan T2-203 lists fuse trails as a night light source and marks it plausible-procedural; so are ours.

**Dust.** Rate = footfalls/s x kg per footfall x surface factor x dustiness. Dustiness = `clamp(1 - wetness/0.25)`, 0 while it rains. Wetness comes from the weather file's schedule (0 from Jul 1 to the Jul 3 6 pm storm, about 0.3 after it = mud, 1.0 on Jul 4) and, where the file has none, a drying model (21 h) after the documented rains (Jun 25 to 27 07:00; Jul 5 morning): Jun 26-27 no dust, Jun 28 ramps up, Jun 29-30 dusty, Jul 1-3 dusty, Jul 3 evening to Jul 5 none, Jul 7 dusty again. The PDF has **no dust accounts**; the whole layer is inferred / plausible-procedural. Columns, horse strings and wagon trains are sampled on a fixed 0.5 s tick grid, into puffs whose length is chosen by camera distance; the **mass is independent of that cutting and of the frame rate** (tested). `onUnitMarch` builds the column from BT-EVENTS `unit_march` (length = figures / 4.4 per metre, request R-FX-3); `onFall` raises a dust puff where a man or horse falls on dry ground.

**Wind** (the environment layer's own data; nothing chosen here). Speed = the middle of the mph range the weather file gives for the chosen `CF-WIND-SPEED` scenario (recommended 2 to 6 mph; 12 or 2 if the owner picks); direction veers inside the documented S to SSW range (180 to 202.5 degrees) with a slow noise; deterministic gusts (30 percent, 25 s correlation); **Jul 3 13:00 to 16:30 scaled to 0.15** ("no breeze during the Charge", "smoke hangs", secondary), edges 30 min; gust boost before the 18:00 storm (inferred). Days with no recorded direction (Jun 26-30, Jul 4) use S-SSW and are flagged by `env.wind.directionRecorded`. Result: smoke drifts N-NE (T2-122), tested.

**Air dampness** (ground-hugging, lower buoyancy): 0.25 base; 0.6 on Jul 3-4 ("very humid", no number); 0.85 at the Jul 2 foggy dawn until the sun is 8 degrees up; 0.8 on wet ground; 1.0 in rain. All inferred / plausible-procedural.

## 3. Battle fog and sight lines

* Far small-arms shots (beyond 150 m +/- 20 percent hysteresis from the camera, decided at spawn) become **one fog event per 32 m cell per second**: mass, mass-weighted centroid and spread are conserved (tested), the event is stamped with the **last** shot of its bucket so nothing exists before its cause. The flashes of far shots are kept (a distant volley is seen at night).
* When the store is full, the oldest musket/fog/dust puffs of a 48 m cell are **merged** (mass kept, tested), not dropped. Only light-only events (flashes, fuse trails, at most 0.1 s old) can be evicted as a last resort, counted in `stats().evicted`; in a 3,000 shots/s stress test it stays 0.
* `fx.sightlineOD(a, b)`, `fx.visibility(a, u)` (distance to 2 percent contrast) and `fx.fogMap(x0, z0, size)` (column optical depth, mean height and thickness per 128 x 128 cell, for ground/soldier/billboard shaders) give the sight-line obscuration of a sustained fight. Diagnostic from the test (model output, **not a validation**): six guns firing four rounds a minute for 10 minutes in the Jul 3 near-calm give 34 m visibility through the battery and a clear view behind it (plan T2-122: "visibility inside batteries tens of metres").

Rules kept (tested minute by minute for every fire and scenario): **nothing burns before the window start**; inside a **date-only window** the fire is drawn at half intensity and marked **"time uncertain"** (the structures layer shows such a change half and half; no ignition time is invented); **after the window the building is a smouldering ruin, never flames**; if the host knows the cause it calls `ignite(id, t)` (refused outside the window) and the fire follows growth, peak, decline from exactly `t` (growth/peak/decline minutes are plausible-procedural, gap FX-G08); Bliss flames end by 13:00 whatever happens. The McPherson barn (RULE-01) is never a fire source. **Not drawn, with reasons in `fires.json not_fires` and the gap register:** a **wheat-field fire** (S-033's "clipped by fire" is gunfire), the **Rogers house and Schmucker Hall** (documented as not burned), Caledonia Furnace and the covered bridge (no position), burning fence rails (cause and extent unrecorded), the Taney house (ruins, not fire).

## 5. Lighting, night and the floor (gate Q10)

Smoke radiance = `albedo * (ambient + direct * HG-phase * self-shadow)`, ambient never below `0.06` (the environment layer's own lowest luminance term) and the shader clamps `lit >= albedo * exposure floor`: smoke is never black at any minute (tested over every 15 min of the battle). The environment's `exposure = floor + (1 - floor) * lum` lifts it exactly as it lifts the terrain. Flash and fire lights add to it. Q10 has no numeric luminance threshold in the PDF; none is invented here.

## 6. Budget, LOD and cost model (soldiers are never touched)

* Sprites per event: `n_full[type] * band_factor[distance band]`, bands 30/120/400/1200 m, factors 1/0.5/0.2/0.08/0.03; a sprite under 1.5 px is not drawn (its mass stays in the fog map).
* Cap per tier from the **measured** prototype cost (Intel Arc, 1080p, WebGPU): 13 ns per sprite pass time, 22 ns by ablation (`render/PLAN.md` B, D, I), times a tier factor. Desktop 4.0 ms (plan G12) = 181,818 sprites; laptop 3.0 ms = 136,363; tablet 2.0 ms at 2x = 45,454; phone 1.5 ms at 3x (not measured) = 22,727. The prototype itself drew up to 4,096 events x 64 = 262,144 and measured 2.7 to 4.5 ms in the dense `line` shot, so the desktop cap equals what was already measured as acceptable.
* Allocation under a cap: classes `flash, burst, gun, fire, musket, dust, fog` in that priority, each with an 8 percent floor; sprites are shared by error-diffusion rounding so the grant is exact. A frame governor (AIMD, `govern(effectMs)`) moves the scale between 0.1 and 1, never the content.
* **UI sentence** (`frame.budget.text`): "Smoke, flash and dust effects are drawn at 73 percent of full density on this device (... sprites, tier phone). Every shot still counts: the battle fog and sight lines keep the full smoke. Soldiers are not reduced." (test asserts the wording and that the sprite count stays under the cap.)
* **G1 on paper:** FX is held to the plan's 4 ms (G12) by construction, and the governor reduces effects before anything else. The prototype's measured frame was 6.2 to 11.0 ms at 1080p with its particle pass already inside that; the FX layer does not add GPU work beyond that class. The soldiers' 10x heavier assets are the FIGURES/ANIMATION budget. **The measurement is on the browser list.**
* CPU (node, on a machine busy with the acceptance run, so an upper bound; not a browser number): `update()` 4.5 ms average with 12,900 live events and 85,000 sprites (desktop tier), 1.5 ms at the phone tier (3,400 events); spawn about 10 microseconds per shot. At the G1 4x throttle the desktop figure would break the 8 ms CPU limit; **use `update(t, cam, {gpuCull: true})` on WebGPU** (the CPU then only packs) or the laptop/phone tier on WebGL2. Spawn does not allocate per shot beyond the 24-double record.

## 7. Interface (BT-EVENTS.md v1)

`fx.attach(bus)` subscribes `fire`, `misfire`, `fall`, `unit_march`, `impact`. `onFire(unitId, figureIdx, t, weaponKind, position, direction, info)`: `info.weaponId` (kit id) selects charge and class (a null id falls to the class stand-in `long_arm`, `carbine`, `repeater`, `handgun`, `cannon` and is counted in `stats().unknownWeapon`); `info.round` selects the projectile (`shot, shell, case, canister, double_canister, ball, buck_and_ball, blank`); numeric audio weapon ids 0 to 17 and class words also resolve; `direction` may be a vector or a heading in the prototype convention. `info.shotId` (optional) is the cause id for later bursts; otherwise the cause is rederived from (unit, figure, `fireT`, weapon). **Requests appended to BT-EVENTS.md section 6:** R-FX-1 an `impact` event (shell burst, case burst, solid strike, ricochet) with `fireT`; R-FX-2 `info.fuze` and `info.fuzeS` on guns; R-FX-3 `unit_march` `info.surface` and `info.lengthM`. Until `impact` exists, shell and case bursts and ground strikes are not drawn (FX cannot invent where a shell lands); canister dust is drawn by FX itself.

Seek: `fx.seek(t)` (called by `update` when time goes backwards or jumps more than 900 s) clears the store and returns `{from: t - 300, to: t}`; the host replays `FirePlan.shotsBetween(from, to, ...)` into `onFire` (BT-EVENTS section 4). Fires and the wind are pure functions of time and need no replay.

## 9. Wiring (after the Release 1 passes; not done)

1. `render-host.mjs` / the renderer: `const fx = createFx({ data: {params, weapons, fires}, environment, geo, groundAt: T.h, tier, api, soldiers: total })`, load the five JSON files from `data/fx3d/`.
2. `anim.bus` -> `fx.attach(anim.bus)`; on seek replay the window `fx.replayWindow()` through `FirePlan.shotsBetween`.
3. Per frame: `const f = fx.update(t, camera)`; upload `f.events` (first `nEvents * 24` floats) to the event texture / storage buffer, `f.cum` to the cumulative texture, `f.windHist` to the wind texture, `f.lights` to the light uniform; draw `f.nSprites` instances of 4 vertices (`uFirst` = 0), blend `ONE, ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA` for all kinds (additive kinds write alpha 0); show `f.budget.text` in the effects panel and `f.uncertainFires` as a "time uncertain" marker; call `fx.govern(measuredEffectMs)` once a second.
4. The depth pre-pass must provide linear view depth in metres (`uDepth`), and the camera `fwd`, `fovY`, `heightPx`.
5. Sound: AUDIO owns `d / c`; FX draws light at `t` and does not delay anything.

## 10. Limits stated honestly

LOD route of a shot (near puff or far fog) is chosen **at spawn** from the camera then; a fast camera move shows pops until the next seek. Sprite sorting is per event back to front on the CPU path and spawn order on the GPU path. The fog map is CPU-rasterised (about 10 Hz is enough). Ground reflection uses a flat ground under each puff. Merged events restart their diffusion clock at the merge instant. Ricochet hops follow a straight line of flight. The light of a flash does not shadow. None of this has been seen.

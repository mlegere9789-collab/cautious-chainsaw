# Battle Theatre: page shell

A separate page with its own renderer. It now runs on the **real history layer, version 1** (`src/history/real.mjs`): the order of battle (726 units), the arrival ledger with its source conflicts as selectable scenarios, strengths, and positions only where the ledger, or a claim of the Official Records position ledgers (version 2 of the layer), names a road of approach (or a formation above the unit has one) and a dated place that the gazetteer can resolve. Most units have no place in the record yet and are shown as **open gaps**, not guessed (counts and every gap: [HISTORY-LAYER-NOTES.md](HISTORY-LAYER-NOTES.md)). The placeholder layer of the earlier shell is kept behind `?history=stub` (it carries the prototype's 165,000-figure crowd that the performance and shell tests use).

Contract 1.5 (`data-contract.md`) reads the day-level positions of Bachelder's 1876 battle maps (sheets 1 to 3) and of the hour-labelled sheet LOC 99447494 (484 claims, `data/daypositions.json`): a brigade, battery or artillery label with exactly one label on its sheet places its regiments or batteries on that day, **documented for the day, the time modelled** (DEC-010, DEC-011); division and corps labels place nothing, and every label is listed on the card with its sigma and its distance from the ledgers' timed places (both shown, neither chosen). **History layer v7 (contract 1.7, DEC-018)** reads the **line state of every Bachelder label** (the legend's First Position, Intermediate, Evening and All Day styles, read by eye: `library/LINE-STATES-NOTES.md`) into a window of the day: the state is documented for (unit, place, state), the window is modelled from the legend's definition and the evening convention (sunset 19:32 LMT, the PDF's labelled convention) unless a complete clock printed beside the line gives the documented time; several labels whose states put them in order (first, intermediate, evening) are each drawn in their window, several labels the states do not order are listed and none is drawn; an evening line with no known daytime place says so on the card (no daytime place is invented); a window that cannot be met from the unit's gate is a reported state offset, no gate moves. The counts, the rules and the largest disagreements are in [HISTORY-LAYER-NOTES.md](HISTORY-LAYER-NOTES.md).

The **structures** are drawn in 3D from the 1863 base-map layers (`docs/basemap1863/features/`): fence and wall panels along the 30 lines the 1868-69 survey sheet confirms, 40 building footprints and 229 borough masses, labelled poles for the 117 structures known only as a point, and ground ribbons for the roads, lanes and railway; their state over time is read from the state schedule (`structures/state_schedule.json`, the port of `check_structures.py state_at`). No footprint, line or dimension is invented, every object has a card with its geometry source, error and tier, and everything not drawn is listed with its reason: [STRUCTURES-3D-NOTES.md](STRUCTURES-3D-NOTES.md).

Contract 1.4 (`data-contract.md`) reads approach roads, stops and departures from the four OR vol. 27 position ledgers (3,503 claims; only claims whose support is verified and whose fidelity check is `passed_full_read` or `corrected` are used), cites on each card the claim, report, printed page, quoted words, tier and checks, and lists the reports that disagree without choosing between them: counts, every claim left out and why, and the conflicts are in [HISTORY-LAYER-NOTES.md](HISTORY-LAYER-NOTES.md).

Contract 1.3 added casualties (procedural shares of printed losses, placed only in printed windows of the timeline, men who fall lie where they fell), guns (drawn only where the kit states a count), per-figure kit manifests and a change-of-formation event: method, ranges and what is not done are in [CASUALTIES-NOTES.md](CASUALTIES-NOTES.md).

## Run

```
node docs/battle-theatre/app/tools/serve.mjs 10760          # any static server rooted at docs/battle-theatre works
http://127.0.0.1:10760/app/index.html                        # opens on WebGL2 (the path that draws weather and effects); add ?api=webgpu for WebGPU, #theatre to open straight into it
```

Query: `?history=stub` (the placeholder layer: 992 stand-in units, 165,000 figures; every datum labelled *Modelled placeholder*), `?api=webgl2` (the default since DD-27; `?api=webgpu` asks for WebGPU, `?api=auto` for WebGPU first), `?demo=tiers` (labels some placeholder units with other tiers only to show the three overlay colours; the card says so), `?home=<url>` (target of "Return to the map"; or the `bt-home` meta tag), and the prototype's own `nearQ midQ nearR midR shadow mid`.

Tests (system Chrome with WebGPU; each file starts its own server and browser):

```
node docs/battle-theatre/app/tests/run-all.mjs                      # all, one after another, about 4 to 12 minutes depending on the load; writes tests/results-*.json
node --test docs/battle-theatre/app/tests/unit.test.mjs             # no browser, 2 s
node --test docs/battle-theatre/app/tests/casualties.test.mjs docs/battle-theatre/app/tests/manifest.test.mjs   # no browser: casualties, guns, formation, manifests
python tools/bt/check_figures.py --write                              # the B.6.12 validator over every drawn figure (Python), writes data/figures-validation.json
node --test docs/battle-theatre/app/tests/leak.spec.mjs             # BT_CYCLES=20 by default
```

## Modelled roads of approach (history layer v6, DEC-015)

The record names no road of approach for 174 leaf units at v5. The plan asks for the strongest evidence-based reconstruction, registered and marked, rather than a hole (Law clause 3), so the units that have a gate and a Bachelder day position take the road that every documented sibling formation of their corps names, provided no report on their line of command names another road, a road the repository cannot place or a road outside the loaded ground. 56 units are drawn this way (32 of Longstreet's corps by the Chambersburg Pike, on Kershaw's report alone; 24 of the Fifth Corps by the Hanover Road, on the 20th Maine row and Day's division). Their cards say MODELLED APPROACH, list the siblings with report, page and printed words, say what is documented (the gate; the day position: sheet, label, error) and what is modelled (the road, the minute at the edge, the place in the column), and link EG-0070. The position on each day is the troop-map position as for any other unit; no gate moves.

## How it reuses the prototype

Imported unchanged (but for two null-guarded hook points in each renderer, `ext.shadow` and `ext.scene`, called at the end of the shadow and scene passes; `src/structures/gpu.mjs` is their only user): `world.mjs` (`loadTerrain`, `buildWorld`), `camera.mjs` (`Camera`), `math.mjs`, `webgpu.mjs` and `webgl2.mjs` (and through them `characters`, `lod`, `fx`, `shaders`). Wrapped, with the reason:

- **`main.mjs` cannot be imported**: it runs on import, awaits at top level, adds window listeners and keeps no handle to undo them. `theatre.mjs` repeats its ~40 lines of orchestration with a teardown.
- **`FlyControls` is not used**: it adds window listeners that cannot be removed. `controls.mjs` replaces it.
- **The renderer's unit and gun tables are static after init** (one linear segment per unit). The shell rewrites them whenever the stage changes (`queue.writeBuffer` on `R.unitsB` and `R.gunsB`; `texSubImage2D` on `R.t.units` and `R.t.guns` in WebGL2). A unit that is not on the field is parked 1,000 km away (culled by the CPU LOD pass and by the GPU), and its guns stop firing. Velocity is zero and the shell moves units each frame, so there is no float-time drift over 18 days.
- **The soldier tables are rewritten too** (contract 1.3): `sold1.x` (fall time, in the animation clock) when a man falls, and the `sold0` slots when a fallen man must stay where he fell or a unit changes formation (`RenderHost.uploadSoldiers`: `writeBuffer` on `R.s0B`, `R.s1B`; `texSubImage2D` on `R.t.sold0`, `R.t.sold1` in WebGL2). Written only when something changed; the shaders and the CPU LOD pass are untouched.
- **Animation time is a separate clock** (`anim.t`), advanced at most 2x while playing and re-anchored on every seek, so gait and smoke stay readable at 60x and float precision never degrades. History time is the sim clock.
- **After a seek the effects are replayed** (26 s in 0.5 s steps, the effect random stream seeded by the target time), so a seek to a time always shows the same smoke.
- **Teardown**: `GPUDevice.destroy()`, `unconfigure()`, `WEBGL_lose_context`. The prototype logs "device lost" with `console.error` for every loss, including a deliberate one; during teardown that one message is filtered. Chrome also prints a warning about `powerPreference` for every `requestAdapter` call the prototype makes; it cannot be avoided without editing the prototype and is the only warning the tests allow.
- The prototype's `init` is one synchronous block of about 300 ms (character bake, pipelines). It is the long task at entry; it cannot be split without editing the prototype.

## Time, purity and the arrival rule

Time is minutes since 1863-06-01 00:00 local mean time (the unit of the timeline file). The scrubber covers 26 Jun - 14 Jul 1863; a chapter marker on the night of 4 Jul leads to the epilogue chapter (18-19 Nov 1863, its own short range, opening at 00:00 on 19 Nov because the timeline file records no time of day for it).

The stage feeds history events to `sim/cache.mjs` (`EventCache`, snapshots every 30 minutes) and computes every unit's place, heading and clip from `state(t)` and `t` alone, so a seek is the same as playing there (gate Q12; tested through the scrubber's input events and the keyboard, and at cache level with `scrubTest`). Presence (gate Q04) is enforced twice: the cache refuses events outside a unit's window (the stub contains one deliberately early event; `arrival.spec.mjs` injects one into the real layer's cache; either way it is refused and the About panel says so), and the stage will not draw a unit whose window has not opened. Arrival is a march: in the real layer a unit appears on its named road outside the loaded ground (the head of a column 120 m beyond the edge, the rest behind it along the road, one column length apiece) and walks in at drill speed (march 1.25 m/s, double-quick 2.44 m/s from Hardee 1861 when the ledger time demands it); the stub places the unit 60 m beyond the edge plus its radius.

## Not done, or not working

- **Formation change moves 33 drawn units** (history layer v6): the two printed “deploys” clauses name Rodes’ division (CL-306) and McLaws’ division (CL-231), and the units drawn under them are Rodes’, Kershaw’s, Barksdale’s and Cabell’s. Only column of fours and line are drawn, on foot units with a printed change; the 90 s length is a tuning value.
- **Casualties are procedural.** No regimental casualty number exists in the record; losses are shares of corps and brigade totals, placed only in the printed windows of 31 timeline rows (one of them contradicted by the OR27 audit, shown on the card); a sixth of the planned falls (3,015 of 17,554) are not drawn because the unit is still marching (in, or to a state-ordered troop-map position); missing men are not animated; the regimental figures printed inside seeded rows are not used. See CASUALTIES-NOTES.md.
- **Guns:** 124 pieces of the 22 drawn batteries that have a stated count are drawn (50 of the 72 drawn batteries have none); the renderer has one gun model and none for limbers, caissons or teams.
- **Per-figure kit:** the renderer can show only the coat's brightness class of a manifest.
- **Works and pre-existing walls are still 2D rings** at the place their location text names (no work or wall has a mapped line in the repository), with their build windows; there is no 3D work or earthwork, and none is derived from a modern rebuilt profile. Fences, buildings, markers and roads are drawn in 3D (see STRUCTURES-3D-NOTES.md for what is not).
- **Weather is shown (chips, rain streaks) but not rendered in 3D**: the prototype has no clouds, wet ground, wind or fog. Night is exposure, light direction (the moon's, from the table) and a cool grade; the sky cannot show stars.
- **Outside the loaded ground** (6.2 x 6.1 km): 2 of the 16 tour stops (2 Eternal Light Peace Memorial, 3 Oak Ridge) and 77 of the 512 monuments are listed but disabled.
- **Entry** takes about 0.5 s warm and 0.8 s cold, with one long task of about 300 ms inside the prototype's renderer init, and sometimes another of 40-55 ms inside it. After the theatre is ready no long task over 50 ms was seen in any test.
- Timeline row text is the printed row split at its column markers; rows whose location or actors wrap oddly may start with a few words of them.
- Audio is not part of this shell (nothing to leak).
- Not run: Safari, Firefox, real phones (a 390 x 844 touch emulation is), a long soak. The WebGL2 tier is tested at 1280 x 720.

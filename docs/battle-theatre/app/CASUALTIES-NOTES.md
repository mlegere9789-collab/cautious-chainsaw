# Casualties, guns, per-figure kit and change of formation: notes

Contract 1.3 of the history layer (`data-contract.md`). Counts below are the ones the layer computes now; `tests/casualties.test.mjs` fails if the numbers quoted here stop matching. The full computed tables (pools, windows per unit, guns, manifests, clauses) are in [HISTORY-LAYER-NOTES.md](HISTORY-LAYER-NOTES.md).

## What the record holds, and what it does not

| Available | Not available |
|---|---|
| a single loss figure printed for 13 corps rows and 8 brigades (PDF B.3 table; Stone Sentinels, a weak source), total only: killed, wounded and missing are not split at any unit level | **any regimental casualty number** (Busey and Martin is print-only and was not read); a split by fate below the army |
| army-level totals and killed/wounded/missing splits from several sources (PDF B.1: Wikipedia, Stone Sentinels, Busey and Martin as quoted, Civil War Medicine museum) and from OR vol. 27 (return total 23,049, Letterman's wounded 14,193) | an OR27 split for the Confederate army (Guild's missing column and grand total are OCR-garbled) |
| 31 rows of the PDF master timeline that print a two-ended window of a fight (or a point with a printed duration), read in `data/engagements.json` | a window for most units: 691 timeline rows are text, their actors are not unit ids |

So **every number below a printed row is a procedural share, tier plausible-procedural, weak source, with its basis written on it**, and the card says so. No regimental number is invented and none is read from anywhere (see "Not used" below).

## Method

1. **Total.** A printed unit keeps its printed figure. A leaf unit (regiment, battalion, battery) on the field takes the loss of its nearest figured ancestor, less the losses printed for that ancestor's figured sub-units, shared by the same leaf units as the strength (`strength.mjs` pools; equal shares, the remainder going to the leaves with the lowest hash of the unit id). The shares add up to the pool exactly (tested for all 21 pools) and no share exceeds the unit's strength share. 564 units carry a number: 21 printed, 543 procedural.
2. **Total range.** `[printed, printed x r]` where r is the largest total any source gives for the army divided by the Stone Sentinels total: Union 23,055 / 22,822 = 1.0102, Confederate 28,063 / 22,709 = 1.2358. It is the army-level spread of the sources applied to the unit, not a claim about the unit. The picture uses the low end, the printed figure (DEC-008).
3. **Fates.** For each of killed, wounded, missing, the lowest and highest fraction any source gives for the army (`data/casualty-sources.json`; Union: Wikipedia, Civil War Medicine, Letterman; Confederate: Busey and Martin as quoted, Civil War Medicine): Union killed 13.7%, wounded 61.6% to 63.0%, missing 23.3%; Confederate killed 13.9% to 20.3%, wounded 54.6% to 66.8%, missing 19.3% to 25.1%. The range of a unit is those fractions applied to its total range. The split that decides which figure lies down is the middle of each fraction range normalised to the total by largest remainder (DEC-008, provisional builder choice, the owner decides). **Caveat:** army-level fractions applied to one regiment. Day-one corps (I, XI) lost far more men missing (prisoners) than the army average; the model understates them.
4. **Time.** Only inside printed windows (`data/engagements.json`; clause table `tools/engagement-clauses.mjs`, built by `tools/build-engagements.mjs`, which refuses a phrase that is not in the row, an unknown unit, or a window that is not the row's own). A unit takes the windows of every formation above it. The sim's loss solver (`sim/loss_solver.mjs`) places each man: totals match exactly, each man falls once, times from the counter-based RNG (`sim/rng.mjs`) so a seek equals sequential play, intensity uniform inside the windows (no printed intensity), front-biased choice of men (the sim's own rule). The solver's plan is scenario independent.
5. **Where a unit stands.** A fall is drawn only while the unit stands on the map: after it has entered and arrived at a printed place, before it leaves. A loss whose time falls while the unit is marching in (101 of the 242 placed units arrive 21 to 72 minutes after their ledger time because the loaded ground ends kilometres from the printed place; see HISTORY-LAYER-NOTES.md) is **not drawn**: it stays in the unit's total and the card says how many were not shown.
6. **The picture.** A killed or wounded man falls (the prototype's fall clip, 1.6 s) and lies. A man who has fallen stays where he fell: the stage rewrites his slot in the unit's frame every time the unit moves or turns, so the unit marches away from him. A missing man (captured, deserted, unidentified dead) is counted but not animated: the renderer cannot show a man leaving the line. The prototype renderer is imported unchanged; the stage rewrites the soldier tables (`sold1.x`, fall times in the renderer's animation clock, and the `sold0` slots) and `RenderHost.uploadSoldiers` writes them when they change. A seek recomputes every fall against the new clock (`stage.rebase()`); going back stands the men up again (tested pixel for pixel on WebGPU and WebGL2).

## Numbers (computed)

170 of the 439 drawn units have a printed window. The solver places 17,574 men in time; 14,553 fall where their unit stands (killed 2,352, wounded 8,902, missing 3,299: the 11,254 killed and wounded lie down) and 3,021 fall while it is marching in or away or not yet arrived and are not drawn (history layer v6, DEC-015: 56 more units are drawn by a modelled road taken from the sibling formations of their corps, so more windows have a unit that stands on the map; the road of those units is modelled, the windows and the position are as for any other unit; history layer v8, DEC-023E: ledger E draws 9 more units (the 2nd Division of the Eleventh Corps and Wiedrich's battery, 17,574 men placed in time instead of 17,554) and the 84th Pennsylvania, documented with the supply train, takes no share; history layer v7, DEC-018: the line states of the troop-map labels time the marches to the day positions, so 17 falls that were shown at v6 now happen while a unit is marching to its evening or intermediate line and are not drawn). The 21 units drawn at the arrival edge only never stand on the map, so none of their losses (for example Merritt's printed window of 3 Jul 15:00-17:30, Hampton's 13:30-13:45) is drawn. 213 leaf units on the field take a window; the remaining procedural shares have no printed window and stay a total (an open gap, never spread over the day). The 31 windows' OR27 audit verdicts: 25 supported, 4 ambiguous in the OR, 1 not found, **1 contradicted** (EN-027, row T3-039, the dawn attack on Culp's Hill: the row prints 05:00-05:30, every OR report puts the onset at 03:30 to 04:00; kept because the PDF row is the project default and only the owner changes defaults; the verdict is on the card).

## Not used, and why

- **Regimental figures printed inside seeded timeline rows**: 151st PA 337 of 467 and 24th MI 399 of 496 (T1-147), 147th NY 207 of 380 (T1-103), 16th ME 298 men and 35 survivors (T1-161), 1st MN 215 of about 262 (T2-184); formation-level figures in T1-108, T1-129, T1-157. The rows are `seeded_unverified`, the OR27 audit could not read the digits, and they do not agree with the procedural strength share that is drawn (the 151st PA is drawn with 308 figures, not 467), so using them would make a regiment lose more than it is drawn with. They are listed here, not silently dropped; the clause notes in `engagement-clauses.mjs` say "NOT used". They would be the first pinned fates (`solveLosses` accepts them) once the strengths are sourced.
- **Named fates** (Reynolds, Fowler, Wilkeson, Barlow, ...): the solver supports pinned fates, but no figure of the drawn crowd is a named man; there is nothing to pin (EG-0001).
- **Guild's Confederate killed and wounded columns** and Wikipedia's 23,000-28,000 range: no fraction can be formed from them.
- **Horse casualties, guns lost, prisoners as an event** (the carry paths to aid stations): not part of this item; no horse or gun casualty is animated.

## Guns

Gun types are stated for 29 of 125 batteries in the kit data; 23 match one battery of the roster by name, 4 are combined entries ("3rd US F&K", "Pa. Independent C & F", "1st Pennsylvania F&G", "1st NY K with 11th NY attached") that are not split, 2 match no roster battery (2nd US A, which belongs to the horse artillery brigade, and Hurt's, a battery inside McIntosh's battalion). Of the 57 drawn batteries 21 have a stated count (118 pieces; the roster’s II Corps and Artillery Reserve batteries came on with the position statements and the road-name concordance of history layer v5): among them the 4th New York Battery (III Corps) states six 10-pounder Parrotts (a monument, a weak source), so its six guns are drawn, each with the nine-man detachment taken out of the battery’s own 249 figures. The other 36 (among them 2nd and 5th ME, 1st NY E, 1st NY L, 1st PA B, 4th US B: Wainwright’s) have no stated count, so **no gun is drawn for them**; each card says “not drawn: no stated gun count”. The mechanism (a stated count draws that many pieces, each with the manual's nine-man detachment, taken out of the battery's own figures) is tested on a constructed layer; limbers, caissons and teams have no model in the renderer and are listed on the card. Provisional choices (DEC-008): visual firing cadence 40 s (inside the PDF's 20-30 s and the manuals' 30-60 s, which stay PENDING OWNER), 14 m between pieces (the prototype's value; the drill texts read hold none).

## Per-figure kit

Every one of the 127,572 drawn figures has a manifest (`src/history/manifest.mjs`, a line-for-line port of `check_kit.manifest_for`; 2,616 golden digests equal). Kit record by `tools/bt/kit_crosswalk.py`: 52 units by `kit_presence.py`, 12 by the cavalry suffix rule, 3 by alias (Cobb's, Jeff Davis, Phillips Legions), 18 by battery name, 33 by their brigade's record, 145 use the default for their army and arm (tier plausible-procedural). Rank private and role rifleman for every figure (no rank structure in the record). The B.6.12 validator ran in Python over all 127,572 figures (127,852 manifests with the disputed variants; `tools/bt/check_figures.py`): 0 fail, 2,350 flagged (disputed dress 1,070, badge unresolved 1,035, Confederate cavalry kepi as a private purchase 245); Python and the shell make the same manifests for every figure; the manifest of the day before each unit's arrival is refused for all 439 units, and `kit_presence.make_presence_fn` agrees with the arrival checker at the gate of the 79 units it resolves. The 140th NY and every other disputed dress carry both variants flagged, none chosen (not drawn today; tested by pointing a drawn regiment at its record). **What the renderer shows of a manifest:** one thing, the coat's brightness class (the renderer's per-figure variation scalar), driven by the wear level (an inferred tuning parameter: "no source quantifies soiling"). Headgear, coat, shirt, trousers, shoes, accoutrements, weapon, bayonet, sword, sash, pack, badge, colours and horse coat colour have no support in the renderer (one soldier model, one palette per army); the card lists them as not drawn.

## Change of formation

`formation` event (contract 1.3), applied to the two printed "deploys" clauses (CL-231, McLaws's division at 15:00 on 2 Jul; CL-306, Rodes's division at about 21:00 near East Cemetery Hill). Neither has a drawn unit under it (no road of approach resolves for those two divisions: the Middletown road of Rodes and the road of McLaws are not in the gazetteer, so those units are open gaps), so neither moves a drawn unit today. Tested on a constructed layer that points CL-231 at the Iron Brigade: the figures blend from the column of fours to the line over a smoothstep (the largest step in 2 s is under 6% of a figure's whole move: no snap), a formation the unit has no layout for is listed in `stage.unsupported`, a unit not on the map at the clause time gets no event. Default length 90 s is a tuning value (no drill-manual figure for the time of a deployment is in the repository).

## Ribbon text

"Version 0 of the record" is **kept**: it describes the record, not the code. The record is seeded and unverified (every timeline row `seeded_unverified`, `claim_support_verified` false on every ledger row, 218 of 376 position clauses unresolved, casualties and kit procedural); the layer's own code version (v1) does not make the record version 1. The long text now also says "losses and kit are procedural shares" (`src/theatre.mjs`; `tests/real.spec.mjs` asserts both).

## Defects and limits found

- 101 of the 242 placed units arrive 21 to 72 minutes after their ledger time (a data property of the loaded ground, reported before this item); with it, 17% of the planned falls (3,015 of 17,554 at history layer v7) are not drawn. A larger loaded ground or a procedural approach would change that; neither is mine to add.
- The fallen figure's body orientation follows the unit's heading (the lying pose turns if the unit turns after he fell); his place does not move.
- A crew figure of a drawn gun is not given a fall (only the battery's own block is).
- The Confederate and cavalry corps' losses are shared equally over regiments and batteries (the known distortion of the strength shares): the 5.2% loss rate of the Cavalry Corps gives each cavalry regiment about 20 men, the I Corps 130 to each battery and regiment of its pool.
- `fate_basis` applies army-level fractions to single regiments (above).
- The loss solver's front bias makes killed men come from the head of the column and missing men from the rear: the sim's own rule (PDF D.3.2), not a historical claim.

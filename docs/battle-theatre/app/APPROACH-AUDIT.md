## What was asked and what was found (v6)

The question (brief sibling-roads): 164 leaf units were open gaps at v5 because no report names a road of approach for them or any formation above them (or the road named is one the repository cannot place). Plan rule (PDF Law clause 3, gating rule 3): where the record cannot supply a datum, build the strongest evidence-based reconstruction, register it, mark it in the product and escalate it; a concealed gap is a violation, a visible modelled reconstruction is not. **The rule (DEC-015).** A unit takes the road documented for the OTHER formations of its corps only if (a) every documented sibling formation names the same gazetteer road, that road crosses the loaded ground and at least one sibling exists; (b) no report for the unit or any formation above it names a road the repository cannot place (the road from Scotland, the route by Horner's Mill, a railroad), another road of approach, or a road outside the loaded ground (Black Horse Tavern Road), and the reports do not disagree; (c) the unit has a gate and a single-label day position. Stuart's cavalry is not covered by the sibling rule at all. **The result.** 56 units in 8 records are drawn: 32 of the First Corps of the Army of Northern Virginia (Chambersburg Pike, from Kershaw's report) and 24 of the Fifth Corps (Hanover Road, from the 20th Maine row and Day's division report). 108 leaf units stay open, each with its reason on its card. Every drawn unit comes from its arrival gate (no gate moved), stands at its Bachelder day position as any other unit does, and its card says what is documented (the gate, the day position: sheet, label, error) and what is modelled (the road, the minute at the edge, the place in the column).

## The reasons, in words

* `road_outside_ground`: a road is printed for the line of command but it does not cross the loaded ground (Black Horse Tavern Road, AP-07): no other road is chosen for the unit
* `road_not_resolvable`: a report of the line names a road, and the repository holds no such road (Heidlersburg, Middletown, the Gettysburg road, the road from Scotland, the Waynesborough pike, a railroad), or two roads of the road layer could carry the name; none is guessed
* `route_not_a_road`: the passage names a route by a mill or a village (Horner's Mill), not a road: the repository holds no such road, and none is inferred from the map
* `reports_disagree`: the reports of the same formation print different roads; none is chosen (the disagreement is listed)
* `siblings_disagree`: the documented sibling formations of the corps name different roads (for example the Second Corps: Early the Harrisburg Road, Rodes the Carlisle Road): none is chosen
* `no_sibling_road`: no other formation of the corps has a documented road of approach, so there is nothing to take the road from
* `sibling_road_no_day_position`: the sibling road is available, but the unit has no single-label day position (a Bachelder sheet), so a drawn unit would only stand in a column at the edge of the ground: not drawn
* `cavalry_own_road_only`: Stuart’s cavalry: drawn only by a road documented for its own brigade or division, and only earlier marches are printed (no day position either)
* `no_figure`: a headquarters, staff or grouping entry: the record gives it no figure

### What was asked and what was found

The question (brief road-concordance): 108 of the 285 open-gap leaf units of v4 were open because their reports name a road that the repository does not hold (the Heidlersburg, Middletown and Fairfield roads, “the Gettysburg road”, the road from Scotland, the route by Horner's Mill, railroads), and 37 Second Corps leaves would be drawn by admitting position claims that print a road (DEC-013 a). v5 answers both, with the evidence in `library/ROAD-CONCORDANCE.md`: 120 units are drawn that were not (v4: 263 drawn, 285 open; v5: 383 drawn, 164 open). **Position statements.** A position or night_position claim whose printed words put a corps or formation ON a road before it came to the field is a road of approach of tier inferred (the Second Corps on the road between Taneytown and Gettysburg, the Artillery Reserve on the Taneytown road, the Second Cavalry Division on the Hanover road 3 miles out). Every other position claim that prints a road (38 on open lines) says where a line, a battery, a camp or a headquarters stood inside the field, or is the road of another place, and is not used; each has its reason below. **Period names.** Three names are resolved to a road the gazetteer holds, each with an argument and rejected alternatives and none with a period map that prints the name (tier inferred): the Heidlersburg road is the Harrisburg Road, the Middletown road is the Carlisle Road, and Kershaw's “Gettysburg road” from Fayetteville is the Chambersburg Pike for that passage only. The road from Scotland, the route by Horner's Mill, the Fairfield road and the Gettysburg road of other passages stay unresolved, with the reason in the concordance. No road was traced and no road was aliased by guess.

The rule used for a road of approach (DEC-009, DEC-012, extended by DEC-013 a and DEC-014): the road is printed in the claim's verified quote, in its printed place or road words, or in the cited OR record as confirmed by `tools/verify_claim_phrases.py` (a compiler's bracketed gloss is never evidence); it is a line of the gazetteer that crosses the loaded ground, or a period name that a RESOLVED entry of the concordance covering this claim identifies with one; the claim says the unit marched, moved, arrived or (kind position) stood ON the road before it came to the field (not where it stood, formed, camped or crossed inside the field, and not the road of another place); no other report of the same formation prints another road (the reports of a unit that disagree with the formation above are listed in `approach_block` and the unit takes no road). A road named only for a corps applies to the formations under it by inheritance and a nearer printed road overrides it. The time is the unit's gate; the entry is where the road crosses the edge of the loaded ground; the order in the column is the roster order (plausible-procedural, EG-0005).

### The reasons, in words

* `road_outside_ground`: a road is printed for the line of command but it does not cross the loaded ground (Black Horse Tavern Road, AP-07): no other road is chosen for the unit
* `road_not_resolvable`: a report of the line names a road, and the repository holds no such road (Heidlersburg, Middletown, the Gettysburg road, the road from Scotland, the Waynesborough pike, a railroad), or two roads of the road layer could carry the name; none is guessed
* `road_name_ambiguous`: the name printed is not the gazetteer's and two roads could carry it
* `reports_disagree`: the reports of the same formation print different roads; none is chosen (the disagreement is listed)
* `earlier_march`: a march before the battle, not the approach to the field
* `earlier_leg`: an earlier leg of the march outside the loaded ground; another claim prints the leg that enters the field
* `position_not_approach`: the road is where the unit stood, formed, camped or crossed inside the field: no approach is stated
* `no_quote`: an inferred claim with no verbatim quote: the road cannot be checked against printed words
* `no_road_named`: the passage names no road (a direction, a town, “the road”)
* `route_not_a_road`: the passage names a route by a mill or a village (Horner's Mill), not a road: the repository holds no such road, and none is inferred from the map
* `movement_black_horse`: the road named is the Black Horse Tavern road, which AP-07 prints for the division and which does not cross the loaded ground (no other road is chosen for the unit)
* `movement_road_not_resolvable`: the road named is the “Fairfield road” or the road leading from Gettysburg to Fairfield: two roads of the road layer could carry the name (DEC-007 item 3), so none is chosen
* `movement_order`: a dispatch or an order printed as a movement (what a commander told another to do), not the road a unit marched on to the field
* `movement_in_field`: a move made after the unit stood on the field, or inside it; the road named is where it moved, not the road it came onto the field by
* `movement_road_unnamed`: no road is named: only “the road”, a wrong road, or a direction
* `movement_wagon_train`: a move of the wagon train or its guard; not the approach of the unit's line
* `road_of_another_place`: the road named is a road of another place (the Taneytown to Emmitsburg road, the Chambersburg to Carlisle road), not the gazetteer road of the same name
* `position_in_field`: a position statement that prints a road says where a line, a battery, a camp or a headquarters stood inside the field (the road is a reference for the place): no approach is stated
* `road_not_printed`: the road stands only in the compiler's bracketed gloss or summary, not in the claim's printed words (a bracketed gloss is never evidence)
* `record_text_only`: the road word stands only in the report text around the quote (a position beside a road, a camp, a crossing, a line of battle, an earlier leg, an order, or another formation's statement); the claim's own words name no road of approach for this unit (read in full, no approach statement found)

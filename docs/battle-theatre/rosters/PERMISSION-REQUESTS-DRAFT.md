# Draft permission requests (NOT SENT; the owner sends them from the project address)

Why: the 30 June 1863 company muster rolls (NARA compiled service records) are the best open evidence of who was actually present at Gettysburg. FamilySearch hosts them but forbids bulk download without written permission (reply time stated as about 45 days). The NPS Soldiers and Sailors database is the other bulk route and states no reuse terms.

## 1. FamilySearch (bulk use of Civil War compiled service records, NARA M-series)

Subject: Request for written permission: non-commercial educational use of Civil War muster-roll data for the Gettysburg battle

Hello,

I run the Gettysburg Interactive Map (https://gettysburginteractivemap.com), a free educational site with no ads or accounts. I am building a roster section listing the soldiers of both armies who were present at the Battle of Gettysburg, with a source citation for every name.

I would like written permission to download, for this one purpose, the company muster rolls dated 30 June 1863 (and the related compiled service record cards) for the regiments and batteries engaged at Gettysburg (about 570 units), to extract the names and present/absent status, and to publish the extracted names and status on the site with attribution to FamilySearch and the National Archives. I will rate-limit all requests, will not redistribute the images, and will follow any technical limits you set. If bulk download is not possible, please tell me the permitted route.

Thank you,
Mike Legere
gettysburginteractivemap@gmail.com

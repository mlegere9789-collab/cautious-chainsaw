Evidence labels as in `PLAN.md`: **Measured** (a command run for this report produced it), **Documented** (a source text says it; the source is named), **Inferred** (a stated rule on printed text), **Not run**. ## 0. Summary

* **41 unit files** under `rosters/data2/confederate/nc/` (33 infantry regiments and battalions, 4 cavalry regiments, 4 batteries), **54,403 lines in all** (4,362,304 raw bytes, 987,866 gzipped), format `roster2/2` (`pipeline2/r2lib.py`), each with a flag file `<unit>.flags.json.gz` (623,095 bytes in all). One line per name entry of the printed roll, exactly as the text layer prints it (name, rank, remarks), with the printed page and the character offset of the name in the pinned text. **No unit is a gap**: all 41 units of the work item have a file.
* **One printed source is parsed**: John W. Moore, *Roster of North Carolina Troops in the War between the States* (Raleigh, 1882), four volumes, each read in **two Internet Archive scans** (8 texts, `NC-MOORE-1882.<vol>` and `.<vol>~b`; section 3). For each unit the scan with the better measured score is the reading of the file (1 from NC-MOORE-1882.1; 14 from NC-MOORE-1882.1~b; 2 from NC-MOORE-1882.2; 11 from NC-MOORE-1882.2~b; 8 from NC-MOORE-1882.3; 2 from NC-MOORE-1882.3~b; 1 from NC-MOORE-1882.4; 2 from NC-MOORE-1882.4~b); the other scan is the second reading of the reconciliation only, and **no line of a unit file is taken from it**. * **Not parsed**: the 12 printed notes inside the rolls (Moore: "Note.", prose, listed in section 5), the 1,350 tokens of text between a section heading and the first roll heading (the list of abbreviations and the like, counted in `diagnostics/<unit>.json`), Moore's Recapitulation (read only for the printed totals of section 1), and **the Clark histories of 1901** (present-for-duty figures and officer lists inside narrative: not fetched, not read; gap EG-ROS-NC-05). No name, date or figure is invented, completed or repaired.

One page image was looked at, to settle one damaged name (section 5, item 12). **Not run**: the independent sample of page images (R-G9; roster-qa), a second reading of the damaged lines from page images, any browser or timing run.
* **No label of presence** is written into any line (DD-ROS-01). Derived marks live in the flag files, never in the lines: `ocr_uncertain` (8,483 of 54,403 lines match a damage pattern, section 2), `on_rolls_july_1863` (inferred from printed dates only: 29,810 yes, 11,210 no, 13,383 unknown), `names_gettysburg` (3,834 lines whose remarks name Gettysburg, a lower bound) and `gettysburg_dated` (1,203 of these also print 1, 2 or 3 July 1863). ## 2. The unit files

One gzip JSON-lines file per unit, `[offset delta, name, rank, remarks, remark delta]` lines with carried state lines `co` (company), `pg` (printed page) and `rk` (the rank heading above the lines, as printed), one **segment** per source text (`NC-MOORE-1882.<vol>` or `.<vol>~b`, layout `flow`; every unit has exactly one: no line is read from a second scan). Lines are in the order of the printed page. The writer is `r2lib.write_unit` (deterministic bytes).

* **name** (`name_printed`): the tokens of the head of the entry as the text layer prints them, joined by single blanks, with the printed commas and periods. Moore prints privates "Surname, Given, e. date; County co.; remarks" and officers "Given Surname, Rank, [cm. date;] County co.; remarks"; the head is cut where the entry begins (a surname-first head with an enlistment or commission marker, or a given-name-first head with a rank phrase); a damaged name stays damaged. Running heads ("North Carolina State Troops." and "Company X. Nth Regiment.") and page numbers are left out.
* **rank**: where the entry prints a rank (7,725 lines) it is the printed rank; every other line carries the rank of the **heading** above it (`^PRIVATES`, `^NON-COMMISSIONED OFFICERS`, `^OFFICERS`, `^FIELD AND STAFF`), carried as printed.
* **company**: the letter of the company heading above the line (the printed letter; where the scan damaged it, the letter the sequence of headings gives, flag C). **`FS` is the code of the field and staff** of the regiment or battalion (812 lines): it is not a company. A battery that is one company of a Moore section holds that company only.
* **remarks**: every other token of the entry in the order of the text layer (county, event, date, remarks); 54,346 of 54,403 lines have remarks. Where the text layer inserts running heads or page furniture in the middle of an entry, the remark tokens skip them (the verifier allows a step-over of at most 200 characters inside the leaf): 36 lines have such a step-over.
* **Rights per name**: the segment names the source text; `data2/sources/NC-MOORE-1882.json` holds the eight items, their dates and their sha256; `rosters/sources/conf-nc.json` is the tracked pointer file (section 3).

**Flag codes** (flag file, by row number from 0 over the whole file; `ocr_uncertain` is the union of N H C R Y G M W; the rules are those of `nc_flags.py`, heuristics on the printed cell, never a change to a line). Totals over the 41 units:

| code | lines | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| N | 1,918 | name text damaged: a digit or a mark inside a name token, a lower-case first letter, a capital inside a word, or a name that is only a surname |
| H | 2,329 | the head of the entry was cut by a rule that is a reading: a head recovered backwards from a marker inside the remarks of the line above, a weak marker (c. o. a. for e.), a head with no enlistment marker (county or event word right after the name), an officer head without rank, a stray mark taken out of the name |
| C | 1,968 | the company heading above the row is damaged: the company letter is the one the sequence of headings gives (company_inferred lists the stretches) |

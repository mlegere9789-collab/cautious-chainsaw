# Massachusetts (Union): rosters of the 24 units at Gettysburg (work item union-ma, OA03)

Written 14 November 2026 by the roster workstream. Evidence labels as in `PLAN.md`: **Measured** (a command run for this report produced it), **Documented** (a source text says it; the source is named), **Computed** (arithmetic on those), **Inferred** (a stated rule on printed text), **Not run**. A label says what supports a statement; it never lowers a gate. Every number below is computed by `pipeline2/union_ma/ma_stats.py`, `ma_reconcile.py` and `ma_changes.py` from the unit files and the work files in `GOVERNANCE/horde/ws-rosters/union-ma/`, and this report is written by `ma_report.py`; none is typed by hand.

## 0. Summary

* **24 unit files** under `rosters/data2/union/ma/` (the 1st, 2nd, 7th, 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th, 13th, 15th, 16th, 18th, 19th, 20th, 22nd, 28th, 32nd, 33rd and 37th Infantry, the 1st Company Sharpshooters, the 1st Cavalry and the 1st (A), 3rd (C), 5th (E) and 9th Batteries: the Massachusetts units of the Official Records organization table of the Army of the Potomac for 1-3 July 1863), **33,787 lines in all**, format `roster2/2` (`pipeline2/r2lib.py`), each with a flag file `<unit>.flags.json.gz`. One line per name entry of the printed roll, exactly as the text layer prints it (name, rank, remarks), with the company, the printed page and the character offset of the name in the pinned text. No line carries a label of presence (DD-ROS-01).
* **One printed source is parsed**: *Massachusetts Soldiers, Sailors, and Marines in the Civil War*, compiled and published by the Adjutant General (Norwood, Mass., 1931 to 1933), volumes 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6, Internet Archive items `massachusettssol11931mass`, `...21931mass`, `...31931mass`, `...51931mass`, `...61931mass` (the OCR search text of each, pinned by sha256). The three other volumes and the 1937 index are pinned and hashed as well and are not parsed (section 3). The rolls give the whole war service of every man who was ever on the unit's rolls, not the men present on 1-3 July 1863.
* **Every line is checked against the text before it is written** (name tokens at the recorded offset, remark tokens in order, printed page of the leaf, rank, offsets that only grow: the parser stops on a defect) and again by `pipeline2/verify_unit.py --all`: **33,787 of 33,787 lines pass, 0 name defects, 0 page defects, 0 problems** (ledger hashes matched); a second production run gives identical bytes for all 48 files (section 4).
* **The first run of 13 November wrote 33,748 lines; this run found and corrected defects in them (section 5, item 1) and writes 33,787.** Reading the earlier roll files of the NE family (`rosters/data/NE/massachusetts`, a different parser on a different text file) against the new files showed 39 entries whose head the OCR had damaged beyond the rules (added), 49 names cut at the wrong place (a surname split by a blank, "Jr," taken for a name, "1st" read as "Ist", a company letter in quotes taken into the next name, an alias; three of them placed by review), two section headings that only a reader could place, and a number alone on its line at a page break that was dropped as a page number (26 lines now keep it). **403 lines were added or changed in all (39 added, 49 given another name start, 208 another company code, 81 shortened and 26 lengthened remarks); none was removed.**
* **Against the earlier roll files** (22 of the 24 units have one: 31,520 lines; the 11th Infantry and the 1st Company Sharpshooters have none): the names agree line by line, in page order, for **31,518 of the 31,562 new lines of those units (99.86 percent)**; 42 lines are in the new files only and **0 in the earlier files only**. Of the 42, 10 stand inside the remarks of an earlier line (the earlier parser swallowed them) and 32 are in no earlier line (26 of them on printed page 668 of the 9th Infantry). Company codes differ for 426 lines (four units, all explained: section 5). Remarks are equal for 31,451 of 31,518 aligned lines; the others differ by page furniture the earlier files kept or by a following entry the earlier parser swallowed (section 5). The differences are listed line by line in `GOVERNANCE/horde/ws-rosters/union-ma/differences-<unit>.csv` (537 rows), none resolved by guessing.
* **Against the strength** (section 1): the lines are 2.2 to 11.9 times the strength values found (the baseline value or an Official Records statement), as a whole-war roll must be. For 6 units a statement of the Official Records (quoted, with report and printed page) is set beside the baseline value; for 3 units (the 11th Infantry, the 1st Company Sharpshooters, the 1st Battery) the repository has no strength at all.
* **Presence**: the volumes print no muster roll of 30 June 1863. **1,588 lines** print a remark that names Gettysburg (an OCR-tolerant match, a lower bound; 1,271 of them with a date from 1 to 3 July 1863 in the same clause); for every other man the page says "on the rolls, names not recovered" and nothing else. Marks in the flag files, inferred from printed dates only: on the rolls in July 1863 **yes 11,928, no 19,863, unknown 1,996** (rule, repair and sample reading: section 4).
* **Rights**: no text of this item can carry the basis "public domain (United States publication before 1929)": the volumes were printed 1931 to 1935 (the index 1937). Each text of the pointer file `rosters/sources/union-ma.json` carries what the files show: the Internet Archive flag `NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT`, the printed imprint with its offset, a search of the whole OCR text for a copyright notice (none in 8 of the nine texts, a misread character in volume 8), the title leaf and its blank verso of the five parsed volumes read from page images (no notice), and the arithmetic of the term. The decision is the owner step 4 (section 3).
* **Not done or not possible** (registered, section 6): the independent sample of page images (R-G9) was not run; the damaged lines are not read a second time from images; the 1937 index, the 1868-70 Record and the Burial Report of 1863 are not read for names; two baseline batteries match no Massachusetts battery of the Official Records. Every unit of the item has a file; no unit was left to a later run.

## 2. The unit files

`rosters/data2/union/ma/<unit>.jsonl.gz`, gzip, fixed header (identical bytes for identical content), UTF-8, `roster2/2`: line 1 the header, line 2 the one segment `{"s":"MA-SSM-1931","v":"<volume>","t":"MA-SSM-1931.<volume>","lay":"flow"}`, then rows `[offset delta, name, rank, remarks, remark delta]` with carried state lines `{"co":...}`, `{"pg":...}` and `{"rk":...}`. `name` is the OCR tokens of the head joined by single blanks, **verbatim** (stray marks of the scan inside the head are kept in the name only where they stand between name tokens; marks at the two ends of the name, of the rank and between the second dash and "Res." are taken out of the fields and listed in the flag file under `head_marks`); `rank` is the printed rank between the two dashes; `remarks` is every other printed token of the entry from "Res." (or what the OCR left of it) to the head of the next entry, in full: residence, age, occupation, dates of enlistment, muster and every change, and the cross-references ("See Co. K 2d Mass. Hy. Arty."); the running head, the page number and the sub-heading "Three Years' Service" are left out. `co` is the company letter of the heading above (A to M), `FS` (field and staff), `NCS` (non-commissioned staff), `BAND`, `UNAS` (unassigned) or empty (the batteries and the sharpshooters print one list; a company heading whose letter is damaged). A man is printed once under each organization he served in; the same man can therefore stand on several lines of a unit file and on lines of other units; nothing is merged.

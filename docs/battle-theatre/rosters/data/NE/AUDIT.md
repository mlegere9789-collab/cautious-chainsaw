# NE family: independent audit and repair log (30 October 2026)

Scope: the roster family NE built in `data/NE/` (Maine, Massachusetts, New Hampshire). Method follows PLAN 4.3 and 4.4 in spirit, on a reduced scale: a seeded sample of 60 rows, a recount of the totals, a check of the source hashes and of the access status of every source, then repairs of systematic defects. Nothing here replaces the formal independent test of PLAN 4.4 (about 40 strata, 628 rows each): the auditor read the builder's code, so the sample is a defect finder, not gate evidence. Every number below was measured in this run unless marked otherwise.

## 1. Verdict

* The family is **not faithful enough to publish**. In the 60-row sample, **45 rows (75 percent) were right in every field and 15 had at least one defect**; after the repairs below, 49 of the 60 would be right (4 of the 15 were fixed by rule).
* Gate R-G1 (names, 99 percent): **not met**. 4 of 60 rows had a name spelling error against the page image or the second source (6.7 percent, one-sided 95 percent upper bound 14.6 percent), and 3 more had age or residence fused into the name cell (7 of 60 with any name-field defect).
* Gate R-G3 (labels, 99 percent, zero hard errors): **not met**. 1 hard error in 60 (a man labelled Present-Documented whose own return dates his discharge before the battle; upper bound 7.7 percent), 2 soft disagreements with a second reader, 2 wrong basis codes with the right label. The hard error is a member of a class (a name matched across companies to the 1898 list) that the audit found 15 times in the population and repaired.
* Gates R-G2, R-G5, R-G9 were not tested (no date-exactness sample, no casualty list, no formal stratified test). R-G6 holds for the hashes (section 3). R-G7 now holds with tombstones (section 5). R-G4 holds (every unit has a reconciliation status).
* Two defects of the builder's report: the headline row total of 51,170 counts one file fewer than the 51,435 rows on disk (the UNMAPPED 1st Battery file, 265 rows, is excluded by design; stated in section 2); and the README claimed entry counts agree within 1 percent, which is true for Massachusetts but hid 27 swallowed-entry rows (section 5).

## 2. Totals recount

* Per-unit counts (rows and labels) in `stats.json` equal a recount of the CSVs for all 36 covered units, before the repairs (0 mismatches).
* Family total before repairs: 51,170 stated; 51,435 rows on disk including `massachusetts/1st-battery-...-unmapped.csv` (265 rows: 126 Absent-Documented, 139 Unknown); 51,435 - 265 = 51,170. Consistent, but the unmapped file is outside `units_with_names` and the label totals.
* After the repairs: 51,016 rows (51,170 - 188 tombstoned + 34 recovered), labels Unknown 19,370, Absent-Documented 24,974, Present-Documented 6,226, Duplicate-Xref 446 (recounted from the CSVs, equal to the regenerated `stats.json`).
* The builder's unit coverage statement (36 of 47 units; 8 Vermont infantry regiments, 1st Vermont Cavalry, U-ART-MAF and U-ART-MAE missing) is correct.

## 4. The sample (seed 41)

Draw (`pipeline/NE/audit_sample.py`, Python `random.Random(41)` over the 36 mapped CSVs, 51,170 rows): one random row from each of the 36 files (strata: every regiment and battery), 12 random rows from the officer pool (field, staff, company officers; 771+201+79 rows), and 12 Present-Documented rows, 4 per state. 60 distinct rows: 18 Maine, 34 Massachusetts, 8 New Hampshire; 12 officers; 18 with a Present-Documented label, 23 Absent-Documented, 19 Unknown. Each row was checked against the cached source text (`tmp/library/rosters/NE/`): name spelling against the source line, rank, company, unit, the date or remark that drives the label, and the label against PLAN 2.2. Page images were read for the 4 New Hampshire rows #33, #34, #35 and #58 (pages 901, 43, 278 and 74 of the Ayling volumes); the Maine rows were compared with the 1898 participants list and with the 1863 return text layer; the Massachusetts rows with the volume text layer. Rows with their verdicts are in `AUDIT-sample.csv`. Pilot pages (`QA-SAMPLE.md`) were not excluded: one New Hampshire row (2nd NH p.43) lies on a page the builder had read.

Honest limits: 60 rows give wide bounds (name error 4/60: 6.7 percent, upper 14.6 percent; any defect 15/60: 25 percent, upper 35.9 percent). The sample cannot show that a gate is met, only that these are not.

## 6. What was found and could not be fixed

1. **Maine name spelling (OCR)**: the text layer misreads letters (`Elbridgo`, `II` for `H`, `Cvirrier`, `Zeeman`); NH the same (`Bdwin`, `Irving'`). Needs a second OCR or image reading (G02). Rate in this sample 4 of 60; the builder's own check measured 6 of 132.
2. **Maine `rank_as_printed` holds the section heading** (`privates`, `joined`, `sergeants`), not the man's own rank, for all 13,710 return rows; officers listed in a table inside a privates section keep the section word. The rank of the man needs the page images or a column re-parse.
3. **Maine residence and enlistment columns** are noisy (ditto marks read as `66`, `cc`, `“`; 2,755 of 13,522 residence cells blank or noise; age still blank in 1,066 return rows) and the muster-date cell often carries remark words. No column re-parse was attempted.
4. **Merged name cells (472 rows)** hide men that have no row of their own. The count of hidden men is unknown (at least one per merged row).
5. **Maine participants-list matching across companies** (148 rows flagged `company-differs-between-return-and-participants-list`) can attach a 1898 listing to a different man of the same name; only the 15 contradicted ones were demoted. The other 133 should be re-matched with the person's age and residence.
6. **Maine `remarks_as_printed` truncation** where a continuation line fell into the next row (sample row #9). Not measured.
7. **Label questions that need an owner or rule decision**, not code: a man captured or wounded in June 1863 with a parole or hospital record in August (sample #18) is Unknown today; PLAN 2.2 rule 9 could be read to make him Absent-Documented. The Dec 1863 return's `In western gunboat service` (sample #7) and similar present-state remarks are Unknown. Both are carried, not decided.
8. **Basis codes**: `ONROLL-NO-STATUS` is used for desertions dated across the battle (sample #16) and `OCR-DATE-LOST` for rows whose date is legible but whose punctuation defeated the reader (sample #31). Fixing needs a broader event-segment reader for Massachusetts; the fraction is not measured.
9. **Company letters**: the 2nd NH rows with company `?P` (printed `Co. F`) and other illegible company tokens stay marked `?x`.
10. **The repair is rule-based and unchecked on images**: the 242 relabelled rows were read from their own remark text, not from page images. A fresh independent sample of the repaired strata is required (PLAN 4.4) before any of this can be called verified.
11. **Vermont** (8 infantry regiments, 1st Vermont Cavalry) is still not obtained: the item `cu31924080774148` kept answering 503 or 504 during this audit; no Vermont rows exist, so any statement that the NE family is complete is false.
12. **OOB gaps** (U-ART-MAF, U-ART-MAE, the 11th Massachusetts, the 5th and 6th Maine Batteries) are the builder's findings and were not re-examined.

# C-VASCGA: independent audit of 30 October 2026

Auditor: a second agent that did not write the parsers. Same machine rules as the builder (below-normal priority, no browser, no build, one short foreground command at a time). Only the cached text and word-box files of `tmp/library/rosters/C-VASCGA/files/` were read (no network request was made). The frozen pre-audit state is in `_pre_audit/` (csv/, scripts/, stats.json, units.csv, sources.json, README.md, QA-SAMPLE.md, progress.md, SOURCES.csv and GAPS.md as they stood before this audit). Every changed parser carries comments beginning `AUDIT 2026-10-30`.

**Verdict: the family does not pass gates R-G1, R-G3 or R-G9 as built. After the repairs below the defects found are fixed in the parse outputs, but three rounds of sampling each found a new defect class, so the family is not yet at the point where another sampling round would be clean. The formal PLAN 4.4 sample (628 rows per stratum, page images, a reader who never saw the classifier) was not run and cannot be claimed.**

## 4. Not fixed, and why

1. **Warren names (11th Georgia, 1,140 rows).** The print and the scan damage the names (3 of the 4 sampled rows wrong by eye; unflagged slips such as `Striikland` for Strickland remain). Only a second reading of the page images can fix them (owner decision G02). The names and rank columns also split words across cells. Treat every Warren name as unverified.
2. **3rd SC Company F (lines 24824-26488 of the scan).** Two alphabetical privates runs (Abercrombie to Nance, Hollingsworth to Workman) and repeating rank headings suggest the scan mixes two rolls or lost pages (p. 451 to 452, printed `home, a leg having been amputated` begins mid-entry). The rows are kept under Company F with the forced rank flagged. The leaf images of that range are needed to settle it. Residual suspect pairs: `Willis, Eli`, `Boozer, B. F`, `Buzbee, J. M` (3rd SC) read twice at the same printed page below the 80 percent similarity threshold; flagged `same-name-pair`, not merged.
3. **Formal gates R-G1 to R-G3, R-G9.** Not passable by this audit: 181 rows read in three rounds against the text layer, not the page images, by a reader who also read the parsers; PLAN 4.4 asks for 628 rows per stratum from page images with a reader that never saw the classifier. Page images exist for four pages only (`img/`, `QA-image` entries).
4. **Label judgement calls** (above): Doles promotion-out, Loehr `First year`, Salley roll omission. They are marked in the data or here; whether they stay Absent-Documented is an owner or second-reader decision. Salley entries with `absent, sick ... since July 1, 1863` and no Gettysburg remark (about 10 rows) stay Unknown (under-claim).
5. **Doles-Cook, Nichols, Page, Beale, Loehr remarks** are histories or lists written years after the battle; the large Unknown share is correct and is not an error.
6. **Beale and the 9th VA Cavalry**: the book prints rolls but no dates or status; all 1,580 rows stay Unknown. 7 same-name groups (14 rows) in one company are kept as `same-name-pair`.
7. **Units**: no unit is Reconciled; none can be until per-man presence exists (PLAN G01).
8. Not checked: the OOB unit assignment of the 380 Appomattox officer rows beyond a read of the regiment text of 60 of them (all consistent); Gaines hospital names (87 rows) beyond 7 rows read in the sample; `units.csv` reconciliation figures other than re-running `finalize.py`.

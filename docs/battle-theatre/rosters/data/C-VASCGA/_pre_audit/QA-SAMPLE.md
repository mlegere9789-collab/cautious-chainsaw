# C-VASCGA: self-check against page images (30 rows asked, 87 compared)

Method: for four sources the page image was fetched from the Internet Archive (`page/nN_wNNNN.jpg`, files kept in `tmp/library/rosters/C-VASCGA/img/`, hashes in `acquired.json`) and every row of the page was compared with the CSV row built from the text layer. The pages were chosen after the parsers were written and were not used to tune them, except that two Doles-Cook defects found here (below) were fixed afterwards. This is one reader, not independent, and not random: it does not replace the independent sample of PLAN section 4.4.

Totals: 87 rows compared; 3 label errors found and fixed (2 Doles-Cook, 1 Loehr rule that touched 38 rows); the 2 weak Salley labels were under-claims, not errors; 1 silent row drop (Doles-Cook) found and fixed, and a recall check (`scripts/recall_dolescook.py`) found 137 more header lines of that type in the Doles-Cook text, all now read (12 lines left are company-heading continuations, not men); name defects in 2 of 73 rows of the three clean sources and in 9 of 14 rows of Warren. Rows not sampled: every other page, every other source (Nichols, Irby, Beale, Page, Tompkins, the two hospital and parole lists). Their rows were read against the text layer only.

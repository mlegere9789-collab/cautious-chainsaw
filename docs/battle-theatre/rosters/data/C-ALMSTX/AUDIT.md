## Systematic defects found and repaired (frozen pre-audit copy in `_pre_audit/`)

1. **Stray backspace characters in the regexes** (`rlib.py` and `park_parse.py`: `\b` had been written as a control character). Effects: the rule "Gettysburg campaign is not Gettysburg" never worked (Oates: E. P. Head and William Crane were Present-Documented on "Gettysburg campaign" wording; three more rows wrongly CONFLICT); the Park lists' "killed in battle" context was never applied (21 men killed at Seven Pines, Sharpsburg and similar left Unknown) and two text fragments ("Mr. Brandon", "Confederate Congress") became rows.
2. **Separation date taken from the wrong date in the sentence** (`rlib.py`): the first date of a segment, often an enlistment or wound date, was read as the separation date, and enlistment-verb rules took any date. Now the date must follow the separation or enlistment verb; "re-enlisted", "elected", "appointed" and "commissioned" are no longer first enlistment; "discharged his duties", "since the war", "died at the age of" are not separations. ENL-AFTER rows fell from 73 to 61 (false ones removed: Duncan Hall, John Parish, Council A. Bryan, Holliman, Espey, James R. Edwards and others).
3. **Loose battle-name list** (`rlib.py`): "stone", "ball", "harper", "plains", "frazier", "sharps", "gaine", "slaughter", "new orleans", "warrenton", "vicksburg", "rappahannock", "boonsboro", "mechanicsville", "pensacola" matched surnames, rifles, towns and later battles ("died since war in New Orleans", "killed by a Minie-ball", Longstreet's sharpshooters killed in 1864 were Absent). Replaced by anchored patterns; a bare "killed at" a safe battle counts only with a kill-like verb; curly apostrophes normalised (Brewer's "Gaines' Mill" had matched by luck of the substring). Net Absent-Documented labels fell by 46 while 62 new Hurst, Register and Florida rows were added; the losses are rows that rested on a surname, a rifle or an ambiguous place.
4. **"undated-separation" misuse** (`rlib.py`): separation words with a dated post-battle event or a later campaign (killed at Hanover Junction 1864, died at age 81) were marked unreadable (rule 13). They are now on-roll-undetermined. Unreadable-or-ambiguous rows fell from 1,217 to 1,038.
5. **Mississippi Register** (`ms_parse.py`): (a) paragraphs holding several role markers kept the first rank for everyone and dropped the other men (company officers after "Captain - X. First Lieutenant - Y" and the staff paragraphs; Conner was Lieutenant-Colonel instead of Major); OCR-damaged markers ("Colondls", "Sergeants-Major", "Commissaries") are now recognised; (b) in a comma list of names the whole remainder was given to the first name and the later names were dropped (Haley, Willing, Rea got Ramsey's, Barlow's and King's separations); the remark now goes to the last name, others become rows with no remark, flag `multi-name-entry`; (c) unit ranges overlapped: the 48th Mississippi took the Jeff Davis Legion (25 duplicate rows) and the Jeff Davis Legion took the Madison and Confederate Guards artillery sections; fixed to 41190-41604 and 41608-41803. Rows 1,486 to 1,532.
6. **Hurst 14th Alabama** (`hurst_parse.py`): lines starting with a full given name ("Pinkston, John discharged") and fused two-column lines were absorbed into the previous man's remark, 80 rows carried another man's text (53 of them Absent labels, some correct by chance) and men were dropped; Recapitulation totals leaked into remarks. Rows 1,113 to 1,189, 20 labels corrected.
7. **Oates** (`oates_parse.py`): a lost paragraph break left a second man inside the previous entry (John G. Key took William B. Lee's 1864 enlistment: Absent). Entries split where another surname begins "Name was N years old" (3 more rows; same-surname embeddings are not detected).
8. **Florida** (`fl_parse.py`): (a) "McKinnie. James" style lines (period for comma, no dates) were glued to the previous name: 37 merged names recovered; (b) a remark wrapped with a hyphen or indented onto the next man's line was given to that man. Page image djvu p.103 shows "Wounded, Gaines' Farm June 27, '62; captured at / Gettysburg; discharged from prison May, '65" belongs to Parker, W. M., not Peterson, J. B.; 12 label changes on the 2nd Florida alone. The indentation rule is a heuristic (CA13). Rows 3,606 to 3,651.
9. **Polley** (`audit_postfix.py`, new): 130 ranks printed in the name cell ("Moore, D. D., Capt.") were Private; ranks now read from the cell (name unchanged). 11 labels withheld (Unknown, `label-withheld-attribution`) where the label cannot be tied to one man: unreadable name cell ("V.", "Pa^ke^L"), two names in one cell, or a bare name between the man and the remark (Benton/Finley). One man whose name cell held only the rank ("3d Lieut.", Chas. A. Graham) was named from the next segment, flag `name-from-next-segment`.
10. `consolidate.py` now skips `_pre_audit/`; `run_all.sh` rebuilds all CSVs, stats, coverage and the 30-row check; `audit_dlimg.py` fetched the three page images.

Net effect: rows 14,257 to 14,423; Present-Documented 702 to 701; Absent-Documented 1,817 to 1,771; Unknown 11,738 to 11,951; unreadable-or-flagged share 13.6 to 12.2 percent. The 12 defective sample rows were re-checked in the rebuilt CSVs and are now correct (rows 3, 7, 14, 15, 22, 23, 25, 26, 41, 44, 53, 57), except that row 7 is withheld as Unknown because its name is unreadable.

A fresh 73-row verification sample (seed 4141, spread over 9 sources, drawn from the repaired data) found two further Hurst fusions ("Brown, Wm", "Orr, Wm" with a two-letter given name), fixed, and a minor name-cell artefact ("killed Joseph E. Henery", Polley, name kept as printed). No hard error remained in that sample.

## Not fixed or not verifiable (also in GAPS.md CA12 to CA16)

* Names and remarks missing from the Florida OCR layer (6.1 percent of rows have an empty name; Campbell, M. M. has no remark in the text layer). Needs a second OCR or the images.
* Only 4 page images have been compared by eye (builder 2, audit 1 Florida page plus its own earlier two); R-G1, R-G2 and R-G9 (about 40 strata, 300 to 600 rows each) are not met. This audit is not the full 4.4 test.
* OCR spelling cannot be judged from OCR text: "Eurkhalter" (Quitman, probably Burkhalter), "Sharps*" and similar are kept as printed.
* Brewer captains' company letters are inferred from list order (460 rows) and unverified; the Register's "remark belongs to the last name" convention is a reading, flagged.
* The claim "units_with_names 45": 44 unit ids carry rows; C-ART-Brown-J has none (it is the same company as C-ART-Brown-C per the README).
* Estes rows list a man as a field officer at some date; they are not 1863 presence (e.g. Archer, colonel of the 5th Texas, commanded a brigade at Gettysburg).
* Rows remain per source (one man appears several times); no cross-source merge.

# NY family: independent audit (30 October 2026)

Scope: the roster family built in `data/NY/` (88 OOB entries, 111,031 names as reported). Method follows `rosters/PLAN.md` sections 4.3 and 4.4 as far as one session allows. Result in one line: the family as built did NOT pass R-G1, R-G2 or R-G3; twelve systematic parse defects (about 6,800 men missing or mislabelled) were found and repaired; R-G1, R-G2 and R-G9 are still not met after the repairs, and the cause (the OCR text layer) cannot be repaired from the text alone.

A frozen copy of everything the builder delivered is in `_pre_audit/` (`rosters_pre_audit.zip` with the 87 CSVs, the summary files, and `pipeline/` with the eight `ny_*.py` scripts as they were). The repaired outputs replace `rosters/`, `stats.json`, `reconciliation.json`, `unit-table.md`, `build_stats.json`, `roh_unresolved.csv` and `README.md`.

## 1. How the sample was drawn and checked

* Seeded draw, `random.Random(41)` over every man row of the 87 CSVs (cross-reference lines excluded). 60 rows: 3 officers labelled Present-Documented, 7 other officers, 20 Present-Documented (casualty remarks), 30 others by basis (ONROLL-NO-STATUS 7, SEP-BEFORE 6, ENL-AFTER 5, OCR-DATE-LOST 4, DATE-NOT-PRINTED 2, NO-RECORD-AFTER 2, ABS-NOTATION 2, OCR-CONFLICT 1, CONFLICT 1). One unit per row where possible: 59 different units (infantry, cavalry, artillery and extra units). The pages are disjoint from the pages the builder used in `QA-SAMPLE.md`.
* Each row was compared with (a) the cached PDF text layer of its page (`tmp/library/rosters/NY/`, file named in `source_id`, page `source_page_pdf`, 0-based) and (b) a render of that page (PyMuPDF, 140 to 150 dpi crop around the entry) read by eye: name, rank, company, unit, every date and the remark that drives the label; the label was then derived from the rule sheet (PLAN 2.2) and compared. 59 of the 60 entries were located on the page image (KELLY, JAMES, 40th NY p167, matched the text layer but the crop landed on a namesake).
* Not independent in the PLAN 4.4 sense: the auditor read the classifier in order to repair it, the sample is 60 rows, not 628 per stratum, and the sample was used to find defect classes. The post-repair columns below show that the repairs work on these rows; they are not a fresh error-rate estimate. The sample is far below what R-G9 needs (about 40 strata, at least 299 rows with zero errors each), so R-G9 is **not met and no stratum passes**.

## 3. Totals, hashes, sources

* Totals in `stats.json` were recounted from the CSVs (man rows; `Duplicate-Xref`, `EXTRA-*` and the `U-1NYLA-REG` remainder excluded, as the README defines them). The builder's figures were **reproduced exactly** before the repairs (111,031 names: Present-Documented 4,867, Absent-Documented 60,841, Unknown 45,323) and the rebuilt figures were reproduced exactly again (116,549 names: Present-Documented 4,930, Absent-Documented 64,040, Unknown 47,579; roll-status counts agree). The README's Roll of Honor (1,577 lines, 935 unresolved) and second-OCR (4,617 pairs, 82.0 percent agreement) figures were recounted and were right. Re-running the original code on the 120th NY reproduced the shipped CSV, so the shipped data was consistent with the shipped code. (GAPS rows G-NY3 and G-NY4 carried older numbers, 39,119, 6,217 and 77.7 percent, that did not match the README; corrected.)
* Hashes: all 264 entries of `acquired.json` were re-hashed against the files in `tmp/library/rosters/NY/`: 0 mismatches, 0 missing. Two caveats: 7 entries (`nysmm_140th.html`, `nysmm_agreports.html`, `nysmm_cw_index.html`, `nysmm_unit_history.html`, `probe_102nd_muster.pdf`, two `probe_140_battles_*.bin`) were reconstructed after a lost concurrent write and their sha256 was recomputed from the cached file, so for them the match proves nothing; 8 entries carry no HTTP status.
* Sources: all fetches were plain GETs to `www.nysmm.org` (197 files), `museum.dmna.ny.gov` (42) and `archive.org` (18), user agent `gettysburg-map-research/1.0`. `robots.txt` of both museum hosts was re-read: it disallows only `/application/*` paths. The Internet Archive items used (`newyorkatgettysb0001unse`, `newyorkatgettysb00foxw`, `annualreportadj02unkngoog`, `warofrebellion012701rootrich`, `cu31924092925167`) were re-read through the metadata API: none has `access-restricted-item`, none is in a lending or print-disabled collection. No 403, challenge, login or paywalled host appears in the log; no Ancestry, Fold3, FamilySearch, HathiTrust page service or Find a Grave file is in the cache. **No lending-restricted or bot-blocked source was used.** The museum footer "all rights reserved" remains an owner position question (G-NY8).

## 5. What could not be fixed (reported, not hidden)

1. **Name and digit OCR errors** (about 8 percent of names and a similar share of dates in the sample): the text layer reads C as O, V as Y, O as Q, 4 as 1, 5 as 3. `name_as_printed` is the text layer as read and was not corrected (no invented names). A page-image or second-OCR read of the name and date columns is needed (owner decision G02, GAPS G-NY4). R-G1 and R-G2 stay unmet.
2. **427 rows (0.35 percent) still hold an unsplit neighbour** (a header with no comma, or a name too garbled for the lenient pattern, such as `W rASHBURN`, `COLBORN, R, JAMES`, `HAGGERTYDANIEL`), and cross-reference lines whose target has two words ("see Julius Roeder") are not split off. The neighbour has no row of his own and the host row can carry his remarks. Gap G-NY11.
3. **Labels that need the page image**: ROMAIN (muster-in "August 2 1, 1863" with spaced digits), YOUST (year read 1861 for 1864) and ABBOTT (the garbled "second1 lieutenant" hides the enlistment) stay Unknown where the page gives Absent-Documented or an on-roll status.

## C. Defects, conflicts and disputable material found IN the PDF (audit)
Internal contradictions:
1. Requirement count: text says 21 requirements (Law cl.1, D.1 heading, App E title, Q18, Section F) but R22 exists (Section A, D.1 V15, App E row R22, Q28, phase 15). Gates listed Q01..Q26, Q28, Q27 (out of order).
2. C-044 default Camp Letterman opening = Jul 20 but T3 conflict table says "Open Jul 22 with Jul 20 flagged". Same item, opposite defaults.
3. C-026 default Sherfy barn fire = Jul 2 evening but T3-132, T3 conflict table, B.8.2 and S-037 all say Jul 3 afternoon is most plausible.
4. C-034 default Monterey Pass wagons = 150 (range shown) but T3 conflict table says about 250 (+ over 1,300 prisoners).
5. C-033 Imboden departs "Jul 4 afternoon" but T3-158 says wounded train begins Jul 4 evening, T3 conflict table "Jul 4 evening".
6. Hunt's Jul 3 guns: C-028 default 05:30, yet the Culp's Hill row in the T3 conflict table says "animate guns 04:30" and E-11 says about 04:00.
7. Spencers at Hanover Jun 30: stated as documented in T3-083/B.6.10/B.6.15.3, but D.2 row V9 calls it "disputed".
8. Hazard's report: introduction cites 28 guns/six batteries, the report lists five batteries/24 guns (PDF notes this itself).
9. Gun totals 366 vs 372 (US) and 282 vs 281 (CS) (PDF notes). Charge casualty sums 6,240 / 6,840 vs 6,555 (PDF notes).
10. Site latitude 39.83 N (B.10.2) vs 39.82 N (S-086).
11. Part E Section A requires "22 requirements" heading but Section F says R01-R21 only.
Rule violations by the PDF's own standard (Section E.2, App B): rows tiered "documented" whose only support is Wikipedia, forums or Stone Sentinels/HistoryNet-type aggregators (e.g. T3-060 "Wikipedia only"; most of B.6.2-B.6.9 "documented: [Wiki-...]"; B.6.15 "documented via reproduction standards (not a period source)"). Under the PDF's own definition these are inferred/lead until a non-lead source is read. The Official Records, Busey & Martin, Coddington, Pfanz, Thomas were NOT read by the authors, so every number is provisional.
Disputable "known corrections" that I carry as disputes, not facts:
12. 140th NY "regulation blue; Zouave dress Jan 1864": an earlier agent read the 146th NY history stating both the 140th and 146th received Zouave dress on 3 Jun 1863. Do not hard-code either until a primary source is read (the PDF's own Law forbids calling it documented).
13. Hancock countermand of Hunt's ceasefire rests on secondary accounts; primary texts unread.
14. Sickles' advance default 14:00 (C-010) sits between noon and 16:00; modern accounts commonly place the forward move mid-afternoon; carry the range.
15. Shoe-story, Lee/Pickett meeting, "only civilian killed", Wade house 150+ strikes are secondary and traditional; keep tiered.
16. Weak sources named by the PDF itself: EPP timeline (terse, unreferenced), CivilWarTalk threads, Padre Steve blog, Women History Blog, Stone Sentinels copy errors, Goodreads blog, Authentic Campaigner posts, IMFDB, WeAreMighty, Boston Globe weather piece, Wikipedia OOB (summary slips), Michigan site claims (125 killed, 1,500 prisoners at Falling Waters: unsupported).
Conflicts between the PDF and the owner's later instructions (need an owner decision, not mine):
17. Audio: PDF Section S / Q23 / C.10 require all audio labeled "modern reconstruction"/synthesized, and say no recordings; owner later said real period-accurate recordings and no "synthesized" label needed. Both cannot hold. Not decided here.
18. Engine/delivery: PDF recommends UE5 + offline path with engine chosen by benchmark; owner wants a browser page (own renderer, WebGPU). Compatible only if the Phase-1 benchmark and a Decision Log entry choose it.
19. Rosters: PDF has no roster requirement (B.5 says no Gettysburg-wide roster exists; best-documented subset is ~45,000 casualties). Owner's roster section is an addition, sourced from NPS Soldiers & Sailors, state rosters, muster rolls; FamilySearch/NPS permission requests unsent.
20. Rufus Dawes, and every commander below the handful in B.4, have NO documented appearance in the PDF; PDF Law cl.3/K.7 mandates register entries and an owner escalation, not invention.
21. Individual soldiers' positions/actions are unknowable (Law cl.3); PDF requires plausible-procedural tagging and a visible overlay; owner's 97% target must be defined on documented unit-level quantities.
22. "100%" claimed by the owner vs PDF verdict O3 "Literally impossible": PDF's honest claim is "documented at regiment level, plausible at individual level".

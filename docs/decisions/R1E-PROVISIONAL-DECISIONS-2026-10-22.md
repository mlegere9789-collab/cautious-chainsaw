# Release 1 (train R1e): decisions taken without the owner, 2026-10-22

The owner's order for this round was to ask nothing, take the conservative decision and log it. Each item is a decision the owner can reverse; the way back is stated. None of them lowers a gate. Items from the other agents of the round are appended by them below their own heading.

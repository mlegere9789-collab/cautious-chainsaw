## ANIM (T0-ANIM-B11: long tasks at 4x CPU, the engine's solve in a task of its own)

| # | Decision | Why | Way back |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | On a slow device (solve cadence 2 or 3, or an engine frame that costs 3.4 ms or more on average) the engine solves the layout of the NEXT frame in a task of its own after the current frame, from the marks where that frame will have them, and the next frame takes it like a carried layout (refresh, exact repair at once if a frame touches a neighbour). It is thrown away when the frame is not the plain successor (zoom, selection, another set of marks, a cut). VISIBLE: nothing intended (the solve of a frame is made from positions a frame old at most, as the carried frames of the cadence already are; counted in node: zero overlaps and zero unrepresented units in 3,200 frames at cadence 2 and 3, soft clearance counts within the run-to-run spread of the plain engine). | B11 failed in half the runs because the frame that solves (20 to 70 ms of solve at 4x) is also the frame that renders the map, lays out the page and, at 8x, commits React: 50 to 90 ms tasks. | A2 | The same pre-solve at cadence 1 ("slow mode") when the moving average of the engine's frame cost is 3.4 ms or more (off under 2.55). | A 4x phone at 1x runs at 57 to 58 fps, so the governor keeps cadence 1 and every frame solves in the animation-frame task: 3 to 5 solves of 20 to 50 ms per 10 s.

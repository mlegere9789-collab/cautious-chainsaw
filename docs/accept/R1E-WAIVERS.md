Reading the table: "assertions" counts the assertions of the source waiver of the same route (the `R1-text-*` and `R1-controls-*` entries share the text and controls assertions of the page waiver). "spec files" are the specs in `tests/t0` that mention a patch id of that page's ledger or the page itself; they are where the replacement is asserted a second time, in real Chrome, on each build (`tools/t0/run_suites.mjs`).

What this does not show: that a visitor prefers the replacement, or that the owner accepts it. That is the open question the basis B records. The owner can accept all B entries with one yes (`docs/accept/triage/REGRESSION-TRIAGE.md` section 6 lists them in plain words).

# Licences and attribution (modern-feature inventory and removal)

Only public-domain or openly licensed sources are used by this part of the 1863 base map. No Google Earth, Google Maps, Bing, Maxar or Mapbox imagery or tiles are fetched, scraped, traced or compared against.

| Source | What is taken | Licence | Attribution to show | Where it was read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| USDA Farm Service Agency, National Agriculture Imagery Program (NAIP), 2013-06-04 flight, 1.0 m, 4 bands (R,G,B,NIR) | the base photograph; also 2010, 2015, 2017, 2019, 2022 flights for the comparison (tmp only, not committed) | Public domain: the file's own metadata says "placed in the public domain and may be used and reproduced without permission or fee" | "NAIP imagery provided by USDA Farm Service Agency" | Microsoft Planetary Computer STAC (`https://planetarycomputer.microsoft.com/api/stac/v1`, collection `naip`) which mirrors the USDA files; the same quads are published by USDA and USGS. Read by HTTP range requests with the service's anonymous read token. |
| USGS 3D Elevation Program, 1 m DEM from lidar project `PA_South_Central_B1_2017` | elevation for terrain-change analysis | Public domain (USGS) | "USGS 3DEP" | `https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer` |
| USGS National Hydrography Dataset, large scale | water bodies (303 in the box) | Public domain | "USGS NHD" | `https://hydro.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/nhd/MapServer` layers 9, 12 |
| USGS The National Map, Structures | hospital, fire stations, cemetery points (cross-check only) | Public domain | "USGS The National Map" | `https://carto.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/structures/MapServer` |
| US Census Bureau TIGERweb, Transportation | road and railway centre lines (cross-check of OSM; segments OSM lacks are added) | Public domain | "US Census Bureau TIGER/Line" | `https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/Transportation/MapServer` layers 2, 3, 6, 8, 9 |
| Adams County, Pennsylvania, GIS: Building Outlines 2023, GIS Centerline, Gettysburg NMP boundary | 16,209 building footprints, street centre lines | County public records, served openly by the county's ArcGIS server. No licence text is published on the services (their `copyrightText` is "ACOPD" or empty). Used as a read-only reference, footprints are not redistributed in the committed outputs beyond their class, id and geometry in `data/inventory_features.geojson.gz`; ask the county before any wider reuse. | "Adams County GIS" | `https://mapping.adamscountypa.gov/arcgis/rest/services/AGOL/` (`Building_Outlines/MapServer/0`, `Centerline/MapServer/103`, `State_Federal_Lands/MapServer/3`) |
| OpenStreetMap | roads, paths, parking, power lines and towers, fences, memorial points (873), buildings (cross-check), land use, water | ODbL 1.0 (open, attribution and share-alike for the database) | "(c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL" | Overpass API, queries in `scripts/fetch_osm.py` |
| Wikipedia, "List of monuments of the Gettysburg Battlefield" (revision 1369062240) | 512 monument coordinates with dedication year and MN number | CC BY-SA 4.0 | "Wikipedia contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0" | MediaWiki API `action=parse` |

Not used, and why: HMDB (all rights reserved), Google/Bing/Maxar/Mapbox imagery (their terms forbid derivative or offline use), Esri World Imagery (terms), any user-uploaded ArcGIS Online layer of Gettysburg monuments (no stated licence), the NPS ArcGIS organisation (searched, no Gettysburg monument or road layer is published there).

Network behaviour: read-only, one request at a time, 1 to 5 s between requests, descriptive User-Agent, responses cached under `tmp/b1863/modern/`. Wikidata's query service answered HTTP 429 and was not retried.

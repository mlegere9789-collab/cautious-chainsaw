# Roads, railroads, bridges, waterways, fences and walls of July 1863: sources, methods, licences

Owner: `docs/basemap1863/features/roads-rail-water/` and this file. Generated by `scripts/gen_sources_md.py` from the data files on 2026-10-19; every number below comes from a script in `scripts/` or is marked as read, not measured.

## 0. What this is, and what it is not

**This is a RECONSTRUCTION.** No aerial photograph of the battlefield from 1863 exists. Every feature here is inferred from documents written or drawn later (the earliest large-scale survey of the ground is G. K. Warren's, made 1868-69, five years after the battle) and from the modern ground. It must be labelled as a reconstruction everywhere it is shown. It does not claim to be an exact or complete copy of the 1863 landscape: the claim it makes is limited to what each feature record states (sources, method, positional error in metres, confidence).

Rules kept: nothing is drawn that no source supports; where a feature is unknown the record says `Unknown` and carries no geometry or an explicit flag, and the neutral surroundings are left alone; every feature has an id, its sources (title, page or sheet, URL, type), method, positional error and confidence. Source types: **A1** primary map or record made 1863-69 by a government survey or participant; **A2** later first-hand account or photograph; **B** secondary (NPS scholarship, compilations, modern open data). Confidence: **Documented** (a source names the feature and its place), **Estimated** (supported by class rules or modern geometry, place inferred), **Contested** (sources disagree), **Unknown**.

## 3. How each class was built

1. **Public roads (ten roads of the borough, county and township roads)** - the road is catalogued from NPS statements (the ten-road list, county-road list, individual road histories) and the 1876 Bachelder/Warren drawing; its geometry is the centreline of the TIGER/Line 2023 edge on that route. Rationale: the 1863 pikes and county roads were re-surfaced in place, so the modern centreline is within a few metres of the modern photograph and very likely within the width of the 1863 road, EXCEPT where a later realignment is documented (Taneytown and Emmitsburg Road widenings and fence setbacks, 1930s, `NPS-CLI-EMM` p90; United States Avenue realigned 1935-43). Positional error stated per road. Confidence is `Estimated` until the period-map check (section 9, passes 5-7) confirms the same line on the map.
2. **Lost lanes and traces** - from the period map and NPS texts; written to `lanes.geojson` only with a source for each (section 5).
3. **Railroads** - the Gettysburg (Hanover Junction, Hanover and Gettysburg) Railroad reached the borough in 1858 (NPS: tracks had reached the town from the east; the westward extension was graded but not laid). The modern CSX Hanover Subdivision is on the same corridor; its geometry is used with error 30-40 m, confidence Estimated. The Round Top Branch (1884-1942) and the Gettysburg Electric Railway (1893-1916) are post-1863 and flagged `present_1863: false`.
4. **Streams** - NHD flowlines (modern course), with the NPS caveat that courses have changed over 140 years; ditches/artificial paths through modern ponds are flagged `Unknown`. Modern ponds (NHD waterbodies, 154 in the box) are NOT 1863 features: NPS names only two ponds on the 1863 battlefield, McPherson's (still exists) and Biesecker's (removed); neither is located by a source read.
5. **Bridges and fords** - by class rule from NPS (bridges on the turnpikes and state roads; fords everywhere else), at the TIGER x NHD crossing; not individually documented, so `Estimated`, error 30 m.
6. **Fences and walls** - the NPS rebuilds fences on documented 1863 lines in the 1863 styles (`NPS-TREAT-2004` p69), and OSM mappers traced those fences. A mapped fence is a candidate for an 1863 line, not evidence: some park fences stand in non-historic positions (CLI First Day p58, p61-62 give examples). All 392 are `confidence: Unknown`, `present_1863: null`, until each is checked against the Warren/Bachelder fence drawing.

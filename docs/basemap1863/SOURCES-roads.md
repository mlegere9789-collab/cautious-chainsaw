# Roads, railroads, bridges, waterways, lanes and fences of July 1863: sources, methods, accuracy, licences

Owner: `docs/basemap1863/features/roads-rail-water/` and this file. Generated by `scripts/gen_sources_md.py` on 2026-10-19 from the data files; every number below comes from a script in `scripts/` or a record in `verification/`, or is marked as read, not measured.

## 0. What this is, and what it is not

**This is a RECONSTRUCTION.** No aerial photograph of the battlefield from 1863 exists. Every feature here is inferred from documents and maps made later (the earliest large-scale survey of the ground is G. K. Warren's of 1868-69, five years after the battle; the Bachelder sheets used here are its 1876 reduction) and from the modern ground. It must be labelled as a reconstruction wherever it is shown.

It does not claim to be an exact or complete copy of the 1863 landscape. What it claims is limited to what each feature record states: id, category, sources (title, sheet or page, URL, type), method, positional error in metres, confidence. Source types: **A1** primary map or record made 1863-69 by a survey or participant (the Warren/Bachelder sheet); **A2** later first-hand account or photograph; **B** secondary (NPS scholarship, compilations, modern open data). Confidence: **Documented** (a source names the feature and a map or document places it), **Estimated** (supported by class rules, modern geometry or a partial check; place inferred), **Contested**, **Unknown**. Where a feature is unknown the record says `Unknown`; nothing is drawn for it.

**Measured accuracy, in one paragraph.** The period map used for the checks (Bachelder 1876 first-day sheet, LOC `cw0325000a`) was registered to the ground with the b-maps agent's Warren control points plus 26 hand-read road lines; the leave-one-out error of that registration is **18.6 m (RMS) on the control points and 14.3 m (RMS, median 7.6 m) on the road lines**, and it is worse (not measured) where there are few constraints (far north-west, south-west, east of Rock Creek). Against that registration the catalogued roads show a drawn double line along 63 percent of samples (median over 28 roads with at least 4 samples; the same test displaced sideways finds one in 6 percent). The positional error stated on each feature is 15-35 m for roads, 20-25 m for the railroad where the sheet was used and 30 m for streams.

## 3. How each class was built

1. **Public roads and neighbourhood lanes on a modern line (29 records)** - the road is catalogued from NPS statements (the ten-road list, county-road list, individual road histories) and its geometry is the TIGER/Line 2023 edge(s) of that route, because the pikes and county roads were re-surfaced in place. For each road the period map was then tested (`scripts/map_check.py`): a drawn double line along the road within +-16 px (32 m) of the registered position. Confidence is Documented when an NPS text names the road and the sheet shows it; Estimated when only the sheet (RD-025 to RD-029, five roads that no NPS text read here names) or only class rules support it. The sheet is dated 1868-69, so a road "drawn on the sheet" is not proof for July 1863; where NPS text does not date a road the record says so.
2. **Lanes** - 21 lanes named by NPS are inventoried with sources; 4 sections are traced from the sheet (Trostle-Weikert-Swisher west and east, Codori east, H. Spangler lane). The Spangler lane trace lies within 7 m (median) of the existing NPS lane, an independent check; the others are 27-36 m from the nearest modern horse-trail line or have none (the lane is lost). The remaining 18 of the 21 lanes have no geometry (stated, not guessed).
3. **Railroads** - the Gettysburg (Hanover Junction, Hanover and Gettysburg) Railroad reached the borough from the east in 1858; the grade west of the borough with its three cuts was graded but not laid. The modern CSX line is on the same corridor; its geometry was **laterally snapped, every 40 m, to the double line drawn on the sheet** (`scripts/snap.py`): median shift 18.0 m (east line, 90th percentile 35.6 m, maximum 40.0 m) and 10.0 m (west grade, 90th percentile 20.4 m, maximum 42.0 m); the snapped lines lie on the drawn lines in the overlay figures `evidence/rail-grade-overlay-*.jpg`. Where the sheet shows no clear line within 34 px or lies off the sheet the modern line is kept. The grade west of the sheet edge is flagged Unknown (RL-002W). The depot (1858) and the Rock Creek trestle (burned 27 June 1863) are points. Round Top Branch, the electric railway, later spurs and the Reading depot (1884) are post-1863 and flagged `present_1863: false`.
4. **Streams** - NHD high-resolution flowlines (modern courses). Compared with the drawn creek at 14 stations: median offset 13.0 m, maximum 50.0 m (`verification/pass6_streams.json`); error stated 30 m. Unnamed channels are Unknown (could be modern ditches). Modern ponds (154 NHD waterbodies in the box) are not 1863 features: NPS names two ponds on the 1863 battlefield, McPherson's (still there) and Biesecker's (removed), and neither is located by a source read.
5. **Bridges and fords** - at TIGER x NHD crossings, type by class rule (bridges on the turnpikes and county roads, fords elsewhere: NPS-TREAT-2004 p79). High-resolution crops of the sheet were read at each crossing: off-map 1, off-sheet 3, probable 2, seen 5, unclear 6 (`verification/pass_bridges.json`). A "seen" symbol upgrades the record to Documented; the position stays the modern crossing (error 30 m).
6. **Fences and walls** - the NPS rebuilds fences on documented 1863 lines (`NPS-TREAT-2004` p69) and OSM mappers traced them, so a mapped fence is a candidate, not evidence (some park fences stand in non-historic places, CLI First Day p58, p61-62). Each of the 307 candidates of at least 40 m on the sheet was tested for a continuous drawn line along it: {'not confirmed': 247, 'too short': 85, 'confirmed': 60}, of which 24 ambiguous (a parallel line within 8 px). The same test on the candidates shifted 300 m sideways confirms 2-4 percent (5-11 of 307), so about 83 percent of the confirmations are expected to be right. Only confirmed, non-ambiguous candidates have `draw: true` (36); the fence style of 1863 is not read from the sheet. A separate list of the NPS fence installations (names, GIS ids, 1863 documentation they cite) is in the CLR Record of Treatment vol. I (pdf p176-235, p271-302) and was read but not converted to geometry: its boundaries are given by field names, not coordinates.

## 5. Registration of the period map (what every check rests on)

`registration_sheet_a.json`: the b-maps agent's affine for the sheet (`docs/basemap1863/maps/transforms/bachelder-1876-sheet1.json`) plus a smooth displacement field (grid 450 px, lambda 3) fitted to 23 Warren control points mapped onto the sheet by SIFT and 26 hand-read road lines. Fit residual: 11.5 m RMS (control points), 10.0 m (road lines). Leave-one-out: control points 18.6 m RMS (median 15.6, max 36.7), road lines 14.3 m RMS (median 7.6, max 29.9). Caveats stored in the file: constraints are dense along the main roads and sparse in the north-west, south-west and east; the hand-read road lines assume the modern centreline is on the 1863 road line; the numbers are leave-one-out values, not an independent ground survey.

## 6. What is uncertain or not done (Unknown, kept visible)

- **Dating**: the sheet is 1868-69; NPS says roads and lanes persisted, but a road shown there is not individually dated to July 1863. Records say so.
- **Borough streets**: the borough grid is mostly older than 1863 but each street is not established (pass 4c rejected). 60 edges stay undecided. The Harrisburg, Mummasburg and Carlisle roads end at the borough edge in the data; their 1863 routing to the Diamond is not established.
- **Roads off the sheet** (far west, south, east): geometry is the modern line with no map check; Low Dutch Road and the east part of Hanover Road, the west ends of the Chambersburg Pike and Black Horse Tavern Road have `positional_error_m` 25 with no measurement behind it. The grade west of the sheet is Unknown.
- **Lanes**: 18 of 21 named lanes have no geometry. Fence styles of 1863 are not read from the sheet. 356 of 392 mapped fences are not drawn. The two 1863 ponds are unlocated.
- **Bridges**: only crossings of 1863 roads with named streams; 7 of 19 have a bridge symbol seen or probable on the sheet, the rest are class-rule estimates; stream crossings in the borough (Stevens Run) are Unknown.
- **Streams**: courses are modern; NPS says they have changed; mill-dam ponds and the Rose Run drain (placed underground 1980s) are not drawn.
- **Orchards, woods, wetlands, earthworks, springs other than Spangler's**: not in this layer.

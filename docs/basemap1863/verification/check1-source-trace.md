# Check 1 of 8: source trace of the 1863 reconstruction catalogues

Date of run: 2026-10-19. Method: SOURCE TRACE. Independent of the builders: no builder report was taken on trust; every number below comes from a command run in this pass or a page read in this pass (URLs and sheet numbers given). Tests: `tests/t0/verify-base1863-source-trace.spec.mjs` (7 red V1 tests pinning the defects below, 6 green G1 controls; `--expect-red` exits 0 while the defects stand). Working files (not committed): `tmp/b1863/check1/`.

The base map is a reconstruction. Nothing here says it is 1:1 or 100 percent accurate; this pass measures what the source trace supports and what it does not.

## 1. Verdict

The catalogues are mostly what they claim to be. Every quote I could locate in the NPS and HABS documents is there, every drawn feature has sources, nothing Unknown or not-present is drawn, no Google/Bing/Maxar/Mapbox source is cited. The digitisation of the land cover and the borough from the period sheets is faithful in the sheet frame. But the trace finds features that no source supports (drawn buildings on lettering, a modern roundabout drawn as an 1858 street), provenance records that credit A1 maps that were not read for the feature, and a registration problem that the stated positional errors do not cover. Seven defects, one high, four medium, two low, plus items I could not resolve (section 6).

## 3. Results by category (what held)

* Sources, quotes, pages. The NPS statements I tested are in the cited documents on the cited pages (Treatment Philosophy 2004 p24-26 lanes and cobbled base, p25 McAllister mill lane, p37 hog-pen photograph, p39 Weikert farmstead, p78 and p96 Rock Creek, p80-81 fords and Wheatfield Road "so little travelled ... marked by fences", p81 severely altered farmsteads, p98 marsh 50 yards wide; CLI Pickett's Charge p96 Codori barn 1884, Wentz house site at the NE corner; CLR 1st Corps and CLI First Day for Mummasburg Road 1769 and Nicholson's Gap 1749; CLI Emmitsburg Road for Wheatfield Road 1829; Little Round Top CLR p19 marshy bottom land and Hanaway tract mostly cleared, p21 the clearing date is not confirmed by primary sources). Rose farm structures (nps.gov page), Sherfy barn (Compiler 20 July 1863 as quoted by Gettysburg Daily), Almshouse dates (closed 1964, demolished 1974; the "barn burned 1967" in the catalogue is NOT on the page I fetched), Wikipedia depot and trestle (contract 18 Sept 1858, trestle burned 27 June 1863), the HMDB wheat marker: all found. Christ Lutheran: "about 150 wounded" is on the page; the catalogue's "I Corps divisional hospital 1 July to 15 Aug" and "140-150" are not on that page (one of three citations of that page).
* HABS: PA-265 courthouse (1804 building demolished 1859), PA-360 Old Dorm (1835/1837/1889), PA-361 Black Horse Tavern (1812 as OCR "1312", fieldstone), PA-342 Bryan house ("Standing in 1863. Ref. Brady photograph") all verbatim in the data pages.
* Warren sheet legend (read on the scan): "Every object is represented here as near as possible as it was at the time of the battle", W/B/S letters, fence line styles, and "Certain lines of breastworks are shown which, however, were in many instances thrown up after the fighting ceased". The catalogue's quote of the legend is faithful.
* Digitisation in the sheet frame. Woods (25 of 276), orchards (25 of 206), scattered trees (6), marsh (4): the polygons lie on the tree, orchard and grass-tuft symbols, and the catalogue polygons coincide (median intersection over union 0.985) with the polygons digitised in the plate's own pixels, which lie on the symbols. Woods polygons are often parts of a larger drawn wooded strip (LC-WOOD-0187, -0070, -0155 cover part only).
* Registration of the land cover, independent test. Twenty-six surviving farm buildings whose true ground position is known (footprints of 2023, hand-read on the sheet by another task) lie inside or within 15 m (median 0, p90 6 m) of the land-cover polygon that contains them in the sheet's own pixels. This supports the stated 30-40 m for the land cover in the central block.
* Drawing rules. No drawn feature is Unknown, and no drawn catalogue feature has present_1863 other than true (G1-01). 408 non-Hopkins drawn ids all exist in a catalogue; the 36 drawn fences are exactly the draw:true ones; no source host outside the licence list (G1-02 to G1-06). RL-003 and RL-005 (post-1863 rail), RD-017, BR-004/009/013 (Unknown) and the 356 unconfirmed fences are correctly not drawn. No drawn building overlaps another (334 Hopkins buildings 0 overlaps; 0 non-Hopkins structures within 8 m of a Hopkins building).
* Hopkins inset. All 334 building footprints sit on a solid building symbol (mean grey 32 against paper 211); 42 of 48 streets sit in a street gap (visual; the 6 exceptions are V1-03).
* Removal polygons (1,492) stay clear of the 1863 tree polygons (none intersects one, none closer than 5 m); the 707 add polygons lie 99 percent inside 1863 tree polygons.

Further, smaller points (no test):

* The removal polygons are defined as "outside every 1863 tree polygon plus 12 m"; measured distances are below 12 m for 370 of 1,492 (262 below 11 m, 4 below 8 m). Minor; the effect is that up to a few metres of 1863 tree edge may be treated as modern.
* The removal polygon quote "Open ground (no tree symbols) at this location" is not literally true where the sheet shows single tree symbols; the pipeline does not map clusters of fewer than 7 glyphs (its own note). I saw this in one of ten sampled panels on an overlay whose registration I could not trust (section 6).
* The fence evidence ("a continuous drawn line lies along this mapped fence") cannot tell a fence from a contour or a property line, and its note says the 1863 fence type is "NOT read from the sheet", although the legend of the sheet itself defines the styles (stone wall dotted, post and rail dots and dashes, worm fence zigzag). Of 12 drawn fences shown, 5 sit 20-40 m beside the nearest drawn fence line in my overlay (registration doubt, section 6).
* The dimensions, roofs and materials of the 99 drawn map-symbol structures are class defaults ("footprint typical size for the building type", disclosed in their `method`); the 334 Hopkins buildings take the symbol extent. Positions are sourced; size is not.
* The Adams County 2023 footprints (41 surviving-building outlines used as geometry) have no published licence; SOURCES-structures.md says "ask the county before reuse". This is also in check 8.
* Christ Lutheran source line, Almshouse "barn burned 1967": not on the cited pages (above). Wikipedia is cited as the only source for the trestle burning (RL-007, Estimated, position 40 m): consistent with the label.

## 5. Registration: the stated errors are not validated

Three different georeferences of the same Warren sheet are in use: the maps task's affine raster (claimed leave-one-out 16.5 m), the structures task's thin-plate spline (88 controls, claimed leave-one-out median 10 m), the land cover's similarity plus warp (claimed 30-40 m), plus a fourth, the sheet-a displacement field used for all road, fence, lane, bridge and rail presence checks (claimed 15-19 m). Measured here:

* 343 structure symbols: the spline and the maps affine disagree by a median of 48 m (p90 140, max 189); 61 percent of the symbols differ by more than their own stated error. In the control block (x 3000-6000, y 4500-8000 of sheet a) the median is 11 m, outside it 88 m. The builder's own comparison file records the same.
* 28 surviving buildings with exactly known ground positions (hand-read on the sheet): the maps affine misses them by a median of 32 m (p90 52, max 94; 19 of 28 over 16.5 m); the sheet-a field misses them by a median of 25 px (about 25 m; p90 51, max 56) against 15-19 m claimed. The land-cover polygons and the spline agree with them (the land-cover test above).
* The land-cover polygons and the maps affine differ by a median of 55 m for woods, 68 m for orchards, 102 m for marsh (max 183).

Consequence: any trace I make of a feature on a sheet through the maps raster is good to roughly 40 m only, and the stated 10-30 m errors of plate symbols (and the 15-19 m of the presence checks) hold only in the control block. For the one registration I could test independently (land cover against the 26 survivors) the claim held; for the spline outside the controls and for the field registration I found no independent arbiter. My own presence test along the road lines (ink profile) did not discriminate (contrast over control below 0.1 for many roads) and I do not use it.

## 6. What I could not verify

* The positions of the 1,492 + 707 change polygons (derived from 2017 lidar and the land-cover polygons; checked for geometry only).
* Whether the Warren sheet shows each feature as of July 1863: the sheet is 1868-69 (revised 1873); the legend says the objects are shown as at the time of the battle and I could not test that. Nothing in this pass can separate 1863 from 1868 for a woodlot, an orchard or a fence.
* The 90 curated structures not shown on the sheet, 343 waterways, 347 unconfirmed fences, 214 borough masses (text and sources traced, not the position).
* Quotes in NPS documents that were not cached (the Spangler, Brien, McClean, Leister, Rose-area CLR and 1999 GMP citations, 36 in all, were scripted against cached text: 31 match 0.75 or more on a single page, all 36 match 0.75 or more across the document; the rest are generic statements). Primary documents (damage claims, Official Records) were not consulted by the builders and not by me.
* No independent ground truth for anything in 1863 exists; "verified" above means the cited source says it and, for map features, the sheet shows it at the digitised place within the registration error.

# Verification pass 8 of 8: claims, licences and site images (red team)

Run 2026-10-19 by an independent verifier. Method: read every sentence the layer shows or ships, attack the licence ledger from the inputs, re-derive numbers, re-read sources at the institutions, drive the BASE1863 build in a real browser pane (port 10480, own static server, stopped afterwards). Nothing of the builders' reports was trusted. Failing tests: `tests/t0/verify-base1863-claims-and-licences.spec.mjs` (14 red V8 tests, 3 green controls; `node tests/t0/verify-base1863-claims-and-licences.spec.mjs`). Scratch: `tmp/b1863/check8/`.

Bottom line: the layer does not claim "1:1" or "100 percent" anywhere I could find, and no third-party imagery pixel went into the tiles. But the owner requirement "the site uses only actual images of Gettysburg" is NOT met by what would ship, the one sentence a visitor reads on the map mis-names the imagery, a page still says "real satellite map", and two licence statements are contradicted by committed files.

## What held (attacked and survived)

* No third-party imagery in the tiles. (1) Pipeline code reads one raster base (`C.BASE_TIF`, NAIP) and no network; no Google/Bing/Maxar/Mapbox URL appears in any `.py` under `tools/b1863`, `modern/scripts`, `maps/tools` (G8-03). (2) The base mosaic `tmp/b1863/modern/work/naip2013_1.0m.tif` has the sha256 recorded in the manifest, and a 500 x 500 px window of it is byte-identical (mean abs diff 0 in all four bands, 100 percent identical pixels) to the public-domain scene `m_3907715_sw_18_1_20130604.tif` that I fetched fresh from the Planetary Computer NAIP mirror (`tmp/b1863/check8/naip_indep.py`). (3) Twelve random z16 tiles re-projected onto that NAIP mosaic: untouched classes differ by 3.4 (PHOTO_KEPT), 4.1 (outside sheet), 5.5 (AMBIGUOUS_KEPT) mean abs grey levels (resampling plus WebP), changed classes by 23 to 34 (`tile_vs_naip.py`). Negative control against Mapbox imagery was NOT run (no Mapbox fetch was made); the positive evidence above is what I rely on.
* Site images: all 11 shipped IMG files match their LEDGER.md sha256 (G8-01). Fresh re-reads: Met API objects 268204 and 268205 (isPublicDomain true, O'Sullivan, 1863, accessions 33.65.393 and .394, primaryImage equal to the file used); Commons records for NARA 533312 and 533316 (Public domain, July 1863, sha1 equal to the ledger); LoC item 2006692500 re-read fresh (the builder could not, HTTP 429): "1863 July, c1866", "No known restrictions on publication". LoC items 99448794 (Warren), 99447491 (Bachelder), 2004629149 (Hopkins 1858) exist with the cited titles and the Geography and Map Division free-to-use statement. In the IMG build (`tmp/b1863/images/img-R1c-gated/dist`): 21 pages carry og:image pointing to the one JPEG, og/twitter alt tags present, 3 `<img>` all with alt and caption, manifest has 4 icons and no screenshots, no `data:` images other than two Mapbox GL probes. I looked at the card, the "from the west" figure and the 512 icon: they are real 1863 photographs with the stated crops.
* Claims: no "1:1", "exact", "100 percent" or "accurate" statement outside a negation in `gtb63.js`, `ABOUT.txt`, `manifest.json`, `tiles/README.md`, `PROVENANCE-LAYER/README.md`, `BASE1863-README.md`, `BUILD.md`. The owner's personal address appears nowhere in the checked paths or in the two dists (grep). The site contact address in the page footer is the site's own.
* Pipeline accounting: tile counts and sizes (932 files, 29.27 MB; provenance 919 files 6.92 MB), class areas sum to the 235.35 km2 box, 1,535 ha is 21.4 percent of 7,167 ha, 163.7 km2 outside the sheet, 938 monuments = 873 OSM + 65 Wikipedia, seam ratios 6.292/5.554 and 6.12/5.554 (13 and 10 percent above random, the builder says "about 12"): all reproduce. Browser: the group build loads 16 tiles at z14 and zero Mapbox requests in my pane; the on-map attribution reads "Reconstruction, not a photograph. USDA NAIP 2013, USGS, Library of Congress, NPS, OpenStreetMap contributors".

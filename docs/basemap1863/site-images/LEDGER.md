## What is not claimed

* The photographs are real but they are views of the ground, not of the armies in motion; the Round Top and Trostle prints were exposed in July 1863 after the fighting, the two Met prints are dated 1863 without a month.
* No photograph here is an aerial or satellite view: none exists for 1863. The reconstructed 1863 basemap is a separate, labelled reconstruction (docs/basemap1863/).
* Crops remove the album mounts and handwriting; the full plates are at the record URLs above.

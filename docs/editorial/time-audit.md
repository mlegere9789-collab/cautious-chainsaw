# Time audit: the clock and historical accuracy of the legacy site

> **Status: CANDIDATE, unreviewed. Nothing in this document has been approved by the owner or by a historian, and nothing here ships until they approve it (D-TIME, D-HIST-REVIEW).** It is generated by `node tools/time/validate.mjs --report` from the frozen legacy site (`legacy/dist`, bundle `assets/index-Cg8FAMXN.js`), the 33 proposed anchors (`data-src/time/time_anchors.json`, version 2026-09-30.1, dv 2) and the time-facts specialist's tables (`docs/plan/data/time-facts*.csv`). Do not edit by hand: re-run the script. Real times are the specialist's proposals; 32 of the 102 single-event comparators are the specialist's own estimates and every historical time is unreviewed.

## 1. How far the clocks on the site are from the sourced real times

Over the **102** chronology events that map to a single real event (RELABEL 60, RELABEL-CONTESTED 36, TEXT-VS-LABEL 6); absolute difference between the clock and the specialist's real best time:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| typed chronology label (event card) | 4.8 h | 3.9 h | 5.4 h | 5/102 | 8/102 |
| map clock getTickTime (toolbar, time bar, mobile) | 4.8 h | 3.6 h | 5.7 h | 1/102 | 5/102 |
| 5-minute formula (Compare, Ledger, Camera) | 4.5 h | 3.5 h | 5.3 h | 7/102 | 10/102 |
| proposed 33-anchor model (this candidate) | 21 min | 10 min | 47 min | 86/102 | 96/102 |

- **Events within 30 minutes of the sourced real time: label 5/102, map clock 1/102, 5-minute formula 7/102, proposed model 86/102.** The specialist reports 5, 1, 7 and 86.
- Independent check: the two legacy formulas recomputed from the tick alone reproduce the specialist's printed columns for 102/102 (map clock) and 102/102 (5-minute formula) events.
- **In-sample** fit of the proposal (anchors were chosen using these same events): mean abs error **21.3 min**, median 10 min, RMS 46.5 min; within 15/30/45/60 min: 62/86/93/96 of 102.
- **Leave-one-out** (each of 29 interior non-design anchors predicted from its two neighbours): mean abs error **35 min**, median 28.6 min, RMS **43.8 min**. Least predictable: t518 +96, t340 +93, t515 -86, t451 -77, t240 +68, t568 +51, t90 -48, t326 -48 (true minus predicted, minutes). These are the anchors next to gaps and the first to review.
- Typed real clocks elsewhere in the product (walkthrough stops, monument tooltips, timeline markers, Turning Points, Sandbox presets, vignettes): 59 carry a clock; median gap to the proposed clock **27 min** (32/59 within 30 min) versus **215 min** to today's map clock (1/59). For the walkthrough, monument and marker subset (30): median 14.5 min (21/30) versus 213.5 min (1/30).
- Caveats: the comparators are proposals, not reviewed facts; in-sample figures flatter the model; leave-one-out RMS is the honest predictive number for anchors that sit between two sourced neighbours.

## 6. What this audit does not establish

- It does not verify any 1863 time: the real times are the specialist's proposals (H/M/L confidence 18/66/45; Pfanz, Coddington, Sears, Hessler, Wert, Gottfried and most Official Records were not read). Only a named historian can turn a row from `proposed` to `signed`.
- It does not test the running app: no browser was used. Surfaces are counted by reading the frozen bundle and pages.
- TM-V19 and TM-V20 need the moments registry (WP-TIME-05); TM-V21 finds quotations heuristically and cannot judge whether a wording is correct.

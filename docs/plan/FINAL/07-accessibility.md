## 0. Headline

1. **Lighthouse 90 overstated the state.** It scores two failing audits on one screen (`label` weight 10, `color-contrast` weight 7, total 164): contrast alone gives about 93.9, both about 100 [S app-a11y verifier 01]. By code review 23 of 55 Level A+AA criteria fail, 10 partially pass, 12 pass, 10 are N/A: grade **D desktop / D mobile**, not the audit's C+/C. After the bridge Lighthouse can read 100 with about 26 criteria still failing or partial [E]; no claim may rest on Lighthouse.
2. **The map has no keyboard or non-visual route**: units are canvas pixels, selection is `canvas.onClick`, the app has zero ARIA (96200-113377 [V]), 17 click-only controls, no dialog semantics, and a global handler firing on bare letters. The cure is structural (Data View, unit cursor, dialog/control primitives) and rides the rebuild (D-SOURCE); the bridge buys form-level wins.
3. **Capacity conflict**: the register allots a11y 8-12 d; the a11y-owned scope is **50-82 d** (section 10).
4. **Ordering traps**: every patched byte ships under a new hashed name (main bundle and both lazy chunks) after R0 (D-ORDER); no accessible clock string may be presented as authoritative before D-TIME sign-off.

## 2. Critical bugs (fix first)

1. **Mobile: Play/Pause and step buttons are off-screen.** In Mobile layout at 375px the transport row is 572px wide, `justify-content:center`, no wrap, no overflow → Play sits at x = −130 (Back −213, Prev −172, Next −71, Fwd −29). A phone user cannot start playback from the transport bar. Fix: allow wrap or `justify-content:flex-start` + horizontal scroll, or shrink buttons.
2. **Mobile: Tools sheet bottom rows are covered by the timeline bar.** Legend, Bookmarks, Help, Mini Map and **Desktop View** (the only way back from Mobile layout) are un-tappable — `elementFromPoint` returns the timeline/NPS Auto Tour button.
3. **Dates are wrong by +2 days in places.** Header date label reads "July 5, 1863" on July 3 (and "July 3" on July 1). Follow Your Ancestor lists the 20th Maine's Little Round Top fight on **"July 4"** and the Day‑1 bivouac on "July 3".
4. **Clock vs. history vs. own narrative.** The tick clock disagrees with (a) real history, (b) the site's own text, and (c) itself:
   - Data labels: Longstreet's assault 7:30am, Vincent at Little Round Top 8:25am, Pickett's Charge begins 11:05am, "Johnson advances… at dusk" 12:10pm, "By nightfall" 1:30pm (real: ~4pm, ~4:30pm, ~3pm, dusk, night).
   - Vincent panel shows "8:25am" next to narrative "shot… late in the afternoon".
   - Documentary says first shot "~7:30 in the morning" at Tick 12 (=5:55am by the data's own formula).
   - Main-map clock is 1 hr off the data labels (Tick 17 shows 7:20 AM vs data 6:25am; Tick 451 shows 4:00 AM vs "5:00am July 3"); Compare/Living Ledger use the data formula (Tick 1 = 5:00am) while the map uses 6:00.
   - Sandbox labels scenarios "mid-afternoon (tick 260)" and "~3:00pm (tick 553)" that the data clock puts at ~7:50am and ~12:00pm.
   For a history product this is the most credibility-damaging issue. Likely the tick→clock mapping is compressed relative to real time.
5. **JS error in production:** `Uncaught ReferenceError: setGoldUnit is not defined` in an `onClick` (main bundle) — fired during my session (probably the Monuments/active-unit gold highlight path).
6. **Every first-time visitor loads the app twice.** `sw.js` + `clients.claim()` + `controllerchange → location.reload()` forces a reload on first visit. Lighthouse flags a self-redirect costing ~9s on throttled mobile (LCP 9.0s mobile lab).
7. **Dashboard shows raw variable + "undefined":** a stat card titled `highest_single_regiment_rate` with body `undefined`. Also a developer note visible to users ("…should be reconciled against the underlying research"). On my first load the chart SVG was empty for 5+ s; it rendered on reload (intermittent).
8. **Search filter chips are unreadable:** Regiments/Vignettes/Locations = light text (#E8E6DF) on default button gray (#F0F0F0), contrast 1.10:1.

## 3. High-priority polish

- **Floating "Ask the Historian / Extra Features" overlay collides with content everywhere.** It sits over the story panel text (desktop), the Virtual Walkthrough body copy, the layout chooser cards (mobile & desktop), the Help modal backdrop, and mid-map. It's a script injected outside the React app (`z-index:999999`, collision-mover), and the HTML comments admit the workaround. Integrate it into the real toolbar.
- **Esc doesn't close panels the way the guide/help says:** ignored while cursor is in the Search box, and didn't close the story panel or event card in my test.
- **Layout choice is never saved;** returning visitors get a ~15s "Fast‑forwarding 3 days" splash → "Choose Your Experience" every visit (only `gtbg_onboarded` is stored). On mobile the splash title is clipped ("ETTYSBURC") and the running app shows through.
- **Mobile layout chooser doesn't auto-detect phones;** Desktop card offered to phones; "Power User: keyboard shortcuts" offered on touch.
- **Clutter on load/jump:** after Skip tour I landed on July 2 with legend + story panel + event card + toasts all open. Toasts (stacked "wounded/captured") don't auto-dismiss.
- **Follow Your Ancestor list sorts as text:** 102nd, 104th … 109th, **then** 10th Alabama.
- **Toolbar iconography is inconsistent:** emoji (🗿🔭⭐📏🗺📍🔍) mixed with letters (S F L B ?) at 28×26px targets; the custom SVG icon set used on sub-pages is nicer. Onboarding/Desktop icons (🖥, 🗺) are near-invisible dark-on-dark.
- **Dev-log copy in user-facing text:** Viewshed ("Earlier drafts of this tool tried…", "this deployment doesn't have access"), AR ("we haven't been able to do from here"), Dashboard data note, Ancestor "alias for U‑84NY" / "Commander unrecorded ·" rows in Search. Honest, but not museum voice.
- **Feature-name overpromise:** "What They Could See" isn't a viewshed (it says so). "Ask the Historian" is keyword search: "why did Lee lose the war" returned Pickett + "Archer Captured"; long vignettes dumped as walls of text; last message hides behind the fixed input bar. The onboarding banner calls it "an AI-style historian".
- **Speed label:** "1x (real time)" is ≈2 ticks/sec (10 sim-min per second).
- **Inconsistent counts:** 467 regiments/batteries (home, search index) vs "445+ units" (Help→About).
- **Minimap label stale:** "72 units · tick 1" / "1 units · tick 1" while at other ticks.
- **Distance tool** math is correct (9,311 ft = 3,104 yd = 2,838 m; 42 min at 2.5 mph) but I saw only one endpoint marker and no line.
- **Soldier's Eye View opens Google Maps in a new tab** (not embedded) though the guide implies panoramas; Walkthrough Street View caption looked identical ("48 Reynolds Ave S") for stops 1 and 8 — verify the iframe re-keys.

## 6. Performance (Lighthouse + your RUM PDF)

- **Your RUM (30 days, all devices):** LCP **Poor** (p75 > 4s), INP **Poor** (p75 > 500ms), CLS **Poor** (p75 > 0.25), FCP **Poor** (p75 > 3s), FID Good (<100ms). Spikes on ~Sep 7–8, Sep 18, Sep 26 (FCP/LCP to ~27s; INP to ~2 min — likely outliers/backgrounded tabs, but Sep 7–8 matches your big deploy day). Note the CLS "Poor" badge doesn't match the plotted p75 line, which mostly sits under 0.1 — treat as period-level aggregate.
- **Lighthouse (headless, live):** Mobile — Perf **64**, A11y 90, Best Practices 96, SEO **100**; FCP 4.1s, **LCP 9.0s**, TBT 30ms, CLS 0.073, TTI 9.1s. Desktop — Perf **85**, A11y 90, BP 96, SEO 100; FCP 0.8s, LCP 2.2s, CLS 0.023.
- Root causes: (a) SW self-reload; (b) one 2.28MB JS bundle (604KB brotli), **52% unused** on first load, 3.3s boot time on mobile; (c) 30 JSON fetches on load; (d) render-blocking `site-nav.js/css`; (e) console spam: CSS preload "not used" warnings (cross-world SW mismatch) repeated; (f) no source maps.
- Fixes: route-level code splitting (Extra Features already separate pages, so lazy-load Help/OOB/tour/Historian), let the SW claim without reloading (or defer registration), remove the CSS preload/onload swap, preload the LCP element, lazy-load data by day.
- Good: HTML `max-age=0,must-revalidate`, hashed assets `immutable`, brotli, server response 30ms, total transfer ≈1.6MB, CLS mostly fixed by your reserved-space work.

## 8. Security / hygiene

- Good: HSTS (preload), X-Frame-Options DENY, nosniff, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy, no cookies set, brand 404, no exposed dotfiles.
- **Mapbox public token is in client JS and answered 200 with a foreign Referer** → likely no URL restriction. Restrict to your domain in Mapbox. (A public `pk.` token is expected client-side; restriction is what protects your quota.) The embedded Google key can't be judged from outside; confirm HTTP-referrer + API restrictions in Google Cloud.
- CSP is **Report-Only** (and Netlify's RUM beacon `ingesteer.services-prod.nsvcs.net` is *not* in `connect-src`, so enforcing as written would break RUM). Uses `'unsafe-inline'`.
- Terms invite embedding "its widget", but `X-Frame-Options: DENY` blocks all embedding.
- Privacy page omits Watch Together's PeerJS broker (0.peerjs.com / WebRTC exposes IPs), Mapbox telemetry (events.mapbox.com), and AR's camera/location permissions.

## 9. SEO / metadata

Nits: manifest locks **orientation: landscape** (bad for phones/PWA); `theme` name mismatch ("Animated Battlefield Map" title vs "interactive" domain); 3 `<h1>` in the home HTML (splash + SEO block); the crawler-only `#seo-content` block is well-intentioned but adjacent to "cloaking" patterns — keep it truthful/visible; sitemap omits privacy/terms (fine).

## 11. Accessibility (Lighthouse 90 both; manual findings)

Color-contrast failures (#777 on dark, search chips 1.1:1), unlabeled slider, tiny targets, 9–11px text, many `✕` buttons without labels, layout chooser/tour modals lack obvious focus management, no landmarks in the app shell. Sub-pages are better (semantic `<main>`, headings).

5. Fold the floating overlay into the real toolbar.
6. Persist layout choice; auto-pick mobile; shorten/skip the splash for returning users.

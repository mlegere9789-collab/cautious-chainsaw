Written 2026-09-30 after the release verifier's findings (`docs/verification/t0-verifier-findings.json`, verifier 3) and the second-run fixes. Chapter 02 (`docs/plan/FINAL/02-tranche0-hotfix-runbook.md`) says anything outside its closed list needs an amendment; the plan's own section 14 lists the amendments it proposed. Everything here is **built and documented**; the right-hand column says what still needs the owner. The tooling's own change log is `tools/t0/CHANGELOG.md`; the reasons for the header items are in `ops/header-decisions.md`.

Counts of patches are not repeated here because they move whenever a group changes: the truth for a build is `release/<train>/anchors.json` (`summary.patches`, per group in `patches[].group`) and `version.json` `groups`.

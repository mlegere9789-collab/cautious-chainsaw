Contact

Last reviewed: October 4, 2026. Status: candidate.

Contact and report a problem. Contact is by email only, to one address (gettysburginteractivemap@gmail.com). This page has no form, and it loads nothing from other sites.

General questions

Write a general message (opens your email program; the address is gettysburginteractivemap@gmail.com)

A mistake in the history or the data

Use the project address, and read how corrections work first. Include the page address, what you think is wrong and your source.

Write a correction request (opens your email program; the address is gettysburginteractivemap@gmail.com)

An accessibility barrier

Say what you were trying to do, which page and what got in the way. See the Accessibility Statement.

Report an accessibility barrier (opens your email program; the address is gettysburginteractivemap@gmail.com)

A security problem

If you believe you have found a way to harm visitors or the site, write to the project address and do not publish the details first.

Write a security report (opens your email program; the address is gettysburginteractivemap@gmail.com)

What to expect

The targets are to acknowledge a message about a mistake or an accessibility barrier within 3 days, and to fix the problem, or explain why it stands, within 30 days. These are targets, not promises. [OWNER TO CONFIRM: the response targets (acknowledge in 3 days, fix or explain in 30 days) are the ones you will keep]

Your email address is used only to reply. A correction is credited to you only if you say you agree.

Privacy of this page

This page has no form, no tracking and no script of its own. The shared site menu is the only script it loads. See the site’s Privacy page for the rest of the site.

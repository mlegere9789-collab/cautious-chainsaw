# PERF5 - the first frame of the Virtual Walkthrough and of the Monuments layer, the heat layer of /ground-remembers (group PERF5 + page group PG-zperf5, train R1f)

Registered in train R1f (`trains.json`, cumulative on R1e, which stays frozen). `python tools/t0/r1f_sync.py` copies `patches-next/PERF5.json` and `PG-zperf5.json` to `patches/`; both are written by `python tools/t0/perf5/gen_perf5.py`, which checks every anchor against `release/R1e/dist` and is the place to read the note of each patch.
Tests: `tests/t0/perf5.spec.mjs`. Measurements and the story of what was tried: `docs/accept/PERF5-NOTES.md`. Decisions taken without the owner, with the way back: `docs/decisions/R1F-PROVISIONAL-DECISIONS-2026-10-31.md`.

## The problem

Acceptance check 4 (`tools/accept/checks/c4_input.mjs`) activates every control of the desktop app with real input and counts the long tasks (> 50 ms) of the 450 ms after the activation. On R1e, in 4 of 5 sweeps, the first click on **Virtual Walkthrough** (51, 56, 64, 70 ms) and on **Monuments** (58, 60 ms) was one task over the limit: one `requestAnimationFrame` callback, Mapbox rendering the first frame after the click. The same on the delivered R1d (53 and 69 ms). The eight consecutive clean passes cannot start while it is open.

## What the time was (R1e, desktop 1x)

* **Virtual Walkthrough.** The click task is React's flush plus the Mapbox constructor of a second map (`_setupContainer` forces the layout of the overlay just mounted, 9 ms; `_setupPainter` creates the WebGL context, 7 to 16 ms): 25 to 35 ms, sometimes 43 to 84. The map's `load` handler then added three layers at once (two circle layers, one symbol layer), so the next frame was the first draw of three programs: Mapbox links a program the first time a layer draws with it and the main thread waits for the GPU process (`Program._finalize` -> `getProgramParameter`): 5 to 11 ms per program on the machine of this round, 20 to 30 ms on the harness machine, plus the upload of the satellite tiles: one task of 35 to 70 ms.
* **Monuments.** The click adds two images, two sources (512 + 20 features) and four layers (three circle variants, one symbol layer with its icons) in one task; the frame after it is the first draw of all four programs, the symbol placement and the animation layer's own render step.

Neither could be reproduced at will on the machine of this round: the scoped check 4 on R1e passed (0 defects) and 24 + 24 interleaved cold clicks gave 0 long tasks by the observer, while the exact task lengths (renderer `RunTask` events of a trace) showed the Walkthrough at 21 to 49 ms (3 of 72 loads over 50: 84, 53, 50) and Monuments at 9 to 42 (0 of about 140). The failure needs a slow link: `tests/t0/perf5.spec.mjs` and the bench in `docs/accept/PERF5-NOTES.md` emulate one (an init script that makes the first read of a program's link status busy-wait 15 ms when the GPU process is not done with it, which is what a loaded driver does by itself). With 15 ms, R1e's Walkthrough click has a task over 50 ms in 44 of 82 interleaved loads (median of the longest task 52 to 55 ms; the harness saw 4 of 5 sweeps); R1f in 1 of 24 (median 26 ms).

## What PERF5 does (9 bundle patches, 2 page patches)

Bundle (`PERF5`, `index-<id>.js`):
* **PERF5-stepper.** Two helpers next to PERF4's `gtSlices`: `gtStepper(map, steps, alive, end, pre)` runs the steps one after the other, each in a task of its own that starts after two Mapbox renders (or 120 ms) of the step before; `gtPreIds(map, ids, icons, done, own)` asks Mapbox's own program precompiler for the programs of the named circle layers and the text-SDF symbol program (the parameters `gtPrecompile` / `gtPrecompileExtra` of PERF4 use), one program per timer task, so the GPU process links them while the main thread goes on; with `own` it also drains the queue the precompiler builds for the style's own layers (the satellite raster program) into the same slices.
* **PERF5-wt-defer-map.** The Walkthrough's map is built in a timer task after the first paint of the overlay (animation frame, then timer): the click task is React's flush only (10 to 17 ms) and the overlay is painted before the map's 25 ms.
* **PERF5-wt-style-load / -gated-circle / -gated-num / -gated-dots / -reveal.** The sources and the three layers are added when the style has loaded (was: when the map had loaded, after the satellite tiles) with a zoom range of 24 to 24: Mapbox treats such a layer as hidden (no tiles are requested for its source, nothing is drawn), but its programs can be queued, so the programs are linked while the satellite tiles download. At `load` the stops (circle and number layer: they share one GeoJSON source) are shown in one task by `setLayerZoomRange(id, 0, 24)` (the default range: the original layers have none), one step later the monument dots. The layer order and ids are the original.
* **PERF5-mon-layers / -mon-fix-guard.** First toggle of the Monuments layer: the four layers are added in the click as before but with a zoom range of 24 to 24, their programs queued, the dots shown two renders later, glow, ring and stars (one source) two renders after that. Every later toggle shows all four at once as before; `put()` also restores the zoom range, so a toggle off and on in the middle of the first reveal cannot leave a layer out of its range for good (test T0-P5-04). The style-data repair of the layers (`styledata` handler) waits while the reveal runs (`o.stg`).

Page (`PG-zperf5`, `ground-remembers.html`):
* **PERF5-ground-heat-task (+ -end).** The heat layer (`addSource` with every placement, `addLayer` with its colour ramp, the program precompile: 20 ms at 4x) and the slider / play handlers are set up in a timer task after the `load` event instead of inside the render that fires it. The layer, the source and the paint are unchanged; the heat shows one task later.

## What was found and not changed

* A layer change on a source that is already loading reloads it and Mapbox can lose the symbol bucket (PERF4, test T0-P4-10). That is why the layers are not added one per frame (the first version of this work did: the Walkthrough's number layer and the Monuments' stars would have been added while their source was loading) and why layers that share a source are shown in the same task. 17 cold loads of the Walkthrough (14 numbers rendered in every one) and 20 of Monuments (10 stars in the default view) lost nothing.
* A layer added with `visibility: none` does not give the precompiler the same program parameters as the visible layer (the data-driven circle layers of Monuments linked at the first draw anyway), so the zoom range is used to keep a layer out of the first frames.
* The five long tasks of `/ground-remembers` on the 4x phone (the Mapbox constructor 60 to 70 ms, the first render 55 to 62, the style load 90 to 95, a render with the first tiles 100 to 125, a render with the symbol placement 80 to 100) are single calls inside the Mapbox library, 40 to 50 percent of their time lazy compilation and native work (`(program)`); nothing the page owns is left in them except the 20 ms moved by PG-zperf5. Details and the options that were not taken (they need the owner) are in `docs/accept/PERF5-NOTES.md` and the decisions file.

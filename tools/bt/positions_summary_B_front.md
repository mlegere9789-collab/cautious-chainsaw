# Positions and approach roads, scope B (Union II, III and XII Corps): summary

> **Full-passage fidelity re-check (scope B).** After this overview and the helper notes below were written, every claim of this scope was re-read against 1,200+ characters of the raw report text on each side of its anchor (`RECHECK_B_NOTES.md`; decisions in `tools/bt/positions_B_recheck_*.json`, applied by `tools/bt/positions_claims.py`). Times taken from another sentence were removed from `when.printed`/`t_iso`, glosses (compass, road, unit names, purposes) were narrowed to what the passage prints, hedges and conditions were restored, OCR-damaged digits were un-read, claims whose passage does not support the central fact were downgraded to `inferred`, and claims whose passage speaks of another command are marked `unit_attribution: context` and left out of the coverage verdicts. The prose overview below and the helper notes appended at the end are the first-build reading and may quote a time or label that the claim file no longer carries as printed; the claim file and the generated tables are authoritative.

Scope B of the positions ledger (brief: `GOVERNANCE/briefs/positions.md`). Claim records: `positions_B.jsonl` in this folder (built by `tools/bt/positions_claims.py` from `tools/bt/positions_data_B_*.py`; this summary is generated by `tools/bt/positions_summary.py` and then carries the helpers' notes verbatim). Nothing in this folder changes a default, gate or tier elsewhere. Every conflict is carried with both values and never resolved; the owner decides defaults (CLAUDE.md, Law 6).

## How the claims were made

- Text: OR Ser. I vol. 27, Part 1 (Union reports), Part 3 (correspondence and orders) and, where a Part 2 report speaks of a unit in scope, Part 2; index `library/or27_index.json`; single-witness OCR (no second text yet: OR27-NOTES.md "What remains" item 1). Page numbers come from the index; a `~` marks an interpolated page range and is never cited as exact.
- Every quote (<15 words) is located and re-verified by code at build time; every time phrase, place phrase and road phrase is verbatim within the stated window of the quote (700 characters by default; claims that needed a wider window, such as a dispatch whose date line is far from the sentence, record that in the code, not in the data). Every claim carries its own verified extract (<15 words; the matched sentence fragment of that claim, trimmed by code to 14 words around its time/place phrases when longer); claims taken from one sentence may share the same extract. Clock times damaged by OCR have no `t_iso` (`when.description` says "OCR glyph only"); see `positions_B_overrides.json`.
- Tiers: `documented` where an OR passage states exactly the claim; `inferred` with `reasoning` where it does not (very few: grep `"tier": "inferred"`); `open gap` where no OR passage was found, with the searches tried. Orders (Part 3) are recorded as orders, not as reports of execution; the note on the claim says so.
- "Brigade" claims often rest on the brigade commander's report and on regimental reports; regiments, battalions and batteries that the OR names with their own position or movement carry their own `unit_id` from `history/units.json`.
- The Part 2 (Confederate) reports were searched by unit and commander names; they name few of the Union units in scope (hits for the Second and Twelfth Corps commanders are isolated; Third Corps hits are in the Confederate corps and division reports) and were used only where the helper notes say so.

## Second Corps: approach roads and times found (what decides where units enter the map)

All of this is from the claim file; ids in brackets. The Second Corps reached Gettysburg on the Taneytown road; the OR names the road at brigade level only for the last stretch.

- **June 28:** corps marched from near Sugar Loaf Mountain and was stopped by Meade near Monocacy Junction. General Orders put the Second Corps on the picket front "From Monocacy Junction to the bridge above Carroll Creek" for the night (Part 3 D0839).
- **June 29:** army order D0840: Second Corps to march at 4 a. m. "by Johnsville, Liberty, and Union, to Frizellburg". Hancock: the order was delayed and the corps was "not in motion until 8 a. m."; halted at 10 p. m. one mile beyond Uniontown after 30 miles. Brigades: Brooke left the Monocacy bridge about 6 a. m., 2nd DE at 6 a. m., 53rd PA at 8 a. m., Webb at 6 a. m. (9 p. m. at Uniontown), Hays's division at 1 p. m. (3 a. m. June 30 at Uniontown). Order and execution differ (conflict group on the 4 a. m. / 8 a. m. / 6 a. m. times).
- **June 30:** corps in camp at Uniontown (Hancock; Part 3 D0937 shows no movement). Part 3 D0932 (1 p. m.) orders it to Taneytown and D0919 lists "Second Corps to Taneytown" as the destination of the movement of July 1.
- **July 1:** Brooke's brigade left Uniontown at 6 a. m. (Brooke) or 8 a. m. (Baily), Smyth at 6.30 a. m. (Hays's division, Smyth), Hancock's corps at Taneytown about 11 a. m., corps departure "toward Gettysburg at 1.30 p. m." (OCR "1..30"). Hazard's artillery brigade left Taneytown at 2 p. m. and went into position "3 miles southeast of Gettysburg, on the Taneytown road" at 11 p. m. (claim on us-2c-art, kind arrival_road). Night camps by distance from Gettysburg: Webb about 4 miles; Brooke about 3 miles (arrival about 9.30 p. m.); 2nd DE 5 or 6 miles (about 9 p. m.); Kelly 2 or 3 miles (about 10 p. m.); Hays's division about 3 miles (right of the Taneytown road, 1st DE); 4th Ohio 1 1/2 miles in rear of the cemetery; Hancock "about 3 miles in rear of the town"; 7th Mich 3 or 4 miles south; Devereux (19th Mass) about 2 miles. Two Part 3 documents of July 1 place the corps differently ("now at Taneytown ... held in reserve"; "on the road between Taneytown and Gettysburg").
- **July 2:** march resumed at 3 a. m. (Brooke; the 111th NY, Seeley), 4 a. m. (Smyth places his brigade on the hills; 1st DE arrives about 4 a. m.), 4.10 a. m. (64th NY, which crosses the Taneytown road at 6.10 a. m. and forms at 7 a. m. "a half mile southwest of Cemetery Hill"), 4.30 a. m. (Kelly), "at daylight" (Hancock, Hazard), 5 a. m. (59th NY "on the road leading to Gettysburg"; 7th Mich "on the Gettysburg road"). Arrival: Part 3 D2099 (6.15 a. m.) "The Second Corps is up"; Hancock "about 7 a. m."; brigade times 6 a. m. (145th PA, 2nd DE), 6.30 a. m. (Webb on "Granite Ridge"), 7 a. m., 8 a. m. (53rd PA, Carroll's brigade formed "between Woodruff's battery on the left and the Taney town road on the right"), about 10 a. m. (52nd NY, 69th NY). Position on arrival: Third Division (Hays) on the right "near the cemetery", Second (Gibbon) in the centre, First (Caldwell) on the left; Hays: "moving on the Taneytown road, arrived within about a mile of the town ... a ridge nearly parallel with the road, facing westward".
- **Departure (July 4-5):** army circular D1099 sends "The Twelfth and Second Corps, via Taneytown, Middleburg, and Woodsborough, through Frederick to Middletown". Brigade departures from the field on July 5 at noon (Webb, 53rd PA), about 3 p. m. (2nd DE), 4 p. m. (Brooke, 64th NY, 145th PA), 4.30 p. m. (Kelly), 5 p. m. (Oliver, 145th PA), to Two Taverns by the Baltimore pike (53rd PA, 145th PA name it); 116th PA (Mulholland) has the march on the afternoon of July 4. Not resolved.

The OR has Smyth's brigade "placed in position on the hills overlooking the town" at 4 a. m. and Dent's 1st Delaware arriving "about 4 a. m." (Third Division, gate floor 00:00 Jul 2: no contradiction); Gate us-2c-2d (06:00, EPP): OR 2nd DE in line "about 6 a. m.", 145th PA 6 a. m., Webb 6.30 a. m., 64th NY 7 a. m. (not before 06:00). No OR passage supports 22:30 Jul 1 on the field; the corps night bivouac was 2-6 miles out until about 3-4.30 a. m. Q04 is not contradicted; the 4 a. m. times are listed so that the owner sees them.
2. Ledger night position "Jun 30: Taneytown": the OR (Hancock R076 p.367; Webb; Hays) has the corps in camp at Uniontown on June 30 and at Taneytown only on July 1 about 11 a. m. "Jun 29: gap": the OR gives Uniontown (halt 1 mile beyond, 10 p. m.).
3. placement.json PO-22 (71st PA "sent to Culp's Hill" at night): the OR sends the 71st PA with the 106th PA to the Eleventh Corps front near the cemetery (Hancock R076 p.372; Webb R104; Smith R106), not to Slocum's front; no OR passage puts the 71st PA at Culp's Hill.
4. PO-21 (Carroll to East Cemetery Hill about 20:00): OR "about dark" (Carroll), "nearly dark" (Hancock), 7.30 p. m. (4th Ohio), "about 8 o'clock" (7th WV), 9.30 p. m. (Howard: old position resumed, Carroll between Ames and Wadsworth).

6. PO-05/AC-03/PO-26 (III Corps), PO-36..PO-41 (XII Corps): see the helper notes below.

## Third and Twelfth Corps: headline findings (from the helper notes appended below; full detail and claim ids there)
